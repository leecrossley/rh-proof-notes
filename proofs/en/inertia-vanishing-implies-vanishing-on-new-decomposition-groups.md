# Inertia vanishing implies vanishing on new decomposition groups

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E6%83%AF%E6%80%A7%E7%BE%A4%E4%B8%8A%E7%9A%84%E6%B6%88%E5%A4%B1%E8%83%BD%E4%BC%A0%E5%88%B0%E6%96%B0%E7%9A%84%E5%88%86%E8%A7%A3%E7%BE%A4.md)

Let $\ell$ be prime, $O$ the integers of a finite extension $K/\mathbb Q_\ell$, $e\ge1$, and $\psi:G_{\mathbb Q}\to O^\times$ continuous with $\psi^n=1$ for $n\ge1$. Put $A=(K/O)(\psi^{-1}\varepsilon_\ell^{-e})$. For any place over $p\ne\ell$ let $D_p,I_p$ be decomposition and inertia. The kernel of $H^1(D_p,A)\to H^1(I_p,A)$ has $O$-length at most $v_\ell(n)v_O(\ell)$, and is zero if $\psi|_{I_p}=1$. [`Chr.locUnr`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/CharacterLevelBounds/LocUnr.lean#L38)

**Proof.** Subtract a coboundary to make a cocycle in the kernel zero on inertia. Then $z(gi)=z(g)$, and comparison of $ig=g(g^{-1}ig)$ shows $z(g)\in M=A^{I_p}$. Two such representatives differ by a coboundary with primitive in $M$. Thus the kernel is $H^1(D_p/I_p,M)$, which injects into $M/(F-1)M$ by evaluation at arithmetic Frobenius $F$. Its powers are dense and determine a continuous cocycle, and coboundary values are exactly $(F-1)M$.

If $\psi$ is trivial on inertia, $M=A$ since the cyclotomic character is unramified there. Frobenius acts by $b=\psi(F)^{-1}p^{-e}$; $b\ne1$ because raising to $n$ would give $p^{en}=1$. The nonzero scalar $b-1$ is surjective on $K/O$ by division in $K$, giving zero quotient. Otherwise choose $i\in I_p$ with $\zeta=\psi(i)^{-1}\ne1$. Then $\zeta-1$ kills $M$, and $\zeta-1\mid n$ by subtracting $n$ from the vanishing geometric sum $\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\zeta^j$. The kernel of $\zeta-1$ on $K/O$ has length $v_O(\zeta-1)$ by the cyclic quotient $O/(\zeta-1)$, so $M$ and its quotient have length $\le v_O(n)=v_\ell(n)v_O(\ell)$.

For $\psi$ pulled back from a Dirichlet character modulo $N$, global classes with zero restriction on every finite inertia and on decomposition at primes dividing $\ell N$ also satisfy the decomposition condition at any prescribed finite set $S$. At $p\mid\ell N$ this was assumed. At $p\nmid\ell N$ the character is unramified, so the established injection applies to an already inertia-zero class. This inclusion gives the length comparison in the [rank-four setup](cases-ii-and-iii-share-the-rank-four-length-method.md).
