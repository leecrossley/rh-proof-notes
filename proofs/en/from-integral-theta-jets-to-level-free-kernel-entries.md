# From integral theta jets to level-free kernel entries

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Work with a tied kernel datum $t$ satisfying its arithmetic and theta hypotheses (`t.Hyp`), and assume the host splitting bound is at least $3$. Fix a word $u$, tuple $\mu$, and place $w$ of residue characteristic $p$. The place is level-free if $p$ differs from the word prime and from each of the two auxiliary primes and is absent from the finite set of tuple primes. Suppose $p\ne d$, with $d$ the prime discriminant parameter. For $0\le i<m$ and $N_iN_{i+1}$ even, the conclusion `JetIntegralityBis` is that every coefficient defining the bound `EntGe` for the entry at $u,\mu,w,i$ has $p$-adic valuation at least zero. The proof gives the estimate at this stage for every pair of column classes.

Let $L,L'$ be the two lattices of such a pair. At $w$ the [finite coset kernel formula](reading-the-actual-kernel-as-an-integral-theta-combination.md) gives an integral combination of natural theta translates. At $p=3$, splitting gives $\left(\frac{-d}{3}\right)=1$, hence $d\equiv2\bmod3$. So the [directional integrality result](theta-jets-on-global-homomorphism-lattice-directions.md) applies: after substitution by any list of directions from $Y=A_0\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O_E}(L,L')$ under the simultaneous embeddings $\rho_\tau$, every jet is integral. This property extends to the $R=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_p}$-span of the simultaneously embedded directions by the chain rule for integral linear substitutions.

The coordinates used in the actual entry change the bases at each $\tau$. Let $B_\tau,B'_\tau$ be the matrices of those local bases. In the source, a matrix qualifies as a basis if it is invertible, the lattice vectors have integral coordinates in it, and its columns are finite $R$-linear combinations of the embedded lattice vectors. Let $\gamma_\tau$ be the transported scalar `dgen`. This scalar has the following integral support property. Over a number field $M$ containing all the complex embeddings of $E$ in use, the vector supported at $\tau$, with value $\gamma_\tau$ before transport, belongs to the $\mathcal O_M$-span of the simultaneous embeddings of the fractional ideal $A_0$. This follows from the definition of `IsDgen`: the generator is in the intersection of that span with the $\tau$-axis. After embedding, write the supported vector as $\sum_j u_j(\rho_\lambda(a_j))_\lambda$, with $u_j\in R,\ a_j\in A_0$.

For a pair $a,b$ of input and output indices the matrix unit in the prescribed bases is $B'_\tau E_{ba}B_\tau^{-1}$. It lies in the $R$-span of embedded **global** lattice homomorphisms at $\tau$. To see this, first choose $E$-valued matrices $P,P'$ giving bases after localization at the prime of $E$ induced by $\rho_\tau$. Such bases exist because the localized lattices are finite free over a discrete valuation ring. For each elementary matrix $E_{kj}$, the rational homomorphism $P' E_{kj}P^{-1}$ carries the source into the target after localization. Multiply by a scalar $s_{kj}\in\mathcal O_E$ outside that prime to clear denominators on a finite generating set of $L$. The resulting $A_{kj}$ maps $L$ to $L'$, and $\rho_\tau(s_{kj})$ is a unit. The two matrices $\rho_\tau(P')^{-1}B'_\tau$ and $B_\tau^{-1}\rho_\tau(P)$ have entries in $R$. This follows by expressing either set of basis columns in the other spanning set over $R$. Expanding the product $B'_\tau E_{ba}B_\tau^{-1}$ in the elementary units of the bases $\rho_\tau(P),\rho_\tau(P')$ therefore expresses it as $\sum_k v_k \rho_\tau(A_k)$ with $v_k\in R$, absorbing the unit denominators.

Multiply the two finite expansions. The direction supported at $\tau$ with matrix $\gamma_\tau B'_\tau E_{ba}B_\tau^{-1}$ is $\sum_{j,k} u_j v_k (\rho_\lambda(a_j A_k))_\lambda$. Since $a_j A_k\in Y$, this is an integral direction for the jet estimate. The same conclusion holds after multiplication by the prescribed coordinate scalar of norm one, `c0`. It also holds for every scaling $\kappa_\tau$ satisfying $|\kappa_\tau|\le|\gamma_\tau|$, by multiplying by $\kappa_\tau/\gamma_\tau$ when $\gamma_\tau\ne0$ and taking the zero direction otherwise. These are exactly the one-variable columns of the linear substitution defining the actual series. Its jets are therefore integral.

Finally the kernel operator in `KvalGe` is formed by differentiating against a product of minors with integral coefficients. Each of its coefficients is a finite sum of terms of the form $c_m m![X^m]\Theta$ with $c_m\in\mathbb Z$. To see this, expand the product of minors into monomials, apply the derivatives in the substituted variables, and extract a coefficient in the remaining variables. A derivative monomial with multi-index $m$ extracts $m!$ times the coefficient of $X^m$. Thus every sum has norm at most one. By the definition of `EntGe` this proves the required bound. The stronger bounds needed on signal primes and at $d$ use the orbit and ramified determinant estimates separately.
