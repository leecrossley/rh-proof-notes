# A cross-multiplied polynomial identity splits off character lines

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

## Statement

Let $G$ be a group and $K$ an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. Let $r$ of dimension $n+t$ and $r'$ of dimension $d'$ be semisimple representations of $G$ over $K$. Let $a_1,\dots,a_t$ and $a'_1,\dots,a'_{t'}$ be characters $G\to K^\times$, with $a_i\ne a'_j$ as functions for every $i,j$. Repetitions within either list are permitted. For every $g\in G$ suppose
$$
\operatorname{charpoly}(r(g))\prod_{j=1}^{t'}(X-a'_j(g))
 =\operatorname{charpoly}(r'(g))\prod_{i=1}^t(X-a_i(g)).
\tag{1}
$$
Then there is a semisimple representation $s$ of dimension $n$ such that $r\simeq s\oplus\bigoplus_{i=1}^t a_i$. If $G,K$ carry topologies with continuous group and field operations and $r$ is continuous, $s$ is continuous. In the Galois setting of `PeelAll`, the $a_i$ are also continuous. At every place where $r$ and the $a_i$ have the specified local comparison, the same $s$ retains the local comparison after removing the $a_i$'s eigenvalues and filtration jumps, with multiplicities. The precise local assertion is given below. ## Finding an invariant line

Write $D=\bigoplus_i a_i$ and $D'=\bigoplus_j a'_j$. The two representations $r\oplus D'$ and $r'\oplus D$ are semisimple. Equation (1) is equality of their characteristic polynomials on each $g$, by the block diagonal determinant formula. Their dimensions agree by taking degrees at the identity. They are isomorphic by the character criterion, for which the following argument suffices. The linear span of the combined image of $G$ on their direct sum is an algebra, as it is closed under products. It is finite dimensional and acts faithfully on a semisimple module, so its Jacobson radical is zero. Over $K$ it is a product of matrix algebras. Equality of trace on each group element gives equality of trace on the whole algebra. On the central identity of each factor the trace is the multiplicity of its simple module times the dimension of that module. Characteristic zero then gives identical multiplicities. [Applying the character criterion to transfer a common eigenline](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/SplitLineSubtraction/PEigTransfer.lean#L17)

If $t>0$, the corresponding $a_t$-line of $D$ now supplies a nonzero vector $(v,v')$ in $r\oplus D'$ on which every element acts by $a_t$. For every coordinate indexed by $j$ in $D'$, there exists some $g$ with $a'_j(g)\ne a_t(g)$. The eigenvector equation for that element forces the coordinate to vanish. Therefore $v$ is nonzero and spans an invariant line of $r$ with character $a_t$. This step uses disjointness of the characters as functions, with the choice of $g$ allowed to depend on the coordinate. Semisimplicity splits the line off, so $r\simeq s_1\oplus a_t$ with semisimple $s_1$ of dimension $n+t-1$. If $r$ is continuous, $s_1$ is continuous because its entries are a block of a fixed conjugate of the matrix of $r(g)$, with the inverse block obtained by evaluating at $g^{-1}$. [The last line is a constituent of the first representation](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/SplitLineSubtraction/PPeelOne.lean#L13)

Substitute the direct sum into (1) and cancel the monic factor $X-a_t(g)$ on both sides separately for each $g$. The result has the same form with $s_1$ in place of $r$ and the initial $t-1$ entries of the $a$ list. Induct, with the case $t=0$ taking $s=r$. This constructs one $s$ independently of the place where it will be used. [Induction through the list](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/SplitLineSubtraction/PPeelAll.lean#L17)

## Preserving the local comparison

For this part let $G=\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/E)$ for a number field $E$ with algebraic closure $\Omega$, let $K=\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ for a prime $\ell$, and choose $\iota:K\simeq\mathbb C$. Write $C_w(u;S,J)$ for the project's `Cl` comparison for a continuous representation $u$ at a finite place $w$ with complex eigenvalue multiset $S$ and a multiset $J_\tau$ at each embedding $\tau:E\to K$. When $w\nmid\ell$ it demands that $u$ be unramified at $w$ and that the inverse of a lift of the residue-field Frobenius power map have eigenvalues $S$ under $\iota$. At every compatible place bridge above $\ell$, it demands that $u$ be crystalline and de Rham, that its filtration jumps at each induced embedding be $J_\tau$, and that its Weil–Deligne characteristic polynomial at $g$ be
$$
P_S(g;X)=\prod_{z\in S}(X-z^{-\deg g}).
$$
The period assertion is for all compatible period rings, all extensions of the valuation to $\Omega$, and all inducing bridges there. It is vacuous at a place not above $\ell$. [Definition of the local comparison](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/SplitLineSubtraction/RowDefOfOdd.lean#L33)

Suppose for some $w,S,J$ and given $z_i\in\mathbb C^\times$, integer functions $m_i(\tau)$, one has $C_w(a_i;\{z_i\},\{m_i\})$ for each $i$ and
$$
 C_w\left(r;\ S\sqcup\{z_i\}_{i=1}^t,\quad
             (J_\tau\sqcup\{m_i(\tau)\}_{i=1}^t)_\tau\right).
$$
The conclusion is $C_w(s;S,J)$ for the $s$ already constructed.

For a single removal, identify $r$ with $s_1\oplus a_t$ by its invariant decomposition. Away from $\ell$, inertia is trivial on the sum and hence on $s_1$. The block diagonal Frobenius polynomial factors, and cancelling $X-z_t$ gives exactly the remaining eigenvalue multiset. Above $\ell$, the tensor product with the de Rham period ring and the invariants both commute with direct sums. Their dimensions are bounded above by the two representation dimensions and the sum attains equality, so each summand is de Rham. The induced filtrations are direct sums, so their jump multiplicities add. Subtract those of the character to get the required jumps of $s_1$. [Removing one character from the period comparison](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/SplitLineSubtraction/PClSub.lean#L41)

The proof next uses the $p$-adic monodromy theorem, in the form that a finite-dimensional continuous de Rham representation of the absolute Galois group of a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_\ell$ becomes semistable over a finite extension of that local field. This applies to each summand and to their sum. Working over one common extension, the semistable period module and its descent action commute with direct sums, because the period ring tensor product, invariants and semilinear operators act componentwise. The two Weil–Deligne parameters therefore sum to that of $r$. The crystalline condition for $r$ makes this sum unramified with zero monodromy. Both properties pass to $s_1$, which makes $s_1$ crystalline via the characterization of the crystalline representations among the potentially semistable ones. Multiplying the Weil characteristic polynomials for a direct sum and cancelling $X-z_t^{-\deg g}$ gives exactly $P_S(g;X)$ with the remaining entries. Iterating removes the further entries, giving $C_w(s;S,J)$.
