# Small trace errors give integral lattice congruences

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E8%BF%B9%E7%9A%84%E5%B0%8F%E8%AF%AF%E5%B7%AE%E8%83%BD%E7%BB%99%E5%87%BA%E6%95%B4%E6%95%B0%E6%A0%BC%E4%B8%8A%E7%9A%84%E5%90%8C%E4%BD%99.md)

## Statement

Let $G$ be compact and $r:G\to\mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ continuous semisimple. Let $\Lambda$ be the $\mathbb Z_\ell$-span of $r(G)$, using finite combinations. There is $c\ge0$ depending on $r$ such that for $N\ge0$ and $X\in\Lambda$,
$$
\big(\forall g,\ |\operatorname{tr}(Xr(g))|_\ell\le\ell^{-N-c}\big)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad X\in\ell^N\Lambda.
$$

## Proof

Choose a [finite coefficient field](a-compact-group-representation-uses-one-finite-coefficient-field.md) $L$. Compactness bounds all entries in $\ell^{-b}\mathcal O_L$ for some $b$, so $\Lambda$ is a submodule of a finite free $\mathbb Z_\ell$-module. It is finite free and generated by finitely many $r(g_j)$. Put $W=\mathbb Q_\ell\Lambda$ with the coordinate maximum norm in a fixed lattice basis. Then
$$
E:W\longrightarrow L^s,\quad X\longmapsto(\operatorname{tr}(Xr(g_j)))_j
$$
is injective. An element of the kernel pairs to zero with all $r(g)$, and the [semisimple trace pairing](the-limit-trace-preserves-the-common-inertia-kernel.md) is nondegenerate on the full scalar span. On the compact unit sphere in the lattice coordinates the norm of $E$ has positive minimum, because it never vanishes there. Scaling yields $\|X\|_\Lambda\le C\|E(X)\|$ and the zero-dimensional case is immediate. Choose $c$ with $C\le\ell^c$. The hypothesis forces $\|X\|_\Lambda\le\ell^{-N}$, precisely the required divisibility.

If $r$ is approximated by seed traces to error $\ell^{-N-c}$ and $H=\sum_h a_h[h]\in\mathbb Z_\ell[G]$ satisfies $\rho_k(H)=0$ for each seed, then $\operatorname{tr}\rho_k(Hg)=0$ for all $g$. Approximate the trace of $r(hg)$ term by term and use $|a_h|_\ell\le1$ and the ultrametric inequality. This gives the required small trace for $X=r(H)$ and thus $r(H)\in\ell^N\Lambda$. Exact relations on seeds therefore become integral congruences on the target.
