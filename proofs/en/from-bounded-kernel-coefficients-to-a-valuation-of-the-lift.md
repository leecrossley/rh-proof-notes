# From bounded kernel coefficients to a valuation of the lift

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix a word $u$, a used tuple $\mu$, a level-free place $w$ above $p$, and $i<m$. Let $M_i,M_{i+1}$ be the source and target spaces of column forms of the chosen twisted family over their common coefficient field $K$. Write $\Lambda_{i,w}$ and $\Lambda_{i+1,w}$ for the lattices over the valuation ring at $w$. Use the integral additive valuation $v_w$ on $K$ and put $e_w=v_w(p)$, so the valuations in $\mathbb C_p$ transported along $w$ use $v_w/e_w$. The operator $K_i$ takes a family of class forms $(f_c)_c$ to the family with $c'$-component
$$
(K_i f)_{c'}=\sum_c \frac{1}{n_c}\,K_{i,c,c'}f_c,
$$
where $n_c>0$ is the order of the source stabilizer. Assume all $n_c$ divide a positive integer $n$.

The inputs from the tied kernel are as follows. Each class form of an integral source vector has a rational polynomial representative whose evaluations on the source minors have norm at most $1$. The maps $K_{i,c,c'}$ carry those rational representatives into rational representatives in the target, and the full operator respects the prescribed target space of column forms. The period identity for a pair of classes says that target evaluations on its minors are, up to a scalar unit, evaluations of the source representative on the polynomial $Q_I$ obtained from the determinant jet for that pair. The target evaluation values on these rational representatives lie in the image of $K$ in $\mathbb C_p$. Finally the target local lattice consists of those forms in the target space admitting representatives with integral evaluations on all target minors. These are the rationality, unit, kernel identity, and lattice conditions in the theorem.

Suppose the determinant-jet coefficients for every class pair satisfy the bound $v_p\ge B$. The conclusion is that for every source lattice vector $x$, the target vector can be represented as $K_i x=bz$ with $z\in\Lambda_{i+1,w}$ and $v_w(b)/e_w\ge B-v_p(n)$, where the zero image is allowed.

By [integral projection onto the minor span](determinant-jets-have-bounded-coefficients-in-the-minor-span.md), $Q_I=\sum_J c_J M_J$ for the source minors $M_J$, with $|c_J|_p\le p^{-B}$. Each evaluation of the source representative on $M_J$ has norm at most $1$. Therefore the value on $Q_I$, and hence the evaluation of the pairwise target representative on a target minor $I$, has norm at most $p^{-B}$. Because these target values come from $K$, their $v_w$ valuations are at least $\lceil e_w B\rceil$. Choose $a\in K^\times$ of valuation $\lceil e_wB\rceil$ using the value group $\mathbb Z$. Dividing each target representative by $a$ makes all those evaluations integral. This uses the value group of $K$ even when $e_wB$ is nonintegral or negative.

Write $n=p^k n'$ with $k=v_p(n)$ and $n'$ prime to $p$. Then $n_c^{-1}=p^{-k}r_c$, where $r_c=(n/n_c)/n'$ is in the valuation ring at $w$. For each $c'$, multiply the $a^{-1}$-scaled target representative of every $K_{i,c,c'}f_c$ by $r_c$ and sum in $c$. The resulting representative has integral evaluations by linearity and the ultrametric inequality. Its evaluated form is $(ap^{-k})^{-1}$ times the $c'$-component of $K_ix$. All these components form a member of the prescribed target space, since $K_ix$ does and the space is $K$-linear. The target lattice condition therefore places this scaled family in $\Lambda_{i+1,w}$. Taking $b=ap^{-k}$ proves the stated valuation.

In the application one normally takes $n=\mathrm{DGam}$, a common multiple of the stabilizer orders. The numerical valuation $v_{\mathrm{DG}}=v_p(\mathrm{DGam})$ agrees in the base and twisted chains by the condition `DGamDef`. The parameter $\mathrm{sp}$ is $0$ or $1$; the latter choice includes the hypothesis that the initial-layer stabilizers for used tuples are trivial. At $i=0$ with $\mathrm{sp}=1$ one uses $n=1$. The loss is consequently $v_{\mathrm{DG}}$ for $i>0$ and $(1-\mathrm{sp})v_{\mathrm{DG}}$ for $i=0$. At the degree-zero tuple the identifications with the untwisted chain respect both the operator and the localized lattices, so they transport the same bound without a further cost.

These implications assume the tied kernel identities and rational map on representatives. They explain how to use those statements to obtain the lift bound once supplied. The separate construction of the rationality and normalization data is used through `KmapRat` and the field and lattice compatibility of the tied kernel.
