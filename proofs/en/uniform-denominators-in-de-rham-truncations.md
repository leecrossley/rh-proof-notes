# Uniform denominators in de Rham truncations

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E6%99%B6%E4%BD%93%E6%95%B4%E7%8E%AF%E5%9C%A8%E6%AF%8F%E5%B1%82deRham%E6%88%AA%E6%96%AD%E9%83%BD%E6%9C%89%E7%BB%9F%E4%B8%80%E5%88%86%E6%AF%8D.md)

Let $A=A_{\inf}=W(\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_\ell^\flat})$, $P=A[1/\ell]$, and $(\xi)=\ker\theta$ for the Fontaine map, $\xi\ne0$. Let $A_{\rm cris}^0\subset P$ be generated over $A$ by all $\xi^j/j!$, $A_{\rm cris}$ its $\ell$-adic completion, and $B_{\rm dR}^+=\varprojlim_m P/(\xi^m)$. There is a comparison $j:A_{\rm cris}\to B_{\rm dR}^+$ extending the original map. For every $m\ge0$ and $x\in A_{\rm cris}$, there is $a\in A$ with $\ell^m j(x)\equiv a\bmod{\rm Fil}^m$, where ${\rm Fil}^m$ is the kernel of projection onto $P/(\xi^m)$. Also $B_m=A/(\xi^m)$ is separated and complete for powers of $\ell$ and injects into $P/(\xi^m)$. **Proof.** The [Fontaine kernel description](a-cyclotomic-generator-of-the-fontaine-kernel.md) embeds $A/(\xi)$ in $\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_\ell}$, so it has no $\ell$-torsion. If $\xi^m\mid\ell a$ for $m>0$, first get $\xi\mid a$, cancel $\xi$ in the domain $A$, then induct. Repeat to cancel any power of $\ell$; this gives the asserted injection.

For $\eta=\xi^m$, suppose $a=\eta b_n+\ell^nc_n$ for every $n$. Then $\eta(b_n-b_k)\in\ell^n A$ for $k\ge n$. The cancelling result makes the coefficient of $\ell^n$ divisible by $\eta$; cancelling $\eta$ shows $b_n-b_k\in\ell^n A$. Thus $b_n$ converges to $b$ and $a=\eta b$, proving separatedness. To lift a Cauchy approximation in $B_m$ with successive differences in $\ell^n B_m$, choose representatives successively, subtracting an $\eta$-multiple to make their successive differences lie in $\ell^n A$. Take their limit in $A$ and reduce to $B_m$. The case $m=0$ has zero quotient. Let $R_m$ be the image of $A_{\rm cris}^0$ in $P/(\xi^m)$. Products of divided powers are integral binomial coefficients times divided powers, so those powers span over $A$. At this level powers of degree $\ge m$ vanish, and $v_\ell(j!)\le j<m$ for the others. Consequently $\ell^mR_m\subset B_m\subset R_m$, giving the uniform bound. For each $n$, $B_m\cap\ell^{n+m}R_m\subset\ell^nB_m$, so the induced and intrinsic $\ell$-adic topologies of $B_m$ agree. The subgroup $B_m$ contains $\ell^mR_m$; subtracting a sufficiently late term puts the tail of each Cauchy sequence in $B_m$, where it converges. An element in all $\ell^n R_m$ lies in all $\ell^n B_m$, hence is zero. Thus $R_m$ is complete and separated.

Extend the projection $A_{\rm cris}^0\to R_m$ to $A_{\rm cris}$ by limits. The extensions agree with the transition maps by density and separatedness, so their inverse limit gives $j$. Each image in $R_m$ enters $B_m$ upon multiplication by $\ell^m$, which is the congruence. This is the bounded comparison used in [closing the filtration condition](a-complete-normed-model-from-the-crystalline-integral-ring.md).
