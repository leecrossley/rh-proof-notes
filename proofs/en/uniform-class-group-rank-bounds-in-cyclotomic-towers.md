# Uniform class group rank bounds in cyclotomic towers

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E5%88%86%E5%9C%86%E5%A1%94%E7%9A%84%E7%B1%BB%E7%BE%A4%E7%A7%A9%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E5%BE%97%E5%88%B0%E7%BB%9F%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%8A%E7%95%8C.md)

## Statement

Let $\ell$ be prime and $K/\mathbb Q$ finite abelian containing $\mu_\ell$, or $\mu_4$ when $\ell=2$. For $K_n=K(\mu_{\ell^n})$ some integer $r\ge0$ satisfies $|\mathrm{Cl}(O_{K_n})/\ell\,\mathrm{Cl}(O_{K_n})|\le\ell^r$ for every $n\ge0$. This is `Published.FerreroWashington`, proved by `FW.ferreroWashington`.

## A nilpotent action on the minus part

Suppose a finite abelian group $G$ acts on a finite abelian group $C$, with $J^2=1$ and $\gamma\in G$. Write $D=(1-J)C$, $T=C[\ell]$, and $B=|C/(\ell C+(1-\gamma)C)|$. Suppose $\theta(1-[J])C=0$ and $([\gamma]-1)^N\in(\theta,1+[J],\ell)$. Multiplying the relation by $1-[J]$ shows $U=1-\gamma$ acts with $U^N=0$ on $A=D/\ell D$. Moreover $A/UA$ is a quotient of $C/(\ell C+(1-\gamma)C)$ via $1-J$, so its size is at most $B$. Each map $U^i A\to U^{i+1}A$ has kernel size at most $|\ker U|=|A/UA|$. Multiply these bounds to get $|A|\le B^N$. The kernel and cokernel of multiplication by $\ell$ on $D$ have equal size, so $|D\cap T|\le B^N$.

## Uniform inputs on full cyclotomic fields

Fix $f>1$ prime to $\ell$, take $L=\mathbb Q(\mu_{f\ell^n})$, $M=f\ell^n$, and $J=-1$. Let $G$ act on ideals by the inverse Galois label. Take $c=2f\ell+1$. The [group-ring nilpotence argument](group-ring-nonvanishing-gives-a-uniform-nilpotence-exponent.md) supplies $N,n_0$ and $\gamma=1+f\ell^e$ with the ideal membership for $\theta=\theta_H$ at all $n\ge n_0$. Also $\theta_H(1-[J])=\sum_s g(s)[s]$. Apply the [Stickelberger coefficient criterion](jacobi-sum-factorization-annihilates-the-class-group.md) with coefficients $c,-(c+1)/2,-(c-1)/2$ at $c^{-1},1,-1$, adding at coincident positions. Indeed $c\langle c^{-1}t\rangle_M-\langle t\rangle_M$ is $M$ times the indicated floor, and $\langle-t\rangle_M=M-\langle t\rangle_M$. Thus the coefficient divided by $M$ is $g(t)$, proving annihilation of $(1-J)C$.

To bound $B$ uniformly, take $F=\mathbb Q(\mu_{f\ell^e})$ with $L/F$ cyclic generated by $\gamma$. By class field theory $B$ counts mod-$\ell$ unramified characters of $U=G_L$ invariant under $H=G_F$. Their common kernel $P$ is open in $U$ and normal in $H$. The finite $H/P$ has central subgroup $U/P$ and cyclic quotient, hence is abelian. The number of mod-$\ell$ characters of $U/P$ is at most that of $H/P$, by equality with the sizes of their $\ell$-torsion and inclusion of those torsion subgroups. Outside $\ell$, inertia in $H$ lies in $U$ and maps trivially to $H/P$ since $L/F$ is unramified there and these characters kill inertia. The characters of $H/P$ are thus among the fixed finite set of mod-$\ell$ characters of $F$ unramified outside $\ell$. To see finiteness, $F$ contains $\mu_\ell$ and Kummer classes for such characters have outside-$S$ valuations divisible by $\ell$, with $S$ the places over $\ell$. Divide the outside divisor by $\ell$ to land in the $\ell$-torsion of the $S$-class group, and the kernel consists of $S$-units modulo $\ell$th powers. Both groups are finite. Hence $B\le B_0$ uniformly and $|D\cap T|\le B_0^N$.

## The whole class group

For odd $\ell$, put $A=\ker(1+J)$ and consider the $J$-invariant mod-$\ell$ class characters, counted by $|C/(\ell C+D)|$. A Kummer class $a$ has $(a)=I^\ell$ by being unramified, giving $[I]\in C[\ell]$. Under $J$ the Kummer class is inverted, since the character is invariant and $J$ inverts $\mu_\ell$. Explicitly, if $\beta^\ell=a$, then $g(J\beta)/(J\beta)=\zeta_\ell^{-\phi(J^{-1}gJ)}$ for its character $\phi$. Hence $[I]\in A\cap T$. The kernel of this divisor map is contained in the minus part of units modulo $\ell$th powers. For a CM unit $u$, $u/Ju$ has all absolute values one and is a root of unity. As $1-J$ acts by two on the minus part, invertible modulo odd $\ell$, all minus unit classes come from roots of unity, giving kernel size at most $\ell$. Thus $|C/(\ell C+D)|\le\ell |A\cap T|$. For $x\in A\cap T$, $x=(1-J)(vx)$ with $2v\equiv1\bmod\ell$, so $A\cap T\subseteq D\cap T$. Also $[D:D\cap\ell C]\le[D:\ell D]=|D\cap T|$. Factor the index to get $|C/\ell C|\le\ell |D\cap T|^2$.

At $\ell=2$, the map $1-J$ on $T$ has image in $D\cap T$ and kernel $A\cap T$. The map from $A\cap T$ to $A/D$ has kernel $D\cap T$. It follows that $|C/2C|=|T|\le[A:D]|D\cap T|^2$. The [signature bound](real-unit-signatures-bound-the-two-primary-class-quotient.md) gives $[A:D]\le2^{N[G:\langle\gamma\rangle]}$ with index $\varphi(4f)$. Thus in both cases $|C/\ell C|\le B_1$ independently of $n\ge n_0$.

## Descent to $K_n$

By Kronecker–Weber place $K$ in $\mathbb Q(\mu_{f_0\ell^a})$ with $(f_0,\ell)=1$ and use the bound for $f=f_0(\ell+1)$. Then $K_n\subseteq L=\mathbb Q(\mu_{f\ell^{n+a+n_0}})$ and $[K_n:\mathbb Q]\ge\varphi(\ell^n)$. The degree formula, including $n=0$, gives $[L:K_n]\le f\ell^{a+n_0}$. Restrict mod-$\ell$ class characters from $K_n$ to $L$. The image is unramified and has size at most $B_1$. The kernel factors through the quotient by the normal closure of $G_L$, of order at most $[L:K_n]$, so has at most that many characters. Therefore $f\ell^{a+n_0}B_1$ bounds the class quotient uniformly. Take $\ell^r$ at least this large. This supplies the [finite-layer rank input](how-finite-layer-class-groups-bound-tower-generators.md).
