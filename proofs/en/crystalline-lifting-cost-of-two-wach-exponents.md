# Why crystalline lifting uses the Wach exponent twice

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E5%88%86%E8%A3%82%E7%B4%A0%E5%A4%84%E7%9A%84%E6%99%B6%E4%BD%93%E6%8F%90%E5%8D%87%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E8%A6%81%E4%BB%98%E4%B8%A4%E4%BB%BDWach%E6%8C%87%E6%95%B0.md)

**Proposition.** Let $\ell$ be odd, $K/\mathbb Q_\ell$ finite unramified, $L/\mathbb Q_\ell$ a finite coefficient field containing the images of all embeddings of $K(\mu_\ell)$, with ring $\mathcal O$, and $G=G_K$. Let $T_1,T_2$ be stable full lattices in continuous finite-dimensional crystalline $L$-representations and $F:T_1\to T_2$ an equivariant surjection. Assume the source jumps at all embeddings lie in $[-M,h]$, with $M,h\ge0$ integers. Let $e_W,e_U$ be the exponents from the [filtration-window formula](local-lifting-exponent-from-the-filtration-window.md). Under the comparison inputs below,
$$
\ell^{2e_W+e_U}H^1_f(G,T_2)\subseteq F_* H^1_f(G,T_1).
$$
The finite condition on a lattice is that its rational extension to the crystalline period ring is a coboundary.

## Local comparison inputs

The étale module $D(T)$ has commuting $\varphi,\gamma$ and its Wach submodule is $N(T)$. Work on invariants under the tame cyclotomic group $\Delta$ of order $\ell-1$. Put $X=\pi_0$, the invariant parameter, and $Q=X+\ell$. An allowed Herr pair $(y,z)$ satisfies $Q^My\in N(T)^\Delta$, $z\in XN(T)^\Delta$, with differential $(\gamma-1)y-(\varphi-1)z$.

1. The Wach cokernel of each lattice surjection with source jumps in the window is killed by $\ell^{e_W}$. This includes a crystalline extension onto the trivial lattice.
2. The Herr class map $\operatorname{cl}_T$ is natural, and an allowed pair with zero differential gives an integral finite class. For an extension by $T$ of the trivial lattice, the two differences of a lift of $\ell^e$ times its invariant Wach generator represent $\ell^e$ times the extension class, with the allowed denominators.
3. For $T_0=\ker F$, the comparison gives the exactness, saturation and rational lifting hypotheses in the [integral correction lemma](uniform-integral-correction-of-a-rational-herr-lift.md). The [graded and polynomial argument](killing-the-second-herr-obstruction.md) supplies the uniform annihilator $\ell^{e_U}$ for its second obstruction. The jumps of the kernel are among the source jumps, allowing the source polynomial and bound.

## The two applications of the Wach cokernel estimate

Write $\kappa\in H_f^1(G,T_2)$ as the class of a [crystalline extension](finite-cohomology-classes-as-crystalline-extensions.md) $0\to T_2\to T'\to\mathcal O\to0$. Since the target jumps lie in the source window by strict exactness and the added jump is $0$, the extension remains in that window. Choose $b\in N(T')$ lifting $\ell^{e_W}$ times the invariant Wach generator of the quotient. Average by $(\ell-1)^{-1}\sum_{\delta\in\Delta}\delta$ to make $b$ invariant without changing its image.

The two differences $(\varphi-1)b$, $(\gamma-1)b$ land in the kernel because the quotient generator is fixed. Write them as the coordinates $y,z$ there. Commutation of the operators gives $(\gamma-1)y-(\varphi-1)z=0$. Crystalline comparison gives $Q^My\in N(T_2)^\Delta$. The $\gamma-1$ difference is divisible by the cyclotomic parameter on $N$, whose invariant intersection is generated by $X$, so $z\in XN(T_2)^\Delta$. The comparison sends the pair to $\ell^{e_W}\kappa$. If the comparison uses a unit multiple of the quotient generator, first normalize the lift by its inverse.

Lift $\ell^{e_W}Q^My$ and $\ell^{e_W}(z/X)$ to the source Wach module using the same cokernel bound again. The resulting source pair has the desired coordinate image but its differential may be nonzero in the kernel complex. The third input kills this obstruction by $\ell^{e_U}$ and subtracts an allowed pair from the kernel. We obtain a closed allowed pair $(y_1,z_1)$ with image $\ell^{e_W+e_U}(y,z)$. Its finite class $\kappa_1=\operatorname{cl}_{T_1}(y_1,z_1)$ therefore satisfies
$$
F_*\kappa_1=\ell^{e_W+e_U}\operatorname{cl}_{T_2}(y,z)=\ell^{2e_W+e_U}\kappa
$$
by naturality. The first exponent writes the extension class in allowed integral coordinates, and the second lifts those coordinates, before the separate differential correction.
