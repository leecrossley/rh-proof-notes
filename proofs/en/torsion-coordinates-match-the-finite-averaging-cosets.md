# Torsion coordinates match the finite averaging cosets

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $K$ be the host imaginary quadratic field embedded in $\mathbb C$, and let $\Lambda,\Lambda'$ be the [original and conjugate CM period lattices](the-conjugate-cubic-has-a-normalized-cm-ideal-lattice.md) for an embedding $\rho:\overline{\mathbb Q}\to\mathbb C$ fixing $K$. Write $\phi:(\mathbb Q\Lambda)/\Lambda\to(\mathbb Q\Lambda')/\Lambda'$ for the resulting $\mathcal O_K$-linear isomorphism of torsion groups under the Weierstrass coordinates.

In the rational matrix space over $K$, use a $K$-basis $b_1,\ldots,b_r$ whose tuples under the complex embeddings are complex bases on both sides. When the bases are used for theta spaces, they are orthonormal for the relative trace form. All coordinates of a rational matrix in this basis are regarded as elements of $K\subset\mathbb C$. For a full $\mathcal O_K$-lattice $Y\subset K^r$, let $\Gamma_Y\subset\mathbb C^r$ be the subgroup generated by vectors $w y$ for $w\in\Lambda,\ y\in Y$. Define $\Gamma'_Y$ the same way with $\Lambda'$. This describes both the product basis lattice and the complex lattices generated by scalar periods times a host Hom module, after using the stated basis coordinates.

Choose $k>0$ such that
$$
k\mathcal O_K^r\subseteq Y\subseteq k^{-1}\mathcal O_K^r,
$$
and let $n>0$ be any multiple of $k$. For $c\in(\mathbb Q\Lambda)^r,\ c'\in(\mathbb Q\Lambda')^r$, say $c$ and $c'$ correspond at level $n$ if $\phi([c_j/n])=[c'_j/n]$ in every coordinate. Then correspondence preserves membership:
$$
c\in\Gamma_Y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad c'\in\Gamma'_Y.
\tag{1}
$$
Every $c$ admits a corresponding $c'$, and conversely. In particular choose $k$ simultaneously for two full lattices $Y_1,Y_2$. The relation induces a bijection between
$$
\Gamma_{Y_2}/(\Gamma_{Y_1}\cap\Gamma_{Y_2})
\quad\hbox{and}\quad
\Gamma'_{Y_2}/(\Gamma'_{Y_1}\cap\Gamma'_{Y_2}).
\tag{2}
$$
Hence representatives in (2) can be chosen to correspond at any prescribed level $n$ divisible by that $k$.

**A generator of the period lattice.** Suppose first $c=w y$ with $w\in\Lambda$ and $y\in Y$. Write $a=k y\in\mathcal O_K^r$, so $c_j/n=a_j w/(nk)$. Choose a representative $u'$ of $\phi([w/(nk)])$. Its class is killed by $nk$, since the source class is, so $w'=nk\,u'$ belongs to $\Lambda'$. Consequently $w' y\in\Gamma'_Y$. Its $j$-th coordinate divided by $n$ is $a_j u'$, which is the required image of $a_jw/(nk)$ by $\mathcal O_K$-linearity. Since correspondence is additive and compatible with negatives, summing these choices gives a corresponding element of $\Gamma'_Y$ for every element of $\Gamma_Y$. Reversing $\phi$ gives the analogous choice for every element of $\Gamma'_Y$.

**All lifts and cosets.** If $c'_1,c'_2$ correspond to the same $c$ at level $n$, they differ by an element of $n(\Lambda')^r$. This subgroup lies in $\Gamma'_Y$ because $k\mathcal O_K^r\subseteq Y$ and $k\mid n$. Starting from the chosen image of an element $c\in\Gamma_Y$ in the last paragraph, it follows that every corresponding vector belongs to $\Gamma'_Y$. Interchanging the two sides proves (1), as well as the existence of a corresponding vector for every rational tuple by choosing any lift of its $r$ classes. All classes in this reasoning are torsion, since the bounds on $Y$ put $\Gamma_Y$ inside $k^{-1}\Lambda^r$.

To compare quotient classes, if $c,d\in\Gamma_{Y_2}$ correspond to $c',d'$, then $c-d$ corresponds to $c'-d'$. Their representatives lie in the same coset precisely when $c-d\in\Gamma_{Y_1}$, which by (1) is equivalent to $c'-d'\in\Gamma'_{Y_1}$. Changing the lift of $c$ adds a vector in $n(\Lambda')^r$ and hence keeps the same coset. Every target representative has a corresponding source vector, so the relation on cosets is bijective. This proves (2).
