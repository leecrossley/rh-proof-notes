# Counting twisted characters with class groups and units

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E7%94%A8%E7%B1%BB%E7%BE%A4%E4%B8%8E%E5%8D%95%E4%BD%8D%E7%BE%A4%E6%95%B0%E5%87%BA%E6%89%AD%E6%9B%B2%E7%89%B9%E5%BE%81.md)

## Statement

Let $L$ be totally imaginary and $F/L$ finite cyclic generated by $\gamma$. Take a prime $\ell$ and $1\le c\le\nu$, with $c\ge2$ at two. Suppose $F$ contains a primitive $\ell^\nu$th root $\zeta$ with $\gamma(\zeta)=\zeta^{1+\ell^c}$ and $\operatorname{ord}(\gamma)=m=\ell^{\nu-c}$. Its orbit has length $m$, so $F=L(\zeta)$. These are `Lay`. Take $e\ge1$, $b,B\ge0$ and set $q=\ell^\nu$, $a=(1+\ell^c)^e$, $v=v_\ell(e)$. Write $\mathcal I$ for the free divisor group on finite primes of $F$. Condition `DivDel` on $\alpha$ means $\mathrm{div}(\alpha)=(\gamma^{-1}-a)\mathfrak d$ for some $\mathfrak d\in\mathcal I$. Define $N_a(\alpha)=\prod_{j=0}^{m-1}\gamma^j(\alpha)^{a^j}$. Assume `Few`: every finite family satisfying `DivDel` and having distinct classes of $N_a(\alpha)^{\ell^b}$ modulo $q$th powers has at most $B$ elements.

Let $E_{\gamma,a,q}$ consist of homomorphisms $\varphi:\mathrm{Cl}(F)\to\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z$ satisfying $\varphi(\gamma^{-1}P)=a\varphi(P)$. It is finite and
$$
\#E_{\gamma,a,q}\,q^{r_2(L)}\le B\ell^{(b+v)g+c+v},
\qquad g=2[L:\mathbb Q]+\lfloor\log_2 h_L\rfloor+2 .
$$
This is `SCount` in the [small-index bound](assembling-the-small-index-second-cohomology-bound.md).

## A finite group incorporating units and class groups

Write $Y=F^\times$ additively, $g_0=\gamma^{-1}$, $\delta=g_0-a$, and $Y_{\rm tor}$ for roots of unity. Set $U=\{y:\mathrm{div}(y)\in\delta\mathcal I\}$ and
$$
W=U/(\delta Y+Y_{\rm tor}),\quad Q=\mathrm{Cl}(F)/\delta\mathrm{Cl}(F),\quad
E_U=O_F^\times/(\delta O_F^\times+Y_{\rm tor}).
$$
Covariance means factoring through $Q$, so $\#E_{\gamma,a,q}\le\ell^{v_\ell(\#Q)}$ by the cyclic decomposition. The operator $\delta$ injects on $\mathcal I$: if $g_0x=ax$, iteration gives $(a^m-1)x=0$. Thus $\mathrm{div}(y)=\delta i$ determines $i$. Sending $y$ to its divisor preimage class gives
$$
0\to E_U\to W\to\mathrm{Cl}(F)[\delta]\to0.
$$
If $i$ is principal, subtract the $\delta$ of a generator to leave a unit. Classes killed by $\delta$ have a principal image, giving surjectivity. A unit killed in $W$ lies in $\delta O_F^\times+Y_{\rm tor}$ by injectivity on divisors. Since class group kernel and cokernel of $\delta$ have equal cardinalities, $\#W=\#E_U\cdot\#Q$.

## Unit factor

On the real vector space of units modulo roots of unity, Dirichlet's theorem identifies the action with the hyperplane of sum zero in the complex-place permutation representation. Each complex place of $L$ gives a cycle of length $m$. The absolute determinant of $\delta$ on this cycle is $a^m-1$. Removing the constant direction divides by $a-1$. The index of the image lattice is therefore
$$
\#E_U=\frac{(a^m-1)^{r_2(L)}}{a-1}.
$$
Binomial valuations give $v_\ell(a-1)=c+v$, $v_\ell(a^m-1)=\nu+v$. Thus $q^{r_2(L)}\le\ell^{v_\ell(\#E_U)+c+v}$ and
$$
\#E_{\gamma,a,q}\,q^{r_2(L)}\le\ell^{v_\ell(\#W)+c+v}. \tag{1}
$$

## Weighted norm comparison

The norm operator in additive notation is $\mathcal N=\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}a^j g_0^{m-j}$. Telescoping gives $\delta\mathcal N=\mathcal N\delta=(1-a^m)g_0$. Since $q\mid a^m-1$, the map $f:W\to Y/(qY+Y_{\rm tor})$ induced by $\ell^b\mathcal N$ is defined. Representatives of distinct image classes satisfy `DivDel` and have distinct norm power classes even modulo $qY$ alone, so $\#\operatorname{im}f\le B$.

For a kernel representative, in $Y/Y_{\rm tor}$ write $\ell^b\mathcal N y=qz$. Apply $\delta$ and cancel $q$ in this torsion-free group. With $u=(a^m-1)/q$ the result is $-u\ell^b g_0y=\delta z$. The invertible $g_0$ preserves $\delta Y$, so $u\ell^b$ kills the kernel class. Since $v_\ell(u)=v$, its $\ell$-primary subgroup has exponent at most $\ell^{b+v}$. If $\#W[\ell]\le\ell^g$, then filtering the kernel at powers of $\ell$ gives
$$
\ell^{v_\ell(\#W)}\le B\ell^{(b+v)g}. \tag{2}
$$

## Bounding the first torsion layer

In $Y/Y_{\rm tor}$, a representative $y$ of $W[\ell]$ satisfies $\ell y=\delta z$. The map to $z\bmod\ell$ is well defined since $\delta$ is injective on the torsion-free quotient, and changing $y$ adds an $\ell$th multiple to $z$. It is injective by cancellation. If $\mathrm{div}(y)=\delta i$, then $\mathrm{div}(z)=\ell i$. Also $z\bmod\ell$ is invariant under $\gamma$ since $a\equiv1\bmod\ell$.

Thus count invariant classes in $F^\times/((F^\times)^\ell\mu(F))$ with valuations divisible by $\ell$. For a representative $\alpha$, write $\gamma(\alpha)/\alpha=u^\ell\rho^r$ with $\rho$ generating the roots of unity and $0\le r<\ell$. Norms give $N(u)^\ell=N(\rho)^{-r}$, so there are at most $\ell^2$ pairs $(r,N(u))$. For two representatives with the same pair, the ratio $u_0$ of the units of this formula has norm one. Hilbert 90 gives $u_0=\gamma(z)/z$. Their ratio after dividing by $z^\ell$ belongs to $L^\times$. The extension is cyclotomic and unramified outside $\ell$, so this descended element has valuations divisible by $\ell$ away from $\ell$.

For $S$ the places of $L$ above $\ell$, the classes of these elements modulo $\ell$th powers map by dividing their outside-$S$ divisor by $\ell$ to the $\ell$-torsion of the $S$-class group. The kernel comes from $S$-units. Their rank plus the root-of-unity contribution is at most $2[L:\mathbb Q]$, and the $S$-class group is a quotient of the class group, with at most $\ell^{\lfloor\log_2 h_L\rfloor}$ elements killed by $\ell$. Including the $\ell^2$ choices gives $\#W[\ell]\le\ell^g$. Insert (2) into (1) to conclude.
