# Herr cocycles and integral Galois cohomology

[Chinese proof](../zh/Herr%E4%BD%99%E9%97%AD%E9%93%BE%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E5%92%8C%E6%95%B4Galois%E4%B8%8A%E5%90%8C%E8%B0%83%E5%AF%B9%E5%BA%94.md)

Let \(T\) be a finite free continuous \(O\)-lattice, with \(O\) the integers in a finite extension of \(\mathbb Q_\ell\). Suppose \(G\twoheadrightarrow\Gamma=\Delta\times\Gamma_1\) is the cyclotomic quotient, with kernel \(H\), \(\Delta\) finite of unit order in \(O\), and \(\Gamma_1\simeq\mathbb Z_\ell\) generated topologically by \(\gamma\). Let \(D=D(T)\) be its étale module with commuting \(\varphi,\Gamma\) actions and let \(J:D\hookrightarrow P^H\) be the equivariant period comparison. Identify \(T\) with its image in \(P\). Use the following inputs.

1. The operator \(\varphi-1\) on \(P\) is surjective with kernel \(T\); every continuous \(T\)-valued cocycle restricts to a coboundary on \(H\) in \(P\). [Witt period proof](trace-one-averaging-in-the-witt-period-ring.md)
2. The induced \(\varphi-1\) on \(P^H/J(D)\) is bijective. [Fixed-period proof](frobenius-minus-one-on-the-fixed-period-quotient.md)
3. For \(y,z\in D^\Delta\) satisfying \((\gamma-1)y=(\varphi-1)z\) and any \(b\in P\) with \((\varphi-1)b=J(y)\), the project supplies a continuous \(T\)-valued representative of \({\rm cl}(y,z)\). Its values are \(-(h-1)b\) at lifts \(h\) of \(\Delta\), and \(J(z)-(g-1)b\) at lifts \(g\) of \(\gamma\).

Then \({\rm cl}\) maps onto \(H^1(G,T)\) with kernel the pairs \(((\varphi-1)m,(\gamma-1)m)\), \(m\in D^\Delta\), naturally in \(T\). At \(G_{K_s}\), \(\Delta\) is trivial. For \(G_K\) the project uses the torsion of the actual cyclotomic image, with \(K\) containing \(\sqrt{-1}\) when \(\ell=2\) for the specified decomposition.

**Proof.** Continuous cocycles agree everywhere if they agree on the lifts in input 3, since the zero set of their difference is a closed subgroup and the lifts generate a dense subgroup of \(G\).

Given \(c:G\to T\), write \(c(h)=(h-1)b_0\) on \(H\). Then \(y_0=(\varphi-1)(-b_0)\in P^H\) because \(c(h)\in\ker(\varphi-1)\). By quotient surjectivity choose \(d\in P^H\) with \(y_0-(\varphi-1)d=J(y_1)\). Put \(b_1=-b_0-d\). The cocycle \(u(x)=c(x)+(x-1)b_1\) vanishes on \(H\), descends to \(\Gamma\), and takes values in \(P^H\): for \(h\in H\) compare \(hx=x(x^{-1}hx)\). Since \((\varphi-1)u(x)=(x-1)J(y_1)\), quotient injectivity puts \(u(x)\) in \(J(D)\). Write \(u_D\) for its preimage.

Take \(m_0=|\Delta|^{-1}\sum_{\delta\in\Delta}u_D(\delta)\). The cocycle identity and reindexing give \((\delta-1)m_0=-u_D(\delta)\). Now \(b=b_1+J(m_0)\) satisfies \((\varphi-1)b=J(y_1+(\varphi-1)m_0)=J(y)\), and \(u'=c+(x-1)b\) vanishes on \(\Delta\). Thus \((\delta-1)y=0\) by applying \(\varphi-1\) to \(u'(\delta)\). Write \(u'(\gamma)=J(z)\); comparing \(\delta\gamma\) and \(\gamma\delta\) gives \(\Delta\)-invariance of \(z\). Applying \(\varphi-1\) gives \((\varphi-1)z=(\gamma-1)y\). The formulas in input 3 now recover \(c\) on the dense generating set, hence everywhere.

If the representative of \({\rm cl}(y,z)\) is \((x-1)t\) with \(t\in T\), the formula at lifts of \(\Delta\) gives \(b+t\in P^{H,\Delta}\). Since \((\varphi-1)(b+t)=J(y)\), quotient injectivity gives \(b+t=J(m)\), \(m\in D^\Delta\). The formula at \(\gamma\) gives \(z=(\gamma-1)m\), and also \(y=(\varphi-1)m\). Conversely for such a pair choose \(b=J(m)\) to get the zero cocycle on the generators. Changing \(b\) changes the representative only by a coboundary from \(T\). Compatible maps of lattices, étale modules and periods carry the lift \(b\) and the two generator formulas to the target, proving naturality.

The construction, kernel, representative comparison and applications are `exists_pair`, `exists_of_coboundary`, `onto_and_kernel` and `herr_a`. `clK_natural` and `clNat` establish naturality.
