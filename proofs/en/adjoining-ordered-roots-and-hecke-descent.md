# Adjoining ordered roots and descending to the Hecke ring

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E6%8E%92%E5%BA%8F%E5%90%84%E7%82%B9%E7%9A%84%E6%A0%B9%E5%B9%B6%E6%8A%8A%E5%87%BD%E6%95%B0%E4%B8%8B%E9%99%8D%E5%88%B0%E5%8E%9FHecke%E7%8E%AF.md)

**Proposition.** Let $\mathcal O$ be a domain with fraction field $K$, $X$ a finite nonempty set, and $\mathbb T\subseteq\mathcal O^X$ an $\mathcal O$-subalgebra. Let $P\in\mathbb T[T]$ be monic of degree $t\ge0$, with all $t$ roots of $P_x$ distinct and in $\mathcal O$ for each $x$. Let $p:X'\to X$ be the set of all orderings of roots at each point, and let $\mathbb T'\subseteq\mathcal O^{X'}$ be generated by the pulled-back $\mathbb T$ and the ordered root functions. Then there are $t!$ functions $b_i\in\mathbb T'$ such that every $f'\in\mathbb T'$ is $\sum_i(a_i\circ p)b_i$ with $a_i\in\mathbb T$, and the $b_i$ are $K$-linearly independent on every fiber.

Over any commutative ring $B$, adjoin the first formal root of a positive-degree monic polynomial by $B_1=B[T]/(P)$, a free module of rank $t$. Over $B_1$ divide $P$ by the corresponding monic linear factor. Its remainder is zero, so repeat for the monic quotient of degree $t-1$. The resulting splitting algebra $U$ is free of rank $t!$. At $t=0$ it is $B$. Given an ordered factorization into linear factors over any $B$-algebra, successively evaluate each formal root at its specified root. Each step is well-defined because cancellation of a monic linear factor preserves the remaining factorization. The evaluations give the unique compatible map from $U$.

Take $B=\mathbb T$ and evaluate on every ordering in $X'$. The image of $U$ is $\mathbb T'$ by its generators. The images $b_i$ of a free basis therefore span $\mathbb T'$ over $\mathbb T$. At a fixed $x$, base change to $K$ by evaluation. Each monic quotient operation commutes with this base change. At the first step, $K[T]/(P_x)$ is the product of $K$ over the roots, by the Chinese remainder theorem. In each factor the next polynomial has just the remaining roots. Iterating gives $U\otimes_{\mathbb T}K\simeq\prod_{x'\in p^{-1}(x)}K$ through the ordering evaluations. The images of the free basis form a basis of this product, proving independence.

If $f'$ vanishes off $p^{-1}(Z)$, evaluate on the fiber at any $x\notin Z$. Independence forces every $a_i(x)=0$. Hence for $\pi'\in p^{-1}(\pi)$, $f'(\pi')$ belongs to $\mathfrak c_\pi$, the ideal of values at $\pi$ of functions in $\mathbb T$ supported on $Z$. This gives the descent in the [congruence ideal argument](one-determinant-for-the-congruence-ideal.md).
