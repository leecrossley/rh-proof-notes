# Constructing the normalized block ring at a good point

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E5%9C%A8%E5%A5%BD%E7%82%B9%E6%8D%A2%E5%9F%BA%E5%B9%B6%E6%9E%84%E9%80%A0%E8%A7%84%E8%8C%83%E5%8C%96%E5%9D%97%E7%8E%AF.md)

Let $\mathcal O$ be a DVR with fraction field $K$ and residue field $k$, $X$ finite, $\pi\in X$, and $\mathbb T\subseteq\mathcal O^X$ an integral Hecke function ring. Let $r_x$ be integral representations of $G$ of dimension $n=r+t$, $t\ge1$, with coefficients of $\det(1-Tr_x(g))$ in $\mathbb T$ as functions of $x$. At $\pi$ assume a $K$-linear equivariant decomposition into the given integral representation $\rho_\pi$ of rank $r$ and the integral characters $\chi_1\epsilon^{-(j-1)}$, $1\le j\le t$. Assume $\rho_\pi(G)$ spans the full $M_r(k)$ after reduction.

In the representation span over $\mathbb T$, suppose $e_B,e_1$ are orthogonal idempotents of ranks $t,1$ at every point, and $X_0=e_BX_0e_B$ has $t$ distinct roots in $K$ on every corner. Assume in the chosen decomposition that $e_B(\pi)$ projects to the character blocks, $e_1(\pi)$ is supported on the large block, and $X_0(\pi)=e_B(\pi)r_\pi(g_0)e_B(\pi)$ for a given $g_0$.

**Proposition.** There are normalized data $X',\mathbb T',A$ satisfying the generating functions, fiber independence, determinant integrality and integral surjective block evaluations of the [determinant comparison](one-determinant-for-the-congruence-ideal.md). They give an operator $E$ and scalar $\delta$ with $E^2=\delta E$ and the required block actions. Their construction commutes with the stated coefficient extension.

Replace $r_\pi$ by the given integral standard block representation $r^\sharp_\pi=\rho_\pi\oplus\bigoplus_{j=1}^t \chi_1\epsilon^{-(j-1)}$, and keep $r_x$ elsewhere. The $K$-conjugacy at $\pi$ ensures that sending $\sum_g f_g r(g)$ to $\sum_g f_g r^\sharp(g)$ is well-defined and preserves products, since it acts by conjugation at $\pi$ and by the identity elsewhere. The image is integral directly from the new expressions. Thus this also preserves idempotents and the characteristic polynomials of the corner and the group elements. In the new representation the perturbation $X_0-e_Br^\sharp(g_0)e_B$ vanishes at $\pi$.

By [determinant integrality](determinants-in-the-hecke-ring-without-division.md), the polynomial $\det(Te_B-X_0+(1-e_B))$ has coefficients in $\mathbb T$, and its roots in $K$ are integral. Use the [ordered-root construction](adjoining-ordered-roots-and-hecke-descent.md) to get $X'$ and $\mathbb T'$ with the required functions. Choose $\pi'$ to order the roots by the character blocks at $\pi$. Let $A$ be the span of the pulled-back $r^\sharp$ over $\mathbb T'$. It is a submodule of the finite free $\mathcal O$-module of integral matrix functions on $X'$, so it is finite over $\mathcal O$ and left Noetherian. Indeed left ideals are $\mathcal O$-submodules and their finite $\mathcal O$-generators also generate them as left ideals. The same determinant integrality over $\mathbb T'$ gives the expanded block determinants for $A$.

At $\pi'$ evaluation is integral and block diagonal. Constants give every scalar in a one-dimensional block. On the zero block the span of $\rho_\pi(G)$ surjects after reduction, so Nakayama's lemma on the cokernel in $M_r(\mathcal O)$ gives surjectivity over $\mathcal O$. The [spectral construction](spectral-projection-selects-one-character-block.md) now supplies $E$ in $A$ using the central root functions. It acts by $\delta(\pi')$ on block one and vanishes on the others. The idempotent $e_1$ yields a rank-one quotient summand in block zero.

For a coefficient extension $\mathcal O\hookrightarrow\mathcal O'$, the already distinct complete root sets stay complete, so every ordering over the extended ring has a unique original ordering. The resulting $\sigma:X''\to X'$ commutes with the maps to $X$ and respects the distinguished points. Pullback along $\sigma$ and extension of coefficients send the root functions, Hecke functions and matrices to the corresponding generators over $\mathcal O'$, inducing $A\to A'$. Every evaluation and every distinguished block map commutes with this extension. These are the compatibilities in the [residue-field descent](congruence-length-after-residue-extension.md).
