# Theta movement across the word-prime lattices

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix the project’s column fields $E/F$, with $F$ totally real and $E$ CM quadratic over $F$, and coefficient field $K$. Retain the field and prime data conditions `Fields.Ok` and `Primes.Ok`, and let the map $\operatorname{sel}$ choose an $E$-prime over each $F$-prime. Fix a word $u$, its rational prime $T$, and a twist $\mu$ from the tuple group with excluded rational prime set $S_{\rm ex}$ containing $T$ and the served rational primes $g_j$. The prime set $S_T=\operatorname{Pr.vT}(u)$ consists of the prime divisors of $(\pi_T)$, where $\pi_T\bar\pi_T=T$ and the two factors are coprime. The finite row set $S_{\rm row}$ consists of the served selected primes together with the selected primes in the support of the twist. Write $S_*=S_T\cup S_{\rm row}$. The row levels are determined by the local data `LocCst`:
$$
Q_{\rm row}=\prod_{P\in S_{\rm row}}|\mathcal O_E/P|,\qquad
c_A=Q_{\rm row}^{A},\qquad c_B=TQ_{\rm row}^{B},
$$
where $A,B\ge0$ are the given local exponents. Thus $c_A,c_B>0$.

Fix a host theta base $\mathcal B$ and identification $s$ of column and host data. That identification is the field isomorphism respecting conjugation and the complex block reindexing compatible with the chosen embedding $\sigma:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$. Use the host curve ideal $\mathfrak c\ne0$, with norm coprime to twice the host discriminant parameter, the base period $\delta$, and the given derivative scalings $c_\tau$. The character $\chi$ is multiplicative on the norm-one fractional ideals and on a unitary principal ideal $(z)$ has value $\prod_\tau \sigma_\tau(z)^{-1}$, with the indicated host embeddings. Assume $\operatorname{Norm}(\mathfrak c)$ is coprime to $q_P=|\mathcal O_E/P|$ for all $P\in S_*$. Assume the base has the unnormalized unitary covariance and the source and target theta moves with their determinant-character factors (`EqP`), the self-duality, semicharacters and commensurability for every admitted complex period pair (`LatAll`), and the entire, period-equivariant standard theta in every even pair of ranks (`ThZeroMem`). The unramified base character values are tied to Hecke characters at the chosen embedding (`FromHecke`); the word-prime consequence of this tie is in [the split character calculation](the-word-prime-character-factor-is-independent-of-the-selected-side.md#conjugation-of-character-values).

Fix $0\le i<m$. Write $a=R.N(i)$, $b=R.N(i+1)$, where $R.N(j)=k+j(t+1)$ for even $k$ and odd $t$. In particular $a,b$ are even. Take the true source weight $\lambda_\tau=W.\operatorname{lamTrue}(u,i)_\tau$, any dominant target weight $\lambda'_\tau$ of rank $b$, and the nonnegative shift $n_\tau=\operatorname{nuLast}(W,u,i)_\tau$, the nonnegative part of the determinant shift of the prescribed auxiliary weight `W.nuW u (i + 1)`. Let $N_1,N_2\subset E^a$ and $N'_1,N'_2\subset E^b$ be full self-dual lattices for the standard Hermitian forms. Require $N_1,N_2$, and separately $N'_1,N'_2$, to have equal localizations at every $E$-prime not over $T$. Fix the same source row frames $f$ and target row frames $f'$ on the two pairs.

For each $j=1,2$, put $H_j=\operatorname{Hom}_{S_*}(N_j,N'_j)$, the matrices integral as maps at each selected prime. Assume the same coefficient function $\Xi:\operatorname{Hom}_E(E^a,E^b)\to K$ vanishes outside $D_j=c_A^{-1}H_j$ and is invariant under addition of $C_j=c_B H_j$. For every $x\in D_j$ and every multi-index $m_0$ with block degrees
$$
\sum_{\beta,\alpha}(m_0)_{\tau,\beta,\alpha}
=\sum_{l=1}^a(\lambda_{\tau,l}-\operatorname{detShift}(\lambda_\tau)+n_\tau)
\quad\text{for all }\tau,
$$
assume the derivative $c_\tau$-scaled as in [the jet descent](an-entire-theta-identity-descends-to-the-weighted-jet-operators.md) of the induced theta of level $c_B$ at $x$, divided by the pair's $\chi_F$, lies in $\sigma(K)$. This is exactly the rationality hypothesis `JetsIn` for each box. Denote the corresponding arithmetic kernel maps $V_\lambda\to V_{\lambda'}$ by $k_1,k_2$. They use the distribution `stepT` and the fixed row frames.

Let $o_P(N)$ be the normalized determinant order of a local basis, $d_P=o_P(N_2)-o_P(N_1)$, and $d'_P=o_P(N'_2)-o_P(N'_1)$. These are signed differences of determinant orders. Put $w_P=\operatorname{mUof}(\mathrm{Ch},\mu,i,P)\in K^\times$. Then
$$
k_1=\left(\prod_{P\in S_T}
w_P^{\,d'_P-d_P}\,T^{\,\frac b2d_P-\frac a2d'_P}\right)k_2 .
\tag{1}
$$
The formula is an equality of operators over $K$; the rationality of the finitely many required jets is a hypothesis of the theorem.

### The selected primes and the period ideal

The ambient theta data are those obtained by transporting the host pair along $s$, with period ideal $\mathfrak a=\mathfrak c\,\mathfrak b^{-1}$ transported from the host CM field, where the host ideal $\mathfrak b$ divides the relative different. The base hypotheses give the `Moves` data on this ambient space, in the form used by [the analytic comparison](the-analytic-theta-sums-have-a-common-lattice-refinement.md). Specifically, the normalized theta lies in its complex theta space and changes by the finite intertwiner when just one of the lattices changes. The [host normalization proof](the-determinant-character-cancels-in-the-normalized-theta-moves.md) derives these two statements from `EqP` and the nonzero ideal-character factor. The other hypotheses providing the theta and lattice data are kept explicit here.

Check that the analytic comparison is applicable at $S_*$. There is no conjugate pair in $S_T$ by coprimality of $\pi_T,\bar\pi_T$. The same coprimality holds for the served primes' generators, and the two distinct served primes $g_j$ cannot share a prime of $E$. A prime in the twist support is chosen over a split degree-one prime of $F$ with residue characteristic outside $S_{\rm ex}$, so it cannot coincide with a served or word prime or a conjugate of one. Two selected twist primes above distinct $F$-primes cannot be conjugate, and each $F$-prime has at most one selected prime. It follows that $S_*\cap\overline{S_*}=\varnothing$ and the only primes of $S_*$ over $T$ are those of $S_T$. The underlying rational prime is a uniformizer at each such selected prime and also at each selected row prime. For the word prime and served primes, the norm and cardinality conditions say that the two disjoint factors account for $2[F:\mathbb Q]=[E:\mathbb Q]$ different primes of absolute norm equal to the rational prime, so each has ramification index one. For a twist prime this is the degree-one and unramified condition in `GoodPl`, together with splitting in $E/F$.

At a selected prime $P$, since $q_P$ is a rational prime with ramification index one, the relative different is a unit there. Indeed unramifiedness over $\mathbb Q$ gives unramifiedness over the host's quadratic subfield at that prime. Hence so is $\mathfrak b$. The coprimality of $\operatorname{Norm}(\mathfrak c)$ and $q_P$ makes $\mathfrak c$ a unit there, since $\mathfrak c$ contains its integral norm. Thus $\mathfrak a_P=\mathcal O_P$. At an $E$-prime $Q$ outside $S_*\cup\overline{S_*}$, $Q$ is not over $T$: every prime over $T$ contains $\pi_T$ or $\bar\pi_T$. This verifies the off-set equality required by the analytic comparison.

### The positive analytic scalar

Write $v_P^{\rm pair}(N,N')=b\,o_P(N)-a\,o_P(N')$. Define $e_P=(b/2)d_P-(a/2)d'_P$, an integer because the ranks are even. On $S_T$ the difference of the two pair volume exponents is $2e_P$, with $q_P=T$. At the other primes in $S_*$ the difference is zero by equal localizations. Therefore the positive scalar in the analytic comparison is exactly the image in $\mathbb R$ of the positive rational number
$$
r=\prod_{P\in S_T}T^{e_P}.
$$
Its square is the product of the $q_P$'s to the pair volume exponent differences. Positivity picks this square root. The weights $\sigma(\Xi(x))$ satisfy both box conditions because $\sigma$ is a field embedding. Apply the analytic comparison to these weights. The resulting identity of entire sums has scalar $\sigma(r)$.

The equality remains true after applying the prescribed normalized derivatives and taking their coefficient-field preimages. By [the jet descent](an-entire-theta-identity-descends-to-the-weighted-jet-operators.md), only the listed block degrees enter the polynomial operator, so the jets outside those degrees play no role. This gives a comparison with scalar $r$ for the two sums of jet operators, prior to putting in their framed local determinant factors.

### The framed scalar

For either pair the definition of `stepT` and of the kernel gives its arithmetic sum of jet operators multiplied by
$$
m(N,f)m(N',f')^{-1},\qquad m=\operatorname{muInvDet}.
$$
The local support test in the distribution allows every representative $x\in D_j$, since $c_B$ divides $c_Ac_B$ and $D_j\subseteq(c_Ac_B)^{-1}H_j$. The factor $m$ is the off-row determinant factor times the fixed character product of the row frame. By [the off-row comparison](the-word-prime-character-factor-is-independent-of-the-selected-side.md), passing from pair $2$ to pair $1$ multiplies the source factor by $\prod_{P\in S_T}w_P^{-d_P}$, and the target factor by $\prod_{P\in S_T}w_P^{-d'_P}$. As the target factor is inverted, the resulting factor on the kernel is $\prod_{P\in S_T}w_P^{d'_P-d_P}$. Combining with $r$ proves (1). The family formulation applies this same argument with the actual next-stage weight and supplies both jet-rationality hypotheses from its `JetsAll` assumption.
