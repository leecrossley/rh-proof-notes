# Cases II and III share the rank-four length method

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88II%E7%B1%BB%E5%92%8CIII%E7%B1%BB%E7%9A%84%E9%95%BF%E5%BA%A6%E5%8F%AF%E4%BB%A5%E5%90%88%E7%94%A8%E7%A7%A9%E5%9B%9B%E6%96%B9%E6%B3%95.md)

Fix a primitive complex Dirichlet character $\chi$ of modulus $Q$ and order $o$, and use the [host data and conditions](choosing-among-seven-positive-sign-length-estimates.md), including `Big` for $t$, level $N$, subgroup $H$, and exceptional primes $B_\chi$. Let $\ell$ be odd, $O$ an allowed $\ell$-adic coefficient integer ring, $0<e<t$, and $\psi$ an $O$-valued character modulo $N$ trivial on $H$, with $\psi(-1)=(-1)^e$. Write $V_{\ell,e}(\psi)$ for the localization kernel in $H^1(G_{\mathbb Q},(\operatorname{Frac}O/O)(\varepsilon_\ell^{-e}\psi_G^{-1}))$ at all finite inertia and decomposition groups over $\ell N$, and $v_{\ell,e}(\psi)$ for its $O$-length divided by $v_O(\ell)$. Under the rank-four inputs below, there is $C$ depending only on $\chi$ and the inputs, such that in Cases II and III the length is finite and $v_{\ell,e}(\psi)\log\ell\le(\ell Ne)^C$. Case II has $e\ge2$, even order after removing the $\ell$-power order part and then the $\ell$-component, and $\ell\in B_\chi$ or $\ell-1\mid2e$. Case III has $\ell\notin B_\chi,\ \ell-1\mid2e$, and $\psi\ne1$ of odd order; then $e\ge2$ because $\ell\ge5$.

**Proof.** Let $n_2,n_3$ be the [two-adic depths](two-adic-depths-in-cases-ii-and-iii-have-polynomial-height.md). First use coefficients containing the $2^{n_2}$-th and $2^{n_3}$-th roots. Choose $(n',P)=(n_2,1)$ in II and $(n_3,p_1)$ in III, which uses an extra odd-order character at $p_1$. `dataEx` constructs rank-four symplectic similitude data $D$ with base and residual representations, similitude and characters $\psi_G,\xi,\omega$, and the `Good` and `OfChar` properties. Its set $S$ consists of primes dividing $2\ell NP$. The length $D.\mathrm{sha}$ uses all finite inertia and replaces the decomposition conditions at $\ell N$ by the conditions at $S$. At every added prime the [local restriction comparison](inertia-vanishing-implies-vanishing-on-new-decomposition-groups.md) makes the latter automatic, giving an upper bound for the original length.

Here `SymplecticFourImageDense` supplies automorphy of irreducible points and the finiteness and exclusion of full representations preserving two blocks; `RigiditySymplecticFour` makes their tangent deformations infinitesimal symplectic conjugations. `CohomologyHyp` and `LemmaP` supply the graded formal model. `LemmaM`, `LemmaM'`, `SoV` and `SoFin` compare the kernel length with its degree $-1$ cotangent length and then with valuations of trace differences. `NumberSp4Alt` supplies the integer trace separation and product control at fixed level and weights. `CorX` gives the conductor and residual Bernoulli input for constructing $D$.

By [the common level and exponent](three-similitude-characters-share-one-level-and-exponent.md), take $M=\ell\,2^{n'}NP,\ k=(\ell-1)2^{n'}(2o)$. The characters are trivial on $G_{\mathbb Q(\mu_M)}$ and their orders divide $k$. Let $p$ be the least prime $\ge5$ not dividing $\ell NP$; it avoids $M$ and $S$. The trace separation at $p$ gives one Frobenius $\sigma$ at which every irreducible point has trace distinct from the base trace. The cotangent bound and trace product now give $s=D.\mathrm{sha}<\infty$ and
$$
(s/v_O(\ell))\log\ell\le 2C_0 k[M(e+1)]^A\log p
$$
with $C_0,A$ uniform. The height bounds above make this at most $(\ell Ne)^C$. For arbitrary $O$, extend it in the same algebraic closure to an allowed $O'$ containing a primitive $2^{n_2+n_3}$-th root. The character kernel, order, conductor, components and signs are unchanged by the injection, so all conditions and depths persist. Finite free faithful extension preserves finite length and scales it by the relative ramification index, including preservation of infinite length by strict chains. The valuation of $\ell$ scales likewise, by [coefficient independence](a-single-character-length-is-bounded-by-automorphic-trace-differences.md). Thus the normalized estimate descends to $O$. The RH window calls `countII_and_countIII_of_printsH_of_sp4In`, also using `HypothesesH'` and `LemmaK`.
