# Central idempotents select comparable Hecke summands

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E7%94%A8%E4%B8%AD%E5%BF%83%E5%B9%82%E7%AD%89%E5%85%83%E9%80%89%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%AF%E6%AF%94%E8%BE%83%E7%9A%84Hecke%E5%88%86%E9%87%8F.md)

## Statement

Fix row $D$, normalized twist data $n$, `GaloisPreconditions D n` and `Tie4 D` as in `exists_good_alt`. For each record $i$ let $O_i$ be its coefficient integer ring, $K_i$ its fraction field, and $M_i$ its Hecke lattice with finite character set $X_i$. Let $Z_i$ be the points with the specified automorphic decomposition and $Z_i^{\rm good}$ those passing the two-prime inertia tests. Let $A\ge1$ be the order `restRowsFiniteGroupCard`. Assume the central elements described below. For the records with $Z_i^{\rm good}\ne\varnothing$, there are sets $X'_i\subseteq X_i$ with $X'_i\cap Z_i=Z_i^{\rm good}$. Each retained record has a Hecke-commuting integral endomorphism $\alpha_i$ acting over $K_i$ by $A$ on good eigenspaces and by zero off $X'_i$. At each selected point, every corresponding automorphic representation satisfies `Nc` at all places above both prescribed primes. This gives `CutD.Good`.

At rank $r$, `Nc` requires characteristic polynomial $(X-1)^r$ on wild inertia, zero monodromy, and $\lambda^{\# k_w-1}=1$ for any eigenvalue of a tame inertia generator of multiplicity at least $\operatorname{thr}(w,r)$. The threshold is two at a completely split underlying prime $p$ with $r=1$ or $16r^2<p$, and one otherwise. The [type-label argument](finite-field-type-labels-control-tame-inertia.md) supplies the criterion.

## The integral operator

At each place $v$ above the prescribed primes, group the irreducible finite-group types by monic polynomial label $s_v$, adding their central projectors to obtain $e_{v,s_v}$. The factors over distinct places commute. If $\mathcal T$ is the set passing `goodTuple`, the good projector is $e_0=\sum_{s\in\mathcal T}\prod_v e_{v,s_v}$. By [the central lift](the-group-order-clears-central-projector-denominators.md), `CentralElt` gives $O_i$-central elements $z_{i,s}$ with complex images $A\prod_v e_{v,s_v}$. Sum them over $\mathcal T$ for $z_i$ with image $Ae_0$. The `Pend` data of `Tie4` give a Hecke-commuting integral operator on $M_i$ from $z_i$. By `EigSpan`, if the action on all corresponding complex forms at a point is multiplication by $a\in O_i$, the same holds on its $K_i$-eigenspace. We use $a=A,0$.

## The cut

Select $x$ if some corresponding automorphic form $F$ has $e_0F\ne0$. On $Z_i$ this projector acts by identity on good points and zero on the others. Indeed permute the automorphic decomposition to put the specified one-dimensional twist last. The other summands determine local parameters. `Pend` chooses a type label and `SerLoc` gives the center action there. A good point's witness gives a good tuple and hence identity. Conversely a good tuple for the decomposition supplies a witness of goodness. The decompositions at the same point agree up to permutation. Thus all good points survive by the existence of nonzero forms, and no other $Z_i$ point does. Off the cut all corresponding forms are killed by definition. By `EigSpan` these are the asserted scalars on the Hecke lattice over $K_i$.

For a selected point, at least one tuple contributes $\prod_v e_{v,s_v}F\ne0$. Each factor acts nontrivially and some irreducible type projector inside every such label does so too. The representation therefore contains types with that common good tuple at all specified places. `nc_of_type` gives `Nc`. Other representations giving the same point have the same decomposition up to permutation, hence the same parameters at these split places. The endomorphism is the [integer operator for the lattice comparison](../zh/%E6%88%AA%E5%8E%BB%E4%B8%80%E9%83%A8%E5%88%86%E7%89%B9%E5%BE%81%E4%B8%BA%E4%BD%95%E5%8F%AA%E6%8D%9F%E5%A4%B1%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E8%B5%8B%E5%80%BC.md).
