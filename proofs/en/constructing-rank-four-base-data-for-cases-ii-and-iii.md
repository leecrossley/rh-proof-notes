# Constructing rank-four base data for Cases II and III

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E4%B8%BAII%E7%B1%BB%E5%92%8CIII%E7%B1%BB%E6%9E%84%E9%80%A0%E7%A7%A9%E5%9B%9B%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%95%B0%E6%8D%AE.md)

Use the [Case II or III setup](cases-ii-and-iii-share-the-rank-four-length-method.md), with host, $t,\ell,e,O,\psi$ and all its conditions. Assume $O$ contains primitive roots of orders $2^{n_2},2^{n_3}$ for the [two depths](two-adic-depths-in-cases-ii-and-iii-have-polynomial-height.md), and the residual [coboundary theorem `CorX`](large-two-power-conductors-force-residual-cocycles-to-be-coboundaries.md). Then one can construct `Raw` data satisfying `D.Good` and `Aut.OfCase`. Choose $n=n_2,P=1,\xi_0=1$ in II and $n=n_3,P=p_1,\xi_0\bmod p_1$ of order $\operatorname{ord}\psi$ in III. Let $\xi_2\bmod2^n$ be primitive odd. Set
$$
S=\{p:p\mid2\ell NP\},\quad \xi=(\xi_0)_G(\xi_2)_G,\quad
\Psi=\varepsilon_\ell^e\psi_G,\quad \alpha=\varepsilon_\ell\omega^{-1}\xi,\quad
\beta=\Psi/\alpha,\quad \nu=\alpha\Psi,
$$
with $\omega$ the Teichmüller lift of the residual cyclotomic character. The base is $\operatorname{diag}(\nu,\Psi,\alpha,1)$ and the residual representation its reduction. The symplectic pairs $0,3$ and $1,2$ both give multiplier $\nu$.

**Local choices.** At a chosen place over each rational prime take arithmetic Frobenius $F_v$. At $\ell$ multiply by inertia to arrange $\varepsilon_\ell(F_\ell)=1$ using surjectivity there. In the compact procyclic group of $F_\ell$, the powers $F_\ell^{\ell^{j!}}$ converge to $\varphi$: modulo each finite order the exponent is eventually zero on the $\ell$-part and one on the prime-to-$\ell$ part. In inertia at $\ell$ choose an element with cyclotomic value $1+\ell$ and raise it to a positive power killing $\psi_G,\omega$; the value still has infinite order, giving $s_0$. Put
$$
T=\{\ell\}\cup\{v\in S\setminus\{\ell\}:\ell\mid|\psi_G(I_v)|\},
\quad
T_b=\{\ell\}\cup\{v\mid N:v\ne\ell,\ (\psi_{(\ell)})_v\ne1\},
$$
where $\psi_{(\ell)}$ is the $\ell$-power-order component. Inertia at $v$ affects only the $v$-power component, so $T\subset T_b$. By the host component bound, $T_b$ consists of odd primes dividing $\ell Q$. For $v\ne\ell$ in it, an $\ell$-power quotient of units modulo $v^j$ requires $v\equiv1\bmod\ell$, so the residual cyclotomic value of $F_v$ is $1$ throughout $T$. **Separating character values.** In II the character $\chi_P$ after removing the $\ell$-power-order part and the $\ell$-component has even order. Take $a=c_2(\psi)+1+v_2(o)$, $u=o_{\rm odd}\operatorname{ord}\psi_{(\ell)}$. Then $u$ is odd, $\psi^{2^a u}=1$ by the bounds on each component, and $n_2>c_2(\psi)$, $v^2 2^a\le2^{n_2}$ for $v\in T_b$ by $v\le\ell Q$ and the defining maximum. In III $\psi$ has odd order so $\psi_2=1$; also $\ell\nmid No$, $\chi_P=\psi\ne1$, $T_b=\{\ell\}$ by the Case III conditions. Its order divides $o_{\rm odd}$ and its conductor divides $Q$. Since $p_1\equiv1\bmod o_{\rm odd}$, cyclicity of the units modulo $p_1$ gives a character of order $\operatorname{ord}\psi$ with values in its root group. This $\xi_0$ is even. Take $a=0,u=\operatorname{ord}\psi$; $v^2 2^a\le2^{n_3}$ by the $8\ell^2$ term.

In both cases $n\ge3$, so the [two-adic construction](a-large-two-power-conductor-separates-auxiliary-frobenius-values.md) gives $\xi_2$. The character $\xi$ is unramified on $T_b$, has prime-to-$\ell$ order, and is odd; $\Psi(c)=1$ by the parity of $\psi$. If $(\xi\Psi^i)(F_v)\equiv1$ for $v\in T,\ i\in\mathbb Z$, take the $2^au$-th power to remove $\psi,\xi_0$ and use $\bar\varepsilon_\ell(F_v)=1$. This gives $\xi_2(v)^{2^au}=1$ by injectivity of reduction on two-power roots. The conductor comparison would force $2^n<v^2 2^a$, impossible. This gives `condD` and in particular nontrivial $\bar\alpha$ at $F_\ell$. Also $\bar\Psi=\bar\varepsilon_\ell^{\,b}\bar\chi_P$ for some $b$: reduction kills the $\ell$-power-order part and on the $\ell$-component it factors through units modulo $\ell$. If $\bar\Psi=1$, restrict to inertia at $\ell$, where $\chi_P=1$ and the cyclotomic image is full, to get $\ell-1\mid b$. Reduction is injective on the prime-to-$\ell$ values of $\chi_P$, so this would give $\chi_P=1$. Thus both residual characters are nontrivial, and the union of their two proper kernels cannot cover the group (multiply one element from each of the differences if needed). Choose $g_0$ outside both. **Residual vanishing.** Let $\theta$ be the character with the $\ell$-power order part and then the two-component removed, and $f_0,n_0$ as in the depth formulas. By definition $\ell^{f_0}-1$ is divisible by $4,\ell-1,\operatorname{ord}\theta$, and by $\operatorname{ord}\xi_0$ (in III this is $\operatorname{ord}\theta$). The chosen $n$ has $P\ell\operatorname{cond}(\theta)4^{n_0}\le2^n$, using $\operatorname{cond}(\theta)\mid N$ in II and $\theta=\psi$ of conductor dividing $Q$ in III. Now $\bar\alpha=\bar\xi$, $\bar\Psi=\overline{\omega^e\psi_2\theta}$. The three characters $\bar\alpha,\bar\beta,\bar\nu$ are odd. For each, its odd-modulus primitive part has order dividing $\ell^{f_0}-1$ and conductor dividing $P\ell\operatorname{cond}(\theta)$. Its two-component is $\xi_2^{\pm1}$ times a character of conductor $<2^n$, and thus primitive of conductor $2^n$. These are the `XData` conditions. Apply `CorX` and its local comparison `xvan` to these three twists and their inverses to give `D.XVan`. All ramification, Frobenius, inertia and distinguished-element conditions of `Good` now follow from the choices, and the character decomposition and $S$ give `OfCase`.
