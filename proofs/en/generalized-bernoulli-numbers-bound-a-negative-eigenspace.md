# Generalized Bernoulli numbers bound a negative eigenspace

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E5%B9%BF%E4%B9%89Bernoulli%E6%95%B0%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E7%95%8C%E5%AE%9A%E8%B4%9F%E5%8F%B7%E7%89%B9%E5%BE%81%E5%88%86%E9%87%8F%E7%9A%84%E9%95%BF%E5%BA%A6.md)

Let \(E/\mathbb Q\) be finite abelian and CM, with coefficient DVR \(O\subset\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\) finite over \(\mathbb Z_\ell\) for odd \(\ell\nmid[E:\mathbb Q]\). Let \(N>0\) and \(\varrho:(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^\times\to\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb Q)\) describe restriction to \(E\) of the action on \(\mu_N\): exponent \(t\) restricts to \(\varrho(t)\). For \(e\ge1\), \(k=e+1\), and \(\psi:\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb Q)\to O^\times\), set \(\chi=\psi\circ\varrho\) as a Dirichlet character and assume \(\chi(-1)=(-1)^k\). Write \(\chi^*\) for its primitive character and \(a=N(\prod_{p\le k+1}p)B_{k,\chi^*}\). Let \(H_E(\rho)\) be the continuous \(H^1\) of the discrete \(W=\operatorname{Frac}(O)/O\) with character \(\rho\), restricted to the kernel of localization at all finite inertia and decomposition groups above \(\ell\). Under the local reciprocity input `Rec`,
$$
\operatorname{length}_O H_E(\varepsilon_\ell^{-e})[\psi]\le v_O(a),
$$
where the eigenspace uses the conjugation action below. This is the `PsiBound` input away from primes dividing the field degree. [`psiOf`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PrimitiveFrobeniusFixed/PsiOf.lean#L12)

**Primitive character over \(\mathbb Q\).** Let \(\phi\) be primitive of conductor \(f\) and order prime to \(\ell\), with parity \((-1)^k\). Put \(\alpha=\varepsilon_\ell^{-e}\phi_G^{-1}\), \(a_f=f(\prod_{p\le k+1}p)B_{k,\phi}\). At a prime \(p\mid f\), the inertia image in the units modulo \(f\) is the kernel of reduction modulo \(f/p^{v_p(f)}\), by the local unramified and totally ramified factors of the cyclotomic extension. Primitivity makes \(\phi\) nontrivial on this kernel. A nontrivial root of unity of order \(d\) prime to \(\ell\) differs from \(1\) by a unit: if it reduced to \(1\), its vanishing geometric sum of length \(d\) would reduce to \(d\ne0\). Thus on inertia over \(p\mid f\), \(p\ne\ell\), some \(\alpha(u)-1\) is a unit. Outside \(f\ell\), \(\alpha=1\) on inertia.

If \(\alpha\equiv\varepsilon_\ell\) modulo the uniformizer, the root-of-unity argument forces \(\phi_G=1\) on inertia away from \(\ell\). The [cyclotomic residue case](normalized-cocycles-control-the-localization-kernel.md) makes \(H_{\mathbb Q}(\alpha)=0\). Otherwise for each \(a_0\ne0\) take \(F,z\) from the [floor congruence](floor-coefficients-recover-a-generalized-bernoulli-valuation.md). The element of complex conjugation gives \(\alpha(\delta)=-1\), so \(\alpha(\delta)-1\) is a unit. Apply [the auxiliary-prime estimate](auxiliary-primes-bound-the-cohomology-length-by-one-valuation.md), obtaining \(\operatorname{length}T(a_0)\le v_O(z)\le v_O(a_f)\). Because \(F\) has the prime factors of \(f\ell\), the local conditions for these modules are independent of \(F\). Apply the normalized cocycle comparison at fixed modulus \(f\ell\). In either case \(\operatorname{length}H_{\mathbb Q}(\alpha)\le v_O(a_f)\). For an induced character \(\chi\) modulo \(N\), its primitive \(\phi=\chi^*\) has the same order and sign by the surjective unit reduction. It defines the same character on \(G_{\mathbb Q}\) since restriction to \(\mu_f\) reduces the exponent. The bound \(a\) uses the primitive Bernoulli number and is \((N/f)a_f\), so \(v_O(a_f)\le v_O(a)\). **Restriction and the eigenspace.** Take \(G=G_{\mathbb Q}\), \(U=G_E\), \(d=[G:U]\in O^\times\), and lift \(\psi\) to \(\widetilde\psi\) on \(G\). The actions \(\rho=\varepsilon_\ell^{-e}\), \(\rho'=\rho\widetilde\psi^{-1}\) agree on \(U\), so its cochains and localization conditions coincide. The action under \(\rho\) on a cocycle is \(z(u)\mapsto\rho(g)z(g^{-1}ug)\). Replacing \(\rho\) by \(\rho'\) makes the \(\psi\)-eigenspace into the invariant classes.

Restriction from \(G\) with action \(\rho'\) is injective: a class in its kernel can be normalized to a cocycle zero on \(U\), then descends to \(G/U\) with values in \(W^U\) by normality. Averaging the cocycle identity over the quotient writes it as a coboundary since \(d\) is invertible. Conversely choose representatives \(x_i\) of \(G/U\) and write \(gx_i=x_{\bar g i}u_{g,i}\). For a cocycle \(z\) on \(U\), set \(Cz(g)=\sum_i\rho'(x_{\bar g i})z(u_{g,i})\). The identity \(u_{gh,i}=u_{g,\bar h i}u_{h,i}\) gives the cocycle property, and the open cosets give continuity. When restricted to \(U\) the class is the sum of conjugates of \([z]\), hence \(d[z]\) for an invariant class. Divide by \(d\) for surjectivity. For any local subgroup \(D\), \(U\cap D\) is normal of index dividing \(d\); the same injectivity argument shows that vanishing on \(U\cap D\) implies vanishing on \(D\) for a local class. The reverse implication is restriction. Hence the eigenspace is naturally \(H_{\mathbb Q}(\rho')\). The character \(\widetilde\psi=\chi_G\) by the defining action, with order dividing \([E:\mathbb Q]\), so the rational estimate proves the bound.
