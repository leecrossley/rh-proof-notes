# Two auxiliary primes suffice to sum Case I lengths

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E4%B8%A4%E4%B8%AA%E8%BE%85%E5%8A%A9%E7%B4%A0%E6%95%B0%E5%B0%B1%E8%83%BD%E6%B1%87%E6%80%BBI%E7%B1%BB%E7%9A%84%E9%95%BF%E5%BA%A6.md)

Use the [host data](choosing-among-seven-positive-sign-length-estimates.md) for fixed $\chi$, modulus $Q$, $u,q_1,d,t,N,H,B_\chi$, and $1\le e<t$. For each $\ell$, let $O_\ell$ be the integers in any finite extension of $\mathbb Q_\ell$, $r_\ell=v_{O_\ell}(\ell)$, and $\mathcal X_\ell$ the $O_\ell$-valued Dirichlet characters mod $N$ trivial on $H$ of sign $(-1)^e$. For $\psi\in\mathcal X_\ell$, let $L_\ell(\psi)$ be the $O_\ell$-length of the localization kernel in $H^1(G_{\mathbb Q},(\operatorname{Frac}O_\ell/O_\ell)(\psi_G^{-1}\varepsilon_\ell^{-e}))$, imposing zero classes on all finite inertia groups and on decomposition groups at primes dividing $\ell N$. The character $\psi_G$ comes from the action on $\mu_N$. Write $V_\ell(\psi)=L_\ell(\psi)/r_\ell$ when finite. Under `PropGK` below there are $C\ge0$ and primes $p,p'$ depending only on $\chi$, such that if $q_1,d>p,p'$, all lengths for any finite set $\mathcal L$ of Case I primes are finite and
$$
\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal L}\log\ell\sum_{\psi\in\mathcal X_\ell}V_\ell(\psi)\le(Nt)^C .
$$

**Integer hypothesis.** For each number field $K$ there are nonnegative integers $c_5,A$. For all $N,e\ge1$, $J\le G_N$, and $p\nmid30N|\operatorname{disc}K|$ prime, there is a nonzero integer $D_p$ with
$$
\log|D_p|\le c_5\varphi([G_N:J]) (N(e+1))^A\log p .
$$
For each odd coefficient prime $\ell\ne p$ and each $O_\ell$, if at least one $O_\ell$-valued character of kernel $J$ satisfies `GD` for $K,N,e,\ell$ (in full in the host note), all characters of kernel $J$ have finite $L_\ell$ and $\sum_{\ker\psi=J}V_\ell(\psi)\le2v_\ell(D_p)$. The integer does not depend on the coefficient ring. [`PropGK` and `CorGK`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/UnitaryThreeGroups/KatoUse.lean#L36)

**Proof.** For fixed $K,N,e,J$, take two distinct eligible primes $p,p'$. At $\ell\ne p$ use $D_p$; at $\ell=p$ use $D_{p'}$. In either case the same-kernel sum is at most $2v_\ell(D_p)+2v_\ell(D_{p'})$. For any finite collection with a witness to `GD`, multiplying by $\log\ell$ and summing gives at most $2(\log|D_p|+\log|D_{p'}|)$ by integer prime factorization. This is bounded by $2c_5\varphi([G_N:J])(N(e+1))^A(\log p+\log p')$. [`corGK`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/UnitaryThreeGroups/CorGSumLog.lean#L37)

Use $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-p_K})$ and choose $p,p'>30Qp_K$. For $q_1,d>p,p'$ both remain eligible across all these moduli. Group all the $\mathcal X_\ell$ by their kernels $J$. For a kernel that occurs at a prime, a character there satisfies `GD` by case I, so all the terms for this kernel fit the integer inequality. Each occurring quotient $G_N/J$ is cyclic since it embeds in the multiplicative group of a field. If its order is $s$, precisely $\varphi(s)$ complex characters have that kernel, sending a generator to the primitive $s$-th roots. Such collections for different kernels are disjoint, so the total of $\varphi([G_N:J])$ over occurring kernels is at most $|G_N|\le N$. The full sum is at most $2c_5(\log p+\log p')N(N(e+1))^A$. Choose $b\ge0$ bounding the constant by $16^b$. Since $16\mid N$ and $e+1\le t$, this gives $C=b+1+A$. Finiteness follows from the same applications.

The source uses `orbFacts` and `orbSum` to count by kernel in `countI`. The main route first constructs the [integer hypothesis from traces](one-frobenius-trace-difference-controls-same-kernel-lengths.md), then applies these two bounds.
