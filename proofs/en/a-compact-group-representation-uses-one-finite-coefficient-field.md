# A compact group representation uses one finite coefficient field

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E7%B4%A7%E7%BE%A4%E8%A1%A8%E7%A4%BA%E5%8F%AA%E9%9C%80%E8%A6%81%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E6%9C%89%E9%99%90%E7%B3%BB%E6%95%B0%E5%9F%9F.md)

## Statement

Let $G$ be a compact Hausdorff group and $\rho:G\to\mathrm{GL}_n(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ a homomorphism with continuous entries. All entries lie in one finite extension of $\mathbb Q_\ell$, which can include any prescribed finite extension. This lets the [reconstructed representation](recovering-a-semisimple-representation-from-a-pseudocharacter.md) enter finite-coefficient period modules.

## Proof

Embed $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ in $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. Its image is dense: approximate the coefficients of the minimal polynomial of an algebraic element by rationals and apply simple-root Hensel in the finite extension containing the element to find a nearby root. For any $x$ outside $\mathbb Q_\ell$, choose $y$ in the dense image at distance smaller than the distance from $x$ to any of its other conjugates. Krasner gives $\mathbb Q_\ell(x)\subseteq\mathbb Q_\ell(y)$. The base elements are covered already. Therefore the fields generated by finite sets of this countable image give countably many finite extensions $L_j$ containing any prescribed finite set in at least one of them.

Let $G_j$ consist of $g$ whose matrices $\rho(g),\rho(g^{-1})$ have all entries in $L_j$. This is a subgroup by multiplication and inversion. It is closed by continuity because each complete finite extension is closed in the closure field with the given norm. Their union is $G$ by the finite-set property. Baire for compact Hausdorff spaces gives one subgroup with interior, hence open by translation. Compactness gives finitely many cosets. Adjoin the entries of $\rho(g_i)$ for representatives of these cosets to $L_j$. The resulting finite extension contains every entry because $\rho(g_i h)=\rho(g_i)\rho(h)$. Take a compositum with the prescribed field if needed.
