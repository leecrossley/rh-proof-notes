# The three entry bounds pass to every framed coefficient

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $p>2$ be prime, $S=\mathbb Z/(2\nu)$ with $\nu\ge1$, and $K$ a nonarchimedean normed field of characteristic zero with $\|p\|=p^{-1}$. Fix a top set $T$, a letter matrix $Z_s$ of size $N_0\times N_1$ at each slot, and minors of heights $h_C\le N_0$ with specified output columns $I_C$. The differential polynomial is the product of these minors after left multiplication by independent frame matrices $Y_s$. Pair it with a series $F$ in the letters by applying the differential polynomial at zero, leaving a polynomial in the entries of $Y_s$. Suppose $F$ belongs to the Frobenius power class `InClass` with integral $u_s$ as in [the phase decomposition](a-frobenius-phase-splits-into-pure-top-and-mixed-terms.md). We derive the three coefficient bounds of [the parity argument](odd-shifts-and-column-capacities-give-the-three-served-jet-bounds.md) for these framed minors. [`EGaussK.reduce`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Formalism/ThetaLogCoordinates/UniformThetaSubstitution/FrobeniusLiftNorms/EGaussK.lean#L38)

Give the rational function field in the frame variables the Gauss norm. It extends the given norm isometrically and each variable has norm one. Multiplicativity on polynomials follows by dividing each nonzero polynomial by a coefficient of largest norm and multiplying the nonzero reductions in the residue polynomial ring, which is a domain. The Gauss norm of the evaluated jet is exactly the maximum norm of its coefficients as a polynomial in the frame entries. All scalar substitutions into the class witnesses are isometric. At a top slot, separate every height-one minor. The same first row of its frame matrix defines the linear form $\sum_aY_{s,0,a}Z_{s,a,b}$ for the chosen column $b$. The product of these forms is the top part, with the exponent $e_{s,b}$ the number of such choices yielding $s,b$. All other minors are kept as the lower part. Thus $a_s$ counts height-one top minors, $c_s$ counts all remaining minors at $s$, and $L_s$ is the sum of their heights. These are the exact parameters of the parity bounds. [`EGauss.struct`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Formalism/ThetaLogCoordinates/UniformThetaSubstitution/FrobeniusLiftNorms/EGauss.lean#L111)

For a Hecke degree $i\ge1$, put $J=\{b:0\le b<i\}$. The full exponent used on an even-slot minor is $\max\{\min(h_C,i)-|\operatorname{im}I_C\cap J|,0\}$, and on an odd-slot minor it is $|\operatorname{im}I_C\cap J|$. Each height-one factor in a top slot contributes exactly its indicator from the top-degree part of the payment $N$ in `EntryB`. Each remaining odd-slot minor contributes its entire column count. Even-slot lower minors contribute nonnegative amounts. Hence the full exponent $N_{\rm full}$ is at least $N$. The weighted estimate at $p^{N_{\rm full}}$ follows because extra powers of $p$ decrease the norm. Finally suppose $\varpi^{p^2-1}=p$ in the normed field and $F(Z)=\Theta(\varpi Z)$ for an unscaled theta series $\Theta$. The differential polynomial is homogeneous of degree $D=\sum_C h_C$, so its jet for $F$ is $\varpi^D$ times its jet for $\Theta$. This follows coefficientwise from the factor $\varpi^D$ in each Taylor coefficient of degree $D$. The bound for $F$ contains the additional gain $\rho D$, and $v_p(\varpi^D)=\rho D$, so this term cancels exactly. The resulting floors for $\Theta$ are the ordinary excess gain, $G-\sum_{s\text{ even}}a_s$, and $G$ after multiplication by $p^{N_{\rm full}}$. These are `kernel_a_one_generic`, `kernel_a_two_generic`, and `kernel_b_generic`.
