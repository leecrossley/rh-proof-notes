# How finite-layer class groups bound the generators of a tower module

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E6%9C%89%E9%99%90%E5%B1%82%E7%9A%84%E7%B1%BB%E7%BE%A4%E7%95%8C%E8%83%BD%E6%8E%A7%E5%88%B6%E5%A1%94%E6%A8%A1%E7%9A%84%E7%94%9F%E6%88%90%E5%85%83%E6%95%B0.md)

## Statement

Let $K/\mathbb Q$ be finite abelian, and let $\ell$ be prime. Suppose that $K$ contains a primitive $\ell$th root of unity for odd $\ell$, or a primitive fourth root of unity for $\ell=2$. Put $K_n=K(\mu_{\ell^n})$, $K_\infty=\bigcup_nK_n$, and $H=\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/K_\infty)$ for an algebraic closure $\Omega$ of $K$. For a positive integer $N$, let $D_N$ be the module of locally constant additive characters of $H$ with values in $C=\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell$ that vanish on the intersections with $H$ of every inertia group and every decomposition group above a prime dividing $N$. Put $X_N=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z_\ell}(D_N,C)$.

If $r\ge0$ is an integer such that
$$
\left|\operatorname{Cl}(\mathcal O_{K_n})/\operatorname{Cl}(\mathcal O_{K_n})^\ell\right|\le\ell^r\qquad(n\ge2),
$$
then every $X_N$ is generated by at most $r$ elements. The project uses `Published.FerreroWashington` in precisely this uniform rank form, with a single $r$ for all $n\ge0$ and all such cyclotomic layers over a fixed $K,\ell$. Its implementation is `FW.ferreroWashington`. [Class-group input](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PublishedTheorems/FerreroWashington.lean) · [Implementation](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean)

## Unramified characters are already bounded at finite layers

First take $N=1$ and values in $\mathbb F_\ell$. Write $H_n=\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/K_n)$. For $n\ge2$ the field $K_n$ is totally imaginary. By the unramified correspondence of class field theory, continuous unramified additive $\mathbb F_\ell$-valued characters of $H_n$ are the characters of $\operatorname{Cl}(\mathcal O_{K_n})$. Each factors through its quotient by $\ell$th powers. This finite vector space and its dual have the same cardinality, so there are at most $\ell^r$ characters at every such layer. [Finite-layer rank](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/LevelRk.lean) · [Unramified correspondence](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/KummerTheory/PullOnto.lean)

For any positive $N$, a character $\phi:H\to\mathbb F_\ell$ satisfying the specified inertia and decomposition conditions extends to some $H_n$ with the corresponding conditions at that layer. To see this, choose an open normal subgroup $O$ of the absolute Galois group of $K$ such that $H\cap O\subseteq\ker\phi$. The intersection of the decreasing compact subgroups $H_n$ is $H$, so eventually $H_n\subseteq HO$. Otherwise the nonempty compact sets $H_n\setminus HO$ would have a point in their intersection. Via $HO/O\simeq H/(H\cap O)$ the character extends to $HO$ and hence to this $H_n$. Denote the extension on the chosen layer by $\widetilde\phi$.

The inertia groups away from $\ell$ lie in $H$, where the extension already vanishes on them, since the cyclotomic tower is unramified there. The remaining inertia groups and the required decomposition groups above $N$ are the conjugates of finitely many closed local subgroups, one for each indicated place of the base. The union $E$ of their elements is compact, by continuity of conjugation on compact sets. The compact sets
$$
E\cap H_n\cap\{g:\widetilde\phi(g)\ne0\}
$$
decrease to the empty set as $n$ rises from the chosen layer. The intersection is empty because $\widetilde\phi|_H=\phi$ vanishes at the specified local groups. At some finite layer the set is empty, giving the desired extension. [Extension preserving the local conditions](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/KummerTheory/XdualExt.lean)

If there were $\ell^r+1$ distinct unramified $\mathbb F_\ell$-valued characters of $H$, extend each by the result with $N=1$. Restrict their extensions to a common layer with $n\ge2$. These extensions remain distinct, since they already differ on $H$. This contradicts the finite-layer bound. The additional conditions at $N$ define a subspace of the unramified character space and can only lower its dimension. [Counting in the tower](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/LevUAnti.lean)

## From mod-$\ell$ rank to generators over $\mathbb Z_\ell$

The preceding argument counts $D_N[\ell]$, since $C[\ell]\simeq\mathbb F_\ell$. Its dimension is at most $r$. Dualizing multiplication by $\ell$ gives
$$
X_N/\ell X_N \simeq \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z_\ell}(D_N[\ell],C).
$$
The right-hand side has the same dimension as $D_N[\ell]$. Select $x_1,\ldots,x_r$ whose classes span $X_N/\ell X_N$, padding with zeros if necessary. [The dual kernel and cokernel formula](cohomology-kernels-as-cokernel-lengths-in-cyclotomic-towers.md)

Given $x\in X_N$, write $x=\sum_i a_{i,0}x_i+\ell x^{(1)}$ with $0\le a_{i,0}<\ell$, and continue with $x^{(1)}$ to obtain coefficients $a_i=\sum_{j\ge0}\ell^j a_{i,j}\in\mathbb Z_\ell$. Each $\phi\in D_N$ has finite image by local constancy and compactness of $H$, so $\ell^k\phi=0$ for some $k$. Evaluating the first $k$ steps at $\phi$ kills the remainder $\ell^k x^{(k)}(\phi)$ and gives $x(\phi)=\sum_i a_i x_i(\phi)$. The equality for every $\phi$ proves generation.
