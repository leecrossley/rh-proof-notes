# Finite-order local characters globalize with trivial infinite components

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let \(F\) be a number field, \(S\) a finite set of finite places, and \(\theta_v\colon F_v^\times\to\mathbb C^\times\) a smooth finite-order character for every \(v\in S\). There is a finite-order Hecke character \(\eta\colon\mathbb A_F^\times/F^\times\to\mathbb C^\times\) with local component \(\theta_v\) at each \(v\in S\) and trivial at every infinite place. The conclusion specifies no bound on the order of \(\eta\). [Lean statement](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/ExistsHcharPrescribedTrivial.lean#L82)

## Putting the prescribed characters on an idele quotient

Choose \(m\ge1\) divisible by the orders of the prescribed characters. The arithmetic input is the following finite test for powers. Given \(F,S,m\), there is a finite set \(B\) of finite places disjoint from \(S\) such that any \(x\in F^\times\) with \(v(x)=0\) for all \(v\notin S\) and \(x\equiv1\bmod\mathfrak p_b\) for all \(b\in B\) is an \(m\)-th power in \(F^\times\). The congruence at \(b\) is in the local integer ring. The [finite congruence proof](finitely-many-congruences-detect-powers-of-s-units.md) constructs \(B\) from Frobenius tests in a Kummer extension and descends from the auxiliary cyclotomic field using an enlarged exponent. [Finite test for powers of \(S\)-units](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/ExistsKummerField.lean#L252)

Write \(J=\mathbb A_F^\times\). Let \(U\subset J\) be the open subgroup consisting of ideles whose finite components are all units, are congruent to \(1\) at the places in \(B\), and lie in \(\ker\theta_v\) at \(v\in S\). The infinite components of \(U\) are unrestricted. Let \(\beta\colon\prod_{v\in S}F_v^\times\to J\) insert the prescribed components and use \(1\) elsewhere. On products \(x\,\beta(y)\), where \(x\in F^\times\) is diagonal and \(y=(y_v)_{v\in S}\), the required value is \(\prod_{v\in S}\theta_v(y_v)\).

For this prescription to define a homomorphism on the image of \(F^\times\beta(\prod F_v^\times)\) in \(J/U\), it suffices that the value be \(1\) whenever \(x\,\beta(y)\in U\). In that case \(x\) is an \(S\)-unit and satisfies all the congruences at \(B\), since \(\beta(y)\) has component \(1\) away from \(S\). Hence \(x=z^m\) for some \(z\in F^\times\). At \(v\in S\) we have \(\theta_v(xy_v)=1\) by the definition of \(U\), and \(\theta_v(x)=\theta_v(z)^m=1\). Thus each \(\theta_v(y_v)=1\), proving the required consistency. [The consistency calculation](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/ExistsHcharPrescribedTrivial.lean#L127)

The group \(J/U\) is discrete and abelian. A unitary character on one of its subgroups extends to all of \(J/U\) because the circle group is divisible. Explicitly, when adding a new generator modulo the subgroup, choose any value if it has infinite order in the quotient and choose an appropriate root of the prescribed value of its first positive power otherwise. Zorn's lemma gives an extension to the whole quotient. Pulling back to \(J\) gives a continuous character \(\eta\), trivial on \(F^\times\) and with precisely the required components. It is trivial on all infinite ideles since they lie in \(U\). Finally the quotient \(J/(F^\times U)\) is finite: it is a quotient of the ray class group with finite modulus given by the unit congruences contained in \(U\), and with all infinite factors killed. Thus \(\eta\) has finite order. The Lean proof uses the equivalent finite-order criterion for a Hecke character trivial at infinity. ## From a local Galois character to a totally real extension

For a finite place \(v\) of a totally real field \(F\), let \(D_v\) be its decomposition group in a fixed algebraic closure. Let \(V\) be an open subgroup of \(D_v\) containing its commutator subgroup. Given a finite set \(T\) of finite places excluding \(v\), there is a finite abelian totally real extension \(F_1/F\), split at every place in \(T\), for which \(D_v\cap G_{F_1}=V\). [Lean statement](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/ExistsAbelianTotallyRealExtPrescribedCompletion.lean#L162)

Indeed, the finite abelian group \(D_v/V\) is separated by its complex characters. Apply local reciprocity to each of these characters restricted to the Weil group. It writes the restriction as a finite-order smooth character of \(F_v^\times\). Apply the preceding construction with this character at \(v\), all characters trivial on \(F_u^\times\) for \(u\in T\), and all infinite components trivial. Global reciprocity gives a finite-order Galois character agreeing with the original character on the local Weil group. Density and local constancy give agreement on \(D_v\). To see the factorization through global reciprocity, the Hecke character kills both the diagonal principal ideles and the connected archimedean subgroup. Its kernel is closed, so it kills the closure of their product, which is the kernel of the global Artin map. The compatibility of local and global reciprocity makes the Galois character trivial on the decomposition groups at \(T\) and at the real places. [Local character via reciprocity](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/ExistsAbelianTotallyRealExtPrescribedCompletion.lean#L20) · [Global factorization](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/ExistsAbelianTotallyRealExtPrescribedCompletion.lean#L41) · [Compatibility at the prescribed places](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/ExistsAbelianTotallyRealExtPrescribedCompletion.lean#L85)

Take the field fixed by the intersection of the finitely many global character kernels. Each kernel is open and normal, and their quotient is abelian. All the real conjugations act trivially on the field, so it is totally real. The places in \(T\) split completely for the same reason using their decomposition groups. Finally the intersection of the local character kernels on \(D_v\) is exactly \(V\), by character separation. This proves the assertion.
