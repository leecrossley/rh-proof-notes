# A fresh ramified prime builds each layer of an unramified local extension

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let \(E\) be a CM field with maximal real subfield \(F\) and algebraic closure \(\Omega\). Let \(w\) be a finite place of \(E\) split over \(F\), \(f\ge1\), and \(S\) a finite set of finite places of \(E\). There is a tower of cyclic prime-degree CM extensions \(E=E_0\subset E_1\subset\cdots\subset E_s\) in \(\Omega\) with the following properties.

- At \(w\), every prolongation has trivial ramification in \(E_s/E\) and residue degree \(f\).
- Each step has a witness place of \(E\) outside \(S\), split over \(F\) and split completely in the preceding layers. An inertia element there is nontrivial on the next extension, hence a place over the witness ramifies in that layer.
- Every place of \(E\) nonsplit over \(F\) is unramified in \(E_s/E\).

The construction is used with \(f=n!\) to obtain the unramified local splitting of depth-zero support. ## The two character inputs

All characters below are finite-order characters of \(G_F=\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/F)\) that kill complex conjugations. Their kernels therefore define finite abelian totally real fields. A permissible character may ramify only at places split in \(E/F\).

The first input, given \(f\ge1\) and the place \(v=w|_F\), supplies a permissible character \(r\) unramified at \(v\) whose value at a Frobenius lift there has order exactly \(f\). For a prime power \(f=\lambda^k\), choose a rational prime \(p>2,\,\mathrm{N}v\) completely split in the normal closure of \(E/\mathbb Q\) with
\[
\lambda^{k+1}\mid\operatorname{ord}_{(\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)^\times}(\mathrm{N}v).
\]
A [Kummer-extension and Čebotarev argument](moving-a-kummer-root-forces-a-large-prime-power-order-modulo-a-split-prime.md) supplies such a prime. The multiplicative group \((\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)^\times\) is cyclic. On its quotient by \(\{\pm1\}\), the order of the image of \(\mathrm{N}v\) is divisible by \(\lambda^k\). One can choose a character of the cyclic quotient that sends that image to an element of order exactly \(\lambda^k\): choose a primitive character and raise it to the order of the image divided by \(\lambda^k\). Pull the resulting even character back by the action of \(G_F\) on \(\mu_p\). It kills complex conjugation and is unramified away from \(p\). All places over \(p\) split in \(E/F\), while at \(v\) Frobenius acts on \(\mu_p\) by the \(\mathrm N v\) power. For general \(f\), multiply the characters obtained for its prime-power factors. The orders at Frobenius are pairwise coprime, so the order of their product is \(f\). The second input takes an open subgroup \(U\subset G_F\), a finite set \(T\) of finite places of \(F\), and \(m\ge2\). It supplies a permissible character \(\psi\) with \(\psi^m=1\), trivial on all decomposition groups over \(T\), and a place \(u\notin T\) split in \(E/F\) with \(D_u\subseteq U\) for every prolongation. Some \(t\in I_u\) has \(\psi(t)\) of order \(m\). Here is the construction. Choose a rational prime \(p\) split completely in a finite Galois extension of \(\mathbb Q\) containing the normal closures of \(E\), the fixed field of an open normal subgroup inside \(U\), and the field obtained by adjoining \(\mu_{2m}\) and the \(m\)-th roots of the residue norms of the places in \(T\). Exclude all rational primes below \(T\). Čebotarev supplies this \(p\). It satisfies \(2m\mid p-1\), and each of the prescribed residue norms is an \(m\)-th power modulo \(p\). A character of \((\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)^\times\) of order \(m\) is then even and kills each of those norms. Pull it back along the action on \(\mu_p\). Its values at Frobenius above the excluded set \(T\) are \(1\), and inertia is trivial away from \(p\). At a place over \(p\) the local field is \(\mathbb Q_p\), so \(I_u\) surjects onto \(\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q_p(\mu_p)/\mathbb Q_p)\simeq(\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)^\times\) by total ramification. This gives the required \(t\). ## Building the field while preserving the character at \(v\)

Let \(T\) consist of \(v\) and the places below \(S\). Begin with the character \(r\) from the first input and an exponent \(m_0\) with \(r^{m_0}=1\). We maintain a finite-order permissible character \(c\), an integer \(m\ge1\), and a field in the desired tower obtained by adjoining to \(E\) the field fixed by \(H=\ker(c^m)\). Initially \(c=r,\ m=m_0\), so the field is \(E\). All subsequent characters \(c\) agree with \(r\) on the decomposition groups at \(v\).

If \(m>1\), write \(m=pm'\) for a prime \(p\). Apply the fresh character construction with \(U=\ker c\), the set \(T\), and order \(m\), obtaining \(\psi,u,t\). Replace \(c\) by \(c\psi\) and \(m\) by \(m'\). We have \((c\psi)^m=c^m\) and hence
\[
H'=\ker((c\psi)^{m'})\subseteq H.
\]
On \(H\) the character \((c\psi)^{m'}\) has values in the \(p\)-th roots of unity. Since \(t\in I_u\subseteq \ker c\) and \(\psi(t)\) has order \(pm'\), its value at \(t\) has order \(p\), so \(H'\) has index \(p\) in \(H\). The decomposition group \(D_u\) lies in \(H\) and fixes \(E\); thus \(u\) lies under a place of \(E\) split completely in the field so far. It is outside \(S\). The inertia element \(t\) fixes \(E\) and the field fixed by \(H\), and it fails to fix the field of \(H'\). Restricting the quotient \(H/H'\) to elements fixing \(E\) therefore still has image of order \(p\). Thus the two CM composita form a cyclic degree-\(p\) layer with precisely the needed ramification witness. The new character is permissible, because the product of two such characters still kills all complex conjugations and the inertia at every nonsplit place. The factor \(\psi\) is trivial on the decomposition groups at \(v\), so the character there remains \(r\). After finitely many steps the exponent is \(1\). The final field is \(E\) adjoined with the field of the last character. At the split place \(w\), the local decomposition group identifies with that at \(v\) over \(F\). Inertia acts trivially since the final character agrees with \(r\) there. Its Frobenius image has order \(f\), so the residue degree is \(f\). At the nonsplit places of \(E/F\) all relevant inertia acts trivially because the character is permissible. This proves the assertions.
