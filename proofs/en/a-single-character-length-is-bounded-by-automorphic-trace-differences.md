# A single character length is bounded by automorphic trace differences

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E7%89%B9%E5%BE%81%E7%9A%84%E9%95%BF%E5%BA%A6%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E7%94%B1%E6%9C%89%E9%99%90%E4%B8%AA%E8%87%AA%E5%AE%88%E8%BF%B9%E5%B7%AE%E6%8E%A7%E5%88%B6.md)

Let $\Omega=\overline{\mathbb Q}$, $K\subset\Omega$ imaginary quadratic, $\ell$ odd, $O\subset\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ a DVR finite over $\mathbb Z_\ell$, $N>0$ even, $e\ge1$, and $\psi$ a Dirichlet character modulo $N$ valued in $O$. Assume `GD`: the order of $\psi$ is prime to $\ell$, its component modulo $\ell^{v_\ell(N)}$ squares to $1$, $\psi(-1)=(-1)^e$, $\ell\nmid|\operatorname{disc}K|h_K$, $(N,|\operatorname{disc}K|)=1$, $\ell-1\nmid2e$, and the subgroup of $G_{\mathbb Q}$ fixing $\mu_\ell$ and killing the pullback of $\psi$ with its $\ell$-component removed is not contained in $G_K$. Put $\Psi=\varepsilon_\ell^e\psi_G$, $\eta$ the sign with kernel $G_K$. Let $S$ be the localization kernel of $H^1(G_{\mathbb Q},(\operatorname{Frac}O/O)(\Psi^{-1}))$ at all finite inertia and decomposition groups over primes dividing $\ell N$. Write $V=\operatorname{length}_O S$, $V_n=V/v_O(\ell)$ if finite.

Fix a field isomorphism $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$. The allowed rank-three representations of $G_K$ are:
- `Adm3U_s`: irreducible, with $\rho\otimes\varepsilon_\ell^{-1}$ associated to a regular algebraic polarized cuspidal automorphic representation and compatibility witness `WitA`, with $\mathrm{Level}(\ell,N,(e,0,-e))$. The flags at $\ell$ can be chosen with quotient characters $\kappa_i\ne\varepsilon_\ell\kappa_j$ for all $i,j$.
- `Adm2A`: isomorphic to $1\oplus W|_{G_K}$ for irreducible rank-two $W$ over $G_{\mathbb Q}$, with $W\otimes\varepsilon_\ell^{-e}$ associated to a weight $2e+1$ newform, witness `WitA` at all imaginary quadratic restrictions that remain irreducible, $\mathrm{Level}(\ell,N|\operatorname{disc}K|,(e,-e))$, and determinant an algebraic character of cyclotomic weight zero.

Here $\mathrm{Level}(\ell,M,(m_i))$ requires continuity and diagonalizability on inertia away from $\ell$, with diagonal characters from global characters trivial on the fixing subgroup of $\mu_M$. At each place over $\ell$ it requires a decomposition-group flag with quotient restrictions to inertia $\varepsilon_\ell^{m_i}\chi_i$, each $\chi_i$ from such a character. There the quotients satisfy $\kappa_i\ne\varepsilon_\ell\kappa_j$ for all $i,j$, or the representation is potentially crystalline. Take $g\in G_K$ and $\tau(g)=\Psi(g)+1+\Psi(g)^{-1}$ unequal to $\operatorname{tr}\rho(g)$ for all allowed $\rho$. Under the inputs below $V$ is finite and there is a finite set $X$ of pairwise nonisomorphic allowed representations with $V_n\le2\sum_{\rho\in X}v_\ell(\operatorname{tr}\rho(g)-\tau(g))$, with $v_\ell(\ell)=1$.

It uses `LemmaM`, `LemmaM'` and the equation construction `LemmaP`. With unramified coefficients, `AutomorphyUnramifiedCoefficientsAlt` supplies $g_1$ with the required marks, finite weight-zero algebra, `AutBAlt` giving automorphy and compatibility of the irreducible rank-three points, and `LemWAlt` giving the newform and quadratic compatibility for allowed extensions of the irreducible corner blocks with middle character one. The admissibility input `AdmI_s` and the rank-two level comparison turn these into the indicated allowed classes. `RigidityFirstCase`, `RigiditySecondCase` and `KatoTwoW` supply the cohomological rigidity of the two types of point, with nonpositive-weight vanishing at the base coming from the local and global cohomology inputs. These conditions give `PtHyp` for the upper length estimate. The data from `GD` use $G_{\mathbb Q}$, a decomposition group above $\ell$, sign $\eta$, and base matrix $\operatorname{diag}(\Psi,1,\Psi^{-1})$. The project constructs the unrestricted local ring, the normalized globally constrained ring and its graded model from these data. **Proof.** First choose an unramified coefficient ring $O'$ containing the same values: if $m=\operatorname{ord}\psi$, choose $f$ with $m\mid\ell^f-1$ and use Hensel lifting of roots of unity from that residue field. To compare with $O$, extend both to a common finite ring of integers $O''$ in $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. Finite free extension carries coefficients $\operatorname{Frac}O/O$ to the corresponding quotient over $O''$, and cochains and local kernels extend by the finite basis. A simple factor extends to a module of length the relative ramification index. Infinite length remains infinite by faithful flatness and strict chains. Since the valuation of $\ell$ scales by the same index, normalized lengths coincide, and their finiteness is equivalent. The base traces and `GD` do not change. [Coefficient independence](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/HeightAndVanishing/CoefIndep.lean#L68)

Use the model over $O'$. For a local point $f$ let $\rho_f$ be its restriction to $G_K$. Each two-sided point is either irreducible or has an irreducible corner block and separate middle coordinate by the [rank-three shape argument](rank-three-two-sided-points-give-two-representation-types.md). The stated inputs give its allowed type, including the middle character $1$ in the second case. Thus $d(f)=\operatorname{tr}\rho_f(g)-\tau(g)\ne0$ at each such point. The [upper comparison](carrying-a-graded-orbit-valuation-bound-to-frobenius-traces.md) gives finite cotangent length $\lambda$ and a cover $T$ with one point per diagonal conjugacy orbit, with $\lambda/v_{O'}(\ell)\le2\sum_{f\in T}v_\ell(d(f))$. The [lower comparison](normalized-cocycles-give-derivations-on-the-deformation-ring.md) gives $V\le\lambda$ over these coefficients. Finally the shape argument shows that isomorphic restrictions at two such points imply diagonal conjugacy of their global normalized representations. Hence the images of $T$ give a set $X$ of pairwise nonisomorphic representations with the same summands. Apply normalization and coefficient independence to finish.
