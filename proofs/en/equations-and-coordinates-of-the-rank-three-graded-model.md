# Equations and coordinates of the rank-three graded model

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E5%93%AA%E4%BA%9B%E6%96%B9%E7%A8%8B%E5%92%8C%E5%9D%90%E6%A0%87%E7%BB%84%E6%88%90%E7%A7%A9%E4%B8%89%E7%9A%84%E5%88%86%E6%AC%A1%E6%A8%A1%E5%9E%8B.md)

Let $\ell\ne2$, $O$ the coefficient integer ring, $K=\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, and $\iota:H\to\Gamma$ a continuous homomorphism. Use sign $\eta$, continuous unit character $\Psi$, and base $r_0=(\operatorname{diag}(\Psi,1,\Psi^{-1}),\eta)$ in the [semidirect group](embedding-a-scalar-cocycle-in-a-rank-three-deformation.md), trivial on a set $N\subset\Gamma$. Assume marks $c^2=1,\eta(c)=-1,\Psi(c)=1,\eta(g_1)=1$, $\Psi(g_1)^2-1\in O^\times$. Let $U$ be the unrestricted local lifting ring, $I$ the condition, $B$ the global lifting ring trivial on $N$ and satisfying $I$ on $H$, and $S$ its normalization at $c,g_1$. Use these presentation inputs:

- $U/I$ has graded formal coordinates given by universal matrix entries minus their base values. The compatible characteristic-zero chart at the base, with kernel $I$ before quotient and constant term the base, identifies its rational cotangent coordinates and has dimensions $(1,2,3,4,2)$ in degrees $(-2,-1,0,1,2)$, zero elsewhere. The local condition is stable by conjugations reducing to identity.
- $B$ has a compatible relative graded presentation, with $a_j$ extra matrix-entry coordinates and $a_j+\mathbf1_{\{|j|\le1\}}$ extra equations in weight $j$, in addition to the old local ones.

Then $S$ has a graded model $q:A\to S$, with base $x:A\to O$, homogeneous lifts of the entries, and homogeneous polynomials in entries dense weight by weight. Put $r=a_{-1}+2,\ s=a_{-2}+1,\ r'=a_1+3,\ s'=a_2+1,\ m=a_0+3$. The environment has chart dimensions $(s,r,m,r'+1,s'+1)$ and equation counts $(s,r+1,m,r',s')$. Derivations of weight $-1$ with compatible integral local lift have the global integral lift of [the lower comparison](normalized-cocycles-give-derivations-on-the-deformation-ring.md). The two geometric and tangent properties are proved in [the point extension](moving-a-graded-point-back-to-the-local-deformation-ring.md).

**Model and count.** Write $X,Y$ for old and new variables. Set $\widehat A=O[[X,Y]]/J_{\rm loc}O[[X,Y]]$, keeping the old local relations, and take the finite sums of its homogeneous parts as $A$. Projection to weights commutes with the homogeneous relations. Extend the local chart by using the new variables freely in their assigned weights. In the rank-six representation the weights $1,0,-1$ occur on both blocks, and subtracting base values keeps each entry homogeneous, hence the chart dimensions add the $a_j$.

The equation $r(c)^2=1$ in sign $-1$ implies $M_c=JM_c^{\mathsf T}J$ for its matrix. With zero-based indices the six equations for $M_c=1$ are $(M_c)_{00}-1,(M_c)_{11}-1,(M_c)_{01},(M_c)_{10},(M_c)_{02},(M_c)_{20}$, since reflection determines the others. The two further equations are entries $01,10$ of $M_{g_1}-M_{cg_1^{-1}c}$. These eight have weights $0,0,1,-1,2,-2,1,-1$ respectively and impose precisely the normalization by representability. Their homogeneous lifts plus the extra global relations generate the kernel on $A$: in an expression by homogeneous equations with formal coefficients project each coefficient to the required complementary weight. Adding to the relative counts yields $(a_{-2}+1,a_{-1}+3,a_0+3,a_1+3,a_2+1)$. Comparing with the local dimensions plus $a_j$ gives the stated variables and equations, including $r\ge2$. After normalization $M_c=1$ gives $\Theta(M_\gamma)=M_{c\gamma}$, so the defining variables from rank-six entries become rank-three entries minus their reference values or zero. They lie in the maximal ideal. Truncating a homogeneous formal series by total degree gives homogeneous polynomials in these entries with tail in the corresponding maximal ideal power, proving density.

For a derivation $D:S\to O/(a)$ at the base supported in weight $-1$ and with compatible local integral $D_H$ killing local relations, prescribe old variable derivatives by $D_H(X_i)$. For each new variable of weight $-1$ lift $D(qY_i)$ arbitrarily to $O$; in other weights take zero. These values define a formal derivation by pairing with linear coefficients. On old variables it coincides with $D_H$, hence kills the old relations and descends to $\widehat A$ and $A$. Its reduction agrees with $Dq$ at all variables and therefore everywhere, since the product rule kills terms in the square of the base ideal.
