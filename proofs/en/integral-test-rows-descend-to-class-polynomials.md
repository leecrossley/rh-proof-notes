# Integral test rows descend to class polynomials

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $K$ be the coefficient field with complex embedding $\iota$. Fix the selected true column data $I$ with its laws and a provisional row $Z$. For each tuple $a$, the rational test rows form a $K$-vector space $V_a$ with lattice $M_a$ (`MtildeL`). A row $x\in M_a$ is a family of polynomials at row points. Its polynomial at each point is in the integral weight lattice of that point. On the complex side there is a test space for $a$, with lattice generated by a finite set $S_a$ over the coefficient ring $\mathcal O$ of the family. Assume the following comparisons, as supplied in the provisional row data.

1. There is a common complex row space for each tuple and an isomorphism from it to the complex test space. The linear test comparisons for both the base tuple and every twisted tuple identify the resulting complex test with a period row having the same value at each terminal class representative. The source calls this equality `SameForm`.
2. Each generator $v\in S_a$ has coordinates $g_v$ in a rational $K$-coordinate model. The comparison from that model to complex rows carries its integral coordinate lattice onto the $\mathcal O_K$-span of these generators.
3. Every vector of the rational coordinate model has a lift to $V_a$, preserving its complex test row. Such a lift is in $M_a$ if its coordinates lie in the integral coordinate lattice. Scalars from $\mathcal O$ map into $\mathcal O_K$.

Then for every word $u$, every integral test in the base or twisted test space, and every terminal class $c$, the class polynomial obtained by reducing the test at $c$ is the image under $\iota$ of a rational polynomial in the integral weight lattice there. This is `RowInt`. The reduced polynomial is the one used in [the integral class-period pairing](the-class-period-sum-loses-only-the-stabilizer-denominator.md).

Take an integral complex test $F=\sum_{v\in S_a}c_v v$ with $c_v\in\mathcal O$, and put $g=\sum_v c_v g_v$ in the rational coordinate model. Its complex image is $F$ by linearity, and the coefficient images are integral in $K$. Thus $g$ is in the integral coordinate lattice by the second comparison. Lift $g$ to a row $x\in V_a$; the third comparison gives $x\in M_a$. This constructs one integral rational row for the whole test, which can be used at every class and every word.

At a terminal class $c$, let $P_c$ be its representative. Its row point has the same underlying lower-level lattice as $P_c$. This is the compatibility of the representative map `toRow` with the lattice index $N$. The polynomial $x(P_c)$ is therefore in the integral weight lattice used in the class-period formula, by the defining condition of $M_a$. The complex realization of $x$ takes value $\iota(x(P_c))$ there. Since the complex test pulled back to the period row agrees with that realization at $P_c$, its reduced polynomial is exactly this embedded one. Applying the base and twisted versions of the comparison proves both clauses of `RowInt`.

The argument uses the actual integral coordinates of the provisional row. Existence of a rational row for the entire family of generators, with the comparison just stated, is supplied by the coordinate construction of the provisional rows.
