# Finitely many valuation centres for cyclotomic length bounds

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E5%88%86%E5%9C%86%E5%A1%94%E7%9A%84%E9%95%BF%E5%BA%A6%E7%95%8C%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E5%8F%AA%E9%9C%80%E8%A6%81%E6%9C%89%E9%99%90%E4%B8%AA%E8%BF%9B%E8%B5%8B%E5%80%BC%E4%B8%AD%E5%BF%83.md)

## Statement

Let $\ell$ be prime. Let $X$ be a $\mathbb Z_\ell$-module generated by at most $R$ elements, where $R\ge0$, and let $\gamma$ be an endomorphism whose reduction on $X/\ell X$ is unipotent. Let $u\in\mathbb Z_\ell$ satisfy $v_\ell(u-1)=c\ge1$, with $c\ge2$ if $\ell=2$. Set $j_0=\lfloor\log_\ell R\rfloor+3$, using the natural-number convention $\log_\ell 0=0$. For integers $a,m\ge0$ define
$$
L(a,m)=\operatorname{length}_{\mathbb Z_\ell}\operatorname{coker}\bigl((u^a\gamma)^{\ell^m}-1\bigr).
$$
There exist $s_1,\ldots,s_R\in\mathbb Z_\ell$ such that for every $a,m\ge0$,
$$
L(a,m)\le\sum_{i=1}^{R}\bigl(\max(m,j_0)+c+v_\ell(s_i-a)\bigr).
$$
Also $L(a,m)\le L(a,j_0)+R\max(m-j_0,0)$. We allow infinite lengths and use $v_\ell(0)=+\infty$. These are the estimates in `exists_centres` and `twLen_le_growth`.

## From one layer to the next

The case $R=0$ has $X=0$. Otherwise fix $a$ and write $g=u^a\gamma$. Modulo $\ell$, the space has dimension at most $R$ and $g-1$ is nilpotent, so $(g-1)^R X\subseteq\ell X$. Put $k=\lfloor\log_\ell R\rfloor+1$. Because $\ell^k\ge R$, the Frobenius identity in characteristic $\ell$ gives $(g^{\ell^k}-1)X\subseteq\ell X$. Raising $1+(g^{\ell^k}-1)$ to the $\ell$th power puts the first term beyond $1$ in $\ell^2 X$ by its factor $\ell$, and all higher terms also have image in $\ell^2 X$. Consequently $w=g^{\ell^n}$ satisfies $(w-1)X\subseteq\ell^2 X$ for every $n\ge k+1$.

Write $P=1+w+\cdots+w^{\ell-1}$. Then $P\equiv\ell\,\mathrm{id}\pmod{\ell^2 X}$ and $\ell X\subseteq\operatorname{im}P+\ell^2 X$. The finitely generated module $Y=\ell X/(\ell X\cap\operatorname{im}P)$ therefore has $Y=\ell Y$, so Nakayama's lemma gives $Y=0$. This proves $\ell X\subseteq\operatorname{im}P$ and $\operatorname{length}\operatorname{coker}P\le R$. For a composition $PQ$, the map $\operatorname{coker}(PQ)\to\operatorname{coker}P$ is surjective, and its kernel is a quotient of $\operatorname{coker}Q$. Applying this to $w^\ell-1=P(w-1)$ bounds the increase per layer by $R$. For $m\le j_0$, $g^{\ell^{j_0}}-1$ is divisible by $g^{\ell^m}-1$ as an endomorphism polynomial. Hence $L(a,m)\le L(a,j_0)$. Together these inequalities give the asserted growth bound. ## At the reference layer, reduce to a determinant

Choose a surjection $\mathbb Z_\ell^R\to X$. Lifting the images of its generators under $\gamma^{\ell^{j_0}}$ gives an integral matrix $A$ intertwining this operator with the surjection. Put $v_a=u^{a\ell^{j_0}}$. The cokernel of $v_a A-1$ surjects onto the cokernel defining $L(a,j_0)$. By Smith normal form its length is $v_\ell(\det(v_aA-1))$, including the infinite value when the determinant vanishes. Over an algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q_\ell$ this determinant is $\prod_i (v_a z_i-1)$, where the $z_i$ are the eigenvalues counted with multiplicities. For each $z=z_i$ it suffices to find a centre $s\in\mathbb Z_\ell$ with
$$
\ell^{-(j_0+c)}|s-a|_\ell\le |v_a z-1|_\ell\qquad(a\ge0).
$$
Taking valuations and adding gives the reference-layer bound. The growth bound then replaces $j_0$ by $\max(m,j_0)$.

## Intersect the ultrametric balls

The assumptions on $u$ give
$$
|v_a-v_b|_\ell=\ell^{-(j_0+c)}|a-b|_\ell .
$$
For positive exponents prime to $\ell$, the first term in the binomial expansion of $u^d-1$ has valuation $c$ and all later terms have larger valuation. Raising to the $\ell$th power raises the valuation by one. Indeed the first term has valuation $c+1$, intermediate binomial coefficients are divisible by $\ell$, and the final term has valuation $\ell c>c+1$. Iteration gives $v_\ell(u^d-1)=c+v_\ell(d)$, which proves the distance identity using the exponent difference. [Valuation of a power](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/AdditiveValuationNorms/LteNorm.lean) · [Distance for units](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/AdditiveValuationNorms/PadicUnitsDistanceLeMax.lean)

For fixed $z$, give a ball centred at the integer $a$ the radius $r_a=\ell^{j_0+c}|v_a z-1|_\ell$. If $|z|_\ell\ge1$, apply the ultrametric inequality to $(v_a-v_b)z=(v_a z-1)-(v_b z-1)$ to get $|a-b|_\ell\le\max(r_a,r_b)$. If $|z|_\ell<1$, both error terms have absolute value $1$, which gives the same bound. In any finite collection of balls in $\mathbb Z_\ell$, the centre of a ball of smallest radius belongs to all the balls. Compactness of $\mathbb Z_\ell$ gives $s$ in their full intersection. This proves the required inequality. ## The length window in the project

The tower gives $X$, the action $\gamma$ of a generator and its cyclotomic value $u$. For a layer $[L_1:L_0]=\ell^m$, `Descends` supplies $\operatorname{shaInv}(L_1,\ell,a)=L(a,m)$ in the nonzero twists used in the window. Applying the centre bound gives the first part of `ABound`. Applying the growth bound reduces the remaining comparison to the reference layer. There `shaLeV` bounds the localization kernel by the second cohomology term used in `SecondBound`.
