# Choosing among seven positive-sign length estimates

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%AD%A3%E5%8F%B7%E4%B8%8A%E5%90%8C%E8%B0%83%E7%9A%84%E4%B8%83%E7%A7%8D%E9%95%BF%E5%BA%A6%E4%BC%B0%E8%AE%A1%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E9%80%89%E7%94%A8.md)

Fix a primitive complex Dirichlet character \(\chi\) modulo \(Q\ge1\) of order \(o\). Take \(u\ge2\) and primes \(q_1\equiv3\bmod8,\ d\equiv7\bmod16\), \((q_1d,Q)=1\). Let \(N=\operatorname{lcm}(2^{u+2},Q)q_1d\), \(G_N=(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^\times\), and \(H\) the subgroup of units which are \(\pm1\bmod 2^{u+2}\), in \(\ker\chi\bmod Q\), and squares at \(q_1,d\). This is `Host`. Choose the least prime \(p_K\ge7\) with \(p_K\equiv3\bmod4,\ p_K\nmid Q\), set \(K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-p_K})\), and \(h_*=h_K\). For the odd part \(o_{\rm odd}\) of \(o\), let \(p_1\) be the least prime \(p_1\equiv1\bmod o_{\rm odd}\), \(p_1\nmid2Qp_K\). Both primes exist by Dirichlet and finite exclusion. Put \(B_\chi=\{3\}\cup\{\ell\text{ odd prime}:\ell\mid oQp_Kh_*p_1\}\).

Let \(t>0\), with \(q_1,d\) both strictly exceeding \(2t+1,Q,o,p_K,h_*,p_1\) (`Big`), \(\ell\) odd prime, and \(1\le e<t\). Let \(\psi\) be an \(O\)-valued character of \(G_N\) trivial on \(H\), for an integral domain \(O\). Its \(\psi_r\) at a rational prime is the component at \(r^{v_r(N)}\) pulled back by Chinese remaindering, trivial when \(r\nmid N\). Write \(\psi^{(\ell')}\) for its prime-to-\(\ell\) order part and \(\psi^*_{\ell,0}=\psi^{(\ell')}/(\psi^{(\ell')})_\ell\), the source's `chiP`. One of the following cases applies.

- I: \(\ell\notin B_\chi,\ \ell-1\nmid2e\).
- Z: \(\ell\notin B_\chi,\ \ell-1\mid2e,\ \psi=1\).
- II: \(e\ge2\), the order of \(\psi^*_{\ell,0}\) is even, and \(\ell\in B_\chi\) or \(\ell-1\mid2e\).
- III: \(\ell\notin B_\chi,\ \ell-1\mid2e,\ \psi\ne1\) of odd order.
- IV: \(\ell\in B_\chi,\ e\ge2\), and the order of \(\psi^*_{\ell,0}\) is odd.
- V and Vs: \(\ell\in B_\chi,\ e=1\), according as \(R_1\le2^{c_2}\) or \(R_1>2^{c_2}\). Here \(c_2=v_2(f_{\psi_2})\) for the conductor of \(\psi_2\), \(\theta=\psi^{(\ell')}/(\psi^{(\ell')})_2\), \(f_0=\operatorname{ord}_{(\mathbb Z/\operatorname{lcm}(4,\operatorname{ord}\theta)\mathbb Z)^\times}(\ell)\), \(n_0=v_2(\ell^{f_0}-1)\), and \(R_1=16o(\ell Q)^2 4^{n_0}\). The order \(f_0\) is defined since \(\ell\) is odd and prime to \(\operatorname{ord}\theta\). These last two are `CaseV` separated in `CountV` and `CountVs`.

**Proof of coverage.** The joint maps to units modulo \(2^{u+2}\) modulo \(\pm1\), the image of \(\chi\), and the two square quotients at \(q_1,d\) have kernel \(H\). Their group orders are \(2^u,o,2,2\), so \([G_N:H]\mid2^{u+2}o\). The order of \(\psi\) divides this bound. At \(r\ne2\) dividing \(Q\), a unit supported on the \(r\)-component only affects the \(\chi\) factor, so \(\operatorname{ord}\psi_r\mid o\). At \(r\ne2\) not dividing \(Q\), it affects at most one square factor, so \(\psi_r^2=1\). Also every prime in \(B_\chi\) lies below \(q_1,d\) by `Big`, and \(p_K,p_1\) avoid \(N\) since they avoid \(2Q\) and differ from both auxiliary primes.

For \(\ell\notin B_\chi\) one has \(\ell\ge5\), \(\psi^{(\ell')}=\psi\), and \(\psi_\ell^2=1\). If \(\ell-1\nmid2e\), case I applies. If \(\ell-1\mid2e\), then \(e\ge2\), \(\ell\le2e+1<2t+1\), and \(\ell\nmid2Q\) implies \(\ell\nmid N\). Thus \(\psi^*_{\ell,0}=\psi\), and the trivial, even order, and nontrivial odd order alternatives give Z, II, III. For \(\ell\in B_\chi\), \(e=1\) gives V or Vs, and \(e\ge2\) gives II or IV by parity of the order after removing the two parts. This proves coverage.

**Case I data.** For the length calculation impose \(\psi(-1)=(-1)^e\). In case I, `GD` consists of odd prime \(\ell\), \(e\ge1\), even \(N\), \(\ell\nmid\operatorname{ord}\psi\), \(\psi_\ell^2=1\), this sign, imaginary quadratic \(K\), \(\ell\nmid|\operatorname{disc}K|h_K\), \((N,|\operatorname{disc}K|)=1\), \(\ell-1\nmid2e\), and
$$
\ker(\varepsilon_\ell\bmod\ell)\cap\ker(\psi^*_{\ell,0})_G\not\subseteq G_K ,
$$
with the latter character pulled back via \(\mu_N\). All conditions preceding the display follow from the choices, since \(|\operatorname{disc}K|=p_K\) and \(p_K\nmid N\). At a place over \(p_K\) inertia fixes \(\mu_N\) and \(\mu_\ell\) since \(p_K\nmid N\ell\), so is contained in the intersection. It acts nontrivially on \(K\) since \(p_K\) ramifies there. This proves the last condition. The case estimates are supplied separately to the [positive-sign length sum](positive-sign-cohomology-bound.md).

`hostIndex` and `hostComp` control orders, `bchiFacts` handles excluded primes, `cases` proves coverage, and `gdOfHost` proves the data in case I.
