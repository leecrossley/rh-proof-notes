# The two split sides give the word-prime kernel bounds

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix a word $u$ in a true column chain of length $m$ and its word prime $T$. In a step $i<m$, set $a=N_i$, $b=N_{i+1}=a+t+1$, where $t$ is the odd chain parameter. There are $f$ coefficient embeddings $\tau$. Let $L_i$ be the lattice cut out of the Hermitian lattice by the chosen nonzero residue functional at each selected prime above $T$, and let $L_i^\sharp$ be its Hermitian dual. Use the Weyl value lattices $\mathcal V_w(\lambda_i,L_i^\sharp)$ localized at a place $w$ of the coefficient field over $T$. For both source and target take admitted arithmetic kernel data: the two Hermitian lattices with the residue functionals and frames, and the arithmetic weight function $\Xi_0$, whose values on the box are integral at $w$ and constant on its defining cosets. Assume rationality of the jet indices used by the step, the true-chain weight and lowest-alcove bounds, the split-place character unit law, and the integral moment-closure inputs below. Denote the corresponding one-step coefficient kernel by $K_i$, and write $\delta'_\tau$ for the last weight of $\lambda_{i+1,\tau}$. The two assertions for every $F\in\mathcal V_w(\lambda_i,L_i^\sharp)$ are
$$
\begin{aligned}
T^{fa-\sum_\tau\delta'_\tau}K_i(F)
 &\in \mathcal V_w(\lambda_{i+1},L_{i+1})
 &&\text{at the selected side},\\
K_i(F)
 &\in T^{f(t+1)/2}\mathcal V_w(\lambda_{i+1},L_{i+1}^\sharp)
 &&\text{at the conjugate side}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here the selected side means all the places induced by the embeddings $\tau$ are selected primes, and the conjugate side means all are conjugates of selected primes.

### Reduction and the moment hypotheses

First work with pairs whose lattices before imposing the residue functional are standard at the chosen split primes. On the selected side the moment input gives, for the finite collection of needed indices, the $T$-adic closure of the integral span of $T^{-fa}$ times point tables for maps $x$ whose Hermitian adjoint sends $L_{i+1}$ into $L_i^\sharp$ locally. On the conjugate side the moment input gives the integral closure with factor one, for maps sending the ambient source lattice into the ambient target and preserving the residue functionals. Such a map sends $L_i$ into $L_{i+1}$ on that side's conjugate prime. Pairing with these elements shows that its adjoint sends $L_{i+1}^\sharp$ into $L_i^\sharp$ at the induced place. In both moment inputs the coefficient embedding induces $w$, and the point matrices come with a fixed unit scalar per embedding. Multiplying a lattice map by that scalar after extension leaves it integral. [The weighted moment-table argument](assembling-the-weighted-word-prime-moment-table.md) gives the two support assertions of `MomAt`, together with the rationality and local integrality required by [the moment-to-operator argument](integral-moment-approximants-give-an-integral-jet-operator.md). The jet expression for $K_i$ differs from this operator by the step character scalar, a unit at $w$ by the split-place law.

On the selected side $L_i^\sharp$ is locally the standard lattice, as the relevant dual of the kernel condition is imposed on the conjugate side. Its source basis can be the identity. For the target, choose a standard basis vector $e_j$ on which the residue functional $f_0$ is nonzero. Choose lifts $c_s$ of $f_0(e_s)/f_0(e_j)$ to the local ring. A basis of the target hyperplane lattice has $T e_j$ at $j$ and $e_s-c_s e_j$ elsewhere. These vectors are in the kernel and span it since only the $j$-th coefficient must become divisible by $T$. The basis determinant is $T$. The determinant factor for the jet operator is therefore $T^{-\sum_\tau s_\tau}=T^{\sum_\tau\delta'_\tau}$, where the weight compatibility identifies the target determinant shift with $-s_\tau$. Combining with $T^{-fa}$ gives the first exponent in the assertion.

On the conjugate side the localized Hermitian dual basis is the inverse conjugate transpose of a kernel basis at the selected prime. Both source and target dual basis determinants have the valuation of $T^{-1}$. The operator determinant factor thus has the valuation of $T^{\sum_\tau(s_\tau-s'_\tau)}$. To see this difference, the true weights in the old $a$ positions decrease by $(b-a)/2$: their underlying unshifted weights stay the same and their shift changes from $e-a/2$ to $e-b/2$ at this step. The compatibility equation $\lambda'_{\tau,j}+s_\tau=\lambda_{\tau,j}+s'_\tau$ then gives $s_\tau-s'_\tau=(t+1)/2$. With the unscaled moment table this proves the second factor $T^{f(t+1)/2}$.

For both cases the input Weyl lattice sits inside the dual Weyl lattice used by the jet estimate. On the output, the [lowest-alcove comparison](lowest-alcove-simplicity-identifies-the-two-value-lattices.md) makes the two lattices agree after localization. Thus one gets the claimed Weyl output.

### Return to arbitrary representatives

The rational unitary representatives theorem supplies changes of coordinates $\gamma,\gamma'$ putting the ambient lattices in standard form at the required split primes. The selected primes are split and no pair consists of a prime and its conjugate, as required by the theorem. These changes carry the hyperplane and Hermitian dual lattices with them; the latter uses unitarity. Replace $x$ in $\Xi_0$ by $\gamma'^{-1}x\gamma$. This bijection carries each local homomorphism lattice and each defining box coset to the original one, so the box support, constancy and value integrality are preserved. The rational kernel covariance identifies the standard kernel with the original one conjugated by the two polynomial actions. Both actions transport their localized value lattices bijectively. Pulling back the standard estimates proves the estimates for every pair of representatives. The true-chain kernel entry is this coefficient kernel on the representative data, and the chain identifies the shift $s_{i,\tau}$ of the point formulas with $-\delta'_\tau$.
