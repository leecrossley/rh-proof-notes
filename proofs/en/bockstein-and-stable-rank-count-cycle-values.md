# Bockstein and stable rank count cycle values

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E7%94%A8Bockstein%E5%92%8C%E7%A8%B3%E5%AE%9A%E7%A7%A9%E6%95%B0%E5%87%BA%E5%91%A8%E6%9C%9F%E5%80%BC.md)

## Statement

Let $L$ be totally imaginary, $j\ge0$ even with $j+1<r_2(L)$, $\ell$ prime, $\nu\ge0$, $q=\ell^\nu$, and $B$ a $\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z$-module. For each $G_n=\mathrm{GL}_n(O_L)$ let $\Phi_n:C_{j+1}(G_n,\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z)\to B$ be linear on the bar complex with trivial coefficients and kill boundaries. On cycles, adjoining identity blocks preserves its value, and repeating the matrix block $m\ge1$ times multiplies it by $m$. Put $n_0=2(j+1)+4$ and $T_i=|\operatorname{Tor}H_i(G_{n_0},\mathbb Z)|$. Any finite set of values $\ell^j\Phi_n(y)$ with cycles $y$ at arbitrary ranks has size at most $q^{r_2(L)}T_{j+1}T_j$. This is `SUpper`, used for [weighted norm values](weighted-norm-power-classes-in-matrix-group-homology.md).

## Proof

Stabilize the finitely many chosen representatives to a common $n\ge n_0$. The cited stability range gives torsion orders $T_i$ for both $i=j,j+1$ at all ranks at least $n_0$.

Lift a mod-$q$ cycle $y$ to an integral bar chain $x$. Its boundary is $qw$ for an integral cycle $w$, since the integral chain groups are free. The homomorphism $\beta:y\mapsto[w]$ lands in $H_j(G_n,\mathbb Z)[q]$ and is independent of the lift, because replacing $x$ by $x+qz$ adds $\partial z$ to $w$. Its kernel consists exactly of reductions of integral cycles: if $w=\partial z$, use $x-qz$. There are at most $T_j$ fibers. Differences of values in a fixed fiber come from the kernel, so it suffices to bound values on integral cycles by $q^{r_2(L)}T_{j+1}$.

The functionals induce $\phi_N:H_{j+1}(G_N,\mathbb Z)\to B$. Let $n_i=\ell^i n$, $z_0(x)=x$ and
$$
z_{i+1}(x)=\psi_\ell z_i(x)-\ell^{i+2}\iota z_i(x),
$$
where $\psi_\ell$ repeats $\ell$ blocks and $\iota$ stabilizes to $n_{i+1}$. Each step multiplies the value by $\ell-\ell^{i+2}$, so $\phi_{n_j}(z_j(x))=\ell^j u\phi_n(x)$ with $u=\prod_{i=0}^{j-1}(1-\ell^{i+1})$ a unit modulo $q$. This includes the empty product for $j=0$.

By [the rank bound `BOdd`](repeated-blocks-leave-one-direction-per-complex-place.md), the subgroup spanned by the $z_j(x)$ has rational rank at most $r_2(L)$. For any finite list of values let $D$ be the group generated by their $z_j(x)$. Its torsion embeds in that of $H_{j+1}(G_{n_j},\mathbb Z)$, and its free rank is at most $r_2(L)$. Therefore $|D/qD|\le q^{r_2(L)}T_{j+1}$. The map $u^{-1}\phi_{n_j}$ factors through this quotient as $qB=0$, counting the values in one fiber. Multiply by at most $T_j$ fibers.
