# Embedding a scalar cocycle in a rank-three deformation

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E6%A0%87%E9%87%8F%E4%BD%99%E9%97%AD%E9%93%BE%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E8%BF%9B%E5%85%A5%E7%A7%A9%E4%B8%89%E5%8F%98%E5%BD%A2.md)

Let $O$ be the integers of a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_\ell$, $\ell$ odd, and $W=\operatorname{Frac}(O)/O$ discrete. Let $G$ be a topological group and $\eta:G\to\{\pm1\}$, $\Psi:G\to O^\times$ characters, with $\alpha=\Psi^{-1}$ acting continuously on $W$. Take $c,g_1\in G$ with $c^2=1,\eta(c)=-1,\Psi(c)=1,\eta(g_1)=1$, and $\Psi(g_1)^2-1\in O^\times$ (`Marks`). Let $\mathcal D$ be a family of local subgroups, $B\in\mathcal D$ with some $h\in B$ such that $\Psi(h)^2-1$ is a unit. Let each element of $\mathcal N\subset G$ lie in some subgroup of $\mathcal D$ and have $\eta=\Psi=1$. Set $S=\ker(H^1(G,W(\alpha))\to\prod_{D\in\mathcal D}H^1(D,W(\alpha)))$.

Write $J$ for the matrix with ones on the antidiagonal, $\Theta(A)=J(A^{-1})^{\mathsf T}J$, and $D(g)=\operatorname{diag}(\Psi(g),1,\Psi(g)^{-1})$. The order-two automorphism $\Theta$ fixes $D(g)$, so $r_0(g)=(D(g),\eta(g))$ is a representation in the semidirect product. Its linearized action on matrices is conjugation by $D(g)$, preceded for $\eta(g)=-1$ by $-JX^{\mathsf T}J$. For every $a\in O\setminus\{0\}$ there is an additive injection from $S[a]$ into continuous cocycles $\gamma:G\to M_3(O/(a))$ with this action. They are supported at $(2,1),(3,2)$, of row minus column weight $-1$ for weights $1,0,-1$, vanish at $c,g_1,cg_1^{-1}c$ and $\mathcal N$, and restrict on $B$ to a coboundary supported in the same coordinates.

**Proof.** Since $\alpha(g_1)-1$ is a unit, subtract the coboundary of $z(g_1)/(\alpha(g_1)-1)$ from a representative. This gives a unique representative $z_x$ vanishing at $g_1$, unique since such a coboundary must be zero. Uniqueness gives $O$-linearity. For $x\in S[a]$, $a z_x=0$. Use $O/(a)\simeq W[a]$ via $u\mapsto u/a\bmod O$ to write it as $t_x$. On $\mathcal N$, the local coboundary vanishes since $\alpha=1$. On $B$ its primitive $m_B\in W$ is killed by $a$, by evaluation at $h$ where $\alpha(h)-1$ is a unit. Thus $t_x|_B=(\alpha-1)b$ in $O/(a)$. The relation $c^2=1$ and $\alpha(c)=1$ give $2z_x(c)=0$, hence zero. The cocycle identity with $z_x(g_1)=0$ gives vanishing at $g_1^{-1}$ and $cg_1^{-1}c$.

For matrix units $E_{ij}$ set $X(t)=t(E_{21}-E_{32})$. The signed transpose interchanges the two units and changes their signs, so fixes the difference, and diagonal conjugation multiplies both by $\Psi(g)^{-1}$. Hence $g\cdot X(t)=X(\alpha(g)t)$. Put $\gamma_x(g)=X(t_x(g))$. Equivariance gives the matrix cocycle and preserves all vanishing conditions; on $B$ it equals $g\cdot X(b)-X(b)$, or the coboundary written with opposite convention using $-X(b)$. The $(2,1)$-entry recovers $t_x$, proving injectivity.

In case I the project uses $G_{\mathbb Q}$, $\Psi=\varepsilon_\ell^e\psi_G$, the sign of the imaginary quadratic field, and $B$ over $\ell$. The resulting cocycles give [square-zero deformations](normalized-cocycles-give-derivations-on-the-deformation-ring.md).
