# Three places above the coefficient prime remove the auxiliary exceptions

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

## Statement

Let $E/F$ be a CM number field with $F$ its maximal real subfield, with $[F:\mathbb Q]$ even and $E/F$ unramified at every finite place. Fix its algebraic closure $\Omega$, a coefficient prime $\ell$, and a field isomorphism $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$. Assume every place above $\ell$ splits over $F$, and there are three places $w_1,w_2,w_3$ above $\ell$ with pairwise distinct restrictions to $F$. Suppose $d>0$, $x^2=-d$ for some $x\in E$, and $-d$ is a square in $\mathbb Q_\ell$. Let $U$ be a definite unitary automorphic representation of odd rank $a$ with dominant weights $\lambda_\sigma$ at complex embeddings $\sigma$ of $E$. Put
$$
Q=\{w \text{ split over } F:\ -d\text{ is a square in }\mathbb Q_p\text{ for }p\text{ below }w\},
\qquad
J_\tau=\{k-(\lambda_{\iota\circ\tau})_k:0\le k<a\}.
$$
For each usable $w\in Q$ and each Satake multiset $B$ for $U$ at $w$, write $S=q_w^{(a-1)/2}B$. The objective is a continuous semisimple $r:\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/E)\to\mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ with $C_w(r;S,J)$ at all these places. This comparison consists of the unramified Frobenius eigenvalues outside $\ell$ and, at every inducing bridge above $\ell$, crystallinity, de Rhamness, the filtration jumps $J_\tau$, and the Weil characteristic polynomials $\prod_{z\in S}(X-z^{-\deg g})$ at each Weil element $g$. [Odd-rank spherical comparison on the usable set](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/LimOddOfLimWU3.lean#L61)

Assume the odd-rank limit input `LimOdd` and the crystalline power-sum closure `ClosedPhi` stated below. Then the objective follows. This is the passage from `LimOdd` through `LimU` to `CoreUd`. ## What the limit input supplies

Take a finite set $T$ of nonsplit places containing all those at which $U$ is ramified. The level condition of an automorphic form fixes some nonzero vector under an open subgroup of the finite adeles. Outside finitely many places this subgroup contains the given integral local subgroups. Their invariance of this vector supplies the required unramified condition away from the finite set at nonsplit places. This is the level input `AutT`. [Arguments of the limit theorem](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/LimOddOfLimWU3.lean#L21) · [Obtaining a finite set of nonsplit exceptions](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/AutTHolds.lean#L1)

Choose two places $u,v$ above $\ell$ with distinct restrictions to $F$. The odd-rank limit theorem for $(E,\Omega,\ell,\iota,a,d,T,U,u,v)$ supplies a continuous semisimple $r_{u,v}$ with the following properties.

- At every $w\in Q$ prime to $\ell$ and every Satake multiset $B$ of $U$ there, $r_{u,v}$ is unramified and has normalized eigenvalues $S$ on inverse Frobenius under $\iota$.
- At every $w\mid\ell$ with restriction to $F$ distinct from the restrictions of $u,v$, if $U$ has a Satake multiset there, $r_{u,v}$ is crystalline and de Rham and has the jumps determined by the weights of $U$. This holds for each inducing bridge and embedding as in $C_w$.
- At those places above $\ell$, fix a Satake multiset and an inducing bridge, and fix a Weil element $g$. Write $y$ for the normalized unramified Satake Weil–Deligne parameter with eigenvalue data $S$. There is a family of continuous seed representations $\rho$ of the same rank, all crystalline at the bridge and with one common family of jumps there. For every positive tolerance and every finite number of exponents $N$, the same finite linear combination of seed functions approximates the global trace $\operatorname{tr}r_{u,v}$ uniformly on the Galois group and the specified local sequence $\iota^{-1}\operatorname{tr}(y(g)^j)$ for $0\le j\le N$. The seed function at exponent $j$ is $\iota^{-1}\operatorname{tr}(x_\rho(g)^j)$ for its Weil–Deligne parameter $x_\rho$ at this bridge.

The field assumptions above imply the input's nonsplit conditions: each nonsplit place is unramified in $E/F$ and avoids $\ell$. The [global trace construction](constructing-the-limit-trace-from-dense-frobenius-values.md) and the [joint approximation](one-set-of-coefficients-approximates-global-and-local-traces.md) explain the trace part of this input. The full construction also uses its level and seed hypotheses. ## Obtaining the characteristic polynomial at the allowed places

Fix $w$ and $g$ as in the last item. The closure theorem says that if a continuous semisimple $r$ and a family of continuous crystalline seed representations of the same rank satisfy this joint approximation at a fixed bridge, with a common jump family for the seeds, then the specified power sums equal those of the actual Weil–Deligne parameter of $r$. To reduce it to the one-trace closure, fix $j$. Use tolerance $\epsilon$ and $N=j$ in the approximation, and use the single Weil element $g^j$ in the trace theorem. The trace of the Weil operator at $g^j$ is the power sum of order $j$ at $g$, because the Weil action is a representation. The crystalline condition of a seed implies its semistable condition at the same bridge, as required for that trace theorem. Its conclusion is equality with $\iota^{-1}\operatorname{tr}(y(g)^j)$, and applying $\iota$ gives the ordinary equality of complex power sums. This uses [crystalline trace closedness](arbitrarily-small-crystalline-trace-errors-vanish.md) together with the period comparison constructions cited there. [Reduction of the power-sum closure to trace closure](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ClosedPhiFunctoriality/ClosedPhiOfClosedTr.lean#L72)

Power sums determine the characteristic polynomial. The sum at exponent zero counts the dimension with multiplicity. In rank $a$, if $e_j$ is the $j$th elementary symmetric function of the eigenvalues and $p_j$ their $j$th power sum, Newton's recurrence
$$
j e_j=\sum_{i=1}^j(-1)^{i-1}e_{j-i}p_i,\qquad e_0=1,\quad 1\le j\le a,
$$
determines every coefficient over $\mathbb C$. So $r_{u,v}$ has the desired polynomial at every Weil element for the considered $w$. The de Rham and crystalline assertions already came from the second item. Regular Hodge–Tate jumps take the displayed form $J_\tau$ because the infinitesimal entries at $\iota\circ\tau$ are $(a-1)/2-k+(\lambda_{\iota\circ\tau})_k$. This yields $C_w$ at each allowed $w\mid\ell$. ## Removing the exceptions for one global representation

Take $r=r_{w_1,w_2}$. This already has the required comparison at the usable places prime to $\ell$, where all inducing bridges in the above-$\ell$ clause are absent. For any specified $w\mid\ell$ with a Satake multiset, at most one of the three restrictions of the $w_i$ to $F$ equals the restriction of $w$. Therefore two of the three can be taken as $u,v$ with $w$ outside both auxiliary restrictions. The preceding argument gives the comparison at $w$ for $r_{u,v}$.

For all but finitely many places of prime absolute norm, the place is usable, prime to $\ell$, and $U$ has a Satake multiset there. The first property follows from the [degree-one calculation](degree-one-frobenius-elements-are-dense-at-the-usable-split-places.md), and the Satake property follows from the cofinite split Satake input. Thus $r$ and $r_{u,v}$ have the same polynomials on a dense set of inverse Frobenius lifts. By continuity and the semisimple character criterion they are isomorphic. Transport the local period comparison along this isomorphism. This gives the comparison at the chosen $w$ for the original $r$. As $w$ was arbitrary, it resolves every auxiliary exception. All places above $\ell$ are usable because they split and $-d$ is a square in $\mathbb Q_\ell$.
