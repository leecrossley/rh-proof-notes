# Quadratic restriction costs at most one in cohomology length

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%BA%8C%E6%AC%A1%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E5%8F%AA%E6%8D%9F%E5%A4%B1%E4%B8%80%E5%8D%95%E4%BD%8D%E5%90%8C%E8%B0%83%E9%95%BF%E5%BA%A6.md)

For a number field $E$ and $k\in\mathbb Z$, put $M_k=\mathbb Q_2/\mathbb Z_2(-k)$ with action by the $-k$-th power of the $2$-adic cyclotomic character. Let $\Sha(E,2,k)\subset H^1(E,M_k)$ require zero restriction to the decomposition groups above $2$ and inertia at all other finite places. Write $\operatorname{shaInv}$ for its $\mathbb Z_2$-length, allowing infinity. For $E\subset E'$ in an algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q$, with $[E':E]\mid2$, one has
$$
\operatorname{shaInv}(E,2,k)\le1+\operatorname{shaInv}(E',2,k).
$$
[`shaTwo`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/ShaTwo.lean#L11)

**Proof.** For a subgroup $U'\le U$ of index two in a topological group and any continuous $\mathbb Z_2$-linear action on discrete $M=\mathbb Q_2/\mathbb Z_2$, take $\sigma\notin U'$. A class in the restriction kernel has a cocycle $z$ with $z|_{U'}=0$ by subtracting a coboundary. It is determined by $z(\sigma)\in M^{U'}$, the invariance following from $h\sigma=\sigma(\sigma^{-1}h\sigma)$. Changing such a representative changes its value by $(\sigma-1)m$, $m\in M^{U'}$, and this difference suffices for cohomology equivalence. This injects the restriction kernel into $M^{U'}/(\sigma-1)M^{U'}$. Since $0=z(\sigma^2)=(1+\sigma)z(\sigma)$, its image is killed by two. Any pair of cyclic submodules of $\mathbb Q_2/\mathbb Z_2$ is comparable: representatives in $\mathbb Q_2$ can be ordered by norm and the smaller is an integral multiple of the larger. Comparability passes to subquotients, so a subgroup killed by two has dimension at most one. Thus the restriction kernel has length at most one, also valid for index one. Local inertia and decomposition groups restrict by intersection with $G_{E'}$; the local zero conditions persist. Add the length of the kernel and the length of the image to obtain the displayed inequality. **Comparison fields.** Take the [two-adic tower setup](uniform-generator-count-for-the-varying-two-adic-tower.md), $L_0=K(i)K_qK_d$ with $b=[K:\mathbb Q]$. Then $i\in L_0\subset\mathbb Q(\mu_{4mqd})$ and $[L_0:\mathbb Q]\le8b$, multiplying by at most two at each adjunction. For $e\ge2$, an `IsCompField` $E$ embeds into $\mathbb Q(\mu_N)$, with $2^{e+2},m,q,d\mid N$, and is fixed by the automorphisms acting by exponent $a$ satisfying the subgroup condition modulo $m$ for $K$, both Legendre symbols $1$ modulo $q,d$, and $a\equiv\pm1\bmod2^{e+2}$. Thus it is the compositum of $KK_qK_d$ with the real $2^{e+2}$-th cyclotomic subfield, up to the given embedding. Take $L_1=L_0(\mu_{2^{e+2}})$. This replaces the last congruence by $a\equiv1$; the condition on $i$ follows already. Hence $E\subset L_1$ and $[L_1:E]\mid2$. Since $i\in L_0$, $[L_1:L_0]$ divides the size $2^e$ of the kernel of unit reduction from $2^{e+2}$ to $4$. In particular it is $2^j$ with $j\le e+2$. Apply the restriction inequality to $E\subset L_1$ for each $k\ge1$. This gives the `TwoBase` requirements and the additive one in the subsequent length bound.
