# Completing the local comparisons for the Galois representation

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

## Statement

Let $E$ be a CM number field with maximal real subfield $F$ and conjugation $c$. Fix an algebraic closure $\Omega$ of $E$, a prime $\ell$, a field isomorphism $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$, and an integer $a\ge1$. Let $\pi$ be a regular algebraic cuspidal automorphic representation of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb A_E)$ polarized by a Hecke character $\chi$ of $F$. Here regular algebraicity means that at each $\alpha:E\to\mathbb C$ the infinitesimal multiset $\Lambda_\alpha$ has distinct entries in $(a-1)/2+\mathbb Z$. The polarization imposes $\chi_v(-1)=(-1)^a$ at every real place of $F$ and a conjugate-equivariant dual pairing between $\pi\otimes((\chi\circ N_{E/F})^{-1}\circ\det)$ and $\pi$. [Regular algebraicity](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/AutomorphicMembership/ExpoConjOfIsCsd.lean#L591) · [The polarization convention](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GaloisGeometry/DualIsConjByOne.lean#L49)

Assume the three inputs stated below: spherical existence and comparison (`CoreAll`), comparison for unlinked principal-series support (`R1`), and comparison on principal congruence invariants (`LocalGlobalCompatAtIwahoriFixedPlaces`). There is a continuous semisimple representation
$$
r:\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/E)\longrightarrow\mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)
$$
with the following properties. There is an integer $b$ and there are integer multisets $H_\tau$ for $\tau:E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ satisfying
$$
H_\tau=\{(a-1)/2-z:z\in\Lambda_{\iota\circ\tau}\},
\qquad H_{\tau c}=\{b-h:h\in H_\tau\}.
$$
Each multiset is counted with multiplicity. To state the local assertions, let $w$ be a finite place of $E$, choose an extension of its valuation to $\Omega$, and take a local component $\sigma$ of $\pi$ at $w$. Use any reciprocity map $\operatorname{rec}$ satisfying `IsRecT` for this place and valuation. Write $y=\operatorname{rec}(\sigma\otimes|\det|_w^{(1-a)/2})$, using the project's algebraic normalization. This reciprocity condition specifies a map from the smooth irreducible representations of all ranks to isomorphism classes of Frobenius-semisimple complex Weil–Deligne representations, equipped with local Artin reciprocity and the laws in `LawsT`. The laws include preservation of rank, local Artin reciprocity on characters, the Langlands segment formula, irreducibility for supercuspidals and the depth-zero type formula. Let $x$ be the corresponding Weil–Deligne class from $r$, using $\iota$ and the same chosen valuation, via the away-from-$\ell$ construction if $w\nmid\ell$ and the potentially semistable construction if $w\mid\ell$. [The admissible reciprocity condition](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/IsRecTOfIsRec.lean#L55) · [The local normalizations and period clauses](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GaloisGeometry/IsWeight.lean#L28)

- If $w\nmid\ell$, $x$ and $y$ have the same rank. If $y$ has zero monodromy and its Weil action has trivial commutators, then $x=y$ as Weil–Deligne classes. If $E_w$ is continuously isomorphic to $\mathbb Q_p$ for a prime $p$, $y$ is tame with zero monodromy, and every repeated eigenvalue $z$ at any generator of tame inertia satisfies $z^{q_w-1}=1$ for $q_w=\#k_w$, then $x$ has zero monodromy and the same characteristic polynomials as $y$ on inertia.
- If $w\mid\ell$ and $\sigma$ is spherical, the local representation of $r$ is crystalline and de Rham, with filtration jumps $H_\tau$ at each embedding induced at this place.
- If $w\mid\ell$, $y$ has zero monodromy, and either its Weil action has trivial commutators or the tame hypothesis of the first item holds, the local representation is de Rham and potentially semistable, with filtration jumps $H_\tau$ at each induced embedding. The Weil–Deligne comparison in this assertion means the same rank, the same full class in the commutator case, and zero monodromy and equality of inertia polynomials in the tame case.

At places above $\ell$, the assertions are for every choice of local period rings $(P,A_{\inf})$ admitting the required tying data, for every $\mathbb Z_\ell$-algebra $O$ with topology, and for each local bridge inducing the chosen place on the coefficient field and identifying the local action with the global decomposition group. The embedding in the jump assertion ranges over $\tau:E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ induced by an embedding of the local completion into a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_\ell$ through the given bridge. These are the universal quantifiers of `Props.above`. [The local comparison and embedding quantifiers](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjAlt.lean#L50)

This is `Row0 recT`. It implies `Row0b recT`, which adds to $E_w\simeq\mathbb Q_p$ in the tame clause the size requirement $a=1$ or $16a^2<p$ (using the rank of $y$ in the away clause). The commutator and spherical clauses carry over directly. [Size condition](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean#L15) · [Conclusion Row0b](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean#L69)

## The three inputs

For the global input, call $r$ *weakly attached* if it is continuous and semisimple and, at all but finitely many finite places $w$, is unramified with Frobenius eigenvalue multiset $q_w^{(a-1)/2}B_w$ after applying $\iota$, where $B_w$ is the Satake multiset of $\pi$. The eigenvalues refer to the inverse of a lift of the residue-field power Frobenius, as in `FrobEig`. The spherical input asserts that for every $(E,\Omega,\ell,\iota,a,\pi,\chi)$ as above a weakly attached $r$ exists. For any such $r$ and every place with a Satake multiset, it asserts the prescribed unramified eigenvalues away from $\ell$, de Rhamness and the specified jumps at an inducing bridge, crystallinity at the spherical component, and comparison of all Weil characteristic polynomials with the algebraically normalized unramified parameter. This last clause lies in `CoreAll` and the deduction below uses its de Rham and crystalline clauses. [Weak attachment](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/AutomorphicForms/BlochKatoSelmerVanishes.lean#L68) · [The four spherical clauses](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GaloisGeometry/CyclicStrongAtCusp.lean#L79)

For the toral input, suppose the algebraically normalized local component has supercuspidal support made up of rank-one characters with no pair of characters differing by the twist $|\cdot|$. For every weakly attached $r$, `R1` gives zero monodromy and equality of the two full Weil–Deligne classes away from $\ell$. At an inducing bridge above $\ell$, it gives de Rhamness with the prescribed jumps and potential semistability, zero monodromy, and equality of the characteristic polynomials at every Weil element. The quantifiers range over every permitted reciprocity map; the support condition is `PlainTor`. [The toral comparison input](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Statements/EllipticCurveLevels/IsQg.lean#L13)

For the second local input, assume $\sigma$ has nonzero invariants under $K_1=1+\mathfrak m_w M_a(\mathcal O_w)$ and that $y$ has zero monodromy. Suppose either the normalized component has the toral support just described, or $E_w\simeq\mathbb Q_p$ and its support has no two occurrences of rank at least two which are unramified twists of one another. For every weakly attached $r$ and allowed reciprocity map the input gives zero monodromy for $x$ and equality of $x$ and $y$'s characteristic polynomials for every inertia element. At the inducing bridges above $\ell$ it additionally supplies de Rhamness, the prescribed jumps and potential semistability. [The support condition and the second local comparison input](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/SphericalGaloisCompatibility/PAdicSplitting/LocalGlobalCompatAtIwahoriFixedPlaces.lean#L14)

## Proof

Choose the weakly attached representation furnished by `CoreAll`. Its continuity and semisimplicity are properties of this same choice. For each $\tau$, regular algebraicity gives a unique integral multiset with the displayed complex image, since the map $\mathbb Z\to\mathbb C$ is injective. The [CM weight calculation](cm-conjugation-reflects-hodge-tate-weights-about-a-common-integer.md) gives one integer $b$ for all embeddings. In the notation of the spherical input it gives $\Lambda_{\iota\tau c}=\{a-1-b-z:z\in\Lambda_{\iota\tau}\}$, and subtracting from $(a-1)/2$ gives $H_{\tau c}=b-H_\tau$. [Construction of the integer multisets](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/InertCharpoly/GaloisLocalGlobalOfLocalGlobalCompatibility.lean#L127)

The local components of cuspidal $\mathrm{GL}_a$ representations are generic, and the twist used for $\sigma$ preserves genericity. Thus we may apply the [support recognition proof](tame-inertia-repeated-roots-exclude-repeated-higher-rank-types.md) to $y$. It turns the trivial-commutator and zero-monodromy assumption into the support hypothesis of `R1`. It turns the tame and repeated-root assumptions into a nonzero $K_1$-fixed subspace and the unramified-twist exclusion needed for the second local input. The fixed subspace transfers through the algebraic twist because its character is trivial on $K_1$. In the local comparison, every Weil element of degree zero belongs to inertia. More explicitly, a Weil element has the form $\Phi^d t$ with $d$ its degree and $t$ in inertia. Therefore equality of the polynomials at the inertia arguments already gives the entire degree-zero assertion. Equality at the identity also gives equality of ranks by taking degrees. [Inertia polynomial and rank deductions](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/InertCharpoly/GaloisLocalGlobalOfLocalGlobalCompatibility.lean#L53) · [Genericity and the tame support reduction](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/InertCharpoly/GaloisLocalGlobalOfLocalGlobalCompatibility.lean#L66)

Away from $\ell$, the rank assertion holds by construction of $x$ and the rank law of $\operatorname{rec}$, whether or not the additional local hypotheses hold. The toral case now follows from the first local input. In the tame case the second local input applies and gives precisely the required polynomial comparison and zero monodromy. Above $\ell$, in the spherical case first choose the Satake multiset of the spherical component. The two spherical clauses give crystallinity and de Rhamness. To identify the jumps with our chosen $H_\tau$, apply the de Rham clause with $J=H_\tau$; the required equality for $J$ is the weight calculation already proved. [Comparison away from the coefficient prime](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/InertCharpoly/GaloisLocalGlobalOfLocalGlobalCompatibility.lean#L96) · [Spherical comparison and row assembly](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/InertCharpoly/GaloisLocalGlobalOfLocalGlobalCompatibility.lean#L150)

For the remaining above-$\ell$ assertion, first test whether $y$ has trivial commutators. In that case `R1` gives all Weil characteristic polynomials and both monodromy operators vanish. Equality of the polynomials gives equality of traces, so the Frobenius-semisimple Weil actions are isomorphic, and the intertwiner also preserves the two zero monodromy operators. The project proves this character-determination step for the Weil actions in question in `weil_rep_equiv_of_charpoly_eq`. If the commutator condition fails, the stated disjunction forces the tame branch. There the second local input gives equality on inertia and zero monodromy for $x$; the dimension follows at the identity. In either branch the local comparison input gives potential semistability and the de Rham clause, from which the weight calculation gives the required jumps once more. [Full Weil–Deligne equality at zero monodromy](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/SphericalContragredientMonomial.lean#L39) · [The two comparisons above the coefficient prime](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/InertCharpoly/GaloisLocalGlobalOfLocalGlobalCompatibility.lean#L108)

These properties use a single $r$, one $b$ and one family $H_\tau$. For `Row0b` reuse those choices. A field isomorphism with the size condition supplies the same field isomorphism on forgetting the extra inequality, so the `Row0` tame implication applies. In the `Row0b` clause above $\ell$, map the tame side of the disjunction this way, and use the commutator side unchanged. The resulting Weil–Deligne conclusions restrict in exactly the same manner. [Restriction of the local clauses](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean#L75) · [Row0 implies Row0b](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean#L84)

## Where the three inputs come from

The final source uses the preceding argument with the same reciprocity law at both types of places. In `Development.lean` it builds `CoreAll` from the geometric seed construction, congruence and trace closure, theta ascent and descent. It constructs `R1` from that spherical input. The principal congruence comparison is then constructed from `R1` using support and base change. The resulting `Row0b` is passed to the main argument as the representation input. The construction of the three inputs is a separate part of the reconstruction.
