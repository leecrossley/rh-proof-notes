# Normalized rank-four representations have diagonal conjugacy

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E8%A7%84%E8%8C%83%E5%8C%96%E7%9A%84%E7%A7%A9%E5%9B%9B%E8%A1%A8%E7%A4%BA%E5%8F%AA%E5%89%A9%E5%AF%B9%E8%A7%92%E5%85%B1%E8%BD%AD.md)

Let $K$ have a nonarchimedean absolute value. On the ordered basis $0,1,2,3$ use the alternating form with $\langle e_0,e_3\rangle=\langle e_1,e_2\rangle=1$ and other distinct pairs zero up to antisymmetry. The blocks are $\{0,2\},\{1,3\}$. Let $r,r':G\to{\rm GL}_4(K)$ be similitudes of the form with the same multiplier $\nu$, and $r(G)$ span $M_4(K)$. At $\varphi$ assume $r(\varphi),r'(\varphi)$ block diagonal and entrywise at distance $<1$ from the same $D_0=\operatorname{diag}(d_i)$, with $|d_i-d_j|\ge1$ across blocks. At $g_0$, define $H=r(g_0)+\nu(g_0)r(g_0^{-1})$ and $H'$ analogously. Assume their entries $02,20$ vanish and $H_{22}\ne H'_{00}$, $H_{00}\ne H'_{22}$. If $r\simeq r'$, then there exist $a,b\in K^\times$ such that $T=\operatorname{diag}(ab,b,a,1)$ conjugates $r$ to $r'$.

**Proof.** Let $u$ be an intertwiner. If any cross-block entry is nonzero choose $u_{ij}$ of maximal norm $m>0$ among them. At $\varphi$,
$$
(d_i-d_j)u_{ij}=(u(r(\varphi)-D_0)-(r'(\varphi)-D_0)u)_{ij}.
$$
Since the two errors are block diagonal, every summand on the right involves a cross-block $u$ entry times an error of norm $<1$. The right side has norm $<m$ whereas the left has norm at least $m$. Thus $u$ is block diagonal. From $uH=H'u$ and the indicated zeros take entries $02,20$, obtaining $u_{02}(H_{22}-H'_{00})=u_{20}(H_{00}-H'_{22})=0$. Hence the first block of $u$ is diagonal.

For the symplectic adjoint $*$, $r(g)^*=\nu(g)r(g)^{-1}$, and likewise for $r'$. Adjoints of the intertwining at $g^{-1}$ give $r(g)u^*=u^*r'(g)$. Thus $u^*u$ commutes with all $M_4(K)$ by spanning, so is a nonzero scalar. Hence $u$ is a similitude. The two blocks pair perfectly, so its second block is the inverse transpose of the first times the multiplier in the paired bases, and is diagonal. Its diagonal entries satisfy $u_0u_3=u_1u_2$. With $a=u_2/u_3,\ b=u_1/u_3$, $u=u_3T$, which gives the same conjugation. For the project's characteristic-zero points `eptInt` gives the residual distance $<1$, and the normalization at `D.ph` gives the block zeroes. Its residual characters are different across blocks by `facts`. At $g_0$, the reference coordinates at $0,2$ are $\alpha\Psi,\alpha$, multiplier $\alpha\Psi$. Hence their two reference values in $H$ are $\alpha\Psi+1,\alpha+\Psi$, with difference $(\alpha-1)(\Psi-1)$ a unit by `D.Good`. Both actual points are close to the same reference values, so the two cross differences are nonzero. `Raw.Nrm` supplies the two zero entries.
