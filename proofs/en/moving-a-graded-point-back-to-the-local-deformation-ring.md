# Moving a graded point back to the local deformation ring

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E5%88%86%E6%AC%A1%E7%82%B9%E8%83%BD%E7%A7%BB%E5%9B%9E%E5%B1%80%E9%83%A8%E5%8F%98%E5%BD%A2%E7%8E%AF.md)

Let $O$ be a finite $\ell$-adic coefficient integer ring, with uniformizer $\varpi$, and $K=\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. Take $\widehat A=O[[Z_1,\dots,Z_d]]/J_0$ with integer weights $w_i$, $J_0$ compatible with weight projection and the origin given by the constant term. Put $A=\bigoplus_j A_j$ for finite sums of homogeneous parts. Let $q:\widehat A\twoheadrightarrow S$ be a surjection of complete local $O$-algebras with kernel generated by homogeneous $g_v\in A$. Put $I=(g_v)_A$ and assume $A_0/(I\cap A_0)$ finite over $O$ (`M2`). Then:

1. For every $O$-point $\xi:A\to K$ killing $I$, there are $t\in K^\times$ and $f:S\to K$ local, in the sense of sending the maximal ideal to norm $<1$, with $f(qa)=t^j\xi(a)$ on $A_j$.
2. For local $f$, any $O$-linear derivation $D:A\to K$ at $fq$ killing $I$ lifts to $F:S\to K[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$ of constant part $f$, with infinitesimal part $D(a)$ on $qa$.

**Choosing the scale.** The inverse of an invertible weight-zero element can be projected to weight zero, so $A_0$, hence $C=A_0/(I\cap A_0)$, is local. Its finiteness over $O$ gives $\mathfrak m_C^h\subset\varpi C$ for some $h$ by Artinian reduction. Values in $C$ are integral at $\xi$ and those of its maximal ideal have norm $<1$ by that power inclusion. Put $b_i=\xi(Z_i)$. The weight-zero variables already have small norm. For $w_i>0>w_j$, the origin-vanishing monomial $Z_i^{|w_j|}Z_j^{w_i}$ is weight zero. When both values are nonzero it gives $|b_j|^{1/|w_j|}<|b_i|^{-1/w_i}$. The finitely many bounds allow some scale $|t|\in\ell^{\mathbb Q}$, dense in the positive reals, above all lower bounds and below all upper bounds, omitting an empty side. Then $b_i'=t^{w_i}b_i$ all have norm $<1$. **Extension of evaluation.** Suppose the rescaled point $\tau$ takes its degree-zero values and the coordinate values in a finite $O$-algebra $B$, with all coordinate values in $\mathfrak b=\sqrt{\varpi B}$. The $\mathfrak b$-adic topology of $B$ is complete and separated by finiteness and $\mathfrak b^h\subset\varpi B$. Substitution of the coordinates into formal series converges there. To compare with $\tau$, every fixed-weight series is a finite sum of monomials times weight-zero series, since its exponent vectors have finitely many componentwise minima by Dickson's lemma. This also expresses any weight-zero series without constant term using finitely many positive-total-degree weight-zero monomials by applying Dickson to the nonzero exponent vectors. If the two maps agree modulo $\mathfrak b^n$ on all weight-zero series, subtract constants and use this factorization: the two maps agree on the monomial factors, and their values lie in $\mathfrak b$, so the difference is in $\mathfrak b^{n+1}$. Induction from $n=0$ gives equality. The fixed-weight factorization yields equality at every homogeneous series. Thus substitution kills the homogeneous parts of $J_0$, and kills its full series by keeping all weight parts that occur up to total degree $n-1$ and estimating the omitted tail in $\mathfrak b^n$. It gives the desired map on $\widehat A$.

For the first claim choose as $B$ a finite extension's integer ring containing the rescaled coordinates and the finitely generated integral weight-zero image. The extension kills $g_v$, so descends to $S$ and is local by the coordinate bounds and the norm of $\varpi$. [Extension lemma](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/UnitaryThreeGroups/WeightedPresExistsAlgHomExtensionOfAdicComplete.lean#L227)

**Dual numbers.** Set $\tau(a)=f(qa)+\epsilon D(a)$, a homomorphism killing $I$. Its weight-zero image is finite over $O$; its constant part is integral and the coordinate constants have norm $<1$. A dual number with integral constant is integral over $O$, because subtracting the constant gives a nilpotent and integrality is transitive. Adjoin to the finite weight-zero generators the finitely many $\tau(Z_i)$ and $\tau(Z_i)^{e_i}/\varpi$ for $e_i$ large, with the latter constants integral by smallness. The resulting algebra $B$ is finite over $O$ and the coordinate values belong to $\sqrt{\varpi B}$. Use the same extension argument, killing $g_v$, to obtain $F$. Its constant part is a local point agreeing with $f$ on all variables, hence agrees on the complete ring by truncation and local continuity. Its derivative values on $A$ are as prescribed. This proves the `Model.pts` and `Model.der` properties used in the [upper comparison](carrying-a-graded-orbit-valuation-bound-to-frobenius-traces.md).
