# A Herr coordinate at one generator determines all values

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E5%88%86%E5%9C%86%E7%94%9F%E6%88%90%E5%85%83%E7%9A%84Herr%E5%9D%90%E6%A0%87%E8%83%BD%E7%BB%99%E5%87%BA%E6%95%B4%E4%B8%AA%E7%BE%A4%E4%B8%8A%E7%9A%84%E5%80%BC.md)

Use [integral Herr comparison](herr-cocycles-and-integral-galois-cohomology.md) at $G=G_{K_s}$, with kernel $H$ of $G\to\Gamma_1\simeq\mathbb Z_\ell$ and $g_0$ lifting a topological generator $\gamma$. For a crystalline lattice $T$, let $N(T)\subset D(T)$ be its Wach lattice, over $A=\prod_i O[[U]]$, and $J:D(T)\to P(T)$ the comparison. Use the project's weak topology in which $J(UN(T))$ is closed, the action's orbit maps and the lattice embedding are continuous, and $UN(T)$ is $G$-stable. For $y,z\in D(T)$ with $z\in UN(T)$ and $(\gamma-1)y=(\varphi-1)z$, there is a function $\sigma\mapsto z_\sigma\in UN(T)$ such that, for each $b$ with $(\varphi-1)b=J(y)$, an integral continuous representative $c_b$ of the Herr class satisfies
$$
J(z_\sigma)=1\otimes c_b(\sigma)+(\sigma-1)b,\qquad
(\gamma-1)z_\sigma=(\sigma-1)z.
$$

**Proof.** Choose $b$ and the representative from the Herr formulas so that the right side $Z(\sigma)=1\otimes c_b(\sigma)+(\sigma-1)b$ is zero on $H$ and equals $J(z)$ at $g_0$. It is a continuous cocycle. The subset where $Z(\sigma)\in J(UN(T))$ is closed, and is a subgroup by $Z(\sigma\tau)=Z(\sigma)+\sigma Z(\tau)$ and $Z(\sigma^{-1})=-\sigma^{-1}Z(\sigma)$. It contains $H,g_0$ and hence is all of $G$, by density of integral powers of $\gamma$ in the quotient. Injectivity of $J$ gives the preimages. The values lie in $P^H$ and the cocycle vanishes on $H$, so it factors through the abelian quotient. Comparing the two orders of $g_0,\sigma$ gives $(g_0-1)Z(\sigma)=(\sigma-1)J(z)$, as required. Any two choices of $b$ with the prescribed representative give cocycles agreeing on $H$ and $g_0$, hence everywhere. This proves uniqueness among the continuous cocycles with the specified normalization on $H$ and $g_0$.

For the closedness input, a finite $A$-basis parametrizes $J(UN(T))$ continuously by a finite product of integral series rings. Each is compact in the coefficientwise topology; the period target is Hausdorff. Thus the image is closed.

`eq_top_of_closed` uses the dense subgroup, `mem_and_comm` gives integrality and commutation, and `uniq` compares the normalized choices. `zg_formula_let` specializes the assumptions.
