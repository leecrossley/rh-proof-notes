# The universal framed ring has homogeneous coordinates

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E5%B8%A6%E6%A1%86%E7%9A%84%E6%99%AE%E9%81%8D%E5%8F%98%E5%BD%A2%E7%8E%AF%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E5%BE%97%E5%88%B0%E9%BD%90%E6%AC%A1%E5%9D%90%E6%A0%87.md)

## Statement

Use the assumptions before obstruction finiteness in the [relation bound](second-obstructions-bound-the-number-of-deformation-relations.md), including $O,k,\Gamma,\bar r,r_0$, the form and blocks, local groups and conditions, weighted algebra $B$ and reference lifts $\rho_v$. The framed problem has a universal complete Noetherian local $O$-algebra $R$. It has a finite weighted formal presentation with coordinates from the old variables, the global matrix entries minus reference values, and the frame entries minus the identity. Its defining ideal is closed under taking each weight component, and matrix entries have the indicated weight $w_a-w_b$.

## Universal matrix lifts

First consider continuous lifts to $\mathrm{GL}_d(A)$ for a complete Noetherian local coefficient algebra $A$ with residue field $k$, retaining the matrices. Dual-number lifts are cocycles for the residual conjugation action. On the open kernel $H$ of $\bar r$, they are homomorphisms into the finite additive group $M_d(k)$. `PhiCond` makes the possibilities finite on $H$, since this group is a finite-dimensional $\mathbb F_\ell$-space. Finitely many coset representatives then determine a cocycle on $\Gamma$. Choose a finite set $\Sigma$ of group elements separating all first-order lifts by their matrix values.

Let $P_0$ be the power series ring over $O$ on the entries indexed by $\Sigma$. Evaluate its variables at the differences of the entries from fixed lifts of their residual values. If a finite coefficient ring $A$ is generated by all matrix entries of a lift, $P_0\to A$ is surjective. Two maps $A\to k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$ agreeing on the chosen entries compose with the lift to the same dual-number representation, so agree on every entry and on $A$. This means the chosen entries span the relative cotangent space. Induction on powers of the maximal ideal gives surjectivity.

Fix $\mathfrak m^n=0$. The occurring coefficient rings generated by entries are quotients of the finite ring $P_0/\mathfrak m_{P_0}^n$. Choose an occurring lift $r_n$ to $A_n=P_0/J_n$ with inclusion-minimal kernel. Given a lift to $A'$, generate a local subalgebra $C$ of $A_n\times_k A'$ by pairs of the entries of the two lifts. This gives a lift over $C$ with $\mathfrak m_C^n=0$. Projection onto $A_n$ is surjective, so the kernel of $P_0\to C$ lies in $J_n$. By minimality the kernels coincide and $C\to A_n$ is an isomorphism. The other projection induces $A_n\to A'$ taking $r_n$ to the given lift, uniquely since the entries at $\Sigma$ generate $A_n$.

Universality at each level identifies $A_{n+1}/\mathfrak m_{A_{n+1}}^n$ with $A_n$, and $J_n=J_{n+1}+\mathfrak m_{P_0}^n$. For $J=\bigcap_nJ_n$, this gives $P_0/J=\varprojlim_n A_n$. Indeed representatives can be chosen successively with adjacent differences in $\mathfrak m_{P_0}^n$ and then converged in $P_0$. Starting the same construction at $x\in J_n$ gives $J_n=J+\mathfrak m_{P_0}^n$. The representation and its universality over complete rings follow by taking the limit of their finite-level versions.

## Local conditions and frames

Form the coproduct of the unrestricted universal ring and $B$ in complete local rings, using both sets of variables and relations. Adjoin formal variables for $\alpha_v-1$. Add the equations that the global lift differs from $r_0$ by the block isometry subgroup, that each $\alpha_v$ lies in the subgroup, that $\alpha_{v_0}=1$, and that
$$
r(\phi_v(s))\alpha_v=\alpha_v\rho_v(s).
$$
Quotient also by the ideals defining $L_q$. The residual object satisfies all conditions, so the relation ideal is proper. The resulting ring and universal matrices have the required universal property by the quotient construction.

## Homogeneity from the formal torus

The old coordinates, global deviations and frame deviations distinguish maps from $R$, and therefore span the relative cotangent space by testing dual numbers. A finite subset gives a power series presentation $P\to R$. For $t:X\to A^\times$ with parameters congruent to one, multiplying entry $(a,b)$ by $t(w_a-w_b)$ is conjugation by $\operatorname{diag}(t(w_a))$. This preserves the form condition since the pairing weight sum is constant. The reference lift preserves weight, and the same torus substitution on $B$ makes the frame equations compatible. Local invariance keeps $L_q$. Thus weight scaling a coordinate point again gives a point of $R$.

For each $n$ use $A=(R/\mathfrak m_R^n)[[T_1,\ldots,T_b]]$ with parameters $1+T_i$ on a free basis of $X$. The scaled substitution of a relation vanishes. Only finitely many monomials survive mod $\mathfrak m_R^n$, giving
$$
\sum_{j=(j_i)} c_j\prod_i(1+T_i)^{j_i}=0,
$$
where $c_j$ is the image of its weight-$j$ part. Clear negative powers by one common monomial, and substitute $U_i=1+T_i$. Distinct monomials are independent because this polynomial substitution is invertible. Thus $c_j=0$ for all $j$, and separatedness as $n$ varies gives homogeneity of the ideal. If a candidate coordinate transforms by weight $j$, the same argument applied to any representative series compares the transform with $e\prod_i(1+T_i)^{j_i}$. Its weight-$j$ part already represents $e$. Hence all indicated entries are homogeneous.
