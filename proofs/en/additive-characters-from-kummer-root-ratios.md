# Additive characters from Kummer root ratios

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E4%BB%8EKummer%E6%A0%B9%E7%9A%84%E6%AF%94%E5%80%BC%E6%9E%84%E9%80%A0%E5%8F%AF%E7%94%A8%E7%9A%84%E5%8A%A0%E6%B3%95%E7%89%B9%E5%BE%81.md)

Let $M,F,n,r>0$, $M\mid F$, $K_j=\mathbb Q(\mu_j)$, $U=G_{K_F}$, and $G=G_{\mathbb Q}$. Choose a primitive $M$-th root $\zeta$, and $\delta\in G$ acting by $a\ge0$ on $\mu_M$. Let $y\in K_{Fn}^\times$ be a unit at every finite place not above $r$. Assume for each $u\in U$ there is $z_u\in K_{Fn}$ with $z_u^M=u(y)/y$. Put $P(x)=\delta(x)/x^a$ for $x\ne0$. There is a unique continuous additive $\kappa:U\to\mathbb Z/M\mathbb Z$ such that for any $Y^M=y$ and any of those roots,
$$
\zeta^{\kappa(u)}=\frac{u(P(Y))}{P(Y)P(z_u)}.
$$
It vanishes on all inertia above $p\nmid Frn$. [`IsKol`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PrimitiveFrobeniusFixed/IsAdmissiblePrimeListNil.lean#L76)

**Proof.** The multiplicative map $P$ kills $\mu_M$, so its values are independent of the root choices. The $M$-th power of the ratio is $u(P(y))/(P(y)P(u(y)/y))=1$ by commutation on the abelian $K_{Fn}$. This defines the unique exponent. Use $u(z_v)z_u$ for $z_{uv}$; commutation on $K_{Fn}$ makes $P(u(z_v))=u(P(z_v))$, and $u$ fixes $\mu_M$, so the exponents add. The open subgroup fixing $K_{Fn}$ and $P(Y)$ is killed by the formula using $z_u=1$, proving continuity. At $p\nmid Frn$, inertia fixes $K_{Fn}$, and $P(Y)$ is a unit at all places above $p$, since $Y^M=y$ and $\delta$ permutes those places. The ratio for inertia is therefore a root of unity reducing to $1$. Since $p\nmid M$, the geometric sum prevents a nontrivial such root. The [derivative argument](the-auxiliary-ratio-already-has-a-root-in-the-cyclotomic-field.md) supplies the required roots in $K_{Fn}$. **Getting the twist.** More generally suppose $\kappa:U\to\mathbb Z/M\mathbb Z$ is additive and continuous, vanishes on inertia outside $d>0$ with $F\mid d$, and $w$ is killed by $M$ in a module over a commutative ring $O$. Let $\beta:G\to O^\times$ be trivial modulo $M O$ on $U$, and lift each unit $t\bmod F$ to $\sigma_t$. Define
$$
\nu(u)=\sum_t\kappa(\sigma_t^{-1}u\sigma_t)\beta(\sigma_t)w.
$$
It is additive, continuous, killed by $M$, and vanishes on inertia outside $d$ by conjugacy. To evaluate at $gug^{-1}$, for $b$ the exponent of $g$ on $\mu_F$ set $t=bs$. Write $\sigma_{bs}=g\sigma_s h_s$, $h_s\in U$. Additivity makes $\kappa$ invariant under conjugation by $h_s$, and $\beta(h_s)w=w$, so reindexing gives $\nu(gug^{-1})=\beta(g)\nu(u)$. The sum makes sense even if the number of units is divisible by $\ell$, because no division occurs. For $M=\ell^m$, $\beta=\varepsilon_\ell\alpha^{-1}$, and $d=Frn$, this is `IsNu` for the auxiliary-prime argument.
