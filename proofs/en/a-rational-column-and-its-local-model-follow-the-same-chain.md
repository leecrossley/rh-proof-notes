# A rational column and its local model follow the same chain

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix a word $u$, a served place $w$ over $p$, and a used twist $\mu$ with $p\notin Q_p(\mu)$. For each layer $j$, let $V_j$ be the twisted arithmetic vector space over the number field $L$, $\Lambda_j\subseteq V_j$ its integral lattice, and $\eta_j$ its map to the corresponding complex column space. Let $F_j$ be the charted local space over $K_w$ with lattice $M_j$, and $c_j:V_j\to F_j$ the comparison maps semilinear along a fixed embedding $L\hookrightarrow K_w$. Assume the image of a rational basis in each $V_j$ is $K_w$-linearly independent, and assume each complex lift $K_j^{\mathbb C}$ maps $\eta_j(V_j)$ into $\eta_{j+1}(V_{j+1})$. Finally assume the terminal comparison sends a valuation bound on $c_m(y)$ relative to $M_m$ back to the same normalized lower bound on $y$ relative to $\Lambda_m$. These are the local comparison, rationality and terminal-lattice inputs of `CmpLite`. [The coordinate descent argument](a-valuation-descends-by-comparing-coordinates-in-a-local-basis.md) explains the terminal-lattice input under the matched basis hypothesis. By rationality and injectivity of $\eta_{j+1}$, the complex lift defines an $L$-linear map $K_j^L:V_j\to V_{j+1}$ with $\eta_{j+1}K_j^L=K_j^{\mathbb C}\eta_j$. On the $K_w$-span of the image of the $j$-th basis define $K_j^{\rm loc}$ by $c_j(b)\mapsto c_{j+1}(K_j^L b)$. Independence makes this a linear map, and it can be extended to $F_j$ by choosing a complement and sending it to zero. It satisfies $K_j^{\rm loc}c_j=c_{j+1}K_j^L$ by semilinearity. Any compatible extension agrees on the span, and the resulting chains agree there by induction, as each intermediate image lies in the next span. This is why the choice of extension makes no difference to the actual column. For an untwisted source vector $x$, start the rational chain with its image $x_\mu$ in the twist. The initial identification says $c_0(x_\mu)=c(x)$, the local comparison of the original vector. Put $y_0=x_\mu$ and $y_{j+1}=K_j^L y_j$. Inductively $\eta_j(y_j)$ represents the $j$-step complex lift and $c_j(y_j)$ is the matching $j$-step local lift. In particular the terminal local estimate on the lift of $c(x)$ gives the arithmetic terminal lattice estimate on $y_m$. This yields `ColGeTw`. For the base twist the source and terminal base-to-twist maps are the given identifications, and their inverses preserve the localized lattices. Indeed a scalar times an integral localized vector remains the same scalar times an integral localized vector under the inverse linear map. The complex base embedding also agrees with that base twist, hence pulling back $y_m$ proves `ColGe0`. These implications give `CmpV` for every admissible $u,\mu,w$ with the chosen comparisons, the input in [the served column transfer](the-served-product-estimate-transfers-to-an-actual-column.md).
