# Patching quadratic base changes gives one representation over the original field

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

## Statement

Let $E$ be a CM number field, $\Omega$ an algebraic closure, and $\ell$ a prime. Fix a field isomorphism $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$, a rank $a\ge1$ and a cuspidal automorphic representation $\pi$ of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb A_E)$. Suppose that for every finite place $w_0$ of $E$ there are infinitely many distinct quadratic CM extensions $K/E$ inside $\Omega$ with all of the following properties.

- Every decomposition group at $w_0$ in $G_E=\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/E)$ is contained in $G_K=\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/K)$. Equivalently, $w_0$ splits completely in $K/E$.
- Some finite place of $K$ is ramified over a place of $E$ at which $\pi$ has a Satake multiset.
- For every cuspidal base change $P$ of $\pi$ to $K$ in the sense below, there are a set $Q_K$ of places of $K$ and a continuous semisimple $r_K:G_K\to\mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ that satisfies the spherical comparisons for $P$ at all places in $Q_K$ where $P$ has a Satake multiset. The set $Q_K$ contains all but finitely many of the prime-norm places of $K$ and every place above $w_0$.

Use quadratic base change in the following form. If a quadratic extension has a ramified place over a spherical place of $\pi$, a cuspidal base change $P$ exists, with the properties `IsLift`. Its Satake eigenvalues at $w'\mid w$ are the $f$th powers of the original ones, where $f=[k_{w'}:k_w]$. Its infinitesimal character at $\alpha:K\to\mathbb C$ is that of $\pi$ at $\alpha|_E$. At a place whose completion is identified with the base completion by an isomorphism over $E$, the local components match under that identification. The hypothesis of ramification at a spherical place is used for the existence of a *cuspidal* lift. [Quadratic base change used in the proof](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GaloisGeometry/IsWeight.lean#L82) · [Conditions for an extension to serve one place](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/PatchOneAlt.lean#L72)

Then there is one continuous semisimple representation $\rho:G_E\to\mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ with the spherical comparisons for $\pi$ at every finite place with a Satake multiset. These are unramifiedness and the normalized inverse Frobenius eigenvalues at $w\nmid\ell$, and the crystalline and de Rham comparisons, the jumps from the infinitesimal character, and the normalized Weil characteristic polynomials at inducing bridges for $w\mid\ell$. This is `stepDown_of`. [Global descent from the local serving conditions](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/JoinEndHolds.lean#L92)

## Patching from degree-one information

To understand the gluing step, let $B_w$ be any partially defined family of unique Satake multisets on $E$. Say a continuous semisimple representation of $G_K$ matches this family at degree one if for all but finitely many finite places $v$ of $K$ of prime norm, the multiset $B_{v|E}$ is defined and the representation is unramified with normalized inverse Frobenius eigenvalues $q_v^{(a-1)/2}B_{v|E}$ under $\iota$. This is `AttOne`. It includes every inverse Frobenius lift for every valuation above the chosen place. [Matching a system of multisets at degree-one places](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/PatchOneAlt.lean#L34)

This property survives restriction further up a finite tower. Indeed, if a place at the higher field has prime norm, its residue field is a prime field, and the place below it has the same residue field. Restriction therefore preserves its required exponent, inverse Frobenius data and the triviality on inertia. Finitely many exceptional places downstairs give finitely many exceptional places upstairs. The property also survives conjugation by $G_E$, since the extension $K/E$ is normal and conjugation permutes its places, preserves their norms and restrictions to $E$, and carries their valuation, Frobenius and inertia data along. [Restriction along a tower](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/OneTower.lean#L35) · [Conjugation of places and Frobenius elements](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/OneConj.lean#L16)

Over any number field, two continuous semisimple representations matching the same degree-one family are isomorphic. Their multisets agree wherever both exist, and [degree-one Chebotarev](degree-one-frobenius-elements-are-dense-at-the-usable-split-places.md#the-density-argument) makes the associated inverse Frobenius lifts dense after discarding the two finite exceptional sets. The equality of traces then extends by continuity, and the semisimple character criterion gives the isomorphism. This comparison proves that for each quadratic $K/E$ the matching trace is invariant under $G_E$-conjugation. For two extensions $K,K'$ it also proves equality of the traces on $G_{KK'}=G_K\cap G_{K'}$, using the tower property. Restrictions to this normal subgroup remain semisimple by the [normal-subgroup argument](infinitely-many-quadratic-kernels-determine-a-common-representation.md#a-finite-set-of-twists-to-avoid). [Degree-one uniqueness](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/IsoOne.lean#L14) · [Trace invariance on one kernel](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/PatchOne.lean#L27) · [Trace agreement on an intersection](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/PatchOne.lean#L44)

The groups $G_K$ are the distinct open kernels of the quadratic characters of the extensions $K/E$. Apply [quadratic-kernel patching](infinitely-many-quadratic-kernels-determine-a-common-representation.md) to an infinite family of matching $r_K$. The output is continuous semisimple $\rho$ with $\rho|_{G_K}\simeq r_K$ for all but finitely many members. [Patching the Galois restrictions](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/PatchOne.lean#L83)

## Using the extension that serves a specified place

Fix a place $w_0$, choose a serving extension $K$ as in the statement, and take a cuspidal base change $P$. By assumption there is a representation $s_K$ with the spherical comparisons for $P$ on $Q_K$. This representation matches the degree-one family for $\pi$. To see this, at a prime-norm place $v$ of $K$ the relative residue degree over $E$ is one. For all but finitely many such $v$, the original place admits a Satake multiset $B$, $v$ is prime to $\ell$, and $v\in Q_K$. The base change formula for $P$ uses exponent one there, so both the Satake multiset and residue cardinality in the comparison are exactly the ones needed.

Form an infinite family of all quadratic extensions for which some matching representation is available, and choose one matching $r_K$ for each. It is infinite since any fixed $w_0$ already gives infinitely many serving extensions. Patch this family as above. For each $w_0$, choose a serving $K$ outside the finitely many exceptions of the patch. For this $K$ the representation $s_K$ from the spherical comparison is isomorphic to the chosen $r_K$ by degree-one uniqueness. Consequently its spherical comparisons at places above $w_0$ hold for the restriction of the *same* globally patched $\rho$. [Building the degree-one family from the spherical row](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/JoinEndHolds.lean#L103) · [Selecting an extension outside the patching exceptions](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/JoinEndHolds.lean#L126)

Since $K/E$ splits at $w_0$, choose $v\mid w_0$ with $K_v\simeq E_{w_0}$. The global decomposition groups at a fixed valuation of $\Omega$ above these places are the same subgroup. The residue cardinalities, inertia and inverse Frobenius coincide, so the unramified comparison transfers directly. Above $\ell$ the inducing local fields are identified by the same completion isomorphism. Thus the local representation, period invariants, filtration and Weil operators agree under transport. Each local embedding for $E_{w_0}$ corresponds to one for $K_v$, and the infinitesimal character of $P$ restricts to that of $\pi$. This transfers the crystalline, de Rham and jump assertions. The normalized Weil polynomial likewise transfers, since exponent one in the base change formula leaves $B$ unchanged. This proves the comparison for $\rho$ at every $w_0$.
