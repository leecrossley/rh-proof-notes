# Large two-power conductors give Bernoulli units

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E5%A4%A7%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%8C%E8%BF%9B%E5%AF%BC%E5%AD%90%E7%BB%99%E5%87%BABernoulli%E5%8D%95%E4%BD%8D.md)

Let $\ell$ be prime, $d\ge1$, $Q=\ell^d$, $4\mid Q-1$, $s=v_2(Q-1)$. Let $f\ge1$ be odd and $n\ge0$ with $f4^s\le2^n$. Let $\phi,\xi$ be primitive Dirichlet characters in $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ modulo $f,2^n$ with $\operatorname{ord}\phi\mid Q-1$ and $\phi(-1)\xi(-1)=-1$. For $\chi=\phi\xi$ modulo $f2^n$, extended by zero on nonunits, the weighted value $B_{1,\chi}=(f2^n)^{-1}\sum_{a=0}^{f2^n-1} a\,\phi(a)\xi(a)$ has $\ell$-adic norm one. This is `LemmaX`.

**Integral rewriting.** Set $N=2^n,m=n-s,R=f2^s$, so $m\ge s\ge2$, $1<R\le2^m$. Let $\mathcal O$ be the valuation ring, $k$ its residue field, and $\mathcal B$ the subring whose residues are fixed by $x\mapsto x^Q$. It contains $\phi$'s values and $N^{-1}$ since $\ell$ is odd. For $u\bmod N$, sum $\phi(a)$, and $a\phi(a)$, over $0\le a<fN$, $a\equiv u\bmod N$; call these $S(u),A(u)$. Translating by $ft$ and wrapping around the full interval preserves $\phi$. Hence $S$ is a constant $S_0$ by coprimality, and for $0\le t\le N$ the weighted change is
$$
A(u)-A(u-ft)=ftS_0-fN\sum_{0\le a<ft,\ a\equiv u\bmod N}\phi(a),
$$
where the last term is the decrease $fN$ at the wrapped terms. Put $g(u)=(A(u)-A(0))/(fN)$. Choose $0\le t<N$ with $u-ft=0\bmod N$; the formula shows $g(u)\in\mathcal B$ even if $f$ has an $\ell$-factor. Because $\xi$ is nontrivial, its sum is zero, so $B_{1,\chi}=\sum_u \xi(u)g(u)$ is integral. In residues the formula for $t=2^s$ becomes
$$
g(u)-g(u-R)=c-\mathbf1_{[u]<R}\phi([u]),\qquad c=2^s S_0/N,
$$
where the short sum has at most one term since $R\le2^m\le N$. [Partial sums](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/LevelCohomology/LxPartial.lean#L15)

**Filtering by Frobenius.** In $k$, if $\sum_i z_i b_i=0$, $b_i^Q=b_i$, with $z_i=0$ or $z_i^{2^n}=1$, then the sum restricted to $z_i^{2^s}=1$ is zero. Indeed $Q\bmod2^n$ generates the subgroup $1\bmod2^s$ by the exact valuation under squaring, of order $2^{n-s}$. Apply those $Q$-Frobenius powers and sum. The geometric sums in $z_i^{2^s}$ vanish unless that root is $1$, when the sum is $2^{n-s}$, a unit. Now assume $B_{1,\chi}=0\bmod\mathfrak m$. Replacing $u$ by $xy$ for any odd unit $y$, dividing by $\xi(y)$, and applying the filter yields $\sum_{x\in E}\xi(x)g(xy)=0$ for $E$ the units with $\xi(x)^{2^s}=1$. [Filter](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/LevelCohomology/SumFilterTwoPowRootsEqZeroOfSumEqZero.lean#L4)

**Two boundary terms.** Primitivity gives $\xi(1+2^{n-1})=-1$. Writing a unit as $(-1)^\epsilon 5^j$, $\xi(5)$ has order $2^{n-2}$, so $x\in E$ has $2^{m-2}\mid j$, and $x\equiv\pm1\bmod2^m$. These orders survive reduction. Subtract the filtered sum at $y=1-R$ from that at $y=1$. For $x\equiv1$, $x(1-R)\equiv x-R\bmod N$ since $2^s\mid R$. Thus the difference contributes $\xi(x)c$ with boundary $-\xi(x)\phi([x])$ if $[x]<R$. The only representative there is $1$, giving $-1$. For $x\equiv-1$, $x(1-R)\equiv x+R$; the difference contributes $-\xi(x)c$ and boundary $\xi(x)\phi([x+R])$ when $[x+R]<R$. The only representative is $R-1$, corresponding to $x=-1$, and the contribution is $\xi(-1)\phi(-1)=-1$ since $f\mid R$. The constant sums vanish within each sign class: multiply by $1+2^{n-1}$, which preserves both the filter and sign modulo $2^m$ and reverses $\xi(x)$. Thus the difference of the zero sums is $-2\ne0$, a contradiction. The Bernoulli value is integral with nonzero residue, hence is a unit, giving the [residual coboundary input](large-two-power-conductors-force-residual-cocycles-to-be-coboundaries.md).
