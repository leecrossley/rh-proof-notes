# Descending cup-product vanishing from a larger coefficient module

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%9D%AF%E7%A7%AF%E5%9C%A8%E5%A4%A7%E6%A8%A1%E4%B8%AD%E6%B6%88%E5%A4%B1%E5%90%8E%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E5%9B%9E%E5%88%B0%E5%B0%8F%E6%A8%A1.md)

**Proposition.** Let $D$ act compatibly on modules over a commutative ring. Let $\beta:U\times Y\to N$ and $\beta':U\times V\to N'$ be equivariant bilinear pairings, with equivariant $j:Y\to V$, $i:N\to N'$ satisfying $\beta'(u,j(y))=i(\beta(u,y))$. Suppose $i_*:H^2(D,N)\to H^2(D,N')$ is injective. If $a:D\to U$ and $b:D\to Y$ are one-cocycles and $jb$ is a coboundary, then $a\cup_\beta b$ is a coboundary in $N$. In continuous cohomology take the maps and cochains continuous, including the one-cochain used below.

Take $v\in V$ with $j(b(g))=gv-v$. The cup-product cocycle is $z(g,h)=\beta(a(g),g b(h))$. Its differential vanishes by expanding the one-cocycle identities and equivariance. In the larger module define $f(g)=-\beta'(a(g),gv)$. Then
$$
\begin{aligned}
df(g,h)&=gf(h)-f(gh)+f(g)\\
&=-\beta'(ga(h),ghv)+\beta'(a(g)+ga(h),ghv)-\beta'(a(g),gv)\\
&=\beta'(a(g),g(hv-v))=i(z(g,h)).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $i_*[z]=0$ and injectivity gives $[z]=0$. Any further equivariant map of coefficients preserves coboundaries.

In the project $Y$ is the column ideal, generated by $b(g)=(u(g)-1)E$. After including $Y$ in the normalized block ring $A$, this cocycle is the coboundary of $E$. The cocycle $a$ comes from the dual integral lattice in a row. The pairing is the matrix product $\beta(a,y)=\operatorname{inc}(a)y$ and uses the cyclotomic action on its value module. For each finitely generated $\mathcal O$-module $B$ with the specified topology and trivial untwisted action, the [natural invariant isomorphism](integral-local-tate-invariant-from-finite-levels.md) identifies $H^2(G_{E_w},B(1))$ with $B$. It follows from finite local Tate duality and compatible inverse limits. Naturality makes $H^2$ for $Y(1)\hookrightarrow A(1)$ injective, providing the descent hypothesis.

Finally the coefficient map from $\phi:N_{\rm col}\to M_0$ sends the descended cup product to a continuous coboundary for the zero-block quotient lattice. Applying local invariants gives pairing zero. Switching the two degree-one factors changes the sign of the class, which preserves zero. This gives the required orthogonality for the [row colength comparison](local-duality-and-the-zero-block-row-colength.md).
