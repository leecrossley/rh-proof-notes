# Twisting and split base change give the spherical comparison for every polarized form

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

## Statement

Let $E$ be any CM number field with algebraic closure $\Omega$, $\ell$ a prime and $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$ a field isomorphism. Let $a\ge1$ and let $\pi$ be a regular algebraic cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb A_E)$ polarized by $\chi$ on $E^+$. Its polarization has signs $(-1)^a$ at every real place and $\pi^c\simeq\pi^\vee\otimes((\chi\circ N_{E/E^+})\circ\det)$. Write *weakly attached* for a continuous semisimple representation $r:G_E\to\mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ which is unramified at almost all finite places and has eigenvalues $q_w^{(a-1)/2}B_w$ under $\iota$ on inverse Frobenius there, with $B_w$ the Satake multiset.

Assume that weak attachment is known for all good conjugate-self-dual forms, and that any weakly attached representation for such a form has the four spherical comparisons at every split place of the CM extension at which the form is spherical. A form is good if the field contains the square root of a negative rational integer, the real degree is even, and every nonsplit place is unramified over the real subfield, prime to $\ell$, and spherical for the form. The four comparisons are unramified Frobenius eigenvalues at places away from $\ell$, and de Rhamness with the prescribed jumps, crystallinity and the normalized Weil–Deligne polynomials at each inducing bridge above $\ell$. This input is `Core`. It follows from the [quadratic descent for conjugate-self-dual forms](three-quadratic-stages-extend-the-spherical-comparison-to-conjugate-self-dual-forms.md) and weak uniqueness, as explained below.

Then $\pi$ admits a weakly attached representation, and each weakly attached representation for $\pi$ has the four spherical comparisons at every spherical place. This is `CoreAll`. The further inputs are the rank-one reciprocity comparisons and the strong quadratic base-change rules specified in the proof. [Combining the two reductions](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineInputs/CoreAllOfRowStarCoreInp.lean#L538)

## From the existence row to comparisons for all choices

Two weakly attached representations for the same cuspidal form have the same traces at almost all spherical places. These places include all but finitely many prime-norm places, so [degree-one density](degree-one-frobenius-elements-are-dense-at-the-usable-split-places.md#the-density-argument) and continuity give equality of traces on $G_E$. The semisimple character criterion then identifies the representations. Thus the one representation with all spherical comparisons given by quadratic descent supplies those comparisons for any weakly attached representation of that form. Its spherical data also are enough for weak attachment by taking all but finitely many places, because a cuspidal form is spherical outside a finite set. ## Removing conjugate self-duality by an algebraic twist

First let $\pi$ be good, and fix a split spherical place $w$. By the [polarization twist](an-unramified-algebraic-twist-removes-the-polarization-character.md), choose an algebraic Hecke character $\kappa$ unramified at $w$ such that $\pi'=\pi\otimes(\kappa\circ\det)$ remains good and is conjugate self-dual. Write $m(\sigma)$ for the integer infinity exponent of $\kappa$. Let $\mu=L(\kappa,m)$ be the continuous character given by global reciprocity. For an unramified place $w$, the rank-one comparison gives Frobenius value $\kappa(w)$ outside $\ell$, and at an inducing bridge above $\ell$ gives a crystalline, de Rham line with jump $-m(\iota\circ\tau)$ and with Weil character the local Hecke character transported by local reciprocity. In the unramified case the latter takes value $\kappa(w)^{-\deg g}$ at $g$. The same statement applies to $\kappa^{-1}$ with exponents $-m$. This is the rank-one input used in the [odd-to-even argument](two-odd-rank-lifts-recover-the-even-rank-galois-representation.md), with the local Weil parameter identified as in `AlgebraicCharacterWD`. [The continuous rank-one characters and their local comparisons](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/SplitLineSubtraction/SubtractOf.lean#L132) · [Using the algebraic character Weil parameter](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineInputs/CoreAllOfRowStarCoreInp.lean#L219)

For existence, apply this twist at any split place above $\ell$, which exists since $\pi$ is good. Choose a weakly attached $r'$ for $\pi'$ by `Core`. Then $r=r'\otimes\mu^{-1}$ is weakly attached to $\pi$: at almost every place the automorphic twist multiplies all Satake values by $\kappa(w)$, and the character twist multiplies normalized inverse Frobenius eigenvalues by $\kappa(w)^{-1}$. Unramifiedness is preserved outside the finite ramification sets, and twisting by a continuous character preserves continuity and semisimplicity. [Existence after untwisting the conjugate-self-dual form](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/ContinuousTwOfContinuous.lean#L51)

For local comparisons of a given weakly attached $r$ for $\pi$, apply `Core` to $r\otimes\mu$ for $\pi'$ at $w$. Its Satake multiset is $\kappa(w)B_w$. Away from $\ell$, divide the inverse Frobenius eigenvalues by $\kappa(w)$ and use the unramified inverse character to recover the comparison for $r$. Above $\ell$, the inverse character is crystalline and de Rham. Tensoring the crystalline and de Rham comparisons with this line recovers the corresponding properties of $r$. The infinitesimal character of $\pi'$ is that of $\pi$ translated by $m(\sigma)$, so the prescribed jumps of the twisted representation are those of $\pi$ translated by $-m(\iota\circ\tau)$. The inverse character contributes the opposite translation. Finally the tensor rule for the potentially semistable Weil–Deligne parameter multiplies all eigenvalues at $g$ by the scalar of the character at $g$. The normalized Satake parameter of $\pi'$ is obtained from that of $\pi$ by the same scalar. Cancelling the common nonzero scalar gives equality of the original characteristic polynomials, using the rule $P_{cA}(X)=c^aP_A(X/c)$. This proves all four comparisons for good forms at their split spherical places. ## Removing the good-field restrictions by quadratic base change

Fix any place $w_0$ and use the [good split family](imaginary-quadratic-twists-of-the-field-give-good-split-base-changes.md) to get infinitely many quadratic CM extensions $K/E$ which split at $w_0$, have their places above $w_0$ split over $K^+$, and admit good cuspidal base changes $P$. The cusp and base-change rules are those in the [quadratic tower proof](three-quadratic-stages-extend-the-spherical-comparison-to-conjugate-self-dual-forms.md#cuspidality-and-conjugate-self-duality-in-the-tower), using ramification above a spherical place. The lift carries the polarization by $\chi\circ N_{K^+/E^+}$. Its real signs are unchanged because each real completion of $K^+$ over one of $E^+$ is locally $\mathbb R$. Its norm pullback to $K$ is the pullback of $\chi\circ N_{E/E^+}$ under $N_{K/E}$, by transitivity of norms in the two towers. Thus at almost all places the old polarization relation on Satake multisets is preserved by taking the residue-degree powers, and strong multiplicity one gives the polarization of $P$. Regular algebraicity follows since the infinitesimal characters pull back along the embeddings. [Polarization and regular algebraicity of a base change](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/PolarizedPairBaseChangePolarizedSameWeight.lean#L206)

If $r$ is already weakly attached to $\pi$, then $r|_{G_K}$ is weakly attached to $P$. Semisimplicity survives restriction to the normal subgroup. Outside a finite set, inertia stays trivial and inverse Frobenius at a place $v$ of $K$ acts by the $f$th power of inverse Frobenius at its restriction to $E$, with $f$ the relative residue degree. The normalized eigenvalues and the Satake multiset therefore both undergo the same power operation, since $q_v=q_w^f$. At $w_0$ the local degree is one. The two completions and decomposition groups identify, and the split rule leaves the Satake multiset unchanged. If $\pi$ is spherical at $w_0$, apply the comparisons for the good $P$ at a place above it and transport back via that identification. The local period facts and the Weil polynomial descend in the same manner as in the [gluing proof](patching-quadratic-base-changes-gives-one-representation-over-the-original-field.md#using-the-extension-that-serves-a-specified-place). Since $w_0$ was arbitrary, this gives the comparisons for any already weakly attached representation. ## Obtaining weak attachment in the general case

To construct $r$ in the first place, choose one family containing every quadratic $K/E$ for which a good cuspidal lift $P_K$ exists. It is infinite because the good split family exists for each $w_0$. Choose such a lift and a weakly attached $r_K$ on each $G_K$ using the good case. These match the degree-one family of $\pi$ by the Satake power rule with exponent one. Apply [degree-one patching](patching-quadratic-base-changes-gives-one-representation-over-the-original-field.md#patching-from-degree-one-information) to get a continuous semisimple $\rho$ which restricts to the chosen $r_K$ up to isomorphism for all but finitely many $K$. At any spherical place $w\nmid\ell$, some good split $K$ serves $w$ outside those finitely many exceptions. Both chosen and serving lifts in a particular $K$ are isomorphic by strong multiplicity one, since their Satake multisets agree at almost every place by the base-change rule. Thus the chosen $r_K$ has the good comparison at a place above $w$. Its embedding of the local decomposition group is surjective onto the group over $w$ because $w$ splits. The comparison for $\rho$ there gives both triviality on inertia and the prescribed inverse Frobenius eigenvalues. Since all but finitely many places are spherical and prime to $\ell$, this proves weak attachment. The restriction argument with this $\rho$ now gives the remaining comparisons, completing the spherical input to the [Galois row](completing-the-local-comparisons-for-the-galois-representation.md).
