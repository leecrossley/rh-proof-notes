# A Frobenius trace difference product is a nonzero integer

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88Frobenius%E8%BF%B9%E5%B7%AE%E7%9A%84%E4%B9%98%E7%A7%AF%E6%98%AF%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E9%9D%9E%E9%9B%B6%E6%95%B4%E6%95%B0.md)

Let $q\ge7$, $e\ge1$ be integers. Take a finite labelled list $t_s\in\mathbb C$, $s\in S$, with $|t_s|\le\sqrt q+2$, each $q^e t_s$ an algebraic integer, and every field automorphism of $\mathbb C$ acting on the list by a permutation of labels. Repeated values are allowed. Let $z_a$, $a\in A$, be a finite root-of-unity list with the same permutation property. Put $E(q,e,z)=q^{-e}z+1+q^ez^{-1}$. Then there is $D\ne0$ in $\mathbb Z$ with $\log|D|\le |A||S|(2e\log q+1)$. Moreover for $\ell\nmid q$, a field isomorphism $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\to\mathbb C$, and $g$ in a group $\Gamma$, suppose each representation $\rho:\Gamma\to{\rm GL}_3(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ in a class has a label $s(\rho)$ giving $\operatorname{tr}\rho(g)=\iota^{-1}(t_{s(\rho)})$, with equal labels implying isomorphism. For any finite sets $X_a$ of pairwise nonisomorphic representations in the class, the differences are nonzero and
$$
|D|_\ell\le\prod_{a\in A}\prod_{\rho\in X_a}
\big|\operatorname{tr}\rho(g)-\iota^{-1}(E(q,e,z_a))\big|_\ell .
$$

**Proof.** Put $Q=q^e$. The reverse triangle inequality gives $|E(q,e,z_a)|\ge Q-1-Q^{-1}>\sqrt q+2$, since $\sqrt q\le q/2$ and $3+1/q<q/2$ for $q\ge7$. Thus $F_{a,s}=Q(t_s-E(q,e,z_a))\ne0$, and $F_{a,s}=Qt_s-z_a-Q-Q^2z_a^{-1}$ is integral. Also $|F_{a,s}|\le Q^2+Q(\sqrt q+3)+1\le2Q^2$, hence its log is at most $2e\log q+1$. The product $D=\prod_{a,s}F_{a,s}$ is invariant under complex automorphisms permuting both lists. In a finite normal field containing the factors, automorphisms extend to $\mathbb C$, so $D\in\mathbb Q$. It is a nonzero algebraic integer, hence a nonzero integer with the asserted log bound. Under $\iota^{-1}$, each factor has norm at most $1$ by integrality. For each $a$, $s(\rho)$ is injective on $X_a$; discard the other factors to enlarge the product. In the retained factors $Q$ has norm one since $\ell\nmid q$. This proves the inequality. **Imaginary quadratic application.** For $K$ imaginary quadratic, $N,e\ge1$, $p\ge7$ prime to $N|\operatorname{disc}K|$, and $w\mid p$ in $K$, take $q=\mathrm Nw=p^f$, $1\le f\le2$, and $g$ inverse arithmetic Frobenius. The project supplies two such trace lists for the allowed rank-three class and for the allowed $1\oplus$ rank-two class. Their sizes are at most $c_1[N(e+1)]^{A_1}$ and $c_2[N|\operatorname{disc}K|(e+1)]^{A_2}$ for constants depending only on $K$. The list inputs encode classification by entire eigensystems, so equal labels imply isomorphism even if some traces coincide. Take the disjoint union of lists, assigning an overlapping representation to the first. With $Y=N(e+1)$, $|S|\le(c_1+c_2|\operatorname{disc}K|^{A_2})Y^{A_1+A_2}$ and $2e\log q+1\le5Y\log p$. The resulting bound
$$
\log|D|\le5(c_1+c_2|\operatorname{disc}K|^{A_2})|A|Y^{A_1+A_2+1}\log p
$$
is the integer input to the [same-kernel comparison](one-frobenius-trace-difference-controls-same-kernel-lengths.md). The two trace classification theorems remain upstream of the product calculation.
