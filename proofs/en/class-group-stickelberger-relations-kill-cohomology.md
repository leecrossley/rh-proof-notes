# Class group Stickelberger relations kill cohomology

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E7%B1%BB%E7%BE%A4%E7%9A%84Stickelberger%E5%85%B3%E7%B3%BB%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E6%B6%88%E5%8E%BB%E4%B8%8A%E5%90%8C%E8%B0%83.md)

Let $N\ge1$, $E\subset K_N=\mathbb Q(\mu_N)$, $\ell\mid N$ prime, $e\ge1$, and $a>0$ odd and prime to $N$. If $\ell=2$ assume $E\supset\mu_4$. Set $\Gamma=\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb Q)$, $W=\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell$, and $S=\{v:v\mid\ell\}$ in $E$. Let $\Sha(E,e)$ be the kernel of localization at $S$ in $H^1(G_{E,S},W(-e))$, with action $\varepsilon_\ell^{-e}$, for the given absolute place lifts. Write $\rho$ for restriction of cyclotomic automorphisms to $E$. Let $z\in\mathbb Z_\ell[\Gamma]$ be the [integral coefficient lift](integer-coefficients-approximate-higher-stickelberger-elements.md) of
$$
(1-a^{e+1}[\rho(a)])\left(
 -\sum_{b\in(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^\times}
 \frac{N^e}{e+1}B_{e+1}([b]_N/N)[\rho(b)]\right).
$$
Then $z\cdot(\varphi(N)x)=0$ for every $x\in\Sha(E,e)$. The action is the Galois conjugation action with the indicated coefficient twist.

**Class group relation.** For $M>0$, $a$ odd and prime to $M$, put $J(b)=(a[a^{-1}b]_M-[b]_M)/M$, $C(b)=(a-1)/2-J(b)$. For each ideal class $A$ of $K_M$, $\sum_b C(b)\sigma_b^{-1}A=0$. Indeed the [Stickelberger relation](../zh/Jacobi%E5%92%8C%E7%9A%84%E7%B4%A0%E7%90%86%E6%83%B3%E5%88%86%E8%A7%A3%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E6%B9%AE%E7%81%AD%E7%B1%BB%E7%BE%A4.md) gives annihilation by $\sum_b J(b)\sigma_b^{-1}$ using the coefficients $a,-1$ at $a^{-1},1$. The constant term acts by the norm on ideals, which extends a rational principal ideal and is zero on classes. When $M>2$, any continuous additive $F:G_{K_M}\to W$ zero on all finite inertia factors as $\lambda\circ\mathrm{Art}$ through the Hilbert class field. The field is totally imaginary. The Artin map to the class group commutes with conjugation since a conjugation takes the arithmetic Frobenius of an ideal to that of its conjugate. Therefore for every $u\in G_{K_M}$,
$$
\sum_b C(b) F(\widetilde\sigma_b^{-1}u\widetilde\sigma_b)=0 ,
$$
where $\widetilde\sigma_b$ is an absolute lift of $\sigma_b$. **Application at a finite layer.** A continuous representative of $x$ has finite image by compactness and discreteness; choose $r$ with $\ell^r$ killing it. Its restriction $F_x$ to $U_\infty=\ker\varepsilon_\ell\subset G_E$ [extends](cyclotomic-tower-restriction-gives-a-finite-level-character.md) to a continuous additive $F_j$ on $G_{E(\mu_{\ell^j})}$, zero on finite inertia. Take $m\ge\max(r,j,2)$ and $M=N\ell^m$; $F_j|_{G_{K_M}}$ satisfies the conditions of the class group relation. Let $Q_M=-\sum_b[b]_M^e C(b)[\rho_M(b)]$, where $\rho_M$ is the lifted restriction map. For $u\in U_\infty\cap G_{K_N}$, both $u$ and $w_b=\widetilde\sigma_b^{-1}u\widetilde\sigma_b$ fix $\mu_M$. The value of $[\rho_M(b)]x$ on $u$ is $\varepsilon_\ell(\widetilde\sigma_b)^{-e}F_x(w_b)$. Since $\varepsilon_\ell(\widetilde\sigma_b)\equiv[b]_M\bmod\ell^m$ and $F_x(w_b)$ is killed by $\ell^m$, the factor $[b]_M^e$ cancels the twist. Thus $F_{Q_Mx}(u)=-\sum_b C(b)F_j(w_b)=0$. Increase $m$ to the integral approximation threshold $Q_M\equiv z\bmod\ell^r$. Then $Q_Mx=zx$, so $F_{zx}$ vanishes on $U_\infty\cap G_{K_N}$. For $u\in U_\infty$, $u^{\varphi(N)}$ lies in that subgroup by its action on $\mu_N$. Additivity gives $F_{\varphi(N)zx}(u)=F_{zx}(u^{\varphi(N)})=0$. Injectivity of restriction at $e\ne0$ gives the asserted annihilation.
