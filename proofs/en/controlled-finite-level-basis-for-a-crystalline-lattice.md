# A controlled finite-level basis for a crystalline lattice

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%99%B6%E4%BD%93%E6%A0%BC%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E5%9C%A8%E6%9C%89%E9%99%90%E5%88%86%E5%9C%86%E5%B1%82%E5%8F%96%E5%BE%97%E5%8F%97%E6%8E%A7%E6%8D%A2%E5%9F%BA.md)

Let \(K,L/\mathbb Q_\ell\) be finite, \(O=\mathcal O_L\), \(K_0\) the maximal unramified subfield of \(K\), \(K_j=K_0(\mu_{\ell^j})\), and \(K_\infty=\bigcup_j K_j\). Assume \(s\ge1\) (and \(s\ge2\) at \(\ell=2\)), \(K\subset K_s\), and \(L\) contains all \(\mathbb Q_\ell\)-embedding images of \(K_s\). Set \(A=O[[U]]^{[K_0:\mathbb Q_\ell]}\), \(X=(1+U)^{\ell^{s-1}}-1\), \(Q=\Phi_{\ell^s}(1+U)\). For a finite free \(G_K\)-stable \(O\)-lattice \(T\) with a fixed \(\mathbb Z_\ell\)-basis, let \(d=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z_\ell} T\), \(M(\sigma)\) denote the action matrix, and \(A_T(a)\) multiplication by \(a\in O\).

The project assumes \(T[1/\ell]\) is crystalline over \(G_{K_s}\) with jumps in \([-M_0,h_0]\) for \(M_0,h_0\ge0\). It supplies a finite free \(A\)-lattice \(N\) in the étale module, with basis mapping by comparison \(J\) to a basis over \(B=O\otimes W(\mathbb C_\ell^\flat)\), \(Q^{M_0}\varphi(N)\subset N\), \(Q^{h_0}N\subset A\varphi(N)\), stable under cyclotomic \(G_{K_s}\)-action trivial modulo \(U\) and under induced maps from lattice endomorphisms of \(T\). Use the period comparison and Fontaine specializations described below. Set \(b=M_0+h_0\), \(n=\min\{q\ge0:b\le\ell^q(\ell-1)\}\) and \(m=2s+n+1\). There exist \(D\in{\rm GL}_d(\mathbb C_\ell)\), \(g\in G_{K_m}\), and a continuous representation \(U_m:G_{K_m}\to{\rm GL}_d(\mathcal O_{K_m})\) such that:

- \(D\) and \(\ell D^{-1}\) have integral entries.
- \(DU_m(\sigma)=M(\sigma)\sigma(D)\), with \(U_m=I\) on \(G_{K_\infty}\).
- \(v_\ell(\chi(g)-1)=m\) and each entry of \(U_m(g)-I\) has absolute value \(<\ell^{-1/(\ell-1)}\).
- For each \(a\in O\), \(D^{-1}A_T(a)D\) has entries in \(\mathcal O_{K_m}\).

**Proof.** Put \(B^+=O\otimes W(\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_\ell^\flat})\). In the [period matrix argument](two-wach-lattices-agree-after-inverting-the-parameter.md), use the first height \(M_0\) for the coordinate matrix and the second \(h_0\) for its inverse. This gives \(X^{M_0}J(N)\subset B^+\otimes T\) and \(X^{h_0}(B^+\otimes T)\subset B^+\cdot J(N)\). Hence every \(X^b t\), \(t\in T\), is an integral-period linear combination of \(z_y=X^{M_0}J(y)\), \(y\in N\).

Fix compatible roots \(\zeta_{j+1}^\ell=\zeta_j\) primitive of order \(\ell^j\). The Fontaine specialization \(\theta_n=\theta\varphi^{-n}\) is Galois-equivariant and sends the Teichmüller representative for level \(j\) to \(\zeta_{j+n}\). It sends \(U\) to \(\zeta_{s+n}-1\) and \(X\) to \(c=\zeta_{n+1}-1\ne0\). Let \(\Psi(y)\) be the integral coordinate column of the specialization of \(z_y\). With \(\mathbb Z_\ell\)-basis \(o_1,\dots,o_f\) of \(O\) and \(A\)-basis \(b_1,\dots,b_r\) of \(N\), we have \(d=fr\). Form \(D\) with columns \(\Psi(o_j b_i)\). Specializing coefficients in \(A\) gives \(\Psi(y)=D\,{\rm co}(y)\) with integral entries in \({\rm co}(y)\): expand the specialized scalar in the \(o_j\)-basis. Integral-period scalar multiples of these columns are also in \(D\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_\ell}^d\) by expanding the scalar and using \(o_jy\in N\). Specializing the expressions for \(X^bt\) gives \(c^b\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_\ell}^d\subset D\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_\ell}^d\). As \(v_\ell(c^b)=b/(\ell^n(\ell-1))\le1\), this implies \(\ell I=DZ\) for an integral \(Z\). Thus \(D\) is invertible and \(\ell D^{-1}=Z\).

For the finite coefficient field, each specialized component idempotent becomes a tuple of zeros and ones in \(\mathbb C_\ell\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}L\) under the embeddings of \(L\). Invert the matrix of the conjugates of the \(o_j\); the idempotent's original coordinates are algebraic. They are fixed by \(G_{K_s}\) by the coefficient comparison, hence in \(K_s\). The value of \(U\) is in \(K_m\) with absolute value \(<1\). Evaluating a component's integral power series by finite truncations therefore gives coefficients in \(\mathcal O_{K_m}\), by completeness and integrality of the specialization.

For \(\sigma\in G_{K_m}\), equivariance and the fact that \(\sigma\) fixes \(c\) give \(M(\sigma)\sigma(\Psi(y))=\Psi(\sigma y)\). Consequently the columns of \(U_m(\sigma)=D^{-1}M(\sigma)\sigma(D)\) are the finite-level coordinates of \(\sigma(o_jb_i)\), hence integral. The cocycle relation is the representation relation since these coordinates are fixed by \(G_{K_m}\); applying it to inverses gives invertibility over the integer ring. Continuity follows from the formula. The cyclotomic action is trivial on \(G_{K_\infty}\). Using \(\Psi(ay)=A_T(a)\Psi(y)\) and finite-level coordinates gives the claimed scalar matrix conjugates.

Finally choose \(g_0\in G_{K_s}\) with \(v_\ell(\chi(g_0)-1)=s\), and take \(g=g_0^{\ell^{s+n+1}}\). The valuation formula for powers of principal units gives the valuation \(2s+n+1=m\), so \(g\in G_{K_m}\). Use the [strict norm estimate](a-high-cyclotomic-power-is-strictly-close-to-identity.md) at \(j=s+n\). Its hypothesis holds on \(N\) for \(g_0\), and its specialized coefficients of \((g-1)(o_j b_i)\) are exactly the columns of \(U_m(g)-I\).
