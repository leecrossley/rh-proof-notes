# A valuation descends by comparing coordinates in a local basis

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $L$ be a number field with a finite place $w\mid p$, local integer ring $O_w\subset L$ and discrete valuation $v_w$ with $v_w(p)=e_w>0$. Let $K$ be the fraction field of a DVR $O$ of uniformizer $\varpi$, with $\operatorname{ord}_{\varpi}(p)=e>0$. Assume a field embedding $\sigma:L\to K$ identifies $O_w$ with $\sigma^{-1}(O)$. Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional $L$-space with global lattice $\Lambda$, and assume the localized lattice $\Lambda_w$ is free over $O_w$ and spans $V$. Let $c:V\to V'$ be $\sigma$-semilinear with a basis having $K$-linearly independent images. Suppose $M\subset V'$ is the $O$-span of $c(\Lambda)$. If $c(y)\in\varpi^{\lceil e s\rceil}M$, then $y$ has normalized valuation at least $s$ in $\Lambda_w$. The source expresses the latter as a factorization $y=a z$ with $z\in\Lambda_w$ and $a=0$ or $v_w(a)\ge e_w s$. Let $\pi_w\in O_w$ have $v_w(\pi_w)=1$. Units of $O_w$ map to units of $O$, as both they and their inverses are in $O_w$. Writing each nonzero $a\in L$ as $\pi_w^{v_w(a)}$ times a unit gives
$$
\operatorname{ord}_{\varpi}(\sigma(a))=
\operatorname{ord}_{\varpi}(\sigma(\pi_w))v_w(a).
$$
Taking $a=p$ identifies the normalized valuations $e^{-1}\operatorname{ord}_{\varpi}(\sigma(a))=e_w^{-1}v_w(a)$. This gives the scalar comparison for every real $s$ with a rounded exponent. [`valScalar_of` and its use at a place](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/ChartComparison/AlgebraMapUnitZpow.lean#L224)

Take an $O_w$-basis $(b_i)$ for $\Lambda_w$. Its images under $c$ are independent over $K$: a change of $L$-basis maps to an invertible matrix over $K$. For $x\in\Lambda$, expand $x$ in the $b_i$. The coefficients belong to $O_w$, so $c(x)\in\sum_i O\,c(b_i)$. It follows from the span assumption that $M$ lies in this span. Expand $y=\sum_i a_i b_i$. Membership of $c(y)$ in the scaled lattice gives $\sigma(a_i)\in\varpi^{\lceil e s\rceil}O$ for every $i$, by independence. If $y\ne0$, choose a nonzero $a$ of least $v_w$ among the coefficients. Then $z=y/a$ belongs to $\Lambda_w$, and the scalar comparison yields $v_w(a)\ge e_w s$. For $y=0$ use $a=z=0$. This proves the terminal-lattice input in [the rational-column comparison](a-rational-column-and-its-local-model-follow-the-same-chain.md). [Comparing lattice spans and extracting a scalar](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/ChartComparison/ComparisonZeroWeightBase.lean#L317)

In the project, localization of the finitely generated full lattice gives a basis over the DVR at $w$. One can select a minimal spanning subset of a finite set of generators over this DVR: a linear dependence over $L$ can be divided by its coefficient of least valuation, giving a relation with one unit coefficient and thus removing one generator. The comparison of chart lattices supplies the equality identifying $M$ with the generated local lattice.
