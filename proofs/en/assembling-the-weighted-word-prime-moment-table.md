# Assembling the weighted word-prime moment table

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix a word prime $p=T(u)$ and an adjacent step of source rank $a=N_i$ in the tied arithmetic chain. The chosen factorization is $p=\pi\bar\pi$, with $f$ selected primes $P\mid\pi$, each of norm $p$. Use a place $w$ of the coefficient field $K$ above $p$ and an embedding $\iota:K\to\mathbb C_p$ inducing $w$. Take an admitted pair of standard-at-$p$ decorated lattices $N,N'$, with nonzero residue functionals $f_P,f'_P$. Off the word prime the arithmetic box weight $\Xi_0$ is periodic modulo the stated congruence lattice and integral at $w$. Its word-prime factor $\phi_T(x)$ is the indicator of $xN_P\subseteq N'_P$ and $f'_P(xy)=f_P(y)$ for every $P\mid\pi$, $y\in N_P$. Write $\Xi=\phi_T\Xi_0$. For a jet index $m$ in the rationality subset, the actual table entry is
$$
J(m)=\sum_{x\bmod Z}\Xi(x)J_x(m),
$$
over the box lattice modulo its congruence lattice $Z$. Here $J_x(m)\in K$ is the rational read of the induced theta jet at $x$, divided by the fixed algebraic determinant character $\chi_F$, whenever $\Xi(x)\ne0$. The same statement may define arbitrary terms where $\Xi(x)=0$. On the conjugate side $\iota(J)$ is in the integral closure of the unscaled point tables supported on maps preserving the residue functionals. On the selected side it is in the integral closure of $p^{-fa}$ times the point tables supported on maps whose adjoints are integral on the target hyperplanes. These assertions use the local moment translates at a unit period and the period-lattice move and compatibility laws.

### Combining cosets and passing through the coefficient field

Let $C$ be an intermediate period sublattice for regrouping the box terms. For the conjugate side take $C=0$; for the selected side take the lattice $cg\bar\pi(M\cap M'')$ in [the selected-side proof](character-orthogonality-explains-the-selected-moment-denominator.md). Here $M,M''$ are the period lattices of the original and companion targets. For that $C$, period-lattice localization gives $y\in C$ integral in the homomorphism lattice, and $yN_P$ is in the target hyperplane at every selected prime. Away from those primes $y$ lies in the congruence lattice for $\Xi_0$. Therefore both weights $\phi_T$ and $\Xi_0$ are constant under adding $C$. They are also constant under $Z$, since $Z$ has a factor $p$ at the selected primes and the prescribed level away from them. In a finite quotient by $Z$ choose first representatives modulo $Z+C$ and then representatives $j$ of $C/(C\cap Z)$. Each coset of $Z$ occurs once as $x+j$. This makes its contribution at an outer $x$ the common scalar $\Xi(x)$ times the inner jet sum over $j$.

The embedding $\lambda$ of the algebraic numbers agrees with $\iota$ on the fixed complex image of $K$. At the rationality indices and with $\Xi(x)\ne0$, the embedded inner sum is therefore its complex jet sum under $\lambda$, times $\lambda(\chi_F)^{-1}$. This step only uses the rationality precondition for $m$. The determinant character $\chi_F$ has norm one at $w$ by standardness and the split determinant unit law. The factor $\iota(\Xi(x))$ has norm at most one. Thus these two factors can be put into the coefficients of the integral approximants. For a zero weight, use the zero approximant.

On the conjugate side, [the residue condition](character-orthogonality-gives-integral-conjugate-side-moments.md) puts each point of the inner closure in the same class modulo $p\operatorname{Hom}_P$ as $x$, hence it preserves $f_P,f'_P$ as $x$ does. On the selected side, [the companion closure](character-orthogonality-explains-the-selected-moment-denominator.md) already has the required operator support and factor $p^{-fa}$. To sum the closures, for any finite list of $m$'s choose inner approximants to the same prescribed error. At a point present in several summands add the weights; the nonarchimedean inequality keeps them integral. The same inequality bounds the error of the finite sum. This proves the two assertions for the actual weighted table.

### Choice of the local moment normalization

At the word prime the CM base is split, the prime is odd, and it is prime to the curve ideal norm. The analytic moment theorem then supplies a period $\Omega$ of norm one. The differential generators $c_\tau$ are algebraic and units at this admissible prime, so $u_\tau=\lambda(c_\tau)/\Omega$ has norm one. The field and lattice comparisons ensure that the period lattices and rational translate arguments in both branches use the same $u_\tau$. For the selected branch apply the bracket-twisted law with the factor $\pi$ which has norm $<1$ at the chosen embeddings; for the conjugate branch use $\bar\pi$. This accounts for the opposite torus ratios in the two proof branches. The resulting table is the moment hypothesis needed for [the word-prime kernel bounds](the-two-split-sides-give-the-word-prime-kernel-bounds.md).
