# Counting rational eigensystems by imaginary quadratic restriction

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E7%94%A8%E8%99%9A%E4%BA%8C%E6%AC%A1%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E6%95%B0%E5%87%BA%E6%9C%89%E7%90%86%E6%95%B0%E5%9F%9F%E4%B8%8A%E7%9A%84%E6%9C%AC%E5%BE%81%E7%B3%BB%E7%BB%9F.md)

Fix $n\ge2$. Assume [polarized CM counting](labelling-representations-by-their-full-hecke-eigensystems.md) `CountPAlt` and `AdmResNAlt`: for every imaginary quadratic $K$, odd $\ell$, $M\ge1$, strictly decreasing integral weights $\mathbf m$ of common reverse pair sum $w$, the restriction of an allowed rational representation (`AdmRNAlt`) that stays irreducible belongs to the polarized allowed class (`AdmPNAlt`) with the same $M,\mathbf m,\iota$. The rational class consists of continuous irreducible representations, unramified at $p\nmid M$ with $p\ne\ell$. Then with constants $c,A$ depending only on $n$, for all such $|m_i|\le H,M,p$ a single `EigData` at $n,p,\mathbf m,w$ of size $\le c[M(H+1)]^A$ classifies all these rational representations across odd $\ell\ne p$ and $\iota$. This is `CountQAlt`. [`countQ_alt`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/ThresholdArguments/DenseThresholdCases/FrobeniusTraceCountBoundOfCmEigensystemCount.lean#L255)

**Twist bound and restriction.** For an $n$-dimensional $r$, an isomorphism to $r\otimes\delta$ gives a nonzero matrix $U_\delta$ on which conjugation acts by the character $\delta$ or its inverse according to order of conjugation. Distinct characters give linearly independent vectors: in a relation of minimal support, apply a group element distinguishing two of the characters and subtract the original relation multiplied by the first eigenvalue. The resulting nonzero relation has fewer terms. Thus there are at most $n^2$ self-twists. [`twCard`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/CrystallineBaseChange/TwCard.lean#L5)

For the kernel $H$ of a quadratic $\delta$, choose $s\notin H$. If $r$ is irreducible and $r|_H$ reducible, a nonzero proper $H$-invariant space $U$ has $U\cap r(s)U=0$ and $U+r(s)U=V$, since both are $G$-stable using normality and $s^2\in H$. The signs $+1,-1$ on the summands give an intertwiner for the twist. Conversely irreducibility on $H$ makes such an intertwiner scalar by Schur, unable to anticommute with $r(s)$. If $r,r'$ have isomorphic irreducible restrictions, identify them on $H$. Their $s$-matrices induce the same conjugation there, hence differ by a scalar. Squaring gives the same $r(s^2)$, so the scalar is $1$ or $-1$. Thus at most two rational isomorphism classes lie over each irreducible restriction. [`resTwo`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/CrystallineBaseChange/ResTwo.lean#L171)

**Uniform labels.** Choose $n^2+2$ imaginary quadratic fields with pairwise coprime discriminants using distinct primes $q_i\equiv3\bmod4$. At most one is ramified at $p$. Keep $n^2+1$ others $K_i$, and one place $v_i\mid p$ in each, of residue degree $f_i\in\{1,2\}$. The self-twist bound ensures each $r$ stays irreducible at some $K_i$, so the assumed counting theorem gives it a label $s\in S_i$ there. [Quadratic family](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/CrystallineBaseChange/QuadFam.lean#L18)

For each $s$ take all multisets $B$ with $\{b^{f_i}:b\in B\}=\beta_i(s)$ counting multiplicities. There are at most $2^n$, by listing the $n$ entries and choosing a root each. The actual inverse eigenvalue multiset at rational inverse arithmetic Frobenius is among them: the geometric Frobenius at $v_i$ equals the $f_i$-th power modulo inertia and $r$ is unramified there. The bounds for the moduli and their product follow by taking $f_i$-th roots at $q_i=\mathrm Nv_i=p^{f_i}$. Integrality for $p^u b$ and $p^u b^{-1}$ follows from integrality of the $f_i$-th powers by monic equations and transitivity. Field automorphisms permute the candidates by permuting $s$ and acting on $B$.

Assign two labels to each triple $(i,s,B)$, with the same multiset and with automorphisms leaving the extra binary label unchanged. For fixed $\ell,\iota$, a triple classifies the restricted representation and has at most two rational isomorphism classes above it by the quadratic argument. Choose an isomorphism class representative when nonempty and use the two labels to distinguish that class and the other possible class. This gives the required classification. There are at most $2^{n+1}\sum_i|S_i|$ labels; the finitely many fields from which the $K_i$ are chosen depend only on $n$, so common constants work for all $p$. For example take the sum of their exponents and $2^{n+1}(n^2+1)$ times the sum of their coefficients.
