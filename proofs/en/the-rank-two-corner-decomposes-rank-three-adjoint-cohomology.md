# The rank-two corner decomposes rank-three adjoint cohomology

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E7%A7%A9%E4%BA%8C%E8%A7%92%E5%9D%97%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E5%88%86%E8%A7%A3%E7%A7%A9%E4%B8%89%E7%9A%84%E4%BC%B4%E9%9A%8F%E4%B8%8A%E5%90%8C%E8%B0%83.md)

Use the [single-character setup](a-single-character-length-is-bounded-by-automorphic-trace-differences.md) with `GD` and let $r$ be a `TypeII` local point. Put $H=G_K=\ker\eta$, $k=\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, $c$ the marked conjugation. Assume `CohInp` for inertia polynomials, the weight-zero base vanishing, and the data for this type. Assume the rank-two extension $W$ constructed below has $W\otimes\varepsilon_\ell^{-e}$ associated to a weight $2e+1$ newform with the imaginary quadratic compatibility `WitAQ` as provided by `LemWAlt`. Use `KatoTwoW` (for $W$ with these witnesses satisfying `Used`, $W$ and $W\otimes\eta$ have vanishing Bloch–Kato $H^1$) and `RigiditySecondCase` (for the continuous corner extension with $\det W=\eta$ and this automorphy, the indicated four-dimensional adjoint extension has vanishing). Then every continuous adjoint cocycle in $M_3(k)$ with the Bloch–Kato local conditions is a global coboundary. These conditions mean a coboundary on inertia away from $\ell$ and a coboundary after adding crystalline periods at $\ell$.

**The corner extension.** The middle character $\kappa$ on $H$ reduces to $1$ and on inertia belongs pointwise to $\{\Psi,1,\Psi^{-1}\}$ by the [characteristic polynomial comparison](a-deformation-triangular-in-both-directions-is-the-base-point.md). If $\Psi(g)\not\equiv1$ this forces $\kappa(g)=1$. If $\Psi(g)\equiv1$ away from $\ell$, its root-of-unity order is prime to $\ell$, so $\Psi(g)=1$ and the prescribed inertia kernel is killed. At $\ell$ in the same residual case, choose $s$ with $\Psi(s)^2-1$ a unit and compare $gs,s$, both of whose $\Psi$-values reduce to values different from $1$. Thus $\kappa$ is everywhere unramified with image in $1+\mathfrak m_k$. Reduction at all precisions through the ideal class group, of order prime to $\ell$, gives $\kappa=1$ as in that proof. The determinant on $H$ is likewise $1$ by its characteristic polynomial on inertia and its base residue. Hence the corner $U_g$ has determinant $1$, also outside $H$ since $M_{hc}=M_h$. With $D_2=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1)$, put $W(g)=U_g$ on $H$ and $U_gD_2$ on the other coset. The sign action on $\mathrm{SL}_2$ equals conjugation by $D_2$, since $J_2(U^{-1})^{\mathsf T}J_2=D_2UD_2$. Thus $W$ is a continuous ordinary representation, irreducible already on $H$, with $\det W=\eta$, $W(c)=D_2$. [Type II facts](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/UnitaryThreeGroups/CoboundaryLemmaNoColumn.lean#L135)

**Four summands.** The four-dimensional corner matrices carry $W(g)XW(g)^{-1}$ on $H$ and $-J_2X^{\mathsf T}J_2$ at $c$. They give `AdZero2`, with conjugate duality pairing $J_2$: $W(g)^{\mathsf T}J_2 W(cgc^{-1})=J_2$ for $g\in H$ by the determinant-one identity $M^{\mathsf T}J_2D_2MD_2=(\det M)J_2$. Apply the second rigidity input. The middle coordinate $kE_{22}$ carries $\eta$, covered by [the base weight-zero case](nonpositive-weight-cohomology-at-the-base-is-coboundary.md).

For $t=\pm1$ set
$$
p_t(X)=\binom{X_{12}+tX_{23}}{X_{32}-tX_{21}},\qquad
s_t\binom{u}{v}=\begin{pmatrix}0&u&0\\-tv&0&tu\\0&v&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Conjugation on $H$ acts on each $p_t$ by $U_g=W(g)$: the column $(X_{12},X_{32})$ transforms by $U_g$, and for the row $(X_{21},X_{23})$ acting by $U_g^{-1}$, the column $(X_{23},-X_{21})$ transforms by $U_g$ since $\det U_g=1$. At $c$, $-JX^{\mathsf T}J$ swaps the two horizontal positions with negative signs and the two vertical positions likewise, giving $tD_2$ on $p_t$. Thus these are $W$ and $W\otimes\eta$. Each $p_t s_t=2$, $p_t s_{-t}=0$; the two components are recovered by $(s_1p_1+s_{-1}p_{-1})/2$. This gives the direct sum of four equivariant pieces. [Adjoint decomposition](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/TheoremsMuLinked/GaloisRepresentationTypes/ReducibleThreeDim.lean)

The `Used` conditions here include the field and prime-to-$\ell$ order data, $\det W=\eta$, level $N|\operatorname{disc}K|$, weights $(e,-e)$, an ordinary flag at $\ell$ with inertia quotients $\varepsilon_\ell^{\pm e}\chi$ for the same $\chi$ trivial on the fixing group of $\mu_\ell$, and $\eta=1$ on that inertia. They also require inertia trace $\Psi+\Psi^{-1}$, residual trace $\eta\Psi+\Psi^{-1}$, and irreducibility on $G_{K(\mu_{\ell^\infty})}$. The source verifies these in `used_of_alt` using `typeII_ord` and `typeII_irr_cyc`. Thus the Kato input applies to both two-dimensional pieces. The four projections preserve the local coboundary conditions by equivariance, so each projected cocycle is a global coboundary. Reassemble the primitives to get the conclusion.
