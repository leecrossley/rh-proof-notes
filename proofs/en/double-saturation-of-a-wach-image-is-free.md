# Double saturation of a Wach image is free

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E5%8F%8C%E9%87%8D%E9%A5%B1%E5%92%8C%E7%9A%84%E5%AD%90%E6%A0%BC%E4%BB%8D%E6%98%AF%E8%87%AA%E7%94%B1%E6%A8%A1.md)

Let $O$ be a discrete valuation ring, $\ell\in O$ a nonzero nonunit, $R=O[[U]]$, $A=R^n$ with $n\ge1$, and $E$ a flat $A$-algebra with $A\hookrightarrow E$. Let $D_1,D_2$ be finite free $E$-modules and $j:D_1\to D_2$ surjective. Suppose $N_i\subset D_i$ satisfy $E\otimes_A N_i\simeq D_i$, with $N_2$ finitely generated and projective over $R$, and $P=j(N_1)\subset N_2$. Define
$$
S=\{x\in N_2:\ell^kx\in P,\ U^kx\in P\text{ for some }k\ge0\}.
$$
Then $S$ is free of rank $\operatorname{rank}_E D_2$ over $A$, and $E\otimes_A S\simeq D_2$.

**Proof.** Taking maxima of exponents shows $S$ is a submodule between $P$ and $N_2$; Noetherianity makes it finite. Flatness embeds $E\otimes_A S$ into $D_2$, and its image contains the $E$-span of $P$, which is all of $D_2$.

Work in one $R$-component. If $\ell x=Uy$ with $x,y\in S$, first find $z\in N_2$ with $x=Uz$. In a free module containing $N_2$ as a summand, taking constant terms gives $\ell x(0)=0$ coordinatewise. Thus $x$ is divisible by $U$, and projecting the quotient back to $N_2$ gives $z$. Since $U$ acts injectively, $\ell z=y$. Some $\ell^{k_1}y\in P$ and $U^{k_2}x\in P$, so $\ell^{k_1+1}z,U^{k_2+1}z\in P$. Thus $z\in S$. This proves that $\ell$ acts injectively on $S/US$; every nonzero element of $O$ divides a power of $\ell$, so $S/US$ is finite torsion-free over $O$, hence free. Lift a basis. By Nakayama the lifts generate $S$. Any relation among the lifts has coefficients divisible by $U$ by reduction modulo $U$. Divide by $U$ using injectivity and repeat to see that the coefficients lie in $\bigcap_{m\ge0}U^mR=0$. Thus the lifts are a basis.

In each component the idempotent factor $E_i$ is nonzero by injectivity of $A\to E$. Extending scalars from $R$ to $E_i$ identifies the rank with that of $D_2$, since rank over a nonzero commutative ring can be read in a residue field. The component bases therefore assemble into an $A$-basis.

For flatness in the application, $E$ is $\ell$-adically complete and $U$ is invertible in $E$. The project checks that each residue modulo $\ell^m$ comes from a fraction $a/U^j$ and that $\ell^m E\cap A=\ell^m A$. These give $A[1/U]/\ell^m\simeq E/\ell^m E$, with injectivity proved by clearing $U^j$ and applying the intersection identity. Passing to limits identifies $E$ with the $\ell$-adic completion of $A[1/U]$, flat by Noetherian completion and localization.
