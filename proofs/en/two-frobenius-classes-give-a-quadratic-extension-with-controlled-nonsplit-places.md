# Two Frobenius classes give a quadratic extension with controlled nonsplit places

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

## Statement

Let $Y$ be a CM number field with algebraic closure $\Omega$. Suppose $Y=E(\delta)$ for a number field $E$, where $\delta^2=q>0$ with $q$ a rational integer, and there is an automorphism $s$ of $\Omega$ that fixes every conjugate of $E$ pointwise and sends $\delta$ to $-\delta$. This is the real-step hypothesis `RealStep`. Let $T$ be any finite set of finite places of $Y$.

There is a quadratic CM extension $Z/Y$ inside $\Omega$, with even degree of its maximal real subfield over $\mathbb Q$, containing $x$ with $x^2=-d$ for $d>0$. It satisfies the following conditions.

- Every place in $T$ splits in $Z/Y$, and some place outside $T$ ramifies in $Z/Y$.
- At a place $w$ of $Z$ that is nonsplit over $Z^+$, the restriction to $Y$ is outside $T$, the extension $Z/Z^+$ is unramified, and the restriction to $Y$ splits in $Z/Y$. The real place below $w$ has residue characteristic different from $2$, residue size greater than $3$, and is unramified over its rational prime.
- Every $w$ above $T$ is usable for $d$, meaning it splits over $Z^+$ and $-d$ is a square in $\mathbb Q_p$ for the rational prime $p$ below $w$.

This is `exists_quadratic_CM_ext_split_at`. The unramified assertion at every place of $Z/Z^+$ follows from the second item and unramifiedness at split places. ## Choosing two rational primes

First fix a positive modulus $M$ and a bound $B$. Let $c_\infty$ be complex conjugation on $\Omega$ via a chosen complex embedding. It fixes $\delta$ because $\delta^2>0$, and sends any root of unity $\zeta$ to $\zeta^{-1}$. Consider
$$
\sigma_1=c_\infty s,\qquad \sigma_2=s^{-1}.
$$
Each sends $\delta$ to $-\delta$. The square of each fixes every conjugate of $E$: for $\sigma_1$ this follows since $s$ fixes both a given conjugate and its complex conjugate, and $c_\infty^2=1$. Since the square also fixes $\delta$, it fixes every conjugate of $Y=E+E\delta$. [Two automorphisms and the parity of their actions](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/InertiaQuadraticRamification/TwoPrimes.lean#L37)

Take a primitive $M$th root of unity $\zeta$ and a finite Galois extension of $\mathbb Q$ in $\Omega$ containing $\zeta$ and all conjugates of $Y$. By the [Chebotarev theorem](degree-one-frobenius-elements-are-dense-at-the-usable-split-places.md#the-density-argument), choose rational primes $p_2>p_1>B$, prime to $M$ and unramified in this extension, with the respective Frobenius classes of $\sigma_1,\sigma_2$. At a suitable place above each $p_i$ the representative is exactly $\sigma_i$ on the extension. Hence $\sigma_i\zeta=\zeta^{p_i}$. In fact reduction intertwines the two maps, and it is injective on the roots of order dividing $M$ because $p_i\nmid M$. It follows that
$$
(s\zeta)^{-1}=\zeta^{p_1},\qquad
\zeta=(s\zeta)^{p_2},\qquad
p_1p_2\equiv-1\pmod M.
$$
At an arbitrary valuation of $\Omega$ above either $p_i$, the image of its decomposition group in the chosen finite Galois group is cyclic generated by a conjugate of $\sigma_i$. Any element of that group fixing $\delta$ acts by an even power, since $\delta$ has order two under the generator and this sign is invariant under conjugation. The square of the generator fixes the normal closure of $Y$. Thus every element of the decomposition group at $p_i$ that fixes $\delta$ fixes $Y$. [Chebotarev and the even-power condition](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/InertiaQuadraticRamification/PrimeWith.lean#L109) · [Congruence of the product and both decomposition conditions](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/InertiaQuadraticRamification/TwoPrimes.lean#L72)

## Adjoining the imaginary square root

Choose a positive integer $C$ divisible by $2,3$, the rational primes below $T$, and every rational prime ramified in $Y/\mathbb Q$. In the Lean construction one includes the norms of the places where inertia can move $Y$ over $Y^+$ or over $\mathbb Q$. The displayed choice has this same effect. Apply the prime construction to $M=8C$ and to a bound at least $C$. Set $d=p_1p_2$, and take $Z=Y(x)$ with $x^2=-d$. The valuations at $p_1$ in $Y$ give $d$ valuation one, since $p_1$ is unramified in $Y$ and $p_2\ne p_1$. Thus the extension is quadratic and ramifies there. This place lies outside $T$. The field is CM because it is the compositum of $Y$ with an imaginary quadratic field. Its maximal real subfield $Z^+$ contains $Y^+$, and the degree over $Y^+$ equals two by the degrees of the two CM extensions. [The modulus and the choice of the two primes](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/ExistsQuadraticCMExtSplitAt.lean#L38) · [Ramification at the first prime](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/ExistsQuadraticCMExtSplitAt.lean#L59)

Since $-d\equiv1\bmod8C$, $-d$ is a square in $\mathbb Q_p$ for every prime $p$ dividing $C$, by the [square-root calculation](real-quadratic-steps-separate-the-restrictions-of-places-above-a-prime.md#constructing-the-extensions). In particular the extension splits over every place in $T$. The square condition also gives the second part of usability at every place above $T$. The other part follows from the calculation for nonsplit places below.

## Controlling a nonsplit place

Let $w$ be nonsplit in $Z/Z^+$ and choose a valuation $A$ of $\Omega$ above it. There is a $g$ in the decomposition group over $Z^+$ which acts nontrivially on $Z$, because restriction gives the nontrivial decomposition group of this nonsplit quadratic extension. As $x$ is purely imaginary in each complex embedding, it generates $Z$ over $Z^+$ and $g(x)=-x$. Also $Z=Z^+Y$ since $Y$ contains a nonreal element, so $g$ does not fix $Y$. But $\delta$ is totally real and belongs to $Z^+$, so $g$ does fix $\delta$. [A nonsplit decomposition element moves both the imaginary root and the old field](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/InertiaQuadraticRamification/NonsplitOfSqrtConductor.lean#L189)

The rational prime below $w$ cannot divide $C$, since at those primes the roots of $X^2+d$ lie in $\mathbb Q_p$ and a decomposition element fixes them. Nor can it be $p_1$ or $p_2$, by the preceding decomposition property for elements fixing $\delta$. Thus $w$ avoids $T$, has residue characteristic different from $2,3$, and lies above a prime unramified in $Y/\mathbb Q$. The root $x$ gives an unramified or split local extension at such a place, since $X^2+d$ is separable modulo the residue prime, and either splits by simple-root lifting or generates an unramified quadratic extension. Consequently the place is unramified in $Z/\mathbb Q$, hence also in $Z^+/\mathbb Q$ and $Z/Z^+$, and its residue size exceeds $3$. In the project the condition at $Z^+$ is expressed by nondivisibility of the different. [Unramifiedness and exclusion of the prescribed places](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/ExistsQuadraticCMExtSplitAt.lean#L103) · [The conditions at the nonsplit real places](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/ExistsQuadraticCMExtSplitAt.lean#L121)

It remains to show that $Z/Y$ splits below this nonsplit $w$. Write $F=Y^+$. At the place under consideration both $Y/F$ and the local quadratic extension obtained by adjoining $x$ to $F$ are unramified or split. In the decomposition group over $F$, their actions therefore factor through the procyclic quotient by inertia. The element $g$ found above belongs to that group and does not fix $Y$, so the map to $\operatorname{Gal}(Y/F)$ is nontrivial. Its kernel in the procyclic quotient consists of the even powers, interpreted as the index-two closed subgroup. Those elements act trivially on $x$ too, since the action on $x$ has order at most two. Thus every element of the decomposition group over $Y$ fixes both $Y$ and $x$, proving splitness in $Z/Y$. [The splitting criterion using even Frobenius powers](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/InertiaQuadraticRamification/InertSplit.lean#L1)

The construction can avoid any finite set of additional places in its ramification choice, by including them in $T$ before choosing $C$. This is what lets it supply infinitely many quadratic extensions when the splitting and nonsplit requirements for the original $T$ are held fixed.
