# Cohomology kernels as cokernel lengths in cyclotomic towers

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E5%88%86%E5%9C%86%E5%A1%94%E4%B8%8A%E7%9A%84%E5%90%8C%E8%B0%83%E6%A0%B8%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E5%8F%98%E6%88%90%E4%BD%99%E6%A0%B8%E9%95%BF%E5%BA%A6.md)

## Statement

Let $\ell$ be prime and $F$ a number field that contains a primitive $\ell$th root of unity for odd $\ell$, or a primitive fourth root of unity for $\ell=2$. In an algebraic closure $\Omega$, put $F_\infty=F(\mu_{\ell^\infty})$ and $H=\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/F_\infty)$. Choose $\gamma\in\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/F)$ whose image topologically generates $\operatorname{Gal}(F_\infty/F)$, and put $u=\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}(\gamma)$.

Let $C=\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell$. Let $D$ be the module of locally constant additive characters $H\to C$ that vanish on the intersections with $H$ of all inertia groups and of all decomposition groups above $\ell$. Put $X=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z_\ell}(D,C)$. Use the conjugation action $d(g)\phi(h)=\phi(g^{-1}hg)$ on $D$ and the inverse dual action $x(g)v=v\circ d(g^{-1})$ on $X$. For a nonzero integer $e$ and a finite cyclotomic layer $L/F$ of degree $\ell^m$, where $m\ge0$,
$$
\operatorname{shaInv}(L,\ell,e)=\operatorname{length}_{\mathbb Z_\ell}\operatorname{coker}\bigl((u^e x(\gamma))^{\ell^m}-1\bigr).
$$
On the left is the length of the kernel of localization at all places above $\ell$ on $H^1$ with coefficients $C(-e)$, for the maximal extension unramified at finite places away from $\ell$. Lengths may be infinite. This is `step0`. [Length bounds for the resulting operator](finitely-many-valuation-centres-for-cyclotomic-length-bounds.md)

## Restriction detects the cohomology class

Write $G=\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/L)$ and let $\rho(g)$ be multiplication by $\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}(g)^{-e}$ on $C$. Inflate a cohomology class from the unramified quotient to $G$ and represent it by a continuous cocycle $z(gg')=z(g)+\rho(g)z(g')$. Coboundaries $z(g)=(\rho(g)-1)c$ vanish on $H$, where the action is trivial. Thus restriction to $H$ gives a well-defined additive character.

If $z|_H=0$, the cocycle factors through $G/H$. For a topological generator $t$ of $G/H$, the unit $\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}(t)$ has infinite order. Since $e\ne0$, multiplication by $\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}(t)^{-e}-1$ on $C$ is surjective. Subtract a coboundary to make $z(t)=0$. The cocycle then vanishes on all powers of $t$ and, by continuity, on the quotient. This coboundary also comes from the original unramified quotient, since the action factors through it. The same proof applies to a decomposition group $G_v$ above $\ell$, using $G_v/(G_v\cap H)$. The cyclotomic image of this decomposition group is an infinite closed subgroup of the tower group $\mathbb Z_\ell$. Indeed a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_\ell$ cannot contain all the roots of unity in that tower. Thus vanishing on $G_v\cap H$ makes the restriction to $G_v$ a coboundary. ## A twisted character extends to a cocycle

Take $\phi\in D$ with $\phi(ghg^{-1})=\rho(g)\phi(h)$ for all $g\in G,h\in H$. Lift a topological generator of $G/H$ to $G$ and let $P$ be the closure of its powers. We have $G=PH$. A procyclic profinite group decomposes into its prime components by applying the Chinese remainder theorem to its finite cyclic quotients and passing to the inverse limit. The $\ell$ component of $P$ maps onto $G/H\simeq\mathbb Z_\ell$. It is therefore itself $\mathbb Z_\ell$ and maps isomorphically. The remaining components make up $P\cap H$ and have no finite $\ell$-group quotient. Local constancy gives a finite $\ell$-group image for $\phi$, so $\phi$ vanishes on $P\cap H$. [Vanishing on the kernel](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/KummerTheory/VanishCyc.lean)

Define $z(ph)=\rho(p)\phi(h)$. Two decompositions differ by an element of $P\cap H$, on which both $\phi$ and the twist act trivially, so the definition is independent of the decomposition. The continuous surjection $P\times H\to G$ is a quotient map by compactness and the Hausdorff property, hence $z$ is continuous. In the formula $(ph)(qk)=(pq)(q^{-1}hq)k$, use $\phi(q^{-1}hq)=\rho(q)^{-1}\phi(h)$ and additivity to obtain $z((ph)(qk))=z(ph)+\rho(ph)z(qk)$. Also $z|_H=\phi$. [Extending the cocycle](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/KummerTheory/ExtCocycle.lean)

Away from $\ell$, inertia lies in $H$, so $z$ vanishes and $\rho$ is trivial there. Both descend to the unramified quotient. At every $v\mid\ell$, the defining conditions of $D$ give $z|_{G_v\cap H}=0$, so the local argument above gives a coboundary. Conversely, the restriction of a class in the localization kernel satisfies both conditions of $D$. The cocycle identity yields the required twisted equivariance. Thus the restriction is an isomorphism onto the submodule fixed by the twisted action $d_e(g)=\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}(g)^{-e}d(g)$. The construction works for every choice of local decomposition groups lifting the indicated places. [Surjectivity](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/KummerTheory/ResInfOnto.lean) · [Restriction isomorphism](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/NumberFieldTowers/Imports.lean)

## A single generator and duality

Under the root-of-unity hypothesis, $\operatorname{Gal}(F_\infty/F)\simeq\mathbb Z_\ell$. The degree-$\ell^m$ layer is unique, and the image of $G$ is topologically generated by the image of $g=\gamma^{\ell^m}$. Inner conjugation by $H$ acts trivially on its additive characters. Each locally constant character is fixed by conjugation by a sufficiently small open subgroup of $G$ by compactness of $H$. Its image is killed by a power of $\ell$, so some open subgroup also fixes it under the cyclotomic scalar action. Consequently a character fixed by $g^{-1}$ is fixed by the closure of its powers in the quotient. The localization kernel is therefore $\ker(d_e(g^{-1})-1)$. [Uniqueness of layers](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/NumberFieldTowers/LayerUniq.lean) · [Generator of a layer](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/NumberFieldTowers/LayerGen.lean) · [Invariants under one generator](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/NumberFieldTowers/ShaDGen.lean) · [Torsion characters](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/NumberFieldTowers/DinfTors.lean)

For a $\mathbb Z_\ell$-module $M$ write $M^\vee=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z_\ell}(M,C)$. For any endomorphism $T$ we have
$$
\operatorname{coker}(T^\vee)\simeq(\ker T)^\vee.
$$
Indeed $C$ is divisible and therefore injective over the discrete valuation ring. By Baer's criterion a map from a nonzero principal ideal $(a)$ extends because multiplication by $a$ on $C$ is surjective. Thus restriction $M^\vee\to(\ker T)^\vee$ is surjective. Every functional in its kernel factors through $M/\ker T\simeq\operatorname{im}T$, and extends from $\operatorname{im}T$ to $M$, so this kernel is $\operatorname{im}T^\vee$. Dualizing a composition series shows equality of finite lengths, because the simple module $\mathbb Z_\ell/(\ell)$ is self-dual. Duals of arbitrarily long strict chains give arbitrarily long strict annihilator chains, as $C$ detects every nonzero element by first taking a map on its cyclic submodule. This gives equality for infinite length as well. [Dual kernel](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/NumberFieldTowers/DualKer.lean) · [Length under duality](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/NumberFieldTowers/LenDual.lean) · [Cokernel length](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/NumberFieldTowers/CokerDual.lean)

The dual of $d_e(g^{-1})$ is $\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}(g)^e x(g)=(u^e x(\gamma))^{\ell^m}$. Apply the cokernel formula to $d_e(g^{-1})-1$ to conclude.
