# Summing negative eigenspace lengths after coefficient extension

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%89%A9%E5%A4%A7%E7%B3%BB%E6%95%B0%E7%8E%AF%E4%BB%A5%E5%90%8E%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E6%8A%8A%E8%B4%9F%E5%8F%B7%E9%95%BF%E5%BA%A6%E6%8C%89%E7%89%B9%E5%BE%81%E7%9B%B8%E5%8A%A0.md)

Let \(E\subset\mathbb Q(\mu_N)\) be CM, \(\Gamma=\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb Q)\), and \(\rho:(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^\times\twoheadrightarrow\Gamma\) restriction. Let \(\ell\) be an odd prime not dividing \([E:\mathbb Q]\), \(e\ge1\), \(k=e+1\), \(\epsilon=(-1)^k\), and \(c\) complex conjugation. Write \(\Sha(E,e)\) for the kernel of localization at decomposition groups over \(\ell\) in \(H^1(G_{E,S},\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell(-e))\), \(S\) the places over \(\ell\) and \(G_{E,S}\) unramified elsewhere at finite places. Let \(L\) be the \(\mathbb Z_\ell\)-length of the \(c=\epsilon\) eigenspace. Let \(T\) comprise the \(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\)-valued characters \(\chi=\psi\circ\rho\) with \(\psi(c)=\epsilon\), and define \(A_k(\chi)=N(\prod_{p\le k+1}p)B_{k,\chi^*}\). If [`PsiBound`](generalized-bernoulli-numbers-bound-a-negative-eigenspace.md) holds, giving each \(\psi\)-component over the coefficient ring \(O\) below length at most \(v_O(A_k(\chi))\), then \(L\) is finite and
$$
(\log\ell)L\le\sum_{\chi\in T}-\log|A_k(\chi)|_\ell .
$$

**Coefficient extension.** Let \(K_\ell=\mathbb Q_\ell(\xi)\) for a primitive root \(\xi\) of order the exponent of \(\Gamma\). Its integers \(O\) are finite free over \(\mathbb Z_\ell\) and contain all character values. With uniformizer \(\pi\) put \(r=v_O(\ell)>0\). Tensoring the sequence defining \(W=\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell\) gives \(O\otimes W=K_\ell/O\). With a finite \(\mathbb Z_\ell\)-basis for \(O\), continuous cochains, coboundaries and each local restriction are coordinatewise, since the twist is defined over \(\mathbb Z_\ell\). Thus the localization kernel is \(O\otimes\Sha(E,e)\). The absolute group model gives the same group: outside \(S\) inertia acts trivially and the local conditions give zero on those inertia groups, hence zero on their closed normal closure by the cocycle identity and continuity. The cocycle descends to the unramified quotient. Coboundaries and local zero classes correspond by pullback. The conjugation action is compatible with base change, so the sign submodule extends as well. A simple factor \(\mathbb F_\ell\) becomes \(O/\ell O\) of length \(r\). Hence the extended length is \(rL\), also for infinite length since faithfully flat extension preserves strict chains. **Character decomposition.** Since \(|\Gamma|\) is invertible in \(O\), the projectors \(P_\psi=|\Gamma|^{-1}\sum_g\psi(g)^{-1}g\) are orthogonal idempotents summing to \(1\), by character orthogonality over \(K_\ell\) and inclusion of \(O[\Gamma]\). Their images are the eigenspaces by \(uP_\psi=\psi(u)P_\psi\) and the identity action there. Only \(\psi(c)=\epsilon\) appear, so \(rL=\sum_{\psi(c)=\epsilon} L_\psi\). Lean uses a general upper bound \(rL\le\sum L_\psi\). For each cyclic factor generator \(T_0\) of order \(m\), \(\prod_{\omega^m=1}(T_0-\omega)=0\). For a composite \(AB\), \(B:\ker(AB)\to\ker A\) has kernel \(\ker B\), giving the upper bound by the two kernel lengths. Iterate over the factors of the product and then the commuting generators. On the sign submodule an eigenspace of opposite sign is zero since \(2\) is a unit. This proves the general upper bound used in the code. Each \(A_k(\chi)\in O\) is nonzero by the denominator and parity results. For \(j=v_O(A_k(\chi))\), the normalized absolute value gives \(-\log|A_k(\chi)|_\ell=(j/r)\log\ell\), from \(\ell=\pi^r\) times a unit. Thus `PsiBound` yields \(L_\psi\log\ell\le r(-\log|A_k(\chi)|_\ell)\), with finite length. Sum and cancel \(r>0\), using injectivity of \(\psi\mapsto\psi\circ\rho\). Next the [common integer product](one-integer-sums-the-bernoulli-valuations-across-primes.md) controls these sums across primes.
