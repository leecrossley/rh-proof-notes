# Finiteness of the negative-weight torsion localization kernel

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E8%B4%9F%E6%9D%83%E6%89%AD%E7%BB%93%E4%B8%8A%E5%90%8C%E8%B0%83%E7%9A%84%E5%B1%80%E9%83%A8%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E6%A0%B8%E6%9C%89%E9%99%90.md)

## Statement

Let $\ell$ be prime, $k$ a number field, $O$ a DVR finite over $\mathbb Z_\ell$ with fraction field $E$, $\psi:G_k\to O^\times$ a character with open kernel, and $e\ge1$. Set $M=(E/O)(\psi^{-1}\chi_\ell^{-e})$ with discrete topology. Let $\Sha_k(M)$ be the kernel of restriction in $H^1(G_k,M)$ to every finite inertia group and every decomposition group above $\ell$. Assume finite-coefficient Poitou–Tate duality, local cohomology finiteness and local Tate duality. Assume `LemmaK`, for which only finiteness of $H^2(G_{L,S_\ell},\mathbb Z_\ell(e+1))$ is needed over totally imaginary $L$ containing $\mu_\ell$, or $\mu_4$ when $\ell=2$. Then $\Sha_k(M)$ has finite $O$-length. This is `soFin` and supplies finiteness for [base-point rigidity](nonpositive-weight-cohomology-at-the-base-is-coboundary.md).

## Restriction to an untwisting field

Set $m=\ell$ for odd $\ell$ and $m=4$ for $\ell=2$. Take the fixed field $L$ of the common kernel of $\psi$ and the action on $\mu_m$. The two images are finite abelian, so $L/k$ is finite abelian. Since $m>2$, $L$ is totally imaginary. Over $L$ the coefficients become $(E/O)(-e)$.

Restriction preserves the local conditions. Outside $\ell$ the action is unramified, so a cocycle whose inertia class is zero has zero values there. It descends to $G_{L,S_\ell}$, with class zero at each place over $\ell$. For $\mathbb Z_\ell$ coefficients the [finite-to-integral duality comparison](first-localization-kernels-control-second-cohomology-length.md) identifies the length of $\Sha_L((\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell)(-e))$ with the length of the kernel of localization on $H^2(G_{L,S_\ell},\mathbb Z_\ell(e+1))$. This is finite by `LemmaK`. Tensoring the discrete coefficients with $O$ commutes with cocycles, coboundaries and the localization kernel, since $O$ is finite free over $\mathbb Z_\ell$. Each unit of $\mathbb Z_\ell$-length becomes $e(O/\mathbb Z_\ell)=\operatorname{length}_O O/\ell O$. Thus the image length is bounded.

## The restriction kernel

Put $G=G_k$, $H=G_L$, $\alpha=\psi^{-1}\chi_\ell^{-e}$, and choose $g_1,\ldots,g_d$ lifting generators of $G/H$. Any class restricting to zero has a cocycle representative $z$ vanishing on $H$, after subtracting a global coboundary. The module $\mathcal Z$ of such cocycles embeds in $M^d$ by evaluation, and surjects onto the restriction kernel. Because $G/H$ is abelian and the action is scalar, $z(ug)=z(gu)$. Expanding gives
$$
(\alpha(u)-1)z(g)=(\alpha(g)-1)z(u).
$$
This is a coboundary as a function of $g$. Hence the restriction kernel is killed by the ideal $(a)$ generated by the differences $\alpha(u)-1$, and is a quotient of $\mathcal Z/a\mathcal Z$.

Each finite set in $\mathcal Z$ generates a finite-length submodule $F$. Its image in that quotient has length at most $\operatorname{length}F/aF=\operatorname{length}F[a]$. Taking the supremum over finite sets yields
$$
\operatorname{length}_O\ker\mathrm{res}
\le\operatorname{length}_O\mathcal Z[a]\le d\,\operatorname{length}_O M[a]
=d\,\operatorname{length}_O M^G.
$$
The cyclotomic image of $G_L$ is open in $\mathbb Z_\ell^\times$ since $L/\mathbb Q$ is finite. It contains a unit $u$ of infinite order in $1+\ell^b\mathbb Z_\ell$ for some $b\ge2$. Since $\psi|_{G_L}=1$, $u^e-1\ne0$ kills $M^G$, so this invariant submodule lies in a finite-length torsion layer of $E/O$. Therefore both terms of
$$
\operatorname{length}_O\Sha_k(M)\le
e(O/\mathbb Z_\ell)\operatorname{length}_{\mathbb Z_\ell}\Sha_L((\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell)(-e))
+d\,\operatorname{length}_O M^G
$$
are finite. The [window application](../zh/%E7%AA%97%E5%8F%A3%E9%87%8C%E5%89%A9%E4%B8%8B%E7%9A%84%E5%B0%8F%E6%8C%87%E6%A0%87%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E7%94%A8%E4%BA%8C%E9%98%B6%E4%B8%8A%E5%90%8C%E8%B0%83%E4%BC%B0%E8%AE%A1.md) also needs a quantitative bound in the weight and the number field.
