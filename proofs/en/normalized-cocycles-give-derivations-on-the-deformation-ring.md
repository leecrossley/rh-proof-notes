# Normalized cocycles give derivations on the deformation ring

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E5%BD%92%E4%B8%80%E5%8C%96%E4%BD%99%E9%97%AD%E9%93%BE%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E7%BB%99%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%98%E5%BD%A2%E7%8E%AF%E4%B8%8A%E7%9A%84%E5%AF%BC%E5%AD%90.md)

Let $O$ be a complete DVR with finite residue field and $2$ invertible. Let $G,H$ be topological groups, $\iota:H\to G$ continuous, and $\mathcal N\subset G$. Take characters $\eta:G\to\{\pm1\}$, $\Psi:G\to O^\times$ with all integral powers of $\Psi$ continuous, and the marks $c,g_1$ of [the rank-three base representation](embedding-a-scalar-cocycle-in-a-rank-three-deformation.md): $c^2=1,\eta(c)=-1,\Psi(c)=1,\eta(g_1)=1,\Psi(g_1)^2-1\in O^\times$. Use $r_0=(\operatorname{diag}(\Psi,1,\Psi^{-1}),\eta)$, with weights $w=(1,0,-1)$, and assume $r_0=1$ on $\mathcal N$.

Let $U$ represent unrestricted $H$-deformations, $I\subset U$ a local condition stable under conjugation by matrices identity modulo the maximal ideal, and $x_H:U\to O$ the base point of $r_0|_H$ killing $I$. Let $R$ represent deformations of $G$ trivial on $\mathcal N$, satisfying $I$ along $\iota$, and normalized by $r(c)_{\rm mat}=1$ and equality of the $(1,2),(2,1)$ entries at $g_1,cg_1^{-1}c$. Here $r$ is the universal representation and $x_0:R\to O$ gives $r_0$. Let $A=\bigoplus_j A_j\xrightarrow q R$ be a graded model with $r(g)_{ij}$ lifting in degree $w_i-w_j$, and $x=x_0q$ zero on all nonzero degrees. Select relations $F_i\in A_{-1}\cap\ker q$. Let $\lambda$ be the [cotangent quotient length](first-order-deformations-enter-the-dual-cotangent-module.md) at $x$ for degree $-1$ and these relations.

Assume the model lift property: for $a\ne0$, a derivation $D:R\to O/(a)$ at $x_0$ and $D_H:U\to O$ at $x_H$, both supported in weight $-1$ on the matrix entries, with $D_H(I)=0$ and agreeing modulo $a$ on the corresponding local entries, yield a degree $-1$ integral derivation on $A$ lifting $Dq$.

Let $C=O/(a)$, and let $\mu\mapsto\gamma_\mu$ be an additive injection from an $O$-module $L$ to locally constant $r_0$-adjoint cocycles $G\to M_3(C)$ supported in weight $-1$. Require $\gamma(c)=0$, $\gamma(\mathcal N)=0$, and equality of the $(1,2),(2,1)$ entries of $\gamma(g)r_0(g)_{\rm mat}$ for $g=g_1,cg_1^{-1}c$. Also assume $\gamma(\iota h)=X-\operatorname{Ad}_{r_0(\iota h)}X$ for some weight $-1$ matrix $X$ over $C$. Then $\operatorname{length}_O L\le\lambda$.

**Proof.** Use the square-zero ring $O\oplus\varepsilon C$ with $\varepsilon^2=0$, and define $r_\gamma(g)=(1+\varepsilon\gamma(g),1)r_0(g)$. Multiplication gives the cocycle identity by conjugating the second infinitesimal term across $r_0(g)$. Continuity and the global equations hold from the assumptions. For the local condition, first lift the local conjugation to $O\oplus\varepsilon O$. If $X_{32}=-X_{21}$, lift $X$ to $Y=y(E_{21}-E_{32})$, fixed by the signed transpose branch; its adjoint coboundary is continuous by $\Psi$. Otherwise the two sign branches along $H$ are locally constant: $\Psi(\iota h)(X_{32}-\gamma(\iota h)_{32})$ in $C$ equals $X_{32}$ in one branch and $-X_{21}$ in the other. Those values are distinct and the function is locally constant. Then any weight $-1$ lift $Y$ to $O$ has continuous adjoint coboundary. The conjugate of the local base representation by $1+\varepsilon Y$ has infinitesimal term $Y-\operatorname{Ad}Y$, kills $I$ by conjugation stability, and reduces in the infinitesimal coordinate to $r_\gamma|_H$.

Thus the global and local representations define $F:R\to O\oplus\varepsilon C$ and $F_H:U\to O\oplus\varepsilon O$. Write $F=x_0+\varepsilon D$; the algebra homomorphism condition gives the derivation law and
$$
D(r(g)_{ij})=\gamma(g)_{ij}\Psi(g)^{w_j}.
$$
The analogous local derivation $D_H$ kills $I$, has the required degree and agrees modulo $a$ on the entries. By the lift property $Dq$ lifts to an integral derivation on $A$. A derivation is determined by the universal entries since placing it in the square-zero ring recovers the representing map. The displayed formula therefore gives additivity for $\mu\mapsto Dq$, and injectivity by the homogeneous lifts of all entries and the units $\Psi^{w_j}$. These maps kill $F_i\subset\ker q$. Apply the cotangent dual bound to get $\operatorname{length}_O L\le\lambda$.

In `vlen_le`, apply this to torsion layers $S[b]$ of the local cohomology kernel via the matrix cocycles (the modulus $a$ may be chosen for each layer). The full module is their increasing union since every continuous cocycle of the compact absolute group in a discrete torsion module has finite image. Any finite strict chain is witnessed in a common layer by finitely many elements, so the full length is also at most $\lambda$. The local lift property, stability and representability are the model inputs.
