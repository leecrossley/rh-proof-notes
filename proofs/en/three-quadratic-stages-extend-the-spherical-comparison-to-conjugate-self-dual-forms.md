# Three quadratic stages extend the spherical comparison to conjugate-self-dual forms

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

## Statement

Fix a CM number field $E$, its algebraic closure $\Omega$, a prime $\ell$, a field isomorphism $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$, and $a\ge1$. Let $\pi$ be a regular algebraic cuspidal automorphic representation of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb A_E)$ with $\pi^c\simeq\pi^\vee$, where $c$ is CM conjugation. In the project's conventions this is the polarized scope with $\chi=\eta_{E/E^+}^a$; the sign condition holds at every real place, and the pullback of this quadratic character by the norm is trivial. The assertion is a continuous semisimple $\rho:G_E\to\mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ with the four spherical comparisons for every finite place of $E$ at which $\pi$ has a Satake multiset. These are the unramified normalized inverse Frobenius eigenvalues away from $\ell$, and the crystalline and de Rham comparisons, prescribed jumps and normalized Weil–Deligne polynomials at inducing bridges above $\ell$. [The norm pullback of the polarization](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/CsdScopeOfLift.lean#L81) · [The conjugate-self-dual row](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/LimOddOfLimWU3.lean#L88)

The input is the same comparison on usable places for definite unitary automorphic representations of every rank in the following field range. The CM field $Z$ has even real degree, $Z/Z^+$ is unramified at every finite place, all places over $\ell$ split over $Z^+$, and three have distinct restrictions to $Z^+$. There is $x\in Z$ with $x^2=-d$ for an integer $d>0$, with $-d$ a square in $\mathbb Q_\ell$. A usable finite place is split over $Z^+$ with $-d$ a square in the rational completion below it. In addition, at every nonsplit place the real place below has residue characteristic different from $2$, residue size above $3$, and is unramified over the rational prime. The [odd-rank comparison](three-places-above-the-coefficient-prime-remove-the-auxiliary-exceptions.md) and the [even-rank argument](two-odd-rank-lifts-recover-the-even-rank-galois-representation.md), applied on the [usable density set](degree-one-frobenius-elements-are-dense-at-the-usable-split-places.md), give this input from their respective limit and theta hypotheses. Use also the definite unitary transfer below. Then the full comparison follows. This is the route from `AllUd` through `CoreGLu` to `RowCsd`. ## From the definite unitary form to a general linear form

For the special fields $Z$ in this input, call a conjugate-self-dual regular algebraic cuspidal representation $P$ *good* if at every nonsplit place it is spherical, the place avoids $\ell$, and $Z/Z^+$ is unramified there. A definite unitary transfer, `DescOffT`, gives a rank-$a$ definite unitary automorphic representation $U$ when $P$ is good. At each split place every local component of $P$ is the corresponding local component of $U$ under the split chart, and at every complex embedding the infinitesimal multiset of $P$ is that of the weight of $U$. The precise transfer also chooses a finite nonsplit exceptional set for the level of $U$, disjoint from any prescribed finite set. Here use the empty prescribed set. This is an internal input to the present deduction. [Local and weight matching for the transfer](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GaloisGeometry/MatchOfMatchOffTEmpty.lean#L25) · [Construction of the definite unitary transfer](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/BaseChangeDescent/BcBackOffT.lean#L25)

Choose $U$ by this transfer and apply the all-rank unitary input. If $P$ has a Satake multiset $B$ at a usable place of $Z$, the split chart gives the same Satake multiset for $U$. The normalized residue eigenvalues therefore agree. The weights give the same prescribed jumps, so all four comparisons hold for $P$ on this same set. This is `joinGL_holds`. ## Cuspidality and conjugate self-duality in the tower

At each quadratic extension $K'/K$ in the construction, there is a ramified place above a spherical place of the form being lifted. The quadratic class field character of $K'/K$ is ramified at the latter place. A spherical irreducible representation and its twist by a ramified character of the determinant cannot be isomorphic. Indeed the former is a constituent of an unramified principal series with unramified supercuspidal support; after the twist all characters in that support are ramified. By the cyclic prime-degree base change theorem, a cuspidal form with no self-twist by the defining character has cuspidal base change. The strong lift has the Satake power rule and local and infinite compatibility stated in the [gluing proof](patching-quadratic-base-changes-gives-one-representation-over-the-original-field.md). A nonunitary cuspidal form can be put in the unitary convention by a real power of the norm, which commutes with the lift rules. [Arthur–Clozel, Chapter III, Theorems 4.2(a) and 5.1](https://www.claymath.org/library/cw/arthur/pdf/30.pdf) · [Using ramification over a spherical place](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/QuadLift.lean#L14)

Base change preserves the regular infinitesimal weights under restriction of embeddings. It also preserves conjugate self-duality: at unramified places the Satake multiset at the conjugate place is obtained by taking inverses, and taking the $f$th powers under base change commutes with inversion. Thus the conjugate of the lift and its contragredient agree at almost every finite place. The cuspidal case of strong multiplicity one identifies them. That case says that two cuspidal representations on the same general linear group with equal unramified local components outside a finite set are isomorphic. The resulting form has the polarization by $\eta_{K'/K'^+}^a$, since its norm pullback is trivial and its signs are $(-1)^a$. [Jacquet–Shalika, On Euler Products and the Classification of Automorphic Representations II, Theorem 4.4](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~hj/On%20Euler%20products%20II.pdf) · [Preserving the conjugate-self-dual scope](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/CsdLift.lean#L17)

## The two real steps and the last step

For a form at any stage and a target place $w$, let the bad set consist of $w$, the places over $\ell$, and the places where the form has no Satake multiset. This set is finite since a cuspidal automorphic representation is spherical at all but finitely many places. In the first stage, take any of the infinitely many [real quadratic extensions](real-quadratic-steps-separate-the-restrictions-of-places-above-a-prime.md) splitting this bad set and ramifying outside it, and consider the base change $\pi_1$. In the second stage, starting with $\pi_1$, repeat the construction for its own target place and bad set, and take the base change $\pi_2$ to $Y$. Both have the same regular conjugate-self-dual scope just proved. Choose a place over $\ell$ in the original field. All places above it belong to the prescribed bad set whenever a new quadratic step is chosen. By the two-step real-restriction argument, every CM extension of $Y$ now has three places above $\ell$ with pairwise distinct real restrictions. [The finite set forced to split at each stage](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/BadFinite.lean#L25) · [Two applications of the real steps](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/BadFinite.lean#L85)

For $\pi_2$ and any target place in $Y$, apply the [controlled imaginary quadratic construction](two-frobenius-classes-give-a-quadratic-extension-with-controlled-nonsplit-places.md) to its bad set. It applies because the second real step gives the required $\delta$ with the specified automorphism. The extension $Z/Y$ ramifies outside the bad set and splits at each of its places. Every place above that set is usable. In particular the places above $\ell$ split over $Z^+$ and $-d$ is a square in $\mathbb Q_\ell$. The field has even real degree, the required nonsplit bounds and three distinct real restrictions above $\ell$. For its base change $P$, every nonsplit place of $Z/Z^+$ is above a place outside the bad set of $\pi_2$. Such a lower place is spherical and away from $\ell$, and the base change power formula provides the spherical Satake multiset for $P$ there. Unramifiedness of the extension over the real field is part of the quadratic construction. Thus $P$ is good and the unitary transfer applies. The usable places contain all but finitely many prime-norm places of $Z$, by the [prime-norm argument](degree-one-frobenius-elements-are-dense-at-the-usable-split-places.md#why-a-prime-norm-place-is-usable), and all places above the specified target. [Goodness and the precise usable set on the third extension](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/CrystallineDescent/BadFinite.lean#L66)

For each target in $Y$ there are infinitely many such serving extensions, since one can add every ramified place of any given finite family to the prescribed set and demand fresh ramification. Apply [quadratic gluing](patching-quadratic-base-changes-gives-one-representation-over-the-original-field.md) to obtain the complete spherical comparison for $\pi_2$ over $Y$. This supplies the premise for the same gluing operation at the second real stage, using the whole set of places as the upper comparison set. That gluing gives the complete comparison for $\pi_1$. Finally glue over the first real stage to get the asserted comparison for $\pi$. At each stage the placewise extensions combine to one global representation by the degree-one and finite-exception argument in the gluing proof.
