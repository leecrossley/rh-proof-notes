# Deformation tangent vectors satisfy the Bloch–Kato local conditions

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E5%8F%98%E5%BD%A2%E7%9A%84%E5%88%87%E5%90%91%E9%87%8F%E6%BB%A1%E8%B6%B3BlochKato%E5%B1%80%E9%83%A8%E6%9D%A1%E4%BB%B6.md)

Use the [single-character setup](a-single-character-length-is-bounded-by-automorphic-trace-differences.md) and `GD`. Let $U,I$ give the local condition, and $R,r$ the global ring and universal representation with the normalizations, specified residual inertia kernel killed, and local condition $I$. Set $k=\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. Given $F:R\to k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$ whose constant part $f$ is local (its maximal ideal values have norm $<1$), write $M_g+\epsilon N_g$ for the matrix there, $M_g$ for the constant matrix. Then $\gamma(g)=N_g M_g^{-1}$ is a continuous cocycle in $M_3(k)$ with the adjoint action of $r_f$. At inertia away from $\ell$ it is a coboundary. On $G_{\mathbb Q_\ell}$, its image after tensoring with $B_{\rm cris}$ is a coboundary $gY-Y$ for $Y\in M_3(k)\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}B_{\rm cris}$ with the diagonal action. The latter condition is at a decomposition group associated to an embedding of the algebraic closure. **Cocycle and continuity.** The perturbed representation is $(1+\epsilon\gamma(g),1)r_f(g)$. Multiplying gives $\gamma(gh)=\gamma(g)+\operatorname{Ad}_{r_f(g)}\gamma(h)$, since the squares vanish, and the sign $-1$ acts infinitesimally by $-JX^{\mathsf T}J$. The project's local point evaluation results `ptFin` and `ptCont` place both components of a square-zero map with local constant part in a finite coefficient field and give continuity on compositions with maximal-ideal-adically continuous functions. Apply to the continuous universal entries and then use matrix multiplication and inversion.

**Away from $\ell$.** The kernel of the residual base representation on inertia has finite index by finiteness of the residue field; the defining equations kill it even at the square-zero point. Thus $\gamma$ is zero on that subgroup. In general a characteristic-zero cocycle $z$ zero on a finite index subgroup $H$ is constant on left cosets of the form $gH$. Summing its values $Z$ on these cosets, left multiplication by $g$ gives $Z=[G:H] z(g)+gZ$. Thus $z$ is the coboundary of $-Z/[G:H]$. If it was a coboundary on $H$, subtract that primitive's coboundary on $G$ first. This proves the away condition and gives the descent used at $\ell$.

**At $\ell$.** The map $U\to R$ kills $I$, so the entire square-zero representation locally kills $I$. The `rtPoints` input gives a crystalline matrix representation on the open $H_\ell=\ker\eta_\ell\cap\ker\tau_\ell$ for every specialization $U\to B$ in a finite-dimensional commutative $\mathbb Q_\ell$-algebra $B$ killing $I$; $\tau_\ell$ specifies the finite inertia type and $\eta_\ell$ is the local sign. Here take $B=E[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$ containing the coefficients in $E/\mathbb Q_\ell$ finite. On $H_\ell$ the sign is positive and this is an extension of $V$ by $V$ with class $\gamma$. Crystalline comparison and exactness of crystalline periods give a splitting after tensoring with $B_{\rm cris}$, by lifting a linear splitting on invariant periods. This is the coboundary condition on the matrix module with periods. Apply the finite-index average to descend it to the whole decomposition group and extend to $k$. **Rigidity.** The [fixed point argument](a-deformation-triangular-in-both-directions-is-the-base-point.md) identifies the point with both triangularities. At the base, any nonpositive homogeneous tangent cocycle becomes a global coboundary by [the nonpositive-weight vanishing](nonpositive-weight-cohomology-at-the-base-is-coboundary.md). At a two-sided point, the two [shapes](rank-three-two-sided-points-give-two-representation-types.md) use `RigidityFirstCase` with automorphy, or the modular corner extensions, `KatoTwoW` and `RigiditySecondCase`, respectively. These vanishings apply to every continuous cocycle satisfying the two local conditions just proved, hence give infinitesimal conjugations. Together these are `PtHyp`. The local crystalline theorem and automorphic rigidity are inputs here.
