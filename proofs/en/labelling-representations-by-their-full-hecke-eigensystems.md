# Labelling representations by their full Hecke eigensystems

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E8%A6%81%E6%8C%89%E6%95%B4%E5%A5%97Hecke%E6%9C%AC%E5%BE%81%E7%B3%BB%E7%BB%9F%E7%BB%99%E8%A1%A8%E7%A4%BA%E7%BC%96%E5%8F%B7.md)

Let $K$ be CM, $n\ge2$, and $m_1>\dots>m_n$ integers with $|m_i|\le H$, $m_i+m_{n+1-i}=w$. Take level $M\ge1$, prime $p\nmid M|\operatorname{disc}K|$, $v\mid p$ in $K$, $q=\mathrm Nv$. In `CountKAlt` take $w=1-n$ and the complex family `SigK` of cuspidal representations of ${\rm GL}_n(\mathbb A_K)$ with infinitesimal character $\{m_i+(n-1)/2\}$ at every complex embedding, conjugate self-duality, and invariants by $K(M)$. Here $K(M)$ is the principal congruence subgroup modulo $M$ of ${\rm GL}_n(\widehat{\mathcal O_K})$. In `CountPAlt` take $K$ imaginary quadratic, $w$ arbitrary and the polarized complex family `SigP`, with the same weight and level conditions and the project's polarization character replacing conjugate self-duality. For these constructions use:

1. Any finite collection in the complex family of pairwise nonisomorphic representations has size at most $c[M(H+1)]^A$, $c,A$ depending only on $K,n$.
2. At each finite $u\nmid M$, let $\mathcal B_\pi(u)$ be the unramified Satake multiset scaled by $(\mathrm Nu)^{(n-1)/2}$. It is invariant under isomorphism of $\pi$; any complex field automorphism $\sigma$ carries all these multisets of $\pi$ to those of another member $\pi'$.
3. The inverse multiset $\beta_\pi(v)$ of $\mathcal B_\pi(v)$ has $n$ elements with $q^{w/2}/\sqrt q<|b|<q^{w/2}\sqrt q$, product of moduli $q^{nw/2}$, and $q^u b$, $q^u b^{-1}$ integral for integers $u\ge-\min_i m_i$, $u\ge\max_i m_i$, respectively.
4. For each $\ell\ne2,p$ and coefficient isomorphism $\iota$, the level compatibility `LevelKNAlt` or `LevelPNAlt` puts the automorphic witness of each allowed continuous semisimple $\ell$-adic representation in the chosen complex family, and supplies that the representation is unramified at $u\nmid\ell M$ with inverse arithmetic Frobenius eigenvalues $\mathcal B_\pi(u)$ under $\iota$. This uses the local level and weights, the safe flag condition, and `WitA`.

Then there is one [`EigData` list](from-eigenvalue-ranges-to-frobenius-trace-lists.md) of size at most $c[M(H+1)]^A$, classifying all allowed representations at $v$ with any $\ell,\iota$ in the indicated range. **Proof.** Take $S=\{\operatorname{sys}(\pi)\}$ with $\operatorname{sys}(\pi):u\mapsto\mathcal B_\pi(u)$ over all $u\nmid M$. Any finite subset admits pairwise nonisomorphic representatives by assumption 2, so assumption 1 bounds its size and hence all of $S$. Set $\beta_s$ to the inverse multiset of $s(v)$. Pointwise action by $\sigma$ is a permutation on $S$ since its inverse also preserves $S$. This carries the inverse multiset as required, and assumption 3 gives the remaining eigendata. For each allowed $r$, assign the system of its witness. At fixed $\ell,\iota$, two representations of the same label have the same inverse Frobenius polynomials at every $u\nmid\ell M$, hence the same usual Frobenius traces. Chebotarev density and continuity extend trace equality everywhere, and characteristic-zero Brauer–Nesbitt gives isomorphism by semisimplicity. Assumption 4 supplies the required eigenvalues at $v$. Thus one place provides the recorded value, with the full system distinguishing representations. For the polynomial counting input, the project chooses an archimedean coefficient system $\Xi$ of the given infinitesimal character and dimension $\le c_1(H+1)^{A_1}$ by Weyl dimension. Each counted cusp form contributes to $(\mathfrak g,K_\infty)$-cohomology and hence to level $K(3M)$ automorphic cohomology because $K(3M)\subset K(M)$. The Borel-type counting injection bounds the size of a finite collection by the total cohomological dimension, at most $c_2M^{A_2}\dim\Xi$ by the fundamental-domain bound. Multiply to get $c_1c_2[M(H+1)]^{A_1+A_2}$. The polarized count uses the same two estimates, taken here as the upstream harmonic analysis and cohomology inputs.
