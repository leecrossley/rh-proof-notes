# The Wach kernel is the intersection with the étale kernel

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E5%9C%A8%C3%A9tale%E6%A0%B8%E9%87%8C%E5%8F%96%E4%BA%A4%E6%81%B0%E5%A5%BD%E5%BE%97%E5%88%B0Wach%E6%A0%B8%E6%A0%BC.md)

Let $O$ be the integer ring of a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_\ell$, $A=O[[U]]^n$ with $n\ge1$, and $E$ the $\ell$-adic completion of $A[1/U]$. For a short exact sequence $0\to T_0\xrightarrow\iota T_1\xrightarrow q T_2\to0$ of finite free crystalline $O$-lattices let $D_j,N_j$ be the étale and Wach modules. Use the exact étale sequence and free $A$-lattices with $E\otimes_A N_j=D_j$. The maps respect the lattices and commute with Frobenius and the cyclotomic action. At a good level $s\ge1$, $X=(1+U)^{\ell^{s-1}}-1$ and $Q=\Phi_{\ell^s}(1+U)$ are units in $E$. Assume the lattice conditions $(\gamma-1)N_j\subset UN_j$ and mutual inclusion with the Frobenius span after suitable powers of $Q$. Then $D(\iota)^{-1}(N_1)=N_0$.

**Proof.** Identify $D_0\subset D_1$ and let $S=N_1\cap D_0=\ker(N_1\to N_2)$. It is finite over $A$ by Noetherianity and $E\otimes_A S=D_0$ by flatness. For $\ell x=Uy$ with $x,y\in S$, the constant terms in an $N_1$-basis force $x=Ux'$ for $x'\in N_1$. Since $U$ is invertible in $D_2$, $q(x)=0$ gives $q(x')=0$. Therefore $S/US$ is $\ell$-torsion-free componentwise. As in [the freeness argument](double-saturation-of-a-wach-image-is-free.md), lift an $O$-basis of that quotient, use Nakayama for generation, and divide a relation by $U$ repeatedly for independence. After base change each component has the rank of $D_0$, giving a free $A$-module.

The cyclotomic action preserves $S$, and $(\gamma-1)x=Uy$ with $y\in N_1$ gives $y\in S$ by applying $q$ and cancelling $U$. A power of $Q$ puts $\varphi(x)$ in $N_1$, and the result remains in $D_0$. For the reverse inclusion, if $x\in S$ write $Q^kx\in A\varphi(N_1)$ for some $k$ and expand it as $\sum_{a=0}^{\ell-1}(1+U)^a\varphi(w_a)$, $w_a\in N_1$, using the [integral Frobenius basis](integral-power-series-basis-for-frobenius.md). Applying $q$ gives zero. For an $A$-basis $b_j$ of $N_2$, the $\varphi(b_j)$ form an $E$-basis of $D_2$ by the reverse height condition and invertibility of $Q$. Write $q(w_a)=\sum_j c_{aj}b_j$, with $c_{aj}\in A$. Independence gives $\sum_a (1+U)^a\varphi(c_{aj})=0$ for each $j$. Frobenius coordinates are unique, so all $c_{aj}=0$, hence $w_a\in S$. This proves $Q^k x\in A\varphi(S)$.

Thus $S$ and $N_0$ are two free lattices with the same étale module and both operator conditions. The [period matrix comparison](two-wach-lattices-agree-after-inverting-the-parameter.md) gives the two inclusions after powers of $X$; [Wach uniqueness](wach-lattices-coincide-after-cancelling-denominators.md) cancels the powers and yields $S=N_0$.
