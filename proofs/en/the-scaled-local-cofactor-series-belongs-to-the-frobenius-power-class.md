# The scaled local cofactor series belongs to the Frobenius power class

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix a component $v$ of a served formal model at $p\ge5$, an even orbit length, a layer and its dual frame. Let $K_0$ be the Witt fraction field and $K'$ a normed coefficient extension in which the integer ring of the model maps into the unit ball and $\varpi^{p^2-1}=p$. Let $\ell$ be the height-two logarithm, and $\mu$ the integral inverse of the scaled logarithm $\ell(\varpi T)/\varpi$. The cofactor test series has the form $\Theta=\mathcal T(e^{-\Phi_v}U)$, where $\mathcal T$ is the linear frame, $\Phi_v$ is a sum of the component's bivariate phase over its matrix entries, and $U$ is integral in formal parameters. Assume the two formal comparisons used in `RestBeta` and `RestGc`. In the first, the bivariate phase in formal parameters differs from the normal phase `Gser` by a zero-constant series whose negative exponential is integral. In the second, for the same conjugate coefficients of `Gser` the exponential of the negative sum of these normal phases, after substitution of $\varpi\mu$ on the linear entry variables, equals
$$
\exp\left(-\frac{\varpi^{p+1}}p\,h\right)V
$$
with $V$ integral and $h$ the [cyclic Frobenius phase](a-frobenius-phase-splits-into-pure-top-and-mixed-terms.md) for some integral $u_s$ in the orbit coordinates. The local proof of these comparisons is upstream here. The claim is that $\Theta(\varpi Z)$ belongs to `InClass` for $p,h$ at this component after restriction to its letter variables. The parameter substitutions are compatible because $\ell(\varpi\mu(T))=\varpi T$. In the scaled letter series make the substitution $t(T)=\varpi\mu(T)$ into the expression in formal parameters. The linear frame has integral coefficients and zero constant term, so $t$ composed with it also has integral coefficients and zero constant term. The exponential of the bivariate discrepancy stays integral under this substitution. Each copy and their finite product preserve this property. An additional discrepancy from replacing the phase by a sum of its component copies is also harmless when its negative exponential is integral, by the same argument. Since $U(\ell(T))$ was integral, the rescaled $U$ is integral by the inverse-logarithm substitution. Multiplying all the correction factors gives an integral series $W$, and the scaled kernel is $\exp(-\varpi^{p+1}h/p)W$. Renaming component variables into the full system, and restricting back to the component, respects these operations. In the coefficient of $p^{-i}h^i$ the exponential contributes $(-\varpi^{p+1})^i/i!$. This scalar has valuation
$$
\frac{(p+1)i}{p^2-1}-v_p(i!)=\frac{i}{p-1}-v_p(i!)\ge0 .
$$
Thus $U_i=(-\varpi^{p+1})^iW/i!$ is integral for every $i$. Since $h$ has zero constant term, all coefficient sums are finite and these $U_i$ witness `InClass`. This is the reduction required in [the framed coefficient estimate](the-three-entry-bounds-pass-to-every-framed-coefficient.md).
