# Cyclotomic differences give the conductor–discriminant formula

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E4%BB%8E%E5%8D%95%E4%BD%8D%E6%A0%B9%E7%9A%84%E5%B7%AE%E7%AE%97%E5%87%BA%E5%88%86%E5%9C%86%E5%AD%90%E5%9F%9F%E7%9A%84%E5%88%A4%E5%88%AB%E5%BC%8F.md)

Let $N\ge1$, $K=\mathbb Q(\zeta_N)$, and $E$ an intermediate field, $G=(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^\times$, $H=\operatorname{Gal}(K/E)$, $d=[E:\mathbb Q]$. For each complex character $\chi$ of $G/H$ pulled back to $G$, let $f_\chi$ be its conductor, the least inducing modulus. Then $|\operatorname{disc}(E)|=\prod_\chi f_\chi\le N^d$. Taking logarithms divided by $d$ gives the root discriminant, and the prime divisors coincide with those of the least common multiple of the conductors.

**The different.** Use the standard identities $\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z[\zeta_N]=\mathcal O_E[\zeta_N]$, the monogenic different generated by the minimal polynomial derivative, and transitivity of the different. Taking the quotient of the products of conjugate differences for $K/\mathbb Q$ and $K/E$ yields
$$
\mathfrak D_{E/\mathbb Q}\mathcal O_K
=\left(\prod_{b\in G\setminus H}(\zeta_N-\zeta_N^b)\right).
$$
Its absolute norm on the left is $|\operatorname{disc}(E)|^{[K:E]}$. Let $o_b$ be the order of $\zeta_N^{b-1}$, greater than one. Each factor's norm is $\Phi_{o_b}(1)^{[K:\mathbb Q]/\varphi(o_b)}$ in absolute value by taking the norm first from the field generated by this root. For $j>1$, $\Phi_j(1)=p$ if $j=p^s$, $s\ge1$, and $1$ otherwise. This follows inductively from $\prod_{m\mid j,m>1}\Phi_m(1)=j$ by dividing $X^j-1$ by $X-1$ and evaluating at $1$. Thus for $w_p(o)=1/\varphi(p^s)$ when $o=p^s$, and zero otherwise,
$$
\frac{v_p(|\operatorname{disc}(E)|)}d=\sum_{b\in G\setminus H}w_p(o_b).
$$

**Counting conductors.** For $p\mid N$, write $N=p^\alpha N'$ with $p\nmid N'$. Let $J_j=\ker(G\to(\mathbb Z/(N'p^j)\mathbb Z)^\times)$ for $0\le j\le\alpha$. By the Chinese remainder theorem $v_p(f_\chi)$ is the least $j$ for which $\chi$ kills $J_j$. The fraction of characters killing $J_j$ is $|J_j\cap H|/|J_j|$, since these are the characters of the quotient by its image in $G/H$. Counting levels before the least such $j$,
$$
\frac1d\sum_\chi v_p(f_\chi)=
\sum_{j=0}^{\alpha-1}\left(1-\frac{|J_j\cap H|}{|J_j|}\right)
=\sum_{b\notin H}\ \sum_{0\le j<\alpha,\ b\in J_j}\frac1{|J_j|}.
$$
For $b\not\equiv1\bmod N'$ the inner sum is zero and $o_b=N/\gcd(N,b-1)$ has a prime factor other than $p$. Otherwise put $r=\min(\alpha,v_p(b-1))<\alpha$. Then $b$ belongs to $J_0,\dots,J_r$, $o_b=p^{\alpha-r}$, and the Chinese remainder theorem gives $|J_0|=\varphi(p^\alpha)$, $|J_j|=p^{\alpha-j}$ for $j\ge1$. The inner sum is $1/(p^{\alpha-1}(p-1))+\sum_{j=1}^r p^{j-\alpha}=1/\varphi(p^{\alpha-r})=w_p(o_b)$. For $p\nmid N$ both valuations are zero. Therefore the positive integers in the formula agree at every prime.

`vdField_eq_avg` computes the normalized valuation using the different and `sum_rootWeight_eq_avgCondExp` compares it to the character count. `natAbs_discr_eq_finprod_conductor` concludes the identity, used for the [class number bound](a-cyclotomic-subfield-class-number-bound.md).
