# Two qualitative annihilators of a Wach cokernel

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E6%99%B6%E4%BD%93%E6%A0%BC%E6%BB%A1%E5%B0%84%E7%9A%84Wach%E4%BD%99%E6%A0%B8%E5%8F%AF%E8%A2%AB%E4%B8%A4%E7%A7%8D%E5%B9%82%E6%9D%80%E6%8E%89.md)

Let \(\ell\) be prime, \(K/\mathbb Q_\ell\) local, \(s\ge1\) a cyclotomic level, and \(L/\mathbb Q_\ell\) a coefficient field containing the required roots, with \(O=\mathcal O_L\), in the project's good-level setting. Let \(0\to T_0\to T_1\xrightarrow{f}T_2\to0\) be exact in finite free \(O\)-lattices of \(G_K\) whose \(L\)-representations are crystalline. Put \(A=\prod_{i\in\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z}O[[U]]\), \(X=(1+U)^{\ell^{s-1}}-1\), \(Q=\Phi_{\ell^s}(1+U)\), and let \(E\) be the \(\ell\)-adic completion after inverting \(U\). For the Wach lattices \(N_i\) the claim is
$$
\exists c\ge0\quad \ell^cN_2\subset f(N_1),\qquad U^c N_2\subset f(N_1).
$$
The period model supplies the surjection on étale modules, \(E\otimes_A N_i=D_i\), compatibility of \(f\) with \(\varphi,\gamma\), freeness of the Wach lattices and \((\gamma-1)N_i\subset UN_i\). In both Frobenius directions, a power of \(Q\) puts each vector into the other module, between \(N_i\) and \(A\varphi(N_i)\). The period comparison also gives inclusions after powers of \(X\) between free lattices with these Frobenius properties and the same étale module.

**Proof.** Put \(P=f(N_1)\), and double-saturate it inside \(N_2\) to obtain \(S=\{x:\ell^kx,U^kx\in P\text{ for some }k\}\). The [double-saturation lemma](double-saturation-of-a-wach-image-is-free.md) gives freeness and \(E\otimes_A S=D_2\).

For \(x\in S\), \(\gamma(x)\in S\) because \(\gamma\) fixes \(\ell\), is stable on \(P\) and sends \(U\) to \(U\) times a unit. Write \((\gamma-1)x=Uy\) with \(y\in N_2\). If \(\ell^k x=f(a)\), write \((\gamma-1)a=Ua'\). Then \(U\ell^ky=Uf(a')\), so \(\ell^k y\in P\) by cancellation. Also \(U^{k+1}y=U^k(\gamma-1)x\in P\). Hence \(y\in S\) and \((\gamma-1)S\subset US\).

Fix \(x\in S\). The \(b\in A\) for which \(Q^j b\varphi(x)\in P\) for some \(j\ge0\) form an ideal \(J\), since exponents can be increased before addition. For \(k\) as in the definition of \(S\), the Frobenius property on the source gives \(\ell^k,\varphi(U)^k\in J\). As \(\varphi(U)\equiv U^\ell\bmod\ell\), the radical contains \(U\), so \(U^b\in J\) for some \(b\ge0\). Choose \(j\) large enough that \(Q^j\varphi(x)\in N_2\) and both \(\ell^k\) and \(U^b\) send it into \(P\). Thus \(Q^j\varphi(x)\in S\).

In the other direction, the source property gives \(Q^j y\in A\varphi(S)\) for some \(j\) for every \(y\in P\). Apply it to \(\ell^k x\) and \(U^kx\) with a common \(j\). To recover \(Q^j x\), use the Frobenius images of an \(A\)-basis of \(S\) as a basis over \(E\). They span because the original basis spans \(D_2\) and the \(E\)-span of \(\varphi(D_2)\) contains \(N_2\), by its reverse Frobenius condition and invertibility of \(Q\) in \(E\). They are as numerous as the rank, hence a basis, by the determinant of a right-invertible square matrix over a commutative ring. Each coordinate \(e\in E\) of \(Q^j x\) then satisfies \(a=\ell^ke\in A\) and \(b=U^ke\in A\). The equality \(\ell^kE\cap A=\ell^k A\) gives \(a=\ell^k a_0\) in \(A\). From \(U^k a=\ell^kb\) cancel \(\ell^k\) in \(A\), then \(U^k\) in \(E\), to get \(e=a_0\in A\). This proves \(Q^j x\in A\varphi(S)\).

Now \(S\) has the required two Frobenius properties and cyclotomic congruence. Apply the comparison after inverting \(X\) to \(S,N_2\), then the [uniqueness argument](wach-lattices-coincide-after-cancelling-denominators.md). Thus \(S=N_2\). Taking the maximum of the exponents for a finite generating set gives the stated common \(c\). This \(c\) is qualitative. The [height recursion](two-wach-height-bounds-give-a-uniform-cokernel-bound.md) later bounds the required exponent using the filtration alone.

The stability proofs are `gam_sat`, `φD_satM_gen` and `Qpow_mem_φspan_satM`, with `mem_span_of_smul_mem` giving integral coordinates. The main result `coker_killed` uses `satM_free_and_spans` and `uniform`.
