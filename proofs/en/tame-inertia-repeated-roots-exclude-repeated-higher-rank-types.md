# Tame inertia repeated roots exclude repeated higher-rank types

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%B8%A9%E5%92%8C%E6%83%AF%E6%80%A7%E7%9A%84%E9%87%8D%E5%A4%8D%E6%A0%B9%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E6%8E%92%E9%99%A4%E9%AB%98%E7%A7%A9%E9%87%8D%E5%A4%8D%E7%B1%BB%E5%9E%8B.md)

## Statement

Let $K=E_w$ be a nonarchimedean number field completion, residue size $q$, with Weil group $W_K$, inertia $I_K$ and wild inertia $P_K$. Choose a tame generator $\gamma$ and degree-one Frobenius $\Phi$ with $\Phi\gamma\Phi^{-1}\equiv\gamma^q\bmod P_K$. Assume `IsRecT`. Let $\pi$ be an irreducible smooth generic representation of $\mathrm{GL}_n(K)$, $n\ge1$, with zero monodromy in $y=\operatorname{rec}(\pi)$.

1. Under `Tor`, that each $y([s,t])$ for $s,t\in W_K$ has polynomial $(X-1)^n$, the supercuspidal support is entirely rank one, with no pair $v,v'$ satisfying $v=v'\otimes|\det|$.
2. With $K\simeq\mathbb Q_p$, instead assume trivial wild inertia (`TmW`) and that every repeated root of $y(\gamma)$ at every tame generator satisfies $z^{q-1}=1$ (`Sqf`). Then $\pi$ has nonzero invariants under $1+\mathfrak m_KM_n(\mathcal O_K)$, the same nonadjacency condition holds in its support, and two occurrences of rank at least two cannot be unramified twists of one another.

The support is the multiset of supercuspidals in its inducing data. Generic means admitting a Whittaker model. The results are `plainTor_of_tor` and `mixed_of_tame`.

## Two Weil criteria

For an irreducible complex Weil representation with finite inertia image, assume all commutators of a Weil element and an inertia element have only eigenvalue one. They lie in inertia and have finite order, so are diagonalizable and must be identity. Thus each inertia operator commutes with the whole action. Its nonzero eigenspace is stable, hence the whole space, so inertia is scalar. A Frobenius eigenline is now stable under the Weil group and irreducibility forces rank one.

For a tame representation, if all roots of $r(\gamma)$ satisfy $z^{q-1}=1$, its finite-order matrix satisfies $r(\gamma)^q=r(\gamma)$. The Frobenius relation makes $r(\Phi)$ commute with it and all inertia. The full Weil image is abelian because it is generated by Frobenius and inertia. An irreducible such representation again has rank one.

## Application to the support

The generic segment classification writes $\pi$ as a Langlands quotient of unlinked segments. Its parameter has a nilpotent Jordan block of the segment length on each supercuspidal block. Zero monodromy gives length one everywhere. Thus
$$
y=\bigoplus_j\operatorname{rec}(v_j),\qquad v_j\ne v_k\otimes|\det|\quad\text{for all }j,k.
$$
Each summand's Weil part is irreducible of the rank of $v_j$. Under `Tor` its commutator polynomial divides the total $(X-1)^n$, giving rank one by the first criterion.

Under tame hypotheses each $v_j$ is tame. The [finite label proof](finite-field-labels-count-tame-supercuspidal-types.md) gives each a nonzero principal congruence invariant vector. The assumed depth-zero induction theorem gives this property for $\pi$ from its support. If two occurrences have $v_k=v_j\otimes(\mu\circ\det)$ with $\mu$ unramified and rank at least two, the depth-zero twist law gives equal characteristic polynomials $P$ on tame inertia. Hence $P^2$ divides the full polynomial at $\gamma$. Every root of $P$ is repeated there and satisfies the root-of-unity condition. The second Weil criterion makes this irreducible block rank one, a contradiction. This proves both support restrictions.
