# The second-embedding orbits complete the local sum

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix a served inert prime $p$ with nonzero Frobenius step $h=h_P(p)$ in the finite abelian group $H$ that labels embeddings. There are two copies $\{0,1\}\times H$, and Frobenius acts by adding $h$ in the second coordinate. Put $d=\operatorname{ord}(h)$ and let $R\subset H$ choose one representative of every coset of $\langle h\rangle$. Assume the local and global places are tied, with each local component $v$ having $d$ slots and an initial label $(\beta_v,r_v)$. The component $v_0(t)$ for $t\in R$ is the orbit of $(0,t)$. These are the representatives used for the operator estimate. The tied place condition expresses Frobenius on the slots, so slot $s$ is $(\beta_v,r_v+sh)$. This follows from the Frobenius shift on one step by induction on $s$, and the order of the shift gives the length $d$. [`orbit_positions`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/PlacesOffFirstOrbits.lean#L97)

The slot map is a bijection onto $\{0,1\}\times H$, and all slots of one component enumerate its orbit once. Therefore every component outside the image $v_0(R)$ belongs to the second copy. Each second-copy orbit has exactly one such component. For any $f:H\to\mathbb R$ invariant under translation by $h$, this proves
$$
\sum_{v\notin v_0(R)} f(r_v)=\sum_{t\in R} f(t).
$$
In the source, the second-copy components also have empty top set by the tied top-set condition. The relevant $f$ is $F_{\rm orb}-\mathrm{errF}$ at $(1,t)$ for a layer $j$, when $p\le X''(n)$, and zero otherwise. The gain $F_{\rm orb}$ is invariant under cyclic change of origin. The error $\mathrm{errF}$ uses the sum of the orbit weights and the number of top slots, so it is invariant by the same permutation. Let $D_p=v_p(D_\Gamma(u))$ be the word's normalization valuation, $m$ the number of layers, and $d_0,\ldots,d_{m-1}$ the actual local losses. Assume $d_0=0$ and $0\le d_j\le D_p$ for every $j$. Then $\sum_j d_j\le(m-1)D_p$. On a served inert prime, the gain and error definitions for the family give
$$
\ell=\mathrm{lowS}
=\sum_{j=1}^m {\bf1}_{p\le X''(n)}
  \bigl(F_{\rm inert}(j,p)-E_{\rm inert}(j,p)\bigr)-(m-1)D_p .
$$
Indeed the $m$ individual losses $-D_p$ and the first-layer saving $+D_p$ give the final term. The two-copy orbit comparison `sumI` says that the sum of the first-copy nonnegative floors $\max(g_0,0)$ and the second-copy $F_{\rm orb}-\mathrm{errF}$ dominates the same first-copy sum plus $F_{\rm inert}-E_{\rm inert}$. Canceling the common sum gives the second-copy bound by itself. This is the numerical inequality from the [orbit comparison](from-orbit-valuations-to-the-prime-power-count.md), applied at a served inert prime with both copies. Thus $\ell\le\sum_{j,v\notin v_0(R)}f_j(r_v)-\sum_j d_j$. Finally, assume a provisional admitted formal-group model on the chosen local chain at each served prime, for each used twist away from its excluded primes, with the $d_j$ bounds above. Also assume the filled weights and global orbit shape and the orbit coefficient theorem used in the [cofactor estimate](a-second-embedding-orbit-bounds-its-local-cofactors.md). For $p\le X''(n)$ that estimate supplies $f_j(r_v)$ on each complementary component, and for $p>X''(n)$ admitted integrality supplies the chosen zero bound. The sum just proved is the remaining requirement of `SysTw`. This proves `sysTw_sites`, the hypothesis that supplies the complementary terms in the [served product bound](selected-local-operators-and-ordinary-cofactors-give-a-product-bound.md). The construction of the provisional admitted local model is a separate input.
