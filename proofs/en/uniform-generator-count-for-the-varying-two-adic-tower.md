# A uniform generator count for the varying two-adic tower

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E4%BA%8C%E8%BF%9B%E5%A1%94%E7%9A%84%E7%94%9F%E6%88%90%E5%85%83%E6%95%B0%E8%83%BD%E7%8B%AC%E7%AB%8B%E4%BA%8E%E8%BE%85%E5%8A%A9%E7%B4%A0%E6%95%B0.md)

Let $K=\mathbb Q(\mu_m)^J$ for $m>0$, $J\le(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times$, and $b=[K:\mathbb Q]$. Write $K_p$ for the field fixed by square units in $\mathbb Q(\mu_p)$ for prime $p$. Let $q,d$ be primes with $q\equiv3\bmod8$, $d\equiv7\bmod16$, and $(qd,m)=1$. Put $L=K(i)K_qK_d$, $V_0=G_{K(\mu_{2^\infty})}$, and $V_L=G_{L(\mu_{2^\infty})}$, all in an algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q$. For $g\ge0$, if $C_2(V_0;1)$ is finite with at most $2^g$ elements, then $C_2(V_L;1)$ is finite and
$$
|C_2(V_L;1)|\le 2^{4(g+6b)} .
$$
Here $C_2(V;N)$ allows ramification only above $N$, so $N=1$ means no ramification. [`baseRk`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/IsAlgClosureRat.lean#L69)

**Proof.** Let $G=G_{\mathbb Q}$, $W=G_K$, $B_0=G_{\mathbb Q(\mu_{2^\infty})}$. Both subgroups are normal and $V_0=W\cap B_0$. At an odd prime $p$, $G$ acts transitively on valuations above $p$, and the stabilizer $D_A$ of one of them is the decomposition group. The number of $V_0$-orbits is $[G:V_0D_A]$. The cyclotomic image of $D_A$ in $\mathbb Z_2^\times$ is compact and contains $p$, by lifting arithmetic Frobenius and reading its action on $2$-power roots via reduction.

Let $a=v_2(p^2-1)$, equal to $3$ for $p=q$ and $4$ for $p=d$. Successive squaring of $p^2\in1+4\mathbb Z_2$ adds exactly one at a time to the valuation of its difference from $1$, because the sum with $1$ has valuation one. Thus $p^2$ generates $1+2^a\mathbb Z/2^n\mathbb Z$ for every $n>a$ by order count. The closed subgroup containing $p$ contains $1+2^a\mathbb Z_2$ and the nontrivial order-two class of $p$ modulo $2^a$. It has index at most $2^{a-2}$. This bounds $[G:B_0D_A]$ by $2$ or $4$. Also
$$
[B_0D_A:V_0D_A]=[B_0:B_0\cap V_0D_A]\le[B_0:B_0\cap W]\le b.
$$
The orbit counts are therefore at most $2b,4b$. For $p=q,d$, $(p,2m)=1$, so inertia above $p$ fixes all the $m$-th and $2$-power roots by the residue argument for prime-to-$p$ roots. Hence it lies in $V_0$. First use the [ramification-orbit count](one-character-coordinate-per-new-ramification-orbit.md) twice on $V_0$, allowing $q$ then $qd$, to obtain $|C_2(V_0;qd)|\le2^{g+2b+4b}$. Then adjoin $K_q,K_d$ in turn, keeping the parameter $qd$. The [index-two bound](an-index-two-extension-at-most-squares-the-character-count.md) applies twice since each prime divides $qd$. This gives $|C_2(V_L;qd)|\le 2^{4(g+6b)}$, and imposing full unramifiedness only takes a subspace. Adjoining $i$ has no effect over $\mu_{2^\infty}$. [`ineTwo`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/IneTwo.lean#L29)

**Generator bound.** Assume that for each prime $\ell$ and finite abelian $F/\mathbb Q$ containing primitive $\ell$-th roots (and $i$ at $\ell=2$), some $g\ge0$ satisfies $|\mathrm{Cl}(F_n)/\ell\mathrm{Cl}(F_n)|\le\ell^g$ at every cyclotomic layer $F_n=F(\mu_{\ell^n})$, $n\ge0$. Apply this at the fixed $K(i)$ with $\ell=2$. The [finite-layer argument](how-finite-layer-class-groups-bound-tower-generators.md) gives the initial bound on $C_2(V_0;1)$. Let $X_N$ be the abelian pro-$2$ tower module over $L(\mu_{2^\infty})$ everywhere unramified and split above primes dividing $N$, dual to the corresponding $\mathbb Q_2/\mathbb Z_2$-valued character group. The dual of $X_1/2X_1$ is the space counted above, and the dual for $X_2/2X_2$ imposes additional vanishing on decomposition groups above $2$. Both dimensions are at most $R=4(g+6b)$. Lift generators from modulo $2$ by successive approximations in the tower module to get generation by $R$ elements over $\mathbb Z_2$. This proves the `IsTower` conditions: $L$ totally imaginary, $i\in L$, $R\ge1$, and the bound for $X_2$, uniformly in $q,d$.

`abRk`, `genLE_of_abRk` and `towerTwo` supply this conclusion, and `twoIn` takes it to the window argument, which separately uses `TowerBound`. The class group input named `Published.FerreroWashington` is implemented as `FW.ferreroWashington`.
