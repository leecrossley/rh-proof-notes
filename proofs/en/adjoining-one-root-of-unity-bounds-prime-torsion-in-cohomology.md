# Adjoining roots of unity bounds prime torsion in cohomology

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E5%8A%A0%E4%B8%80%E5%B1%82%E5%8D%95%E4%BD%8D%E6%A0%B9%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E6%95%B0%E5%87%BA%E8%A2%AB%E7%B4%A0%E6%95%B0%E6%B6%88%E5%8E%BB%E7%9A%84%E4%B8%8A%E5%90%8C%E8%B0%83%E7%B1%BB.md)

Let \(E\) embed over \(\mathbb Q\) into \(\mathbb Q(\mu_N)\), \(N>0\), and let \(\ell\) be prime, \(e\in\mathbb Z\). Let \(S\) be the finite places of \(E\) above \(\ell\), \(G_{E,S}\) unramified at other finite places, and \(W(-e)=\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell\) discrete with action \(\varepsilon_\ell^{-e}\). Then \(H^1(G_{E,S},W(-e))[\ell]\) is a finite \(\mathbb F_\ell\)-space of dimension \(g\le8\ell[E:\mathbb Q]\log(\ell N)\).

**Normalized torsion representatives.** Put \(K=E(\mu_\ell)\), \(m=[K:\mathbb Q]\). The degree \(d=[K:E]\) divides \(\ell-1\), \(K\subset\mathbb Q(\mu_{\ell N})\), and \(m\le\ell[E:\mathbb Q]\). For a class \(x\) killed by \(\ell\), let \(z\) represent it and write \(\ell z=df_0\). Choose \(f_1\in W\) with \(\ell f_1=f_0\); then \(z-df_1\) is killed by \(\ell\) pointwise. Restrict the pulled-back representative to \(G_K\); the action on \(W[\ell]\) there is trivial, so it gives a continuous \(\lambda_x:G_K\to\mathbb F_\ell\) unramified away from \(\ell\). Choosing a representative for each \(x\) gives a set injection. Indeed if restrictions agree, their difference vanishes on \(G_K\) and descends as a cocycle of the finite group of order \(d\), taking values in the \(G_K\)-fixed module by normality. Averaging the cocycle equation over that group makes it a coboundary because \(d\) is invertible. The coboundary equality descends to \(G_{E,S}\), so the classes agree. **Kummer count.** Write \(S_K\) for the places of \(K\) above \(\ell\), and choose a primitive \(\ell\)-th root \(\zeta\in K\). Hilbert 90 applied to \(\zeta^{\lambda(g)}\) gives \(g(\beta)/\beta=\zeta^{\lambda(g)}\), \(\beta^\ell=a\in K^\times\). The class \(a\bmod K^{\times\ell}\) determines the character and is independent of \(\beta\), because a second choice differs by a \(K\)-factor, and \(\mu_\ell\subset K\). At \(v\notin S_K\), \(\beta\) is fixed by inertia so its valuation is an integer in the maximal unramified extension, giving \(\ell\mid v(a)\). Denote the subgroup of such \(a\) by \(\mathcal S\). There is an exact sequence
$$
1\longrightarrow \mathcal O_{K,S_K}^\times/(\mathcal O_{K,S_K}^\times)^\ell
\longrightarrow\mathcal S/K^{\times\ell}\longrightarrow\mathrm{Cl}_{S_K}(K)[\ell]\longrightarrow1 .
$$
The right-hand map takes the ideal away from \(S_K\) with exponents \(v(a)/\ell\). Its class is killed by \(\ell\); and any such class has an \(\ell\)-th power principal away from \(S_K\), so lifts. A zero class means division by an \(\ell\)-th power leaves an \(S_K\)-unit. If an \(S_K\)-unit is an \(\ell\)-th power, its root is an \(S_K\)-unit by valuations, giving left injectivity. Here \(\mathrm{Cl}_{S_K}(K)\) is the ideal class group modulo the prime classes in \(S_K\).

The \(S_K\)-unit theorem bounds the left size by \(\ell^{r_1+r_2+|S_K|}\le\ell^{2m}\), from free rank \(r_1+r_2-1+|S_K|\) and the cyclic torsion of roots of unity. The right size is at most the class number \(h_K\). Thus \(\ell^g\le\ell^{2m}h_K\), proving finiteness. By the [class number estimate](a-cyclotomic-subfield-class-number-bound.md), \(\log h_K\le3m\log(\ell N)\). Hence \(g\log\ell\le5m\log(\ell N)\). Convexity of \(1/x\) gives \(\log2\ge2/3>5/8\); divide and use \(m\le\ell[E:\mathbb Q]\). The result controls all layers in the prime-power torsion filtration.
