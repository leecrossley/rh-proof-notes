# The standard theta jet bound at three

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Keep the hypotheses of [the curve model at three](an-integral-cm-model-and-phase-at-three.md). Let $R=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_3}$, $\ell_W,h_W=\ell_W^{-1}$ its formal logarithm and inverse, and $\phi_W$ the modified sigma exponent. Fix a complex period $\omega_0\in L_0$ such that neither $\omega(\omega_0/2)$ nor $\bar\omega(\omega_0/2)$ belongs to $L_0$, for $\omega=(1+\sqrt{-d})/2$. In particular $P_0=\omega_0/2$ represents a nonzero two-torsion class. Let $\theta_0$ be the normalized theta with its simple zero at the half period $P_0$. Let $s_{k,j}$ be shifts in the complex plane indexed by two finite sets, and assume a common annihilating integer $N>0$ prime to $3$ satisfies $Ns_{k,j}\in L_0$ for every $k,j$. For integral coefficients $r_k$, consider
$$
\Theta(Z)=\sum_k r_k\prod_j
 \iota\big(\operatorname{Taylor}_0(U(s_{k,j})\theta_0)\big)(Z_j).
$$
Transport is coefficientwise. Then $\Theta$ is jet-integral. The exact two conditions on $\omega_0$ and the $d$ congruences are the interface `ThetaZJetsThree`; the argument here needs the consequent nonzero half period and the curve hypotheses already established.

For each shift $s=s_{k,j}$, write $F_s$ for its one-variable additive series in the display. The ordinary translated-sigma estimate says that $\exp(\phi_W(t)) F_s(\ell_W(t))$ is integral in $t$ whenever $p$ is odd, the model is good, $P_0$ is nonzero with order $2$, and $s$ has order prime to $p$. In the source this is `intS_exp_mul_translate`. It uses the standard theta as a normalized translate of the modified sigma, with normalization the inverse of the sigma translate's constant at $P_0$. That constant is a unit by the finite torsion cycle argument. The Heisenberg product of translation by $s$ and by $P_0$ changes the combined translate by an exponential of the alternating pairing, a root of unity for the two torsion shifts. The combined shift has order dividing $2N$. The ordinary bound is the integral sigma estimate for the combined translate, using the integral torsion coordinates. It also covers the case of the zero class, where the corrected unshifted sigma is the formal parameter. This is the same shifted sigma product mechanism as [the torsion recurrence](closing-the-torsion-translation-recurrence.md), applied in the normalized integral model $W$. In the formal coordinate $-x/y$ of $W$ here, $a_1=a_3=0$, so inversion is $t\mapsto-t$ and the same paired addition calculation uses the integral coordinates of the translated cubic.

Form the finite sum of products of these corrected series in the parameters $t_j$, with the same coefficients $r_k$. It is ordinarily integral. Substitution $t_j=h_W(Z_j)$ preserves factorial-weighted jets by the invariant derivative argument. The resulting series is $\Theta(Z)\prod_j\exp(\phi_W(h_W(Z_j)))$. Every jet of $\phi_W\circ h_W$ is integral by the model-and-phase result. Thus $\prod_j\exp(-\phi_W(h_W(Z_j)))$ is jet-integral too. Multiplying yields $\Theta$ and proves the assertion.

In the host application, the fourfold isometry expresses four copies of the original translated theta as such a finite sum after transporting to the standard lattice, with coefficients integral at $3$. The two half-period nonmembership conditions for the first CM period are proved using the coefficients $1$ and $-1$ of $\omega$ in a basis for that lattice. The [fourfold frame comparison](the-fourfold-frame-gram-matrix-modulo-the-cm-root.md) then transports the jet bound to integral lattice directions, and repeated fourth jets recover the single-copy bound.
