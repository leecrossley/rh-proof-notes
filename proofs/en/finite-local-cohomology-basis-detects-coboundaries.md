# A finite cohomology basis detects quotient-lattice coboundaries

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E5%B1%80%E9%83%A8%E6%9C%89%E9%99%90%E4%B8%8A%E5%90%8C%E8%B0%83%E7%9A%84%E5%9F%BA%E8%83%BD%E6%A3%80%E6%B5%8B%E5%95%86%E6%A0%BC%E4%BD%99%E8%BE%B9%E7%95%8C.md)

**Proposition.** Let $\mathcal O$ be a DVR with fraction field $K$, let $D$ be a group, and let $V$ be a $K$-linear representation with stable $\mathcal O$-submodule $L$ spanning $V$ over $K$. Write $q:V\to M=V/L$. Let $F\subseteq H^1(D,L)$ be saturated, so $c\xi\in F$ with $c\ne0$ in $\mathcal O$ implies $\xi\in F$. Let $\kappa_1,\ldots,\kappa_j$ be $L$-valued cocycles whose classes give a basis of $F$ modulo torsion. If $x_a\in K$ and $g\mapsto q(\sum_a x_a\kappa_a(g))$ is a coboundary in $M$, then every $x_a\in\mathcal O$.

Write that coboundary as $gm-m$, lift $m$ to $v\in V$, and put $z(g)=\sum_a x_a\kappa_a(g)-(gv-v)$. This is an $L$-valued cocycle. Take a common denominator $c\ne0$ with $cx_a\in\mathcal O$ and $cv\in L$, using $KL=V$. In $H^1(D,L)$ one has $c[z]=\sum_a(cx_a)[\kappa_a]$ because $g(cv)-cv$ is now an $L$-valued coboundary. Saturation gives $[z]\in F$. Write its class modulo torsion with coefficients $b_a\in\mathcal O$ in the stated basis. Comparing coordinates gives $cx_a=cb_a$, hence $x_a\in\mathcal O$.

In block zero let $v_1\in L$ give a surjection $K/\mathcal O\to e_1 M$ by $x+\mathcal O\mapsto q(xv_1)$, with $e_1$ idempotent. At column $l$, suppose $K_{la}(g)v_1=\ell^{e'_l}\kappa_a(g)$ with actions commuting with $K$. If $m_a\in e_1M$ give a coboundary $\sum_a K_{la}(g)m_a$, write $m_a=q(x_av_1)$. The preceding proposition applied to $\ell^{e'_l}x_a$ gives $\ell^{e'_l}m_a=0$. This supplies the [column kernel bound](column-relations-bound-the-kernel-in-three-blocks.md). In the full column relation, the sum of the evaluated cocycle and the coboundary from $n_l$ equals $\Delta\ell^{e'_l}\sum_a q(x_a\kappa_a(g))$ in this block. Passing to cohomology removes the coboundary and leaves a class from the finite submodule under the coefficient maps. The project's finite column condition includes these images.
