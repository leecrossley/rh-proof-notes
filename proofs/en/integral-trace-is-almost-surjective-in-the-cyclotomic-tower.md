# Integral trace is almost surjective in the cyclotomic tower

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E5%88%86%E5%9C%86%E5%A1%94%E9%A1%B6%E4%B8%8A%E7%9A%84%E6%95%B4%E6%95%B0%E8%BF%B9%E5%87%A0%E4%B9%8E%E6%BB%A1%E5%B0%84.md)

## Statement

Let $\ell$ be prime, $K_0/\mathbb Q_\ell$ finite unramified, $K_i=K_0(\mu_{\ell^i})$ and $K_\infty=\bigcup_{i\ge1}K_i$ inside $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. For every finite $E/K_\infty$ there and $a\in K_\infty$ with $|a|_\ell<1$, there is $b\in E$ with $|b|_\ell\le1$ and $\operatorname{Tr}_{E/K_\infty} b=a$.

## A product lower bound for conjugate separations

Fix integral $y$ with set $Y$ of $\mathbb Q_\ell$-conjugates and let $c_y=\prod_{z\in Y\setminus\{y\}}|y-z|_\ell>0$. If $y=P(x)$ for integral $x$ and $P\in\mathbb Z_\ell[X]$, and the $\sigma_k x$ are distinct with $\sigma_k y\ne y$, then
$$
c_y\le\prod_k|x-\sigma_k x|_\ell. \tag{1}
$$
Indeed $\prod_{z\ne y}(P(X)-z)$ vanishes at each of those points. Divide by their monic linear factors successively over the integer ring. The remaining factor is integral at $x$, giving (1).

## Separating conjugates at a high layer

Fix finite $E_0/\mathbb Q_\ell$ generated by integral $y$, with $n$ conjugates, and put $E_i=E_0K_i$. Choose $x$ generating $O_{E_i}$ over $\mathbb Z_\ell$ by [integral monogeneity](one-element-generates-the-ring-of-integers.md). Both $y$ and a primitive $\ell^i$th root $\zeta_i$ are integral polynomials in $x$.

For $0\le\delta<1$ and sufficiently large $i$, any $\sigma$ fixing $K_i$ but moving $y$ has $|x-\sigma x|_\ell\ge\delta$. Otherwise fix $D\ge n$ with $\delta^D\le|\ell|_\ell$ and suppose the difference is below $\delta$. Let $S$ represent the embeddings of $E_i/K_i$ and $f(X)=\prod_{s\in S}(X-sx)$, integral over $K_i$, with $|S|\le n$. Write $\omega=\zeta_i^\ell$. For $1\le k<\ell^{i-1}$ choose $\tau_k$ over $K_1$ with $\tau_k\zeta_i=\zeta_i\omega^k$. On $z\in O_i$, $|\tau_k z-z|_\ell\le|1-\omega^k|_\ell$, by the [integral bases](trace-decomposition-and-the-cyclotomic-tate-operator.md) expressing $z$ in powers of $\zeta_i$ over the unramified base and the unit power-difference bound. Apply this to the coefficients of $f$ and evaluate at $x$, where $f(x)=0$. This gives $\prod_{s\in S}|x-\tau_ksx|_\ell\le|1-\omega^k|_\ell$. Choosing the smallest factor at $s_k$ and using $D\ge|S|$ gives $|x-\tau_ks_kx|_\ell^D\le|1-\omega^k|_\ell$.

If $\tau_ks_k$ moves $y$, use $g_k=\tau_ks_k$. Otherwise use $g_k=\sigma\tau_ks_k$, which moves $y$ and has the same value on $\zeta_i$. The ultrametric inequality bounds $|x-g_kx|_\ell^D$ by the maximum of the above factor and $|x-\sigma x|_\ell^D$, hence by $|1-\omega^k|_\ell$, since $|\ell|_\ell\le|1-\omega^k|_\ell$ by the cyclotomic Eisenstein valuation. The $g_kx$ are distinct because their values on the polynomial $\zeta_i$ are distinct. Apply (1):
$$
c_y^D\le\prod_{1\le k<\ell^{i-1}}|x-g_kx|_\ell^D
\le\prod_{1\le k<\ell^{i-1}}|1-\omega^k|_\ell
=|\ell|_\ell^{i-1}.
$$
This is impossible for sufficiently large $i$, uniformly over $x$ and the embeddings.

## Large traces and the limit layer

For distinct $s,t\in S$, $s^{-1}t$ moves $y$, so the difference of $sx,tx$ has norm at least $\delta$. Fix $s_0$ and let $R(X)=\prod_{s\ne s_0}(X-sx)$, with integral coefficients. Then
$$
\delta^{|S|-1}\le |R(s_0 x)|_\ell
=\left|\sum_{s\in S}R(sx)\right|_\ell
\le\max_{0\le k<|S|}\left|\sum_{s\in S}(sx)^k\right|_\ell .
$$
Choose a maximizing $k$ and $b_0=x^k$. Its trace from $E_i$ has norm at least $\delta^n$. Given $0<\alpha<1$, choose $\delta<1$ with $\delta^n\ge\alpha$ and then take $i$ sufficiently large.

For the given $E/K_\infty$, choose its embedding representatives $S$ over $K_\infty$. Pick finitely many elements of $E$ separating their restrictions and let $E_0$ be the finite base field they generate over $\mathbb Q_\ell$. At large $i$, $S$ is also the embedding representative set for $E_0K_i/K_i$. In fact the finite union of open cosets $sG_{E_0}$ contains $G_{K_\infty}$, so the decreasing compact groups $G_{K_i}$ eventually lie inside it. The cosets are distinct by the separating elements. For $a\ne0$ find $b_0$ above with $\alpha=|a|_\ell$. Its trace $t=\sum_{s\in S}s b_0$ is simultaneously the trace from $E$ and has $|t|_\ell\ge |a|_\ell$. Thus $b=(a/t)b_0$ is integral with trace $a$. For zero take zero.
