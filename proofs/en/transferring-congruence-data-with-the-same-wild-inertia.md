# Transferring congruence data with the same wild inertia element

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E6%8A%8A%E5%90%8C%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E9%87%8E%E6%83%AF%E6%80%A7%E5%85%83%E5%92%8C%E5%90%8C%E4%BD%99%E6%95%B0%E6%8D%AE%E9%80%81%E7%BB%99%E4%BB%BB%E6%84%8FHecke%E8%A1%A8%E7%A4%BA%E6%97%8F.md)

Fix project data $N$ and a local index $i$. Let $X=N.X(i)$ be the finite evaluation set, $Z=N.Z(i)$ the good subset, $\mathcal O=N.O(i)$ the DVR, $k$ its fraction field, $\ell=N.l(i)$, $G=\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/E)$, and $T=N.T(i)\subseteq\mathcal O^X$ the Hecke algebra. Fix character lines $\chi_1,\ldots,\chi_t:G\to\mathcal O^\times$, full dimension $n$ and complement rank $r=n-t$. The condition `GoodR` means each full representation is continuous, semisimple over $k$, and agrees with the Hecke polynomials at the stipulated split Frobenius elements.

**Proposition.** Assume the project Chebotarev input and a family of full and complement representations $r_{0,x},\rho_{0,x}$ satisfying `GoodR` and `TakenWild` below. For any new `GoodR` family $r_x$, any integral complement family $\rho_x$, and $\pi\in Z$ with $r_\pi\simeq\rho_\pi\oplus\bigoplus_j\chi_j$ over $k$, there are `GoodCongruenceObjects` for this pair. One may retain the same $g_0$ chosen at $\pi$ for the original family. It belongs to inertia at some place over $\ell$ and has cyclotomic value $1+\ell^{s_0}u$ with $s_0=N.s0(i)$ and $u\in\mathbb Z_\ell^\times$. If $\ell$ is either of the two distinguished primes of the data, it is in wild inertia at every place over $\ell$ where it belongs to inertia.

## The input `TakenWild`

There is a DVR $A$ finite over $\mathbb Z_\ell$ embedded in $\mathcal O$, with integral models $r_A,\rho_A,\chi_A$ of the full representations over all points, the complements at good points, and the character lines. There is a local and point-separating function algebra $T_A\subseteq A^X$ spanning $T$ over $\mathcal O$. The fraction-field $r_{A,x}$ are sums of pairwise nonisomorphic absolutely irreducible representations. Their group characteristic polynomial coefficients belong to $T_A$ and have equal residual values across $X$. There is a character decomposition at each good point with absolutely irreducible residual complement.

At each $\pi\in Z$ there are $g_0,L,e_B,e_1,X_0$, with $g_0$ as in the proposition. The first and second projections are orthogonal idempotents in the integral group span projecting at $\pi$ to the character part and a line in the complement respectively. With $\varpi_A$ a uniformizer, $X_0=e_B r_A(g_0)e_B+\varpi_A^L S_\pi$ where $S_\pi$ is a corner correction vanishing at $\pi$. At each point the corner characteristic polynomial has $t$ distinct roots and splits over $k$. Its coefficients are integral and the roots therefore lie in $\mathcal O$. ## Transfer to the new family

At the original good point, order the roots and enlarge the function ring by the root functions. The [normalized block ring construction](normalizing-the-block-ring-at-a-good-point.md) gives the integral determinant properties, the surjective complement block evaluation and the root difference product for the rank-one spectral operator. This gives the original `GoodCongruenceObjects` with the given $g_0$. [Construction](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/CharacterValuesInjectiveLength.lean#L225)

The two families read the same Frobenius polynomials. The [semisimple comparison](hecke-polynomials-determine-semisimple-representations.md) therefore gives the intertwining matrices $P_x$. At $\pi$ their two decompositions share the character lines, so [canceling them](cancelling-common-character-summands.md) gives a complement intertwiner $Q$. This uses the complement only at $\pi$. The [integral transfer under conjugacy](integral-congruence-objects-under-pointwise-conjugacy.md) then preserves the projection and corner properties, the root algebra, block evaluation surjectivity and determinants. All inertia and cyclotomic conditions refer to the same element of $G$ and are unchanged by transfer. The result applies the [lattice index comparison](lattice-index-from-column-relations-and-cohomology.md) to whichever representation family the local conditions later require.
