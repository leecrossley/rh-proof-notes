# Transferring a Kummer value from Frobenius to inertia

[Chinese proof](../zh/Kummer%E5%8F%96%E5%80%BC%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E4%BB%8E%E6%97%A7Frobenius%E4%BC%A0%E5%88%B0%E6%96%B0%E6%83%AF%E6%80%A7.md)

Use \(K_N=\mathbb Q(\mu_N)\), \(M,F>0\) with \(M\mid F\), \(G=G_{\mathbb Q}\), \(U=G_{K_F}\), and a primitive \(M\)-th root \(\zeta\). Fix \(\delta\) acting on \(\mu_M\) by \(a\ge0\), and put \(P(x)=\delta(x)/x^a\). For prime \(r\) and prime parameter \(\ell\), the admissible \(d>0\) are squarefree products of primes \(p\equiv1\bmod F\), \(p\ne r\), with \(\ell\nmid\operatorname{ord}_p(r)\). For each \(d\) take \(x_d\in K_{Fd}^\times\) a unit away from \(r\). For \(qd\) admissible with \(q\nmid d\), assume \(s=s(d,q)\ge0\) prime to \(\ell\), and for each \(\tau\) fixing \(K_{Fd}\) and generating the action on \(\mu_q\), and each \(\Phi\) acting by \(q\) on \(\mu_{Fd}\),
$$
\prod_{i=0}^{q-2}\tau^i(x_{qd})=(x_d/\Phi^{-1}x_d)^s,\qquad
x_{qd}\equiv(\Phi^{-1}x_d)^s
$$
at every place above \(q\) for the congruence. These are `Sys`. Take the commuting operators \(D_p(x)=\prod_{i=0}^{p-2}\tau_p^i(x)^i\) with \(\tau_p\) fixing roots of order prime to \(p\) and generating the action on \(\mu_p\). For a list \(q::L\) of distinct admissible primes, \(d=\prod_{p\in L}p\), set \(y=D_L(x_d)\), \(w=D_L(x_{qd})\), \(v=D_q(w)\). Their ratios \(g(y)/y,g(v)/v\), \(g\in U\), have \(M\)-th roots in their respective cyclotomic fields, as in the [derivative proof](the-auxiliary-ratio-already-has-a-root-in-the-cyclotomic-field.md). Use the [Kummer character formula](additive-characters-from-kummer-root-ratios.md)
$$
\zeta^{\kappa_L(g)}=g(P(Y_y))/(P(Y_y)P(z_g))
\quad(Y_y^M=y,\ z_g^M=g(y)/y)
$$
and correspondingly for \(v\). At any chosen place over \(q\), let \(\Phi\) lift arithmetic Frobenius and \(t_q\) belong to inertia with the same action as \(\tau_q\) on \(\mu_q\); such a lift exists by total ramification. Then
$$
\kappa_{q::L}(t_q)=-s\kappa_L(\Phi)\quad\bmod M.
$$

**Proof of transfer.** Frobenius acts by \(q\) on \(\mu_{Fd}\) and belongs to \(U\); \(t_q\) fixes \(K_{Fd}\) and agrees with \(\tau_q\) on \(K_{Fqd}\). Applying the commuting \(D_L\) to the two assumptions gives
$$
\prod_{i=0}^{q-2}\tau_q^i(w)=(y/\Phi^{-1}y)^s,\qquad
w/(\Phi^{-1}y)^s\equiv1
$$
at all places above \(q\), as the operators permute these places. From the cancellation in \(D_q\), \(t_q(v)/v=w^{q-1}(\Phi^{-1}y/y)^s\). Put \(k=(q-1)/M\) and choose \(Z^M=\Phi^{-1}y/y\) in \(K_{Fd}\); the explicit root is \(w^kZ^s\). Divide the Kummer formula for \(v,t_q\) by the \(s\)-th power of that for \(y,\Phi^{-1}\). Using \(P\) multiplicative and \((\Phi^{-1}P(Y_y))^M=P(\Phi^{-1}y)\), the ratio is
$$
\frac{t_q(P(Y_v))}{P(Y_v)}
\left(\frac{P(Y_y)}{(\Phi^{-1}P(Y_y))^q}\right)^s
 P\!\left(\frac{w}{(\Phi^{-1}y)^s}\right)^{-k}.
$$
The first factor reduces to \(1\) by inertia on units, the second by arithmetic Frobenius on units, and the last by the congruence at every place, which stays true after applying \(\delta\). All the roots used are units at these places by their powers. This ratio is in \(\mu_M\), where reduction is injective since \(q\nmid M\). Hence it equals \(1\), giving \(\kappa_{q::L}(t_q)=s\kappa_L(\Phi^{-1})=-s\kappa_L(\Phi)\). **Initial inertia value.** Suppose \(r\nmid F\), \(y\in K_F^\times\) satisfies the root conditions, and at a fixed place \(v_r(r)=1\) suppose \(v_r(\sigma_t P(y))=h(t)\in\mathbb Z\) for all lifts \(\sigma_t\) of \(t\bmod F\). Take \(\rho^M=r\) and \(t_r\) in inertia with \(t_r(\rho)=\zeta\rho\), possible because \(X^M-r\) is Eisenstein over the unramified \(\mathbb Q_r(\mu_M)\), and the extension generated by a root there is totally ramified and Galois. For \(A=\sigma_tP(Y_y)\), \(B=A/\rho^{h(t)}\) is a unit with \(B^M\in K_F\). The inertia ratio \(t_r(B)/B\in\mu_M\) is \(1\) on reduction, hence is \(1\). Therefore \(t_r(A)/A=\zeta^{h(t)}\). In the character formula for \(g=\sigma_t^{-1}t_r\sigma_t\), \(g\) fixes \(K_F\) so take \(z_g=1\), then apply \(\sigma_t\). This gives
$$
[t]\kappa_\varnothing(\sigma_t^{-1}t_r\sigma_t)=h(t)\bmod M .
$$

**The full system.** In the application \(\ell\) is odd, \(M=\ell^m\), \(m\ge1\), \(F=N_0M\) with \((N_0,\ell)=1\), and \(r\equiv1\bmod F\) is prime. Take \(0\le b<F\), \(O\) the integers of a finite extension of \(\mathbb Q_\ell\), \(W=\operatorname{Frac}(O)/O\), \(w_0\in W[M]\), and \(\alpha:G\to O^\times\) trivial modulo \(M\) on \(U\), with sign \(-1\) on complex conjugation. Choose \(\delta\) with \(\alpha(\delta)-\varepsilon_\ell(\delta)\) a unit, let \(a\) represent \(\varepsilon_\ell(\delta)\bmod M\), \(d_0\) its exponent on \(\mu_F\), and \(\beta=\varepsilon_\ell\alpha^{-1}\). Put \(\theta_b(t)=\lfloor b[t]/F\rfloor-\lfloor b[-t]/F\rfloor\), \(z=\sum_t\theta_b(t)\alpha(\sigma_t)^{-1}\).

The [norm and congruence construction](norms-and-congruences-for-auxiliary-cyclotomic-elements.md) gives \(X_d\) and \(s_0(d,q)\) with the same conditions as `Sys` except for factors in \(\mu_{2Fqd}\) in both formulas, uniform across the places for the congruence, and \(v_r(\sigma_t X_1)=\theta_b(t)\). Set \(x_d=P(X_d)^{2N_0d}\). The power \(2N_0qd\) takes the extra factors into \(\mu_M\), which \(P\) kills. Since \(P\) commutes with Galois in these abelian fields, the two relations become exactly `Sys` with \(s(d,q)=q s_0(d,q)\), still prime to \(\ell\). The derivative lemma gives the roots at every list. With \(\nu_L(g)=\sum_t\kappa_L(\sigma_t^{-1}g\sigma_t)\beta(\sigma_t)w_0\), the [weighted character proof](additive-characters-from-kummer-root-ratios.md) gives additivity, continuity, annihilation by \(M\), conjugation law \(\beta\), and unramifiedness outside \(Frd\). Apply transfer at each conjugate place, using that the conjugates of \(t_q\) have the same action on \(\mu_q\). Every term is multiplied by the same unit, giving \(\nu_{q::L}(t_q)=-q s_0(d,q)\nu_L(\Phi)\).

For the empty list, \(y=P(X_1)^{2N_0}\), so applying \(P\) once more gives
$$
h(t)=2N_0\bigl(\theta_b(td_0^2)-2a\theta_b(td_0)+a^2\theta_b(t)\bigr).
$$
The initial inertia formula lets us replace the term in the weighted sum by \(h(t)\alpha(\sigma_t)^{-1}w_0\), since \([t]\equiv\varepsilon_\ell(\sigma_t)\bmod M\). Reindex \(t\) by \(td_0^j\) for \(j=0,1,2\). Lifts of that exponent agree with \(\sigma_t\delta^j\) modulo \(U\), so the reindexed weighted coefficient sum is \(\alpha(\delta)^j z\) modulo \(M\). Thus \(\nu_\varnothing(t_r)=2N_0(\alpha(\delta)-a)^2 z w_0\), a unit times \(zw_0\) since \(\alpha(\delta)-a\) is a unit and \(\ell\nmid2N_0\). These are the two values needed for [the length induction](auxiliary-primes-bound-the-cohomology-length-by-one-valuation.md).
