# The normalized kernel has a common ramified Gaussian

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix the tied kernel family of [the kernel reading](reading-the-actual-kernel-as-an-integral-theta-combination.md), with its readout hypotheses and adapted bases. Fix a word, twist, stage $i<m$, pair of lattice classes, and level-free coefficient place $w$ in class `ramD`. Then $p=\operatorname{resChar}(w)=d$ is the host discriminant prime, with $d\equiv7\bmod8$. Let $L,L'$ be the two admitted lattices and take the adapted bases $P,P'$ at $w$. By the [basis comparison](one-normalization-for-the-inert-kernel-family.md#applying-the-result-to-the-indexed-kernel-family), they are standard local bases and their substitution gives the actual kernel series $\mathrm{Th}$. Assume the normalized theta lattice package at this coefficient embedding. Its relevant data are a self-dual complex period lattice stable under CM, the natural theta section on it, and a fourfold isometry to the standard lattice. The indices of the source and image period lattices over their intersection are prime to $p$, and its theta normalization scalar is a $p$-adic unit. The package is `LatAt` / `LocNorm`. It applies to every admitted pair. Assume also the host field pair and generator conditions of [the ramified frame proof](the-standard-dual-bases-give-the-ramified-paired-form.md), with $|c_\tau|=1$ at this prime.

Then
$$
\mathrm{Th}(X)=\exp\!\left(-S\sum_{t,a,b}u_tX^-_{t,a,b}X^+_{t,a,b}\right)G(X)
$$
where $G$ is jet-integral, $|S|_p\le p^{1/2}$, and $|u_t|_p\le1$. This is the exact `ShapeD` needed for [the discriminant-prime loss](the-half-valuation-loss-at-the-discriminant-prime.md).

Use $L_0=\delta I$ for the normalized CM period lattice of the host elliptic curve. Since $p=d\ge7$, its short model is integral with unit discriminant by [the discriminant normalization](discriminant-normalization-gives-a-good-model-for-p-at-least-five.md). The CM multiplier proof in [the quadratic phase calculation](why-only-the-second-curve-jet-can-lose-integrality.md) gives a coefficient $S=(D^2\phi)(0)$ with $|S|\le p^{1/2}$, for the same curve at every matrix rank and lattice pair. The possible Gram error in the frame calculation is divisible by $\delta_0=\iota(\sqrt{-d})$, so $|S\delta_0|\le1$.

Apply the [actual kernel reading](reading-the-actual-kernel-as-an-integral-theta-combination.md) to express the raw Taylor series as an integral combination of shifts of natural theta functions. In the general `IsComb` statement a term may use another admitted pair $L_1,L'_1$ locally equal to $L,L'$. Suppose integers $a_0,b_0$ prime to $p$ send the two lattices on either side into their partners, as given by local equality. Multiplication by $a_0b_0$ sends $\operatorname{Hom}(L,L')$ into $\operatorname{Hom}(L_1,L'_1)$, by first multiplying the source vector by $a_0$ and then multiplying the output by $b_0$. This also holds after multiplying by the common host coefficient module and taking periods. Therefore if $ct$ is a period for the original pair and $c$ is prime to $p$, the shift is killed modulo the term's period lattice by $a_0b_0c$. It remains prime-to-$p$ torsion. In the actual readout the pair can be kept fixed.

For a single term $T$, transport its Taylor series and the linear coordinates into $\mathbb C_p$. The map $\iota$ extends the chosen embedding of algebraic numbers, so it acts on the algebraic Taylor coefficients as the coefficient embedding does. The complex seam only permutes labels and identifies the forms; it preserves the theta translation law. The Taylor chain rule for linear coordinates gives the same coefficientwise substitution after transport. The normalized lattice package and the [one-variable translated sigma bound](closing-the-torsion-translation-recurrence.md) give the ordinary integral fourfold identity of [the tensor argument](transporting-the-quadratic-theta-phase-through-four-copies.md). In its construction one expands the fourfold image as a finite sum of standard product translates, applies the one-variable bound to each factor, and multiplies back the common phase in each standard coordinate. The precise source input for this step is `TensStd`, obtained from the two finite theta averages and the ordinary translate bound.

Construct the integral chart for the pair $L_1,L'_1$ by [the frame proof](the-standard-dual-bases-give-the-ramified-paired-form.md) with scale $\kappa_\tau=1$, permitted since $|c_\tau|=1$. The fourfold frame comparison supplies the integral matrix $C$ from its directions with $\gamma C=\operatorname{diag}_4(M)$, where $M$ is the actual standard-coordinate substitution. Its Gram form has the desired paired shape up to $\delta_0$ times an integral matrix. The paired coefficients $u_t$ depend on the standard coordinates and the supported generator vectors, so they are independent of $L_1,L'_1$ and of the shift. The tensor argument gives $T(MX)$ with this Gaussian times a jet-integral remainder. Apply it to each term of the raw combination and take the integral linear combination of the remainders after the common Gaussian has been factored out. This gives $G$ and the assertion for the actual kernel.
