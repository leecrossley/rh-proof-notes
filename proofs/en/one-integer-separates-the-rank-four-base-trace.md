# One integer separates the rank-four base trace

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E7%A7%A9%E5%9B%9B%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E8%A1%A8%E7%A4%BA%E7%9A%84%E8%BF%B9%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E7%94%A8%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E6%95%B4%E6%95%B0%E5%90%8C%E6%97%B6%E5%88%86%E7%A6%BB.md)

Fix $\Omega=\overline{\mathbb Q}$, odd prime $\ell$, allowed coefficient integers $O$, and rank-four $D$ with `D.Good`. Let $e=D.e\ge2$, $S=D.S\ni2,\ell$, with finite-order characters $\psi,\xi,\omega$, $\Psi=\varepsilon_\ell^e\psi$, $\alpha=\varepsilon_\ell\omega^{-1}\xi$. The base $r_0$ has ordered diagonal $\alpha\Psi,\Psi,\alpha,1$. Its allowed irreducible characteristic-zero points $\mathcal R_D$ use `EPt`. Assume three characters are trivial on the fixing group of $\mu_M$ and their $k$-th powers are one, $M,k\ge1$. Take prime $p\ge5$ not dividing $M$, $p\notin S$, and an isomorphism $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$ under which $\mathcal R_D$ satisfies `IsAut4Alt`. Under `NumberSp4Alt` below there are universal $C,A$ and a Frobenius $\sigma$ at the chosen place above $p$ with $d_r=\operatorname{tr}r(\sigma)-\operatorname{tr}r_0(\sigma)\ne0$ for all $r\in\mathcal R_D$. If $s=D.\mathrm{sha}$ is finite with the [cotangent bound](rank-four-cotangent-length-counts-only-nonisomorphic-representations.md)
$$
(s/v_O(\ell))\log\ell\le2\sum_{r\in\mathcal T}-\log|d_r|_\ell
$$
for pairwise nonisomorphic $\mathcal T$, then the left side is at most $2C k[M(e+1)]^A\log p$.

**Integer input.** `NumberSp4Alt` provides $C,A$ for all $e\ge2$, $M,k\ge1$, primes $p\ge5$ with $p\nmid M$, and three complex $k$-th roots of unity $z_1,z_2,z_3$. It gives $B\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}$, $\log|B|\le C\varphi(k)[M(e+1)]^A\log p$, before choosing a prime other than $2,p$, coefficient isomorphism, absolute closure or Frobenius lift. At each such choice the trace differences with $\iota^{-1}(p^{e+1}z_1+p^e z_2+pz_3+1)$ are nonzero for all eligible rank-four representations, and the product of their norms for a finite pairwise nonisomorphic family is at least $|B|_\ell$. Eligibility `AdmRAlt` means irreducible and automorphic, with level $M$, weights $(e+1,e,1,0)$, dual twist of weight $e+1$, and the `SafeRA` local conditions. [Statement](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/ThresholdArguments/DenseThresholdCases/AfStmt.lean#L34) · [Integer proof](building-a-trace-difference-integer-from-rank-four-eigenvalue-lists.md)

**Proof.** Take arithmetic Frobenius at $p$ at the place specified by $D$, so $\varepsilon_\ell(\sigma)=p$. The reference trace has the required form with $z_1,z_2,z_3$ the $\iota$-images of $\omega^{-1}\xi\psi,\psi,\omega^{-1}\xi$ at $\sigma$; they are $k$-th roots by hypothesis. The points are irreducible and automorphic, `level` gives the local level and the dual twist whose multiplier is $\alpha\Psi$, and `safe4'` supplies the other local conditions. Applying the input to singleton families proves nonvanishing for every point at this same $\sigma$. Apply it also to $\mathcal T$ and take $-\log$. The sum is at most $-\log|B|_\ell=v_\ell(B)\log\ell\le\log|B|$ by prime factorization. Combine with the assumed length inequality and $\varphi(k)\le k$ to conclude.
