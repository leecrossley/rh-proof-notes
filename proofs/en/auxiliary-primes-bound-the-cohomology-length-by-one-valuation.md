# Auxiliary primes bound cohomology length by one valuation

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E9%80%90%E4%B8%AA%E5%8A%A0%E5%85%A5%E8%BE%85%E5%8A%A9%E7%B4%A0%E6%95%B0%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E6%8A%8A%E5%90%8C%E8%B0%83%E9%95%BF%E5%BA%A6%E5%8E%8B%E5%88%B0%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E8%B5%8B%E5%80%BC.md)

Let \(G=G_{\mathbb Q}\), \(\ell\) odd, \(O\) a DVR finite over \(\mathbb Z_\ell\), \(W=\operatorname{Frac}(O)/O\) discrete, and \(v=v_O\) normalized by a uniformizer, \(v(0)=\infty\). Fix \(m\ge1\), \(N_0\ge1\) prime to \(\ell\), \(F=N_0\ell^m\), \(U=G_{\mathbb Q(\mu_F)}\). Let \(\alpha:G\to O^\times\) satisfy \(\alpha\equiv1\bmod\ell^m\) on \(U\), \(\alpha\equiv-1\bmod\ell^m\) on elements inverting \(\mu_F\), and \(\alpha(g)-\varepsilon_\ell(g)\in O^\times\) for some \(g\). Choose \(\delta\) with \(\alpha(\delta)-1\) a unit. For \(0\le b<F\), choose \(\sigma_t\) acting on \(\mu_F\) by \(t\) for each unit \(t\bmod F\), and define
$$
z=\sum_{t\in(\mathbb Z/F\mathbb Z)^\times}
  \left(\left\lfloor\frac{b[t]}F\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\frac{b[-t]}F\right\rfloor\right)\alpha(\sigma_t)^{-1},
$$
with \([t]\) the standard residue. Suppose \(a_0z\mid\ell^m\) in \(O\). Let \(T(a_0)\) be the module of continuous \(\alpha\)-cocycles \(c:G\to W\) killed by \(a_0\), normalized by \(c(\delta)=0\), vanishing on inertia outside \(F\), and on each \(D_v\) above \(F\) equal to \((\alpha(g)-1)m_v\) with \(m_v\in W[a_0]\). Given the reciprocity input below, \(\operatorname{length}_O T(a_0)\le v(z)\). In Lean, \(W=O\otimes_{\mathbb Z_\ell}(\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell)\) by localization, and \(T(a_0)\) is `ShT`. [`count`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PrimitiveFrobeniusFixed/FrobFix.lean#L143)

**Arithmetic inputs.** Write \(\beta=\varepsilon_\ell\alpha^{-1}\). For \(\gamma=\alpha\) or \(\beta\), `IsNu` with ramification modulus \(d\) means a function locally constant and additive on \(U\), killed there by \(\ell^m\), conjugating by \(\gamma\), and vanishing on inertia outside \(d\).

**Proof.** Let \(S(n)\subset T(a_0)\) require vanishing on every \(D_v\) above \(n\). As \(a_0\mid\ell^m\), \(U\) acts trivially on the values of \(c\), so \(c|_U\) is additive and conjugates by \(\alpha\). If \(c|_U=0\), the abelian quotient and \(c(\delta)=0\) give \(c(g)=c(g\delta)=c(\delta g)=\alpha(\delta)c(g)\), hence \(c=0\). At a prime \(q\equiv1\bmod F\), the decomposition group lies in \(U\), and \(c\) vanishes on inertia. Its restriction is determined by the value at one Frobenius lift, and by conjugacy this detects vanishing at every place above \(q\). The kernel of \(c\mapsto c(\Phi)\) on \(S(n)\) is therefore \(S(nq)\).

For \(\nu\) as in reciprocity, let \((A)\) annihilate \(\nu(U)\) and \((B)\) annihilate a chosen \(\nu(\tau)\) at \(q\), taking the full annihilator ideals. Then \(B\mid A\), and assume \(a_0\mid B\). Write \(A=BE\), \(\nu(\tau)=y(1/A\bmod O)\). Reciprocity gives \(y c(\Phi)=0\), and \(A c(\Phi)=0\) by \(a_0\mid A\). The annihilator formula \(B\mid s\) if and only if \(A\mid sy\) shows \(v(E)=v(A)-v(B)=\min(v(A),v(y))\). Thus \(E\) generates \((A,y)\), so evaluation lands in \(W[E]\simeq O/(E)\) of length \(v(E)\). This also handles \(y=0\). Hence
$$
\operatorname{length}S(n)\le\operatorname{length}S(nq)+v(E),
$$
and a nonzero evaluation makes \(v(E)>0\). Use \(w=1/\ell^m\bmod O\) in input 2. Let \((A_L)=\operatorname{Ann}\nu_L(U)\), \(j_L=v(A_L)\le J=v(\ell^m)\), and consider a list with \(a_0\mid A_L\). If \(S(n_L)\ne0\), choose a nonzero \(c\) and use input 3 with \(D=\prod_{q\in L}q\). The new inertia value from input 2 has annihilator \((A_L)\) because its previous Frobenius value generates the module of values. Thus \(A_L\mid A_{q::L}\). Apply the length estimate with \(A=A_{q::L}\), \(B=A_L\). It gives a strict increase of \(j_L\) and
$$
\operatorname{length}S(n_L)+j_L\le
\operatorname{length}S(n_{q::L})+j_{q::L}.
$$
Since the integer \(j_L\le J\), finitely many steps yield \(S(n_L)=0\), where the right-hand bound is at most \(J\). This proves \(\operatorname{length}S(n_L)+j_L\le J\) at each starting list and proves finiteness at the same time.

At \(r\), \(\nu_\varnothing(\tau_r)=u_r z/\ell^m\bmod O\) has annihilator generated by \(k=\ell^m/z\), with \(a_0\mid k\). In the first length estimate take \(n=1,q=r,A=A_\varnothing,B=k\), so \(A_\varnothing=kE_0\). Then \(\operatorname{length}T(a_0)\le\operatorname{length}S(r)+v(E_0)\). The preceding bound at the empty list gives \(\operatorname{length}S(r)+v(E_0)+v(k)\le J=v(z)+v(k)\). Since \(k\ne0\), cancel its finite valuation, obtaining the claim.
