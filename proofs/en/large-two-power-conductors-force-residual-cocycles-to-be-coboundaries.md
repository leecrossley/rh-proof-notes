# Large two-power conductors force residual cocycles to be coboundaries

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E5%A4%A7%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%8C%E8%BF%9B%E5%AF%BC%E5%AD%90%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E4%BD%BF%E5%89%A9%E4%BD%99%E4%BD%99%E9%97%AD%E9%93%BE%E6%88%90%E4%B8%BA%E4%BD%99%E8%BE%B9%E7%95%8C.md)

Let $\ell$ be prime, $d\ge1$, $4\mid\ell^d-1$, $s=v_2(\ell^d-1)$, and $f\ge1$ odd with $f4^s\le2^n$, $n\ge0$. Let $O$ be a finite $\ell$-adic integer ring mapping by reduction to a field $k$ of characteristic $\ell$. Let $\phi,\xi$ be primitive $O$-valued characters modulo $f,2^n$ of $\operatorname{ord}\phi\mid\ell^d-1$ and $\phi(-1)\xi(-1)=-1$. Let $\gamma$ be the reduction of the pullback of $\chi=\phi\xi$ to $G_{\mathbb Q}$, or its inverse. Suppose $z:G_{\mathbb Q}\to k$ is a continuous $\gamma$-cocycle which is zero, at each inertia $I$, on a subgroup of $I\cap\ker\gamma$ of index prime to $\ell$. Then $z=(\gamma-1)m$ for some $m\in k$. This is `CorX`. [Hypotheses](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/LevelCohomology/UnramifiedCocycleIsCoboundaryOfBernoulliUnit.lean#L14)

**The Bernoulli criterion.** First let $\eta$ be a nontrivial character modulo $M$ of order prime to $\ell$, with residual pullback $\gamma$ different from the residual cyclotomic character. Suppose $B_{1,\eta^{-1}}=M^{-1}\sum_{0\le b<M}b\eta^{-1}(b)$ is a unit. An additive cocycle on $I\cap\ker\gamma$ vanishing on a subgroup of index prime to $\ell$ is zero there: its image is a quotient of index dividing that index and also of $\ell$-power order. Thus $z|_U$ for $U=G_{\mathbb Q(\mu_M)}$ is an additive unramified character and factors through the ideal class group by Artin reciprocity; $M>2$ follows from nontriviality. Choose $a>0$ odd prime to $M$ with $\bar\eta(a)\ne a\bmod\ell$. For $\ell\nmid M$, take $a=\ell$ (here $\ell$ is odd). Otherwise a group element distinguishing the characters gives a unit class modulo $M$, for which one can choose an odd positive representative.

Set $E(a,b)=(a-1)/2-(a[a^{-1}b]_M-[b]_M)/M$. This is integral, and reindexing with $\sum_b\eta^{-1}(b)=0$ gives
$$
\sum_b E(a,b)\eta^{-1}(b)=(1-a\eta^{-1}(a))B_{1,\eta^{-1}},
$$
a unit by both conditions. By the [class group annihilator](class-group-stickelberger-relations-kill-cohomology.md), $\sum_b E(a,b)\sigma_b^{-1}$ kills the ideal class group. Apply to the Artin class of $u\in U$ and then to the additive character $z|_U$. The conjugated value is $z(\sigma_b^{-1}u\sigma_b)=\bar\eta(b)^{-1}z(u)$ by the cocycle rule. Thus the nonzero residual sum multiplies $z(u)$ to zero, giving $z|_U=0$. Descend to the abelian unit group modulo $M$. Choose $g_0$ with $\gamma(g_0)\ne1$ (prime-to-$\ell$ reduction is injective). Comparison of $gg_0$ and $g_0g$ gives $(\gamma(g_0)-1)z(g)=(\gamma(g)-1)z(g_0)$, so $m=z(g_0)/(\gamma(g_0)-1)$ works. **Using the conductor.** The hypotheses give $s\ge2,n\ge4,\ell$ odd, and primitivity gives $\xi(1+2^{n-1})=-1$. By the Chinese remainder theorem take a Galois element acting as $1$ modulo $\ell f$ and $1+2^{n-1}$ modulo $2^n$; its residual cyclotomic value is $1$ and $\gamma$ is $-1$. Hence $\gamma$ is nontrivial and distinct from that character. The order is prime to $\ell$ from both components. The [Bernoulli unit theorem](large-two-power-conductors-give-bernoulli-units.md) applies both to $\chi$ and to its inverse, as parity, orders and conductors persist. Apply the criterion with the character inducing $\gamma$.

**Local condition in the deformation.** `D.XVan` asks for local coboundaries on the specified decomposition groups of $T$ and vanishing on inertia outside $T$ intersected with $\ker\bar r_0$. The residual base is diagonal over a finite field, so its image order is prime to $\ell$. Thus outside $T$ intersecting its kernel gives the required prime-to-$\ell$ index in $I\cap\ker\gamma$. Inside $T$, a local coboundary is zero wherever $\gamma=1$; at a conjugated place the cocycle law multiplies the value on $\ker\gamma$ by the corresponding nonzero $\gamma$-factor, preserving zero. Hence the theorem applies to each desired twist and inverse in the [base data construction](constructing-rank-four-base-data-for-cases-ii-and-iii.md).
