# Real unit signatures bound the two-primary class quotient

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E5%AE%9E%E5%8D%95%E4%BD%8D%E7%9A%84%E7%AC%A6%E5%8F%B7%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E6%8E%A7%E5%88%B6%E4%BA%8C%E8%BF%9B%E7%B1%BB%E7%BE%A4.md)

## Statement

Let $f>1$ odd, $n\ge2$, $M=f2^n$, $L=\mathbb Q(\mu_M)$ and $G=(\mathbb Z/M\mathbb Z)^\times$. Let $J=-1$ act by conjugation on $C=\mathrm{Cl}(O_L)$, written additively, and set $A=\ker(1+J)$ and $D=(1-J)C$. For positive odd $c$ prime to $M$, use $\theta_H$ from [Sinnott nonvanishing](sinnott-finite-differences-imply-nonvanishing.md). If $([\gamma]-1)^N\in(\theta_H,1+[J],2)$, then
$$
[A:D]\le(2^{[G:\langle\gamma\rangle]})^N.
$$
This is `sgnCyc`.

## A signature attached to a class

Write $L^+$ for the real fixed field. Index embeddings by $s\in G$, and write signatures of real nonzero elements in $\mathbb F_2$ with negatives recorded as one. The functions constant on pairs $\{s,Js\}$ form $V$. The signatures of real units form a subspace $S$ stable under $G$-translation. For $x\in A$, take an integral ideal $I$ representing it, with $I\,JI=(b)$. Then $u=Jb/b$ is a unit with $u Ju=1$, hence has modulus one at all embeddings and is a root of unity. Every root of unity is $v/Jv$ for a unit $v$: $1-\zeta$ is a unit for primitive $\zeta$ of order $M$ since $M$ has at least two prime factors and $\Phi_M(1)=1$. Its quotient by its conjugate is $-\zeta$ of order $M$ since $4\mid M$. All roots of unity in $L$ have orders dividing $M$, by comparing $\varphi(M)$ with $\varphi(\operatorname{lcm}(M,r))$ for any extra order $r$. The cyclotomic value formula follows from $m=\prod_{d\mid m,d>1}\Phi_d(1)$ and the prime-power values. Choose $v$ with $v/Jv=u$ so $a=bv$ is real and generates $I\,JI$.

If such a generator $a$ is totally positive, it is a norm by the quadratic Hasse norm theorem. Indeed $L/L^+$ is unramified at every finite place because at a prime $p$ inertia fixes the prime-to-$p$ roots of unity, on which $J$ is nontrivial ($f>1$ at two, and $4\mid M$ at odd $p$). At split places the norm is onto. At inert places norms have arbitrary unit part and even valuation, and $I\,JI=(a)$ makes the valuation even. At real places positivity suffices. Write $a=\alpha J\alpha$. Then $B=I/(\alpha)$ has $B\,JB=1$. Its exponents are zero on fixed primes and opposite on pairs, so $B=E/(JE)$ for some fractional ideal. This gives $[I]\in D$.

Choose one $x,I,a$ for each coset $A/D$ and assign $\operatorname{sgn}(a)+S$. If two choices have the same signature class, some real unit $v$ makes $aa'v$ totally positive. The ideal $JI\cdot I'$ times its conjugate has that generator. Its class $x'-x$ belongs to $D$ by the last argument, proving injectivity into $V/S$.

## Counting signatures via nilpotence

Map $\mathbb Z[G]$ onto $V$ by taking coefficients of $x(1+[J])$ mod two. Each pair's value can be specified there by choosing one position. Let $I_S$ be the preimage of $S$, an ideal by translation stability. It contains $2$ and $1+[J]$ since $(1+[J])^2=2(1+[J])$. To see it contains $\theta_H$, set $k=(c-1)/2$ and $\zeta=\exp(2\pi i/M)$. The real unit $u_c=\zeta^{-k}\sum_{j=0}^{c-1}\zeta^j$ is a unit by the ratio $(1-\zeta^c)/(1-\zeta)$ and real by reversing the terms. At the embedding $s$ it equals $\sin(\pi c\langle s\rangle_M/M)/\sin(\pi\langle s\rangle_M/M)$. The denominator is positive and the numerator nonzero. Its signature is $\lfloor c\langle s\rangle_M/M\rfloor\bmod2$. Translate by $c^{-1}$ and subtract $k$ using the constant signature of $-1$. This gives $g(s)\bmod2\in S$. These are exactly the coefficients of $\theta_H(1+[J])$ mod two by the half-range choice and $g(-s)=-g(s)$. Thus $\theta_H\in I_S$.

On $B=\mathbb Z[G]/I_S\simeq V/S$, multiplication by $[\gamma]-1$ is nilpotent of exponent $N$ by the premise. After further quotienting by its image, one generator per coset of $\langle\gamma\rangle$ suffices. The quotient has size at most $2^{[G:\langle\gamma\rangle]}$. The [nilpotent count](uniform-class-group-rank-bounds-in-cyclotomic-towers.md) bounds $|B|$ by its $N$th power. Combine with the signature injection.
