# Spherical comparison gives the local parameter for unlinked toral support

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let \(E\) be a CM number field with algebraic closure \(\Omega\), \(\ell\) a prime, \(\iota\colon\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C\), \(n\ge1\), and \((\pi,\chi)\) a regular algebraic polarized cuspidal datum of rank \(n\) over \(E\). Let \(r\) be any weakly attached representation. Fix a finite place \(w\) whose algebraically normalized local component \(\sigma^{\mathrm{alg}}\) has unlinked supercuspidal support consisting of \(n\) smooth characters \(\xi_j\colon E_w^\times\to\mathbb C^\times\). Thus no member of the support is another multiplied by the normalized absolute value. Assume the all-rank spherical comparison and the auxiliary character base change inputs of the [spherical lift proof](an-auxiliary-cm-base-change-makes-toral-support-spherical.md). The conclusions, collectively called `R1`, are as follows.

- For \(w\nmid\ell\) and any prolongation of \(w\) to \(\Omega\), the complex Frobenius-semisimple Weil–Deligne parameter of \(r\) has zero monodromy and equals the local Langlands class of \(\sigma^{\mathrm{alg}}\) for every correspondence satisfying the project's extended local Langlands laws.
- For \(w\mid\ell\), use any prolongation and the compatible coefficient and period data in the statement. The local representation is de Rham and potentially semistable. For each local embedding induced by \(\tau\colon E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\), its filtration jumps, with multiplicity, are
  \[
  \left\{\frac{n-1}{2}-z\ \middle|\ z\in\operatorname{infChar}_{\iota\circ\tau}\pi\right\}.
  \]
  Its Weil–Deligne monodromy is zero, and its characteristic polynomial at each Weil element agrees with that of the same normalized local Langlands class. The precise project statement quantifies over any tied prime and \(A_{\mathrm{inf}}\) period data, any coefficient ring over \(\mathbb Z_\ell\) with topology, and every bridge identifying the chosen decomposition representation with the local one. The jump condition ranges over every embedding of the resulting completion into a finite coefficient extension that induces \(\tau\).

The spherical comparison used below supplies unramified Frobenius polynomials away from \(\ell\), and de Rham jumps, crystallinity and the crystalline Weil polynomial at spherical places over \(\ell\), all applied to weakly attached representations. ## The parameter predicted by the local support

Fix a prolongation of \(w\) and any local Artin map \(a_w\colon W_w\to E_w^\times\) in the project's convention. The support uniqueness theorem identifies the supercuspidal support of the Langlands multisegment of \(\sigma^{\mathrm{alg}}\) with \(\{\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n\}\). A segment of length at least two contains two successive absolute-value twists, which violates the unlinked condition. Thus each segment is a singleton corresponding to a rank-one character. The segment law for the extended local Langlands map reduces to the direct sum of the corresponding rank-one parameters. Each rank-one parameter is \(\xi_j\circ a_w\) by local reciprocity and has zero monodromy. The sum therefore has zero monodromy, and its polynomial at \(g\in W_w\) is
\[
P_g(X)=\prod_{j=1}^n(X-\xi_j(a_w(g))).
\]
This proves the formula for any of the allowed correspondences and its associated Artin map. [The local Langlands calculation](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/SphericalGaloisCompatibility/ToralDeRham/RcUnlinkedChars.lean#L22)

## Comparison away from \(\ell\)

Take \(g\in W_w\) of nonzero degree. The spherical lift construction chooses a finite CM extension \(M/E\) fixed by \(g\), a place \(u\mid w\) from the same prolongation, and a spherical component \(P_u\) of a cuspidal base change of \(\pi\). The element \(g\) defines a Weil element at \(u\), because it fixes \(M\) and continues to preserve the valuation and act as an integral power of residue Frobenius. The base-change Satake law gives weak attachment for \(r|_{G_M}\). As \(u\nmid\ell\), the spherical comparison identifies this local Galois parameter with the spherical Satake parameter. This follows from the Frobenius characteristic polynomial in the unramified case, since inertia and the monodromy are trivial and a semisimple Frobenius determines the parameter. The polynomial at \(g\) is consequently \(P_g(X)\) by the calculation of the [auxiliary spherical parameter](an-auxiliary-cm-base-change-makes-toral-support-spherical.md). [Application to the weakly attached restriction](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/SphericalGaloisCompatibility/ToralDeRham/ToralGalois2.lean#L22)

The Weil–Deligne construction is compatible with restriction to a finite extension. In the formula recovering a finite-inertia Weil action from an \(\ell\)-adic representation, the chosen tame logarithm and the monodromy operator may scale by inverse nonzero factors, so the vanishing of monodromy is equivalent before and after restriction. Characteristic polynomials of the Weil action are preserved at the same group element. Apply the spherical argument first to an element of degree \(1\) to conclude that the original operator is zero, and then to each element of nonzero degree to obtain \(P_g\) there. The [Cayley–Hamilton trace argument](nonzero-weil-degrees-determine-the-inertial-characteristic-polynomials.md) compares with the direct sum of characters and gives the same polynomials in degree zero. The two Frobenius-semisimple Weil actions have equal characters and are semisimple, so they are isomorphic. Together with zero monodromy, this identifies the parameter classes. ## Comparison at \(w\mid\ell\)

The same lift, first with \(g\) of degree \(1\), produces a crystalline local restriction by the spherical comparison. Crystalline representations are semistable with zero monodromy. Therefore \(r\) is potentially semistable. Using compatible bridges over the two fields, the potentially semistable Weil–Deligne construction commutes with restriction, so its monodromy downstairs is also zero. For each \(g\) of nonzero degree, repeat the argument with a lift chosen for this \(g\). The spherical Frobenius clause computes its characteristic polynomial over \(M_u\). Both restriction and the calculation of the auxiliary spherical Satake parameter show that this is \(P_g(X)\) over \(E_w\). Apply the same Cayley–Hamilton argument to compare on all of inertia as well. For the de Rham and jump statement it suffices to use one spherical lift, for example the one at the degree-one element. On that lift the de Rham spherical clause supplies the jumps of \(r|_{G_M}\) at \(u\). The filtered de Rham space under a finite local extension from \(K\) to \(L\) is obtained by extending scalars from \(K\) to \(L\), with its filtration. Indeed, one can first pass to a finite Galois overfield of \(L/K\). The additional Galois group acts semilinearly on the invariant de Rham period space and each step of its filtration, so ordinary Galois descent gives the comparison with the invariants over \(K\), respecting each filtration step. In particular full de Rham dimension ascends and descends. After choosing a common finite coefficient extension for the two representations and the embeddings, the component of each filtration quotient for an embedding of \(K\) base changes to the component for an embedding of \(L\) extending it. The dimension of every graded component is unchanged. This proves that the original local representation is de Rham and that the jumps at the relevant paired embeddings are equal. [De Rham descent with finite coefficient models](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/DeRhamDescends.lean#L17) · [Descent of each jump multiplicity](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/JumpsDescend.lean#L19)

For any downstairs embedding occurring in the statement, extend the embedding of the completion to the larger completion in \(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\), using algebraic closedness, and take a finite coefficient extension containing its image. The infinitesimal character of \(P\) at the resulting global embedding is that of \(\pi\) at the restricted embedding, by the infinite-place base-change law. Hence the supplied jump multiset upstairs is exactly the multiset displayed in the statement. The equality of graded dimensions gives the claimed jumps downstairs. The argument applies to every choice of compatible local period data and prolongation.
