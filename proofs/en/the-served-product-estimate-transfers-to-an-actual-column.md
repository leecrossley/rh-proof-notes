# The served product estimate transfers to an actual column

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix tied global data, a word $u$, a served place $w$ over $p$, and a used twist $\mu$ with $p\notin Q_p(\mu)$. Assume the [local product estimate](selected-local-operators-and-ordinary-cofactors-give-a-product-bound.md) holds for its twist system and that the comparison map $c$ has property `CmpV`. This property says that the terminal local lattice valuation of the chain on $c(x)$ transfers at the same lower bound to the global column for $x$ and $\mu$. The transferred assertion `ColGe` is conditional on whether the column is the base or twisted one, and asserts a preimage in the terminal arithmetic space with the indicated local lattice valuation.

Let $R$ be the representative set and $Q\subseteq R$ its chosen operator subset `VV`. With $b_0(r,j)=\max(g_0(r,j),0)$, put
$$
\delta_r=\sum_{j=1}^m\max(g_U(r,j)-P(r,j)-b_0(r,j),0).
$$
The source's input module `NP` lies in the span of the following generators: choose $J\subseteq Q$ and an integral input $F$, scale it by $p^{\sum_{r\in Q\setminus J}\lceil S_v(r)\rceil_+}$, and apply the product of $y_{\iota(r)}^m$ for $r\in J$. Here $\lceil S_v\rceil_+$ denotes the nonnegative integer ceiling of the `Sv` quantity, which dominates $\delta_r$. The other intersections in `NP` restrict ramification and add uniform scaling. The present valuation estimate only needs membership in the generating span. The target for $c(x)\in\mathrm{NP}$ is
$$
\operatorname{ColGe}(x,\operatorname{totS}),\qquad
\operatorname{totS}=\mathrm{lowS}+\sum_{j=1}^m\operatorname{gainS}(p,j).
$$

For a generator with used set $J$, each representative supplies at least $b_0$ at every layer. A used representative additionally supplies $\max(g_U-P-b_0,0)$. Passing from $J$ to the full $Q$ in this numerical sum would add precisely $\sum_{r\in Q\setminus J}\delta_r$. The scaling factor already accounts for at least that much $p$-normalized valuation. Thus the local floor of this generator is at least $\ell+\sum_j\operatorname{gainP}(p,j)$, where $\ell=\mathrm{lowS}$. In the range where `gainP` is defined to vanish, the same inequality follows from the nonnegativity of all representative contributions. The product operators commute with scalar multiplication. Since the terminal scaled lattice is a submodule, linearity of the chain gives the same floor throughout `NP`. Apply `CmpV` to $c(x)$. The global arithmetic term `gainS` is either `gainP` or zero, and `gainP` is nonnegative. Hence the transferred bound implies `ColGe` at $\operatorname{totS}$. This proves the served branch of `Conclusion1` and supplies the column input used by [the period estimate](the-period-bound-at-each-finite-place.md).
