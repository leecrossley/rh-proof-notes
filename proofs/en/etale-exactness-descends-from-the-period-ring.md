# Étale exactness descends from the period ring

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E6%89%A9%E5%88%B0%E5%91%A8%E6%9C%9F%E7%8E%AF%E7%9A%84%E6%AD%A3%E5%90%88%E6%80%A7%E8%83%BD%E4%BC%A0%E5%9B%9E%C3%A9tale%E6%A8%A1.md)

Let \(O=\mathcal O_L\) for \(L/\mathbb Q_\ell\) finite, with uniformizer \(\varpi\) and residue field \(k\). Take \(A=\prod_{i=1}^nO[[U]]\), \(n\ge1\), \(E=\widehat{A[1/U]}\) for the \(\ell\)-adic topology and an embedding \(E\hookrightarrow B\) of commutative rings with \(\ell\in\operatorname{Jac}(B)\). Suppose the étale module \(D(T)\) of each finite free \(O\)-lattice \(T\) is free over \(E\) of the same rank, with an injective comparison \(J_T:D(T)\to B\otimes_O T\), semilinear over \(E\to B\), whose image spans over \(B\). Suppose \(J\) is natural for the lattice maps. Then \(D\) carries a short exact sequence \(0\to T_0\xrightarrow a T_1\xrightarrow b T_2\to0\) of such lattices to a short exact sequence of underlying \(E\)-modules.

**Proof.** Every maximal ideal of \(E\) contains \(\varpi\) by completeness. Since \(E/\varpi E=\prod_i k((U))\), it has the form \(\mathfrak m=(\varpi,1-e_i)\). The extension \(\mathfrak mB\) is proper, for an expression for \(1\) in those generators would yield \(e_i\in\varpi B\). This is impossible for a nonzero idempotent since \(\varpi\in\operatorname{Jac}(B)\).

The \(J_T\)-images of any basis of \(D(T)\) give a \(B\)-basis by square-matrix generation. If \(J_T(M)\) spans for a submodule \(M\subset D(T)\), then \(M=D(T)\). In fact a proper \(M\) gives a simple quotient \(D(T)/M\twoheadrightarrow E/\mathfrak m\) by finite generation and the existence of a maximal submodule. Extend the associated map on a basis to a map \(B\otimes T\to B/\mathfrak mB\). By semilinearity it kills \(J_T(M)\). It also takes a value \(1\) on the image of some vector mapping to \(1\) in the simple quotient. This contradicts \(\mathfrak mB\ne B\).

The original sequence splits over \(O\) since \(T_2\) is free, hence is exact after tensoring with \(B\). Natural injective comparisons give injectivity of \(D(a)\) and \(D(b)D(a)=0\). The image of \(D(b)\) spans after applying \(J_{T_2}\) by surjectivity of the extended \(b\), so \(D(b)\) is surjective by the criterion. Choose an \(E\)-linear section \(s\) since \(D(T_2)\) is free. Then \(J_{T_1}(\operatorname{im}D(a)+\operatorname{im}s)\) spans \(B\otimes T_1\): its span contains the extended kernel and maps onto the target. Apply the criterion again. Every vector in \(\ker D(b)\) is now \(D(a)y+s(z)\), and applying \(D(b)\) forces \(z=0\).

The module criterion is `eq_top_of_span_image`, and `span_range_map` and `exact_of_tie` handle naturality and the section. `map_ne_top_of_tie` uses a more general density argument than the explicit completion here. In `DD_exact` the `adm9` parameter is ignored; the proof uses the comparison and coefficient properties. This supplies the surjection for [the qualitative Wach cokernel](two-qualitative-annihilators-of-the-wach-cokernel.md).
