# Residue-degree divisibility turns mixed depth-zero support toral

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let \(E\) be CM with algebraic closure \(\Omega\), let \(n\ge1\), and let \(r\) over \(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\) be weakly attached, relative to a fixed \(\iota\colon\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C\), to a regular algebraic polarized cuspidal pair \((\pi,\chi)\) of rank \(n\). Let \(w\) be a finite place with \(E_w\simeq\mathbb Q_p\) by a continuous field isomorphism for a prime \(p\). Suppose the local component \(\sigma=\pi_w\) has mixed depth-zero support. This means that \(\sigma\) has a nonzero vector fixed by the first principal congruence subgroup, admits an unlinked supercuspidal support, and its support entries of rank at least two are pairwise inequivalent up to unramified twist. Fix a prolongation of \(w\) and any extended correspondence \(\operatorname{rec}\) for \(\mathrm{GL}\) at this place.

Then there is a finite CM extension \(M/E\) in \(\Omega\), a place \(u\) from the same prolongation, and a regular algebraic polarized cuspidal pair \((P,\chi')\) of rank \(n\) over \(M\). It has weak attachment \(r|_{G_M}\) and the pullback infinitesimal character at every complex embedding. Every element of inertia at \(w\) fixes \(M\) and defines the same automorphism of \(\Omega\) as an element of inertia at \(u\). The normalized component \(T^{\mathrm{alg}}\) for \(T=P_u\) has unlinked rank-one support, and for a correspondence \(\operatorname{rec}'\) at \(u\) the polynomials of \(\operatorname{rec}(\sigma^{\mathrm{alg}})\) and \(\operatorname{rec}'(T^{\mathrm{alg}})\) agree at corresponding inertia elements. This is the `ToTor` input to the [inertial local comparison](an-unramified-toral-lift-compares-all-inertial-polynomials.md). ## Choosing an extension along which cusp forms lift

The place \(w\) is split over \(E^+\). Indeed its completion is \(\mathbb Q_p\), and the completion of \(E^+\) below it is also \(\mathbb Q_p\), so the local degree is one. The residue field has \(p\) elements. Let \(S\) be the finite set of places where \(\pi\) is not spherical. The [field-construction theorem](a-fresh-ramified-prime-builds-each-layer-of-an-unramified-local-extension.md) gives a tower of cyclic prime-degree CM extensions starting at \(E\) and ending at \(M\), unramified at \(w\), with residue degree \(n!\) above \(w\). At every step it provides a finite place of the preceding field over a place outside \(S\) of \(E\), split completely in the earlier tower and ramified in the next step. It also ensures unramifiedness over nonsplit places of \(E/E^+\). The witness at the fresh ramified place includes an inertia element acting nontrivially in the new layer. [Field-construction statement](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/InertiaQuadraticRamification/AsmUnr.lean#L12) · [Application in Lean](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/SphericalGaloisCompatibility/PAdicSplitting/PToTor.lean#L74)

Base change \(\pi\) up this tower. Its component at the selected fresh place of a step is spherical since the place was outside \(S\) and split completely in all the earlier layers. The norm-character self-twist for the new extension would at this place be ramified and is impossible for a spherical local component. Prime-degree cyclic base change therefore gives a cuspidal lift. The polarization character pulls back by the real norm, the conjugate-dual relationship is preserved by the Satake power law and strong multiplicity one, and the regular infinitesimal characters pull back along restriction. At almost all finite places the Galois representation of the lift is the restriction, by the common residue-degree power law for Frobenius and Satake parameters. At every place split completely in the tower, the split-place matching in each step preserves the spherical component, supplying the assertion needed at the next step. This inductively constructs \(P,\chi'\) and the weak attachment. [Single prime-degree step](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/CharpolyFromTrace/StepLift.lean#L19) · [Induction and the split-place invariant](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/CharpolyFromTrace/AllLift.lean#L22)

For clarity, the proof obtains the infinite-place compatibility of an arbitrary weak lift through descent as well. At one prime-degree step the upstairs lift is Galois invariant by the almost-everywhere Satake law and multiplicity one. A cuspidal descent has the required infinite-place character by the strong cyclic descent law. Its weak base change is the same as that of the previous form, so the base-change fiber theorem says that the two descents differ by a finite-order norm-character twist. At complex places this twist is trivial, and hence has no effect on the infinitesimal characters. Therefore the original weak lift has the same pullback infinitesimal character, and this law composes along the tower. ## Applying the depth-zero support comparison

The rank of every member of a supercuspidal support of \(\sigma\) is a positive integer at most \(n\), since the ranks sum to \(n\). Each one divides the residue degree \(n!\). The depth-zero tower comparison used here has the following form. Suppose an extension tower comes with cuspidal weak lifts in all layers, the initial local field is \(\mathbb Q_p\), the final local extension is unramified of degree \(f\), the source component has nonzero first principal congruence fixed vectors, and every supercuspidal rank of that component divides \(f\). Then the final component has supercuspidal support \(\{\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n\}\) of smooth characters of \(M_u^\times\), each trivial on its first principal unit subgroup. For every extended local correspondence downstairs, every local Artin map \(a_u\) and every \(t'\in I_u\) the initial Langlands parameter evaluated on \(t'|_{G_E}\) has characteristic polynomial \(\prod_j(X-\xi_j(a_u(t')))\). The output support is unlinked under the additional mixed hypotheses on the source. The Lean derivation of this theorem uses the finite-field type labels, Shintani lift and comparison of matching traces. [Depth-zero tower comparison](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/RegularSemisimpleFibres/NumberField.lean#L166) · [Derivation from finite-field matching](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/RegularSemisimpleFibres/TypeOfLblHolds.lean#L133)

Apply the theorem with \(f=n!\). The unramified extension identifies the inertia groups in the fixed prolongation, as subgroups of automorphisms of \(\Omega\). With local Artin maps \(a_w,a_u\), norm compatibility gives \(N_{M_u/E_w}a_u(t')=a_w(t)\) for these matching elements. The normalized components are obtained by a common shift of each support factor by the appropriate absolute-value power. This preserves unlinkedness and does not change any inertia polynomial, since the absolute-value character is trivial on inertial images. For the final toral support the inertial polynomial is the product of the rank-one factors \(\prod_j(X-\xi_j(a_u(t')))\) by local Langlands. Thus both normalized polynomials have this common value. This supplies all the statements of `ToTor`.
