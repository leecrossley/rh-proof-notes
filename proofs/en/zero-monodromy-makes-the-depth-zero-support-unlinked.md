# Zero monodromy makes the depth-zero support unlinked

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let \(E\) be a CM field with algebraic closure \(\Omega\), let \(n\ge1\), and let \(\sigma\) be the local component of a cuspidal representation \(\pi\) of \(\mathrm{GL}_n/E\) at a finite place \(w\). Write \(k=E_w\), and let \(K_1=\ker(\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal O_k)\to\mathrm{GL}_n(k_w))\), where \(k_w\) is its residue field. Suppose \(\sigma\) has a nonzero \(K_1\)-fixed vector. Let \(\sigma^{\mathrm{alg}}=\sigma\otimes|\det|^{(1-n)/2}\). Assume that in every supercuspidal support multiset of \(\sigma^{\mathrm{alg}}\), the factors of rank at least \(2\) are pairwise inequivalent up to unramified determinant twist, counting multiplicities. Finally, fix a prolongation of \(w\) to \(\Omega\) and an extended local Langlands correspondence \(\operatorname{rec}\) at \(w\) as in the project. Assume that the monodromy of \(\operatorname{rec}(\sigma^{\mathrm{alg}})\) is zero.

Then the unnormalized component \(\sigma\) has a supercuspidal support multiset \(S\) that is unlinked: no member is an absolute-value determinant twist by exponent \(1\) of another. Its factors of rank at least \(2\) remain pairwise inequivalent up to unramified twist. Together with the \(K_1\)-fixed vector, these are exactly the properties called `Mixed`. ## Proof

A local component of a cuspidal representation of \(\mathrm{GL}_n\) is generic, and twisting by a character of the determinant does not change genericity. The generic part of the Langlands classification expresses \(\sigma^{\mathrm{alg}}\) as an irreducible normalized induction of pairwise unlinked segment representations. Each segment is a positive-length list of consecutive absolute-value twists of one supercuspidal representation. The Langlands law for \(\operatorname{rec}\) assigns to a segment of length \(m\) the Weil–Deligne parameter built from the parameter of its first supercuspidal member and the special representation of length \(m\), with an unramified adjustment. The supercuspidal correspondence gives a nonzero irreducible Weil representation for this first member. If \(m\ge2\), the special factor has a nonzero nilpotent operator with one Jordan block, so the tensor product also has nonzero monodromy. The monodromy of the direct sum is zero by hypothesis, so the monodromy in each summand must be zero. Every segment therefore has length one. [Removing the long segments](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/SphericalGaloisCompatibility/PAdicSplitting/PMixedOf.lean#L60)

In particular the cuspidal support of \(\sigma^{\mathrm{alg}}\) is the multiset of the first members of these singleton segments. Distinct singleton segments differing by the absolute-value determinant twist would be linked. A singleton cannot be its own such twist: its central character on a uniformizing scalar would change by the nontrivial absolute-value power given by the positive rank of the factor. Thus the whole support is unlinked in the stated sense, including comparisons of repeated entries.

Undoing the common normalization twist on the support gives the multiset \(S\) of \(\sigma\). The common twist preserves linkage, and it preserves equivalence up to unramified twist since the latter already permits any unramified determinant character. Hence \(S\) has the same two properties. A conditional proof of the principal-congruence comparison uses exactly this input when the component has higher-rank supercuspidal support.
