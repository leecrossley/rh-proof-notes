# One Frobenius trace difference controls same-kernel lengths

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E7%94%A8%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AAFrobenius%E7%9A%84%E8%BF%B9%E5%B7%AE%E6%8E%A7%E5%88%B6%E5%90%8C%E6%A0%B8%E7%89%B9%E5%BE%81%E7%9A%84%E9%95%BF%E5%BA%A6.md)

Let \(K\) be imaginary quadratic, \(N,e\ge1\), \(J\le G_N=(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^\times\), \(p\nmid30N|\operatorname{disc}K|\) prime, and \(w\mid p\) in \(K\) with \(q=\mathrm Nw\). Let \(\mathcal A_{\ell,\iota}\) be a class of allowed rank-three Galois representations for these \(K,N,e\) and field isomorphism \(\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C\), used consistently in the following inputs. In the project it comprises `Adm3U_s` (irreducible with the inertia bound, flag \((e,0,-e)\), twisted automorphy and local witnesses) and the `Adm2A` representations \(1\oplus W|_{G_K}\) where \(W\) is irreducible of rank two with a newform and imaginary quadratic restriction witnesses, level \(N|\operatorname{disc}K|\), weights \((e,-e)\) and determinant conditions. [Joint inputs and allowed classes](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/ThresholdArguments/DenseThresholdCases/NumberU3US.lean) · [Rank-two class](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/AutomorphyLifting/AutomorphyDescent/AutNewformW.lean#L14)

For \(O_\ell\)-valued \(\psi\), take \(L_\ell(\psi)\) the length in the [Case I sum](two-auxiliary-primes-suffice-to-sum-case-i-lengths.md), from the \(\psi_G^{-1}\varepsilon_\ell^{-e}\) twist with local zero on finite inertia and decomposition dividing \(\ell N\). Write \(V_\ell=L_\ell/v_{O_\ell}(\ell)\) when finite and \(v_\ell(y)=-\log|y|_\ell/\log\ell\). Assume:

1. `OneCharU_s`. For odd \(\ell\), \(O_\ell,\psi\) satisfying `GD`, \(\iota\), \(g\in G_K\), set \(b_\psi(g)=\varepsilon_\ell(g)^e\psi_G(g)\), \(\tau_\psi(g)=b_\psi(g)+1+b_\psi(g)^{-1}\). If this trace differs from every \(\operatorname{tr}\rho(g)\) in the allowed class, then \(L_\ell(\psi)\) is finite and for some finite set \(X_\psi\) of pairwise nonisomorphic allowed representations,
   $$
   V_\ell(\psi)\le 2\sum_{\rho\in X_\psi} v_\ell\bigl(\operatorname{tr}\rho(g)-\tau_\psi(g)\bigr).
   $$
2. `NumberU3U_s`. For any finite set \(A\) of indices with roots of unity \(z_a\) permuted by each complex field automorphism, there is a nonzero integer \(D_p\) with \(\log|D_p|\le c_5|A|[N(e+1)]^{A_0}\log p\), \(c_5,A_0\) depending only on \(K\). For each odd \(\ell\ne p\), \(\iota\), and arithmetic Frobenius lift \(\Phi\) at a place over \(w\), take \(g=\Phi^{-1}\). For any finite sets \(X_a\subset\mathcal A_{\ell,\iota}\), pairwise nonisomorphic within each \(X_a\), the differences measuring \(\operatorname{tr}\rho(g)-\iota^{-1}(q^{-e}z_a+1+q^ez_a^{-1})\) are nonzero and their \(\ell\)-adic norms multiply to at least \(|D_p|_\ell\). [Integer construction](a-frobenius-trace-difference-product-is-a-nonzero-integer.md)

These imply `PropGK`: the bound on \(\log|D_p|\) holds with \(|A|\) replaced by \(\varphi([G_N:J])\) and whenever at odd \(\ell\ne p\) one \(O_\ell\)-valued character of kernel \(J\) satisfies `GD`, all of them have finite lengths and \(\sum_{\ker\psi=J}V_\ell(\psi)\le2v_\ell(D_p)\).

**Proof.** Take \(A\) all complex characters \(\chi\) of kernel \(J\) and \(z_\chi=\chi(q)^{-1}\), defined since \(q\) is prime to \(N\). Coefficient automorphisms preserve the kernel and permute the labelled roots, as required. The count is zero if none exist, and otherwise \(G_N/J\) is cyclic of order \([G_N:J]\) by a faithful character, so the count is its totient.

For a given coefficient ring with one such \(\psi_0\) satisfying `GD`, its values include all roots of order \([G_N:J]\) since it is faithful on the quotient. Hence composing with \(\iota\) identifies the \(O_\ell\)-valued characters of kernel \(J\) with \(A\). Each is a power of \(\psi_0\) prime to the order, preserving the order, the sign (also for odd order, which forces sign \(+1\)), the condition on the \(\ell\)-component and the kernel of the character after removing that component. Thus `GD` holds for each.

At \(\Phi\), reduction of prime-to-\(p\) roots shows the exponent \(q\) on \(\mu_N\) and all \(\ell\)-power roots. Therefore \(\tau_\psi(\Phi^{-1})=q^{-e}\psi(q)^{-1}+1+q^e\psi(q)\), the preimage of the trace prescribed by \(z_\chi\). Taking a singleton in input 2 shows nonvanishing for each allowed trace, then input 1 supplies the sets \(X_\psi\) and finite lengths. Feed those sets into input 2 and apply \(-\log(\cdot)/\log\ell\). The trace valuations sum to at most \(v_\ell(D_p)\), so the length sum is at most twice this. For a number field other than an imaginary quadratic field, `GD` is impossible and \(D_p=1\) suffices. This gives the general quantifier of `PropGK`.
