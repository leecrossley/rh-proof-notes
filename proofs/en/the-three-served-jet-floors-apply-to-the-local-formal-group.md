# The three served jet floors apply to the local formal group

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix an admitted local formal-group model at $p$, a component $v$ and $0\le j<m$. The component's standard conditions give $p\ge5$, even length $2\nu\ge2$, a top set $T$ and dominant integral initial weights $\lambda_s$. Write $N_j=k+j(t+1)$, and let $\nu_s(a)$ be the local Young diagram's row lengths at layer $j+1$ in one-based numbering. Assume the [scaled power-class comparison](the-scaled-local-cofactor-series-belongs-to-the-frobenius-power-class.md) for all parameter-integral test series. Then the cofactor floor of the unoperated local test is $g_A(v,j+1)$, and it also has the second floor $g_B(v,j+1)-A_v$, where $A_v$ is the sum of even top gaps. With the positive Hecke modification of degree $1\le i\le r$, the floor is $g_B(v,j+1)$. This is the triple `VolumeRatio₂`, `PlacewiseLowerBoundEvenShift`, `RestBU` used in the [one-layer operator estimate](the-zero-hecke-term-pays-for-its-own-valuation-loss.md). At a top slot $s$, the height-one columns number $a_s=\nu_s(1)-\nu_s(2)=\lambda_s(1)-\lambda_s(2)$, since the same weight shift occurs in the first two rows. Off $T$ there are no designated top factors. The remaining factor count $c_s$ is therefore $\nu_s(2)$ on $T$ and $\nu_s(1)$ off $T$. The degree $L_s$ of the remaining factors is $\sum_a\nu_s(a)-a_s$, by counting the cells of the diagram by columns and by rows. These are `al`, `cap` and `lsz`. The slot equivalence to $\mathbb Z/(2\nu)$ preserves parity and identifies the positive representative of an opposite-parity difference with reduction modulo $2\nu$. With these substitutions the two gains and the digit loss in the [jet calculation](the-three-entry-bounds-pass-to-every-framed-coefficient.md) are precisely `gA`, `gB` and `de`. The local coefficient ring is a rank-one valuation ring. The normalized absolute value can be extended isometrically to an algebraically closed normed field, in which we choose the root $\varpi^{p^2-1}=p$. The source constructs the norm using $p$ as the reference value. Indeed $p^{-1}$ cannot lie in the local integer ring because intersecting with the base fraction field would put it in the base DVR, where $p$ has positive uniformizer exponent. The extended norm has $\|p\|=p^{-1}$ and cuts out the original integer ring on the original coefficient field. Use this extension in the power-class comparison, so that the norm estimates of the framed result apply. Each cofactor is a product of precisely those minors in the cyclic slot coordinates, with any transpose of the array handled by renaming variables. Restriction to these coordinates leaves its derivative pairing unchanged, since every derivative variable belongs to the chosen component. If a chosen column repeats, the cofactor is zero. Otherwise the weighted polynomial is the ordinary cofactor times the prescribed $p$-power whose exponent counts the selected output columns in the corresponding low-index set, with the complementary count on even slots. The index-wise count equals the count of the image because the column maps are injective. Thus the relation of this $p$-power to the framed estimate is the one already proved there. If $x$ is any original cofactor coefficient and its image has norm at most $p^{-g}$, then $x^b p^{-a}$ is in the original integer ring for each $b>0$, $a\in\mathbb Z$ with $a\le bg$: its extended norm is at most $p^{-bg+a}\le1$. This is the source's `geC` bound. Applying this descent to every choice of columns and every auxiliary coefficient gives the three claimed local floors.
