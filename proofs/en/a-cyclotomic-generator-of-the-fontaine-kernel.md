# A cyclotomic generator of the Fontaine kernel

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E5%88%86%E5%9C%86%E5%85%83%E7%94%9F%E6%88%90Fontaine%E7%89%B9%E5%8C%96%E7%9A%84%E6%A0%B8.md)

Let \(C=\mathbb C_\ell\), \(A=W(\mathcal O_{C^\flat})\), and \(\theta:A\to\mathcal O_C\) the Fontaine map taking \([x]\) to the multiplicative untilt \(x^\sharp\). Choose compatible primitive roots \(\zeta_{\ell^n}\), let \(\varepsilon=(1,\zeta_\ell,\zeta_{\ell^2},\ldots)\) in the tilt, and put \(e=\varepsilon^{1/\ell}\), \(\xi=1+[e]+\cdots+[e]^{\ell-1}\). Then \(\ker\theta=(\xi)\). Use the perfect tilt valuation ring with \(v(x)=v(x^\sharp)\), and the separated, complete Witt ring \(A\) with residue ring \(\mathcal O_{C^\flat}\) modulo \(\ell\). [Lean statement](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/FontaineThetaEqZeroIffFontaineXiElementDvd.lean#L19) · [Witt facts](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/ReciprocityTheory/DualLattices/AinfFacts.lean#L20) · [Scholze, *Perfectoid Spaces*, Lemma 3.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.4914#page=15) · [Bhatt–Morrow–Scholze, *Integral p-adic Hodge Theory*, Example 3.16](https://www.math.uni-bonn.de/people/scholze/integralpadicHodge.pdf#page=25)

**Proof.** Choose compatible roots of \(\ell\) to obtain \(\lambda^\sharp=\ell\), and let \(q=\ell-[\lambda]\in\ker\theta\). If \(\theta(a)=0\), write \(a=[\bar a]+\ell a_1\). Then \(\bar a^\sharp\in\ell\mathcal O_C\), so \(v(\bar a)\ge1=v(\lambda)\); hence \(-\lambda\) divides \(\bar a\). Lift the quotient to write \(a=q b_0+\ell a'\). Since \(\mathcal O_C\) is \(\ell\)-torsion-free, \(a'\in\ker\theta\). Iterate and sum the resulting \(\ell\)-adic series to get \(a=q\sum_j\ell^j b_j\).

The geometric sum shows \(\theta(\xi)=0\), so \(\xi=qu\) for \(u\in A\). Modulo \(\ell\), \(\bar\xi=(e-1)^{\ell-1}\), \(\bar q=-\lambda\). To compute the valuation, a primitive \(\ell^j\)-th root \(\zeta\) reduces to \(1\) in the residue field. For each \(a\) prime to \(\ell\), \((\zeta^a-1)/(\zeta-1)\) is a unit by its geometric sum, which reduces to \(a\). Evaluating \(\Phi_{\ell^j}(1)=\prod_{\ell\nmid a}(1-\zeta^a)=\ell\) gives \(v(\zeta-1)=1/(\ell^{j-1}(\ell-1))\). Now \((e-1)^\sharp\) is the limit of \((\zeta_{\ell^{n+1}}-1)^{\ell^n}\), each of valuation \(1/(\ell-1)\), so the limit has that valuation. Hence \(v(\bar u)=v(\bar\xi)-v(\lambda)=0\). Lift an inverse of \(\bar u\), then use the \(\ell\)-adic geometric series to see that \(u\) is a unit of \(A\). Thus \((\xi)=(q)\).

`kerTheta` proves the first generator, `xiNu` compares valuations, and `ker_theta_eq_span_xi` and `theta_eq_zero_iff_xi_dvd` give the cyclotomic form needed for [divided powers](uniform-denominators-in-de-rham-truncations.md).
