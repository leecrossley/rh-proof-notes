# Two odd-rank lifts recover the even-rank Galois representation

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

## Statement

Let $E$ be a CM number field, $\Omega$ an algebraic closure, $\ell$ a prime, and $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$ a field isomorphism. Let $Q$ be a set of split finite places of $E$ with the following density property. For every finite set $T$ of finite places, the inverses of residue-field Frobenius lifts in $\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/E)$ at places of $Q$ outside $T$ and prime to $\ell$ form a dense subset, allowing all extensions of the valuation to $\Omega$. This is `GoodGuard`. For a definite unitary automorphic representation $U$ of rank $n\ge1$, write $\lambda_\sigma$ for its dominant weight at each complex embedding $\sigma$. For a Satake multiset $B$ of $U$ at a place $w$ of $Q$, the target local data are
$$
S_w=q_w^{(n-1)/2}B,\qquad
J_\tau=\{k-(\lambda_{\iota\circ\tau})_k:0\le k<n\}
\quad(\tau:E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell).
$$
The local comparison $C_w(r;S_w,J)$ means the unramified comparison outside $\ell$, and crystalline and de Rham comparison, specified jumps and Weil–Deligne polynomials at all inducing bridges above $\ell$, as in the [character removal lemma](a-cross-multiplied-polynomial-identity-splits-off-character-lines.md#preserving-the-local-comparison). Call $r$ weakly attached over $Q$ if it is continuous and semisimple and has the unramified comparison for every Satake multiset at all but finitely many places of $Q$ prime to $\ell$. [Density, weak attachment and spherical comparison](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/CoreAtQIffRowQ.lean#L22)

Assume the two-lift statement `TwoUp E n`, as in the [theta construction](two-theta-lifts-share-a-twist-and-have-disjoint-characters.md), for every such $U$ and every choice of $w_0\in Q$. Assume for every odd $d$ that each rank-$d$ definite unitary automorphic representation has a weakly attached Galois representation over $Q$, and that this representation satisfies the comparison for every Satake multiset at every place of $Q$. Finally use the following two inputs. At all but finitely many split places every definite unitary automorphic representation has a Satake multiset. For each algebraic Hecke character $\psi$ of $E$ with integral infinity exponent $p(\sigma)$ there is a continuous Galois character $L(\psi,p)$, satisfying
$$
C_w\big(L(\psi,p);\{\psi(w)\},\{-p(\iota\circ\tau)\}_\tau\big)
$$
whenever $\psi$ is unramified at $w$. Equality of the associated Galois characters for two algebraic Hecke characters implies equality of the Hecke characters. This last input is `CharEx`, including the rank-one comparison at places above $\ell$. [Spherical at almost every split place](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/SplitLineSubtraction/PSatAE.lean#L17) · [Algebraic character comparison and injectivity](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/SplitLineSubtraction/SubtractOf.lean#L136) · [Source of the character input](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/SplitLineSubtraction/SubtractHolds.lean#L53)

Then for each $U$ of rank $n$ there is a weakly attached $r$ over $Q$ that satisfies $C_w(r;S_w,J)$ for every $w\in Q$ and every Satake multiset at $w$. Every weakly attached representation for the same $U$ satisfies the same comparisons. This is the even-rank implication `subtractQ_of`. [Even-rank descent](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/CoreAtQIffRowQ.lean#L195)

## Uniqueness on the dense Frobenius set

Suppose $r,r'$ are weakly attached to the same automorphic representation. Outside the union of the two exceptional finite sets and the finite set where a split Satake multiset fails to exist, the two Frobenius characteristic polynomials agree: choose one Satake multiset and use it in both comparisons. Apply injectivity of $\iota$ to get equality of the characteristic polynomials over $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. Their coefficients are continuous functions of a Galois element, so the equality on the dense set of inverse Frobenius lifts extends to every Galois element. The semisimple character criterion in the [character removal proof](a-cross-multiplied-polynomial-identity-splits-off-character-lines.md#finding-an-invariant-line) gives $r\simeq r'$. Thus whenever a weakly attached representation has the comparison at a chosen place, all weakly attached representations have it there, since a representation isomorphism induces isomorphisms on the period invariants, filtrations and Weil–Deligne parameters. This also upgrades the odd-rank input from existence of one compatible representation to compatibility of every weakly attached representation. ## Recovering a representation at a prescribed place

Fix $w_0\in Q$. Use the two lifts of $U$ with common twist $\kappa$ and two disjoint character lists $\psi_{1,i}$ and $\psi_{2,i}$. They have a common odd rank $n+t$, and $\kappa$ and all the characters of the first list are unramified at $w_0$. Let $r_1,r_2$ be the odd-rank Galois representations of the lifts, put $a_{j,i}=L(\psi_{j,i},p_{j,i})$, and let $\mu=L(\kappa,m)$. The Galois character lists are disjoint because of injectivity of $L$ on the Hecke characters. [Using the lifts at one place](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/CoreAtQIffRowQ.lean#L103)

At a place of $Q$ outside a finite set, $w\nmid\ell$, the characters are unramified, the input has a Satake multiset $B$, and the two lifts have the Frobenius comparisons. Set $S=q_w^{(n-1)/2}B$. The eigenvalues of $r_j(g)$ at an inverse Frobenius lift $g$ are $\kappa(w)^{-1}S$ and the extra entries $\psi_{j,i}(w)$, all after applying $\iota$. The value of $a_{j,i}(g)$ after applying $\iota$ is $\psi_{j,i}(w)$. It follows by multiplying the two multisets with the other list that
$$
\operatorname{charpoly}(r_1(g))\prod_i(X-a_{2,i}(g))
 =\operatorname{charpoly}(r_2(g))\prod_i(X-a_{1,i}(g)).
$$
This equality over $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ extends to all $g$, by the density hypothesis and continuity of each coefficient of the two products. Apply the [character removal lemma](a-cross-multiplied-polynomial-identity-splits-off-character-lines.md). It gives a continuous semisimple representation $s$ of rank $n$ and $r_1\simeq s\oplus\bigoplus_i a_{1,i}$, retaining the local comparisons carried by that summand. [Frobenius to polynomial identity](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/CoreAtQIffRowQ.lean#L52) · [Removing the first character list](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/CoreAtQIffRowQ.lean#L139)

Consider any $w\in Q$ where $\kappa$ and the first list are unramified, including $w_0$. For every Satake multiset $B$ of $U$ there, the first lift gives the multiset with the extras from that list. Its filtration jumps are $J_\tau+m(\iota\circ\tau)$ and the extra jumps $-p_{1,i}(\iota\circ\tau)$. Remove the extra eigenvalues and jumps by the lemma. This yields
$$
C_w(s;\ \kappa(w)^{-1}S,\ (J_\tau+m(\iota\circ\tau))_\tau).
$$
Now tensor by $\mu$. The $C_w$ data for $\mu$ multiply the eigenvalues by $\kappa(w)$ and translate the jumps by $-m(\iota\circ\tau)$. This follows on Frobenius matrices from scalar multiplication. At a place above $\ell$, both representations are crystalline and de Rham, so comparison in the corresponding period rings respects tensor product. The de Rham filtration on a tensor by a line translates by the jump of that line. The Weil–Deligne parameter likewise tensors by the one-dimensional parameter, giving the same eigenvalue rule, with $-\deg g$ in the Weil polynomial. The two operations cancel the two shifts of the lift. Consequently $s\otimes\mu$ has $C_w(s\otimes\mu;S,J)$. It is continuous and semisimple, and satisfies the required local comparison at $w_0$ and weak attachment at almost all places in $Q$. ## Getting one representation at all the places

The density property ensures $Q$ is nonempty. Construct $r$ using one place of $Q$. For every other $w\in Q$, run the same construction with $w$ as the prescribed place to obtain $r_w$. Both $r$ and $r_w$ are weakly attached to the same $U$. The uniqueness argument gives $r\simeq r_w$, so $r$ carries the local conclusions for every $w$. Only the auxiliary lift choices depended on the place. [Comparing the placewise constructions](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/CoreAtQIffRowQ.lean#L200)

The spherical row uses the infinitesimal multiset with entries $(n-1)/2-k+(\lambda_\sigma)_k$. Subtracting these entries from $(n-1)/2$ gives $J_\tau$. Its normalized unramified Weil parameter has characteristic polynomial $\prod_{z\in S_w}(X-z^{-\deg g})$, which is the same normalization as $C_w$. Thus the result supplies the four spherical clauses in the project, namely unramified eigenvalues, de Rham jumps, crystallinity and the Weil–Deligne polynomials.
