# The auxiliary ratio already has a root in the cyclotomic field

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E8%BE%85%E5%8A%A9%E6%AF%94%E5%80%BC%E7%9A%84%E6%A0%B9%E5%B7%B2%E7%BB%8F%E5%9C%A8%E5%88%86%E5%9C%86%E5%9F%9F%E9%87%8C.md)

Fix $M,F>0$ with $M\mid F$, $K_j=\mathbb Q(\mu_j)\subset\Omega=\overline{\mathbb Q}$, $U=G_{K_F}$, prime $r$, and the prime parameter $\ell$. Call $d>0$ admissible if squarefree with each prime factor $p\equiv1\bmod F$, $p\ne r$, and $\operatorname{ord}_p(r)$ prime to $\ell$. Let $x_d\in K_{Fd}^\times$ for every such $d$. For $qd$ admissible with $q$ prime and $q\nmid d$, take $s(d,q)\ge0$. Assume for each $\tau$ fixing $K_{Fd}$ and generating the action on $\mu_q$, and each $\Phi$ acting by $q$ on $\mu_{Fd}$,
$$
\prod_{i=0}^{q-2}\tau^i(x_{qd})
=\left(x_d/\Phi^{-1}(x_d)\right)^{s(d,q)}.
$$
This is the norm part of `Sys`. Its additional unit, congruence and prime-to-$\ell$ conditions enter [the value transfer](transferring-a-kummer-value-from-frobenius-to-inertia.md).

For each admissible prime $p$ choose $\tau_p$ fixing all roots of order prime to $p$ and generating the action on $\mu_p$. Put $D_p(w)=\prod_{i=0}^{p-2}\tau_p^i(w)^i$. For a list $L$ of distinct admissible primes let $d=\prod_{p\in L}p$, $D_L$ the composite of these operators and $y_L=D_L(x_d)$; the empty composite is the identity. Then for each $g\in U$, $g(y_L)/y_L$ is an $M$-th power in $K_{Fd}^\times$.

**Proof.** Over the abelian cyclotomic fields the operators are multiplicative, commute with Galois and commute with one another. For fixed $y$, the ratio of $gh$ is $g(h(y)/y)\,g(y)/y$, so the set of elements giving an $M$-th power in the field is a subgroup. It contains the fixing subgroup of $K_{Fd}$. The remaining group $\operatorname{Gal}(K_{Fd}/K_F)$ is the product of the unit groups modulo the $p\mid d$ by the Chinese remainder theorem, generated by the $\tau_p$ in their respective factors. Thus it suffices to prove the assertion at each $\tau_q$.

Induct on the list length. The empty list lies in $K_F$. For $q\in L$, write $L'=L\setminus\{q\}$, $d'=d/q$, $y'=D_{L'}(x_{d'})$, $w=D_{L'}(x_{qd'})$, and $y_L=D_q(w)$ by commutation. Take $\Phi$ acting as $q$ on $\mu_{Fd'}$; $\Phi^{-1}\in U$ since $q\equiv1\bmod F$. Induction gives $Z_0^M=\Phi^{-1}(y')/y'$ in $K_{Fd'}$. Cancelling successive factors of $D_q$ and using $\tau_q^{q-1}=1$ on the field gives
$$
\frac{\tau_q(y_L)}{y_L}
=\frac{w^{q-1}}{\prod_{i=0}^{q-2}\tau_q^i(w)}
=w^{q-1}\left(\frac{\Phi^{-1}(y')}{y'}\right)^{s(d',q)}
=\left(w^{(q-1)/M} Z_0^{s(d',q)}\right)^M .
$$
The middle equality moves $D_{L'}$ past the norm product and uses the assumed norm relation. The last exponent is integral since $M\mid F\mid q-1$, and the root lies in $K_{Fd}$. This proves the claim on generators. It supplies the roots for [the additive Kummer character](additive-characters-from-kummer-root-ratios.md), and the explicit root will be reduced at the new auxiliary prime for the next step.

`di_gen` reduces to generators, `di_step` writes the explicit root, and `derInv` combines the induction with the commuting-operator lemma `derBasic`. The norm relation in the actual system comes from `laxEx` and `sysOfLax`.
