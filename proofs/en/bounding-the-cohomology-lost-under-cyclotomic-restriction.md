# Bounding the cohomology lost under cyclotomic restriction

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%88%B0%E5%88%86%E5%9C%86%E5%B1%82%E6%97%B6%E4%B8%A2%E5%A4%B1%E7%9A%84%E4%B8%8A%E5%90%8C%E8%B0%83%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E4%BC%B0%E8%AE%A1.md)

## Statement

Let $O$ be a discrete valuation ring, $G$ a topological group, and $U$ a normal subgroup with abelian quotient $G/U$ topologically generated by $s$ elements. Let $M$ be a discrete $O$-module with continuous scalar action $\alpha:G\to O^\times$. Suppose every cyclic submodule of $M$ has finite length and $M^G$ has finite length. Then
$$
\operatorname{length}_O\ker\bigl(H^1(G,M)\longrightarrow H^1(U,M)\bigr)
\le s\,\operatorname{length}_O(M^G)
$$
for continuous cohomology. This is the discrete valuation ring case of `kerResAb`. [Restriction kernel](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/SelmerTransport/KerResAb.lean)

## Evaluate the cocycles on generators

Let $Z_0$ be the module of continuous cocycles vanishing on $U$. A class in the restriction kernel has a representative whose restriction to $U$ is the coboundary of some $m\in M$. Subtract the coboundary $g\mapsto(\alpha(g)-1)m$ on $G$ to obtain a representative in $Z_0$. Evaluation on lifts of the $s$ generators of $G/U$ injects $Z_0$ into $M^s$. Indeed the zero set of a continuous cocycle is a closed subgroup, so if it contains these lifts and $U$ it is all of $G$.

For $g,h\in G$, the commutator $[g,h]$ lies in $U$, and $\alpha([g,h])=1$ since $\alpha$ has abelian image. Thus $z(gh)=z([g,h]hg)=z(hg)$. Expanding this equality by the cocycle rule gives
$$
(\alpha(g)-1)z(h)=(\alpha(h)-1)z(g).
$$
For fixed $g$ the right-hand side is a coboundary as a function of $h$. Therefore each scalar $\alpha(g)-1$ kills the image of $Z_0$ in cohomology.

## Take the length of a scalar quotient

Write $(b)$ for the ideal generated by the scalars $\alpha(g)-1$. The restriction kernel is a quotient of $Z_0/bZ_0$ and $M[b]=M^G$. For any finite-length submodule $F\subseteq Z_0$, additivity of length in the two short exact sequences for multiplication by $b$ gives
$$
\operatorname{length}(F/bF)=\operatorname{length}(F[b])\le s\,\operatorname{length}(M^G).
$$
The inequality uses $F[b]\subseteq (M[b])^s$. The image of $F$ in $Z_0/bZ_0$ thus has length at most this bound. Any finite set in $Z_0/bZ_0$ has lifts generating a finite-length $F$, since the elements lie in $M^s$ and each cyclic submodule of $M$ has finite length. If the quotient had a strict submodule chain longer than the bound, choosing an element in each successive difference would produce a finitely generated submodule of length beyond it. This proves the claimed inequality. [Length via finite submodules](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/SelmerTransport/LenCokerLe.lean)

## Application to a fixed odd-order character component

Use the host conventions of the positive-sign cohomology sum. Fix an admissible $N,t\ge2$, a primitive character $\chi$ of conductor $Q$, an odd prime $\ell\in B_\chi$ and a layer $j\ge0$. Assume `LayerLe` with bound $B\ge0$. In every cyclotomic layer of degree $\ell^j$ over $\mathbb Q(\zeta_{\ell Q})$, the localization kernels for $1\le a<t$ have finite lengths summing to at most $B$. Take a character $\psi$ modulo $N$ trivial on the host fixed group and whose component `chiP` at $\ell$ has odd order. The host property `hostOdd` makes the conductor of $\psi$ divide $Q$. [Positive-sign cohomology sums](positive-sign-cohomology-bound.md)

Write $\ell Q=\ell^v Q'$ with $v\ge1$ and $(\ell,Q')=1$. Set $L_0=\mathbb Q(\zeta_{\ell Q})$ and $L_1=\mathbb Q(\zeta_{\ell^{v+j}Q'})$. Then $[L_1:L_0]=\ell^j$ and $[L_1:\mathbb Q]\le b_0=\ell Q\ell^j$. Restrict to $G_{L_1}$, where $\psi$ is trivial and the coefficient twist $\psi^{-1}\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}^{-a}$ becomes $\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}^{-a}$. Global and local restriction are compatible, so a class in the original localization kernel maps into the layer localization kernel. Extending coefficients scales length by the ramification index, which cancels when we use normalized lengths. The abelian quotient $\operatorname{Gal}(L_1/\mathbb Q)$ is generated by at most $b_0$ elements, so the preceding inequality bounds the kernel of restriction by $b_0$ times the normalized invariant length. [Choice of field](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/HeightAndVanishing/IvField.lean) · [Restriction comparison](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/CharacterLevelBounds/LiftGens.lean)

The invariant bound `H0_local_card_bounded_of_cls_ker_le_ker` states that when $\psi$ factors through cyclotomic action modulo $N$ and $a\ge1$, the normalized global invariant length is at most
$$
\nu(\ell,N,a)=
\begin{cases}
\max(v_\ell(N),1)+v_\ell(a), & \ell\mid N\ \text{or}\ \ell-1\mid a,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$
In particular $\nu(\ell,N,a)\le 2Na$. The project's $V(\ell,a,\psi)$ requires local vanishing at primes above $\ell N$, so is bounded by the length with local conditions above $\ell$ alone. Combining the image and kernel bounds gives
$$
V(\ell,a,\psi)\le\operatorname{length}\operatorname{Sha}(L_1,\ell,a)+2b_0Na.
$$
Upon summing over $1\le a<t$, `LayerLe` controls the first sum and $2b_0Nt^2\le(Nt)^{b_0+3}$ controls the second, using $2b_0\le2^{b_0+1}\le(Nt)^{b_0+1}$. This gives the fixed-character sum bound in `CountIV`, with finite lengths at every twist.
