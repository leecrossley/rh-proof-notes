# Choosing one Frobenius for two value conditions

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E9%80%89%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AAFrobenius%E5%90%8C%E6%97%B6%E6%A3%80%E6%B5%8B%E4%B8%A4%E4%B8%AA%E5%8F%96%E5%80%BC%E6%9D%A1%E4%BB%B6.md)

Let \(\ell\) be an odd prime, \(O\) a DVR finite over \(\mathbb Z_\ell\) with uniformizer \(\pi\), \(W=\operatorname{Frac}(O)/O\), \(G=G_{\mathbb Q}\). Take \(m\ge1\), \(N_0\ge1\) prime to \(\ell\), \(F=N_0\ell^m\), \(U=G_{\mathbb Q(\mu_F)}\), \(\alpha:G\to O^\times\) trivial modulo \(\ell^m\) on \(U\), equal to \(-1\bmod\ell^m\) on inversions of \(\mu_F\), and with \(\alpha(g)-\varepsilon_\ell(g)\) a unit for some \(g\). Put \(\beta=\varepsilon_\ell\alpha^{-1}\). Given prime \(r\) prime to \(F\), nonnegative \(n,n'\), and \(D\ge1\), suppose \(\nu,c:G\to W\) restrict to locally constant additive homomorphisms killed by \(\ell^m\) on \(U\), with conjugation by \(\beta,\alpha\), respectively, and vanishing on inertia outside \(n,n'\), respectively. If \(c|_U\ne0\), there exist a prime \(q\nmid D\) with \(q\equiv1\bmod F\), \(q\ne r\), \(\ell\nmid\operatorname{ord}_q(r)\) and a Frobenius lift \(\Phi\) at \(q\) with \(c(\Phi)\ne0\) and \(\nu(U)\subset O\nu(\Phi)\). [`pick`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PrimitiveFrobeniusFixed/AddPowNsmul.lean#L175)

**Proof.** Let \(M=\ell^m\) and choose \(\rho^M=r\). The homomorphism \(\kappa(u)=u(\rho)/\rho\) from \(U\) into \(\mu_M\) has open normal kernel \(H\subset G\); indeed \(\kappa(gug^{-1})=g(\kappa(u))\), since \(g^{-1}(\rho)/\rho\in\mu_M\) is fixed by \(U\). If an additive map \(a\) on \(U\) has \(M\)-torsion values, conjugates by \(\gamma\), and vanishes on \(H\), it factors through \(\kappa\). Conjugation there is cyclotomic, so \((\gamma(g)-\varepsilon_\ell(g))a(u)=0\) for all \(g,u\). [`radEqv`](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PrimitiveFrobeniusFixed/RadEqv.lean#L22)

Let \(B=\ker(c|_U)\). It is open, and \(H\not\subset B\) by the preceding identity and the unit difference for \(\alpha\). The compact image \(\nu(U)\) is finite. Its cyclic \(O\)-spans are comparable in \(W\), so choose \(P=O\nu(u_0)\) containing all the values. If \(P=0\), take \(K=B\). Otherwise take \(K=\ker(U\xrightarrow{\nu}P/\pi P)\), which is open. A value outside \(K\) generates \(P\). If \(H\subset K\), apply the previous identity to the nonzero map \(U\to P/\pi P\). On an inversion of \(\mu_F\), \(\beta(g)-\varepsilon_\ell(g)=\beta(g)(1-\alpha(g))\) is a unit since \(\ell\) is odd. This would kill that map, a contradiction.

Let \(C=G_{\mathbb Q(\mu_{F\ell})}\). For \(u\) acting by \(1+F\) at level \(F\ell\), and \(g\) inverting there, the conjugacy identity gives \(\kappa(gug^{-1}u)=1\). Yet the cyclotomic exponent of this element is \((1+F)^2\equiv1+2F\not\equiv1\bmod F\ell\). Thus \(H\not\subset C\). The union of any two proper subgroups of \(H\) cannot cover \(H\): if each of two witnesses lies in the other subgroup, their product lies in neither. Suppose \(K,B,C\) covered \(H\). Choose \(x\in H\setminus(B\cup C)\), \(y\in H\setminus(K\cup C)\); then \(x\in K,y\in B\). Their product \(xy\) must lie in \(C\). Now \(x^2y\) cannot lie in \(K\) (it would put \(y\) in \(K\)), in \(C\) (it would put \(x\) in \(C\)), or in \(B\) (it would give \(x^2\in B\), and \(x^M\in B\) with \(M\) odd would give \(x\in B\)). Thus \(H\setminus(K\cup B\cup C)\) is nonempty and open. Chebotarev supplies a Frobenius in any nonempty open set of the absolute group, with prime avoiding a prescribed finite set. Indeed take a coset of an open normal subgroup inside it, use the corresponding conjugacy class in the finite quotient, and choose a place giving that representative. Apply with the open set just constructed, avoiding primes dividing \(DF\ell r\). This gives \(c(\Phi)\ne0\) and \(\nu(U)\subset O\nu(\Phi)\). The actions on \(\mu_F,\mu_{F\ell}\) give \(q-1=Fh\), \(\ell\nmid h\). Also \(\rho\) is fixed by \(\Phi\), so in the residue field \(\rho^{q-1}=1\), as \(q\nmid r\). Consequently \(r^{N_0h}=1\bmod q\), proving the order condition.
