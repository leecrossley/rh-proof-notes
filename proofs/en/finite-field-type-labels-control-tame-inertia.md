# Finite-field type labels control tame inertia

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%9C%89%E9%99%90%E5%9F%9F%E7%9A%84%E7%B1%BB%E5%9E%8B%E6%A0%87%E7%AD%BE%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E7%BA%A6%E6%9D%9F%E6%B8%A9%E5%92%8C%E6%83%AF%E6%80%A7.md)

## Statement

Let $p$ be one of two prescribed primes, completely split in the CM host $E$, with $16n^2<p$ for $n=r+t$. Let a depth-zero type have label $P\in\mathbb F_p[X]$, monic of degree $r$ with nonzero constant, no repeated irreducible factors, and neither $\chi_J$ nor $\chi_J^{-1}$ as a root. Here $\chi_J\in\mathbb F_p^\times$ labels the tame twist at that layer. For an automorphic representation containing this type, assume `admDZ`, `adm1` and the inertia dictionary `Pend`. At the label place, `admDZ` gives zero monodromy and the root multiset of $P(X)(X-u)^t$ with $u\in\{\chi_J,\chi_J^{-1}\}$, and at the conjugate place the inverse-root multiset. `adm1` gives rank $n$. Both places satisfy `Nc`: wild characteristic polynomial $(X-1)^n$, zero monodromy, and each repeated eigenvalue of a tame inertia generator is a $(p-1)$st root of unity.

## Proof

Let $M$ be the indicated root multiset. It has no zero roots. The roots of $P$ are simple by perfectness and exclude $u$. Thus any repeated root is $u$ with $u^{p-1}=1$. Inversion preserves these properties and the size $n$. The dictionary sends each $x$ to a finite-order complex tame character $\omega_x$ that kills wild inertia, is distinct for distinct $x$, and satisfies $\omega_{xy}=\omega_x\omega_y$. On an inertia element $\sigma$ the characteristic polynomial is $\prod_{x\in M}(X-\omega_x(\sigma))$. On wild inertia this is $(X-1)^n$.

For a tame generator $\gamma$, equality $\omega_x(\gamma)=\omega_y(\gamma)$ makes the characters agree on the dense subgroup generated by $\gamma$ and wild inertia, hence everywhere. Injectivity gives $x=y$, so multiplicities are preserved at $\gamma$. A repeated eigenvalue comes from a repeated root, hence its $(p-1)$st power is $\omega_{x^{p-1}}(\gamma)=1$. Complete splitting gives residue size $p$, and the large-prime condition makes the threshold two. This verifies `Nc` with zero monodromy.

In the [cut](central-idempotents-select-comparable-hecke-summands.md), the good tuple tests both the label and its inverse, giving squarefreeness and the two excluded roots. The place-cover property gives every place over $p$ as a label place or its conjugate.
