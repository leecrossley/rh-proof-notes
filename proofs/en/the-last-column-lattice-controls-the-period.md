# The last column lattice controls the period

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Fix a row $D$, word $u$, used tuple $\mu$, and place $w$ of the common coefficient field $K$ over the rational prime $p$. Assume that $p$ is outside the finite tuple-prime set $Q_p(\mu)$. Let $\phi$ be in the base vector space used to form the lift, and let $F$ be in the test module for $\mu$ and in its specified subspace $V^0(\mu)$. Write $P_{u,\mu}(\phi,F)$ for the complex period denoted `perCol u μ (jN u φ) F`. Normalize the local valuation by $\widetilde v_w=v_w/e_w$, with $e_w=v_w(p)$.

Assume the base and twisted point-integrality conditions. Namely a vector of the last global column lattice specializes to the rational point lattice used in the period, with the prescribed comparison between the complex point and column maps. Also assume `PeriodClassSumWeyl` for the base column and `TwistedPeriodAdmissible` for the nonzero used tuple. The first says that for any vector of the base point lattice and any such test $F$, the period belongs to $K$ and has normalized valuation at least $-v_{\rm DG}$ at every $w$, with $v_{\rm DG}=v_p(\mathrm{DGam})$. The second gives the same conclusion for the corresponding twisted point lattice at primes outside $Q_p(\mu)$. Put $\widetilde v_w(0)=+\infty$.

Suppose the full column lift of $\phi$ in the appropriate chain has a representative $y$ in the last column space which is $a z$ with $z$ in the last localized lattice and $a=0$ or $\widetilde v_w(a)\ge s$. This is the local bound `ColGe`. Then $P_{u,\mu}(\phi,F)$ has a value in $K$ under the chosen embedding and satisfies
$$
\widetilde v_w(P_{u,\mu}(\phi,F))\ge s-v_{\rm DG}.
\tag{1}
$$

For a fixed $F$, the period after the comparison with the point space is linear in the column vector, with the embedding of $K$ on scalars. On a vector of the last global lattice, point integrality followed by the class-period bound gives a $K$-value of valuation at least $-v_{\rm DG}$. This extends to $z$ in the localization. Indeed write $z=\sum_\lambda b_\lambda z_\lambda$ with $z_\lambda$ global lattice vectors and $b_\lambda$ integral at $w$. The same combination of their $K$-values represents the complex period for $z$, and the bound holds by the ultrametric inequality. In the base case this uses the untwisted point comparison. For $\mu\ne\mu_0$ the identifications of the test module and of the specialized point give the twisted period exactly, and the tuple-prime condition allows the twisted bound at $w$.

The tie between the last column and the full period identifies the resulting linear functional at $y$ with $P_{u,\mu}(\phi,F)$. Thus that period is $a$ times the $K$-value just obtained for $z$. The zero case is immediate and otherwise the valuation adds, giving (1). The class denominator that appears here is for the terminal period pairing. In the [column-chain estimate](adding-the-layer-valuations-of-the-theta-lift.md) the choice $\mathrm{sp}=1$ saves precisely one such denominator at the first step. That saving is available to absorb the loss in (1).
