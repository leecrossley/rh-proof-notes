# From eigenvalue ranges to Frobenius trace lists

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E4%BB%8E%E6%9C%AC%E5%BE%81%E5%80%BC%E7%9A%84%E6%A8%A1%E9%95%BF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%E5%BE%97%E5%88%B0Frobenius%E8%BF%B9%E5%88%97%E8%A1%A8.md)

For $n,q\ge1$, weights $m_1,\dots,m_n\in\mathbb Z$, $w\in\mathbb Z$, `EigData` is a finite labelled list of multisets $\beta_s$ of $n$ complex numbers. Every field automorphism of $\mathbb C$ permutes their labels while acting on values. With $a=\sqrt q,\ C=q^{w/2}$, their moduli satisfy $C/a<|b|<aC$ and $\prod_{b\in\beta_s}|b|=C^n$. Each $q^u b$ is integral for integer $u\ge-\min_i m_i$, and $q^u b^{-1}$ integral for $u\ge\max_i m_i$. Classification of a class of $\ell$-adic representations at a place uses a map to the labels with equal labels implying isomorphism, and inverse arithmetic Frobenius has eigenvalues $b^{-1}$ under the chosen field isomorphism $\iota$. The same label works for all lifts at the place. [Eigenvalue and trace structures](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/AdmissibleResidues/WinDefs.lean#L18)

For $e\ge0$ define $t_s=\sum_{b\in\beta_s}(qb)^{-1}$ for rank three with $m=(e-1,-1,-e-1)$, $w=-2$; or $t_s=1+\sum_{b\in\beta_s}b^{-h}$ for rank two at $p\ge1$ with $m=(e,-e),w=0$ and $q=p^h,\ h\ge0$. Then $|t_s|\le\sqrt q+2$, $q^e t_s$ integral, and the list retains the permutations.

**Proof of the bound.** In rank three the three moduli $x_i=|(qb_i)^{-1}|$ have product one and lie in $[1/a,a]$. Choose $u,v$ on the same side of one, including equality, and call the third $z$. Then $(u-1)(v-1)\ge0$ gives $u+v\le1+z^{-1}$; $(a-z)(z-a^{-1})\ge0$ gives $z+z^{-1}\le a+a^{-1}\le a+1$. This bounds the sum by $a+2$. The terms $q^{e-1}b^{-1}$ are integral by the maximum weight $e-1$. In rank two the moduli $u,v$ of $b^{-h}$ have product one and are both at most $a$. Thus $(a-u)(a-v)\ge0$ yields $u+v\le a+a^{-1}$, and adding one gives the bound. Integrality follows from $q^e b^{-h}=(p^e b^{-1})^h$ and integrality of $q^e$. Field automorphisms commute with the formulas, preserving the label permutations. **Application to allowed points.** Let $e\ge1$, $v\mid p$ of the imaginary quadratic field $K$, $q=\mathrm Nv=p^h$, with odd $\ell\ne p$, $p\nmid N|\operatorname{disc}K|$. For `Adm3U_s`, $\rho'=\rho\otimes\varepsilon_\ell^{-1}$ has the shifted weights as above, paired sum $-2$, and preserves the flag condition excluding quotient ratios $\varepsilon_\ell$, as well as inertia away from $\ell$. Apply the `CountKAlt` classification to $\rho'$. Untwisting multiplies the eigenvalues at inverse arithmetic Frobenius by $q^{-1}$, since $\varepsilon_\ell(\Phi)=q$; it also preserves isomorphism classes. In the rank-two case choose $W$ with $\rho\simeq1\oplus W|_{G_K}$, of level $M=N|\operatorname{disc}K|$ and determinant weight zero. `NewAdmAlt` and `CountQAlt` classify $W$ at $p$. Frobenius at $v$ projects modulo rational inertia to the $h$-th power of rational Frobenius, and $W$ kills inertia at $p$. Thus the inverse Frobenius eigenvalues are the $h$-th powers of the rational ones, by triangularizing the matrix to take powers, and the extra trace is $1$. Equal labels give isomorphic $W$'s, hence isomorphic restrictions plus one.

The precise `CountKAlt` input, for a fixed field $K$ and rank $n\ge2$, gives $c,A$ so for strictly decreasing integer weights $|m_i|\le H$ with $m_i+m_{n+1-i}=1-n$, level $M\ge1$, and a place over $p\nmid M|\operatorname{disc}K|$, one list of size at most $c[M(H+1)]^A$ classifies for all $\ell\ne2,p$. `CountQAlt` over $\mathbb Q$ allows any common pair sum $w$ and excludes $p\mid M$, with the same type of bound. We use $n=3,H=e+1,M=N$ and $n=2,H=e,M=N|\operatorname{disc}K|$, respectively. With fixed $K$ these bounds are polynomial in $N(e+1)$. Their disjoint union gives the trace input for the [integer product](a-frobenius-trace-difference-product-is-a-nonzero-integer.md).
