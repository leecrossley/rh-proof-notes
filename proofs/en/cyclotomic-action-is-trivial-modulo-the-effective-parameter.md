# Cyclotomic action is trivial modulo the effective parameter

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E5%88%86%E5%9C%86%E7%94%9F%E6%88%90%E5%85%83%E5%9C%A8%E6%9C%89%E6%95%88%E5%8F%82%E6%95%B0%E6%A8%A1%E4%B8%8A%E4%BD%9C%E7%94%A8%E5%B9%B3%E5%87%A1.md)

Let \(K/\mathbb Q_\ell\) be finite, \(s\) the chosen good cyclotomic level, and \(L\) a finite coefficient field satisfying `Good K s L`, which in particular contains the images of the level-\(s\) field under the required embeddings. For a stable lattice \(T\) in a crystalline representation, its Wach lattice \(N(T)\) is finite free over the product of integral series rings, with Frobenius and cyclotomic action. Write \(X=(1+U)^{\ell^{s-1}}-1\), the monic polynomial with simple zeros at the lower-level cyclotomic roots minus one. The action preserves \(N(T)\) and is trivial modulo \(U\) by the Wach conditions. In fact for every cyclotomic element \(g\) with \(v_\ell(\chi(g)-1)\ge s-1\), one has \((g-1)N(T)\subset XN(T)\). The chosen generator has \(v_\ell(\chi(\gamma)-1)\ge s\), so qualifies.

**Proof.** Fix \(x\in N(T)\) and write \(y=gx-x\) in an integral basis. In each component and at every \(\zeta^{\ell^{s-1}}=1\), expand at \(1+U=\zeta\exp(t)\). The valuation assumption makes \(g\) fix \(\zeta\), and the action on the expansion is \(t\mapsto\chi(g)t\), so the degree-\(k\) term of \(y\) has coefficient \((\chi(g)^k-1)x_k\). By the [standard lower-level lattice](the-standard-wach-expansion-at-lower-cyclotomic-levels.md), there are no negative degrees. The constant term vanishes. Thus the expansion lies in \(t\) times the standard lattice. The expanded integral basis is a basis of this lattice, so each coordinate of \(y\) vanishes to order one at the chosen root. Applying the [root test](testing-integral-power-series-at-cyclotomic-roots.md) to all lower-level roots gives divisibility by \(X\) of every coordinate, hence \(y\in XN(T)\).

In a crystalline extension apply this to the lift of the quotient's unit; the kernel comparison descends the \(X\)-divisibility to the lattice of the subrepresentation, yielding its [second Herr coordinate](crystalline-extensions-have-herr-coordinates-with-bounded-denominators.md).

`ex_of_val` and `shift_mem` give the vanishing at each fixed root, `sub_mem_X_of_val` uses the coordinate test, and `gam_mod_π_mod_X` specializes to the generator in `P2`.
