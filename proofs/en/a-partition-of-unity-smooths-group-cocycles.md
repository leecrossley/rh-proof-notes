# A partition of unity smooths group cocycles

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E5%8D%95%E4%BD%8D%E5%88%86%E8%A7%A3%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E6%8A%8A%E7%BE%A4%E4%BD%99%E9%97%AD%E9%93%BE%E5%8F%98%E6%88%90%E5%BE%AE%E5%88%86%E5%BD%A2%E5%BC%8F.md)

## Statement

Let $L$ be a number field, $N,j\ge1$, and $\Gamma=\mathrm{GL}_N(O_L)$ acting on the Hermitian positive cone $X$ at the infinite places. Suppose a smooth bounded $\phi:X\to\mathbb R$ is invariant under overall positive scaling and $\psi_\gamma(x)=\phi(\gamma^{-1}x)$ form a locally finite sum equal to one. Assume the overlap set $O=\{\gamma:\operatorname{supp}\phi\cap\gamma\operatorname{supp}\phi\ne\varnothing\}$ is finite, with closed supports. Let $H$ be a nonnegative left $\Gamma$-invariant height on $G_\infty=\prod_w\mathrm{GL}_N(\mathbb C)$ and suppose $\|(d\phi)_{gI}\circ g\|\le C_\phi H(g)$. These are the `PU` assumptions. For every invariant real homogeneous $j$-cocycle $c$ of $\Gamma$ there is a smooth closed $j$-form $\omega$ invariant under $\Gamma$ and center scalars of common absolute value, with the same cycle values under simplex integration and $\|\omega_{gI}\circ g\|\le C_c H(g)^j$. This is `SWhit`.

## Producing the form and its cycle values

For labelled forms $\beta$ let $\delta$ be the alternating deletion of labels, and set $K\beta(g_1,\ldots,g_m)=\sum_\gamma\psi_\gamma\beta(\gamma,g_1,\ldots,g_m)$. Local finiteness makes this smooth. Deletions cancel in $\delta K+K\delta$ except for deleting the added label, giving identity because $\sum_\gamma\psi_\gamma=1$. Put $\eta_0=c$ as a constant form and $\eta_{i+1}=dK\eta_i$. Each $\eta_i$ is closed and $\delta$-closed by $d^2=0$, commutation with $\delta$, and $\delta K\eta_i=\eta_i$. At $i=j$, the one remaining label is immaterial by $\delta$-closedness. Equivariance of the translates and invariance of $c$ give arithmetic invariance of the resulting form. Center scalars with common absolute value act by overall scaling, hence preserve the partition and the recursion.

For $0\le i\le j$ put $W_i(g_0,\ldots,g_j)=\int_{[g_{j-i}I,\ldots,g_jI]}\eta_i(g_0,\ldots,g_{j-i})$. Thus $W_0=c$ and $W_j$ evaluates the form. For $a=j-i-1\ge0$, define $B_i$ on $j$ labels using $K\eta_i(g_0,\ldots,g_a)$ on $[g_aI,\ldots,g_{j-1}I]$. In $\delta B_i$, deletions through $a$ give $\delta K\eta_i=\eta_i$ except for the missing term of deletion $a+1$. The later deletions are faces of the simplex with the labels of the integrand fixed. Inserting the missing first face and applying Stokes gives $\delta B_i=W_i+(-1)^aW_{i+1}$. Iterating yields $c=\epsilon W_j+\delta B$ with $\epsilon=\pm1$. Take $\omega=\epsilon\eta_j$ to get the right cycle values. Its integration cochain is closed by Stokes.

## Growth

Expand $\eta_j$ into $c$-coefficients times wedges of $j$ derivatives $d\psi_\gamma$. At $x$, pick $\gamma_0$ with $\psi_{\gamma_0}(x)\ne0$ as the irrelevant final label. A nonzero derivative at $x$ implies membership in the closed support, so each active label after left translating by $\gamma_0^{-1}$ lies in $O\cup\{1\}$. The number and magnitudes of the $c$-coefficients are bounded by finiteness and invariance. At $x=gI$ the norm of each pulled-back derivative is at most $C_\phi H(\gamma^{-1}g)=C_\phi H(g)$. Wedge bounds and the finite sum give $C_cH(g)^j$.
