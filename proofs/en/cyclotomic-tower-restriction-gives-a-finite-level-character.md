# Cyclotomic tower restriction gives a finite-level character

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E6%8A%8A%E5%88%86%E5%9C%86%E5%A1%94%E4%B8%8A%E7%9A%84%E4%B8%8A%E5%90%8C%E8%B0%83%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%8F%98%E6%88%90%E6%9C%89%E9%99%90%E5%B1%82%E7%89%B9%E5%BE%81.md)

Let $E$ be a number field, $G=G_E$, $\ell$ prime, $e\in\mathbb Z$, and $\varepsilon_\ell:G\to\mathbb Z_\ell^\times$ the cyclotomic character. Put $U_j=G_{E(\mu_{\ell^j})}$, $U_\infty=\ker\varepsilon_\ell=\bigcap_j U_j$, $W=\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell$ discrete, and $W(-e)$ with action $\varepsilon_\ell^{-e}$. Let $S$ be places above $\ell$ and $G_{E,S}$ the extension group with no other finite ramification. Let $\Sha(E,e)$ consist of classes in $H^1(G_{E,S},W(-e))$ whose restrictions at decomposition groups over $S$ vanish. Restricting a representative pulled back to $G$ defines an additive character $F_x:U_\infty\to W$ independent of representative. It is injective as a map of all $H^1(G_{E,S},W(-e))$ when $e\ne0$ and either $\ell$ is odd or $E\supset\mu_4$. For $x\in\Sha(E,e)$ the character extends to a continuous additive $F_j:U_j\to W$ for some $j$, vanishing on all finite inertia at that layer, and on decomposition above $\ell$.

**Proof.** The cocycle rule becomes addition on $U_\infty$, and coboundaries vanish there. At inertia outside $\ell$, a pulled-back representative is zero; at decomposition over $\ell$ it is a coboundary, hence zero on the intersection with $U_\infty$. Conjugate choices of a place have the same vanishing, since on $U_\infty$, $f(gug^{-1})=\varepsilon_\ell(g)^{-e} f(u)$ by comparing the cocycle rule for $gu=(gug^{-1})g$. If $F_x=0$, $f$ descends to a continuous cocycle on $V=\varepsilon_\ell(G)$; it is constant on cosets of the kernel. This image is compact and infinite, since its finite images have order at least $\varphi(\ell^j)/[E:\mathbb Q]$. Under the hypothesis each finite image is cyclic, using the subgroup $1\bmod4$ at two. The sets of lifts in $V$ of generators of these quotients are decreasing nonempty compact sets. A common element $\gamma$ has dense powers. Then $\gamma^{-e}\ne1$, else all of infinite $V$ would consist of roots of a fixed polynomial. Multiplication by $\gamma^{-e}-1$ is surjective on $W$ by division in $\mathbb Q_\ell$. Choose $w$ with $(\gamma^{-e}-1)w=f(\gamma)$ on the quotient. Subtract its coboundary; the result vanishes on powers of $\gamma$ and hence everywhere by continuity. This proves injectivity. For extension, choose an open normal $H\le G$ with $H\cap U_\infty\subset\ker F_x$, possible by continuity. In the finite quotient by $H$, the descending images of $U_j$ stabilize to that of $U_\infty$. Indeed a coset meeting all $U_j$ meets their intersection by compactness. The factor of $F_x$ through this image therefore defines a continuous additive extension $F_j$ at a sufficiently high layer. Let $C$ be the union of decomposition groups at all absolute places over $\ell$. For each of the finitely many places of $E$ involved, its conjugate groups form a compact image of $G\times D_v$ under conjugation, so $C$ is compact. The set in $C\cap U_j$ where $F_j\ne0$ is compact by discrete values and is disjoint from $U_\infty$. Hence some later $U_i$ is disjoint from it by the decreasing intersection property. Restrict to $U_i$. At inertia outside $\ell$ no adjustment is needed, since this inertia fixes all $\ell$-power roots and lies in $U_\infty$. This proves the local conditions `XDual`, permitting the finite-layer character to factor through the Hilbert class field.
