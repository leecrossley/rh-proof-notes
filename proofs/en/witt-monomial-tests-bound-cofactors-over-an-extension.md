# Witt monomial tests bound cofactors over an extension

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $p$ be prime, $W$ the Witt ring of the formal system and $K_0=\mathrm{Frac}(W)$. Fix a local layer $j$, a component $v$, a phase $b$ of zero constant term, and a cofactor polynomial $P_{v,I}$ for a column choice $I$. Denote by $L$ the $K_0$-linear functional obtained by multiplying a test series by $e^{-\Phi_v}$, making the prescribed letter substitution, applying $P_{v,I}(\partial)$ at zero and taking a specified auxiliary coefficient. Suppose that for every test series $U_0$ over $K_0$ which is integral over $W$ in formal parameters we have $L(U_0)\in p^{\lceil g\rceil}W$. The conclusion is the `geC` bound with exponent $g$ on $L(U)$ over the coefficient field $K_1$ of the system, whenever $U$ in formal parameters has coefficients in the integer subring $O_1$ of that field. Here the local system supplies the map $W\to O_1$ and the fact that membership in $p^{\lceil g\rceil}O_1$ implies the `geC` bound. Let $\ell(T)$ be the formal logarithm used for the parameter change and $E(Z)$ its composition inverse. Each has vanishing constant term and linear coefficient $1$. For a multiindex $\alpha$ put $U_\alpha(Z)=\prod_a E(Z_a)^{\alpha_a}$. Then $U_\alpha(\ell(T))=T^\alpha$, so $U_\alpha$ is an allowed test over $K_0$. It has order at least $|\alpha|$ because each factor $E(Z_a)$ has order at least one. Put $c_\alpha=[T^\alpha]U(\ell(T))\in O_1$. For $d=\deg_\partial P_{v,I}$, the parameter series minus $\sum_{|\alpha|\le d}c_\alpha T^\alpha$ has order at least $d+1$. Substitute $T_a=E(Z_a)$. Order cannot decrease under substitution by series with zero constant term, by checking monomials. It follows that
$$
U-\sum_{|\alpha|\le d}c_\alpha U_\alpha
$$
has order at least $d+1$. Both multiplication by $e^{-\Phi_v}$ and the letter substitution preserve this lower bound on order. The derivative polynomial has degree at most $d$, so the remainder contributes zero. Because the substitution, phase and differential polynomial are defined over $K_0$, evaluation of each $U_\alpha$ commutes with extension of scalars. The hypothesis gives $L(U_\alpha)=p^{\lceil g\rceil}a_\alpha$ with $a_\alpha\in W$, and hence
$$
L(U)=p^{\lceil g\rceil}\sum_{|\alpha|\le d}c_\alpha a_\alpha\in p^{\lceil g\rceil}O_1.
$$
The divisibility yields `geC` at $g$. This finishes the full-coefficient-field step of the [second-embedding bound](a-second-embedding-orbit-bounds-its-local-cofactors.md).
