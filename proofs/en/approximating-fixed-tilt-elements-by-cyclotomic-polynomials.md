# Approximating fixed tilt elements by cyclotomic polynomials

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E5%88%86%E5%9C%86%E5%A1%94%E7%9A%84%E5%9B%BA%E5%AE%9A%E5%80%BE%E6%96%9C%E5%85%83%E7%B4%A0%E5%8F%AF%E7%94%A8%E6%A0%B9%E7%9A%84%E5%A4%9A%E9%A1%B9%E5%BC%8F%E9%80%BC%E8%BF%91.md)

Let $K_0/\mathbb Q_\ell$ be finite unramified, $K_\infty=K_0(\mu_{\ell^\infty})$, and $H=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell/K_\infty)$. Put $R=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_\ell^\flat}=\varprojlim_{x\mapsto x^\ell}\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_\ell}/\ell$. For compatible roots $\zeta_j$ primitive of order $\ell^j$, take $\epsilon_j=(\zeta_{j+i}\bmod\ell)_{i\ge0}$. Let $R_{\rm alg}$ be the subring generated by all $\epsilon_j$ and the compatible $\ell$-power root sequences of prime-to-$\ell$ roots of unity in $K_0$. For $x\in R^H$ and $m\ge0$, there is $y\in R_{\rm alg}$ with $x-y\in(\epsilon_0-1)^m R$. For $w\in(\mathbb C_\ell^\flat)^H$, $s\ge1$, and $m\ge0$, all sufficiently large $b$ allow $y\in R_{\rm alg}$ with $(\epsilon_s-1)^b w-y\in(\epsilon_s-1)^{b+m}R$.

**Proof.** Choose $a\in K_\infty$ with $|\ell|<|a|<1$, and put $\delta=|\ell|/|a|$. If $z\in\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_\ell}$ is $H$-fixed modulo $\ell$, then $c(h)=(h(z)-z)/\ell$ is an integral continuous cocycle. The [integral trace argument](integral-trace-averaging-kills-cyclotomic-tower-cocycles.md) supplies $b_0$ integral with $ac(h)=h(b_0)-b_0$. Then $z'=z-(\ell/a)b_0$ is fixed and $|z-z'|\le\delta$. The fixed-field result there approximates $z'$ by integral elements of $K_\infty$. Choose one $z''$ with $|z-z''|\le\delta$.

Choose $N$ so large that $\delta^{\ell^N}\le|\epsilon_0-1|^m$, and lift the $N$-th coordinate of $x$ to $z$, to which the preceding paragraph applies. The resulting $z''$ lies in some $\mathcal O_{K_j}=\mathcal O_{K_0}[\zeta_j]$ by the cyclotomic Eisenstein polynomial. Modulo $\ell$, it is a polynomial in $\zeta_j$ and the residue representatives from $K_0$ given by zero and prime-to-$\ell$ roots of unity. These are in the $N$-th coordinate image of $R_{\rm alg}$: use $\epsilon_{j-N}$ for $j\ge N$, or $\epsilon_0^{\ell^{N-j}}$ otherwise. For the prime-to-$\ell$ roots the exponent $\ell$ is invertible on their cyclic group. Thus select $y$ with $y_N=z''\bmod\ell$.

For $v=x-y$, the multiplicative untilt of $\varphi^{-N}v$ is congruent to $z-z''\bmod\ell$, so has absolute value at most $\delta$. The tilt norm of $v$ is its norm to the power $\ell^N$, giving $|v|\le|\epsilon_0-1|^m$. In a valuation ring this is the desired divisibility. For $w$, first multiply by a large enough $(\epsilon_s-1)^b$ to make it integral, and use the first claim at precision $b+m$, since $\epsilon_s-1$ divides $\epsilon_0-1$.

**Period application.** In the [period model](fixed-period-completeness-and-etale-closure.md), the zeroth Witt coordinates of $z\in B^H$ in an $O$-basis belong to $(\mathbb C_\ell^\flat)^H$. Apply the claim with a common $b$; the Teichmüller lifts of the approximate coordinates form $z_1\in B^+$ with $t^b z-z_1\in\mathcal F_{b+m}$. After sufficient Frobenius iterations every approximating polynomial lies in the image of $E/\ell E$. Indeed $\epsilon_j$ iterates to $\epsilon_0=\epsilon_s^{\ell^s}$, represented by $(1+t)^{\ell^s}$; prime-to-$\ell$ roots from $K_0$ correspond to constant component coefficients. Frobenius preserves the image and works on the finitely many polynomial generators simultaneously. Set $z_0=t^{-b}z_1$. Then $z-z_0\in\mathcal F_m$ and some $\varphi^r z_0\in E+\ell B$ since $t^{-1}\in E$. This is the approximation for [the fixed-period quotient](frobenius-minus-one-on-the-fixed-period-quotient.md).

`exists_near_Ohat` gives a finite-level approximation, `Ohat_mem` represents the selected coordinate, and `invariant_approx` controls its norm. `flat_invariant_approx` clears denominators, and `teich_mem_perfection` and `fixSub_approx_perfection` give the period application.
