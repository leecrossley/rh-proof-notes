# Crystalline obstructions in positive weight occur in the cyclotomic ratio

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E6%AD%A3%E6%9D%83%E6%89%A9%E5%BC%A0%E7%9A%84%E6%99%B6%E4%BD%93%E9%9A%9C%E7%A2%8D%E9%9B%86%E4%B8%AD%E5%9C%A8%E5%88%86%E5%9C%86%E6%AF%94%E5%80%BC.md)

## Statement

Let $G=G_K$ for a finite local extension $K/\mathbb Q_\ell$, viewed as an open subgroup of $G_{\mathbb Q_\ell}$. Let $E/\mathbb Q_\ell$ be finite and $B$ a finite-dimensional commutative augmented $E$-algebra with nilpotent kernel. Let $\lambda:G\to B^\times$ be continuous with inertia character $\chi_\ell^w$, $w\ge1$. Take $\sigma$ generating the unramified quotient topologically. Under `LocalPublishedTheorems`, every continuous cocycle $c$ in $B(\lambda)$ can be written $c=c_f+c_{\rm exc}$, with $c_f$ a coboundary after tensoring with $B_{\rm cris}$ and
$$
(\lambda(\sigma)-\chi_\ell(\sigma))c_{\rm exc}(g)=0
$$
for all $g$. For $w\ge2$, one can take $c_{\rm exc}=0$. This identifies the adjacent Frobenius factors in the [ordinary criterion](ordinary-equations-detect-crystalline-lifts.md).

## The subrepresentation supporting an obstruction

If $w\ge2$, an inertia element with infinite-order cyclotomic value gives a scalar difference $\lambda-\chi_\ell$ that is a unit in $B$. Take $\delta=1$ in this case. If $w=1$, set $\delta=\lambda(\sigma)-\chi_\ell(\sigma)$. Because the ratio $\lambda/\chi_\ell$ is unramified, the ideal generated by all differences is $(\delta)$. This follows first for integer powers of $\sigma$ from divisibility of $u^n-1$ by $u-1$, then by continuity and closedness of the finite-dimensional ideal. Put $V=B(\lambda)$ and $W=\operatorname{Ann}_B(\delta)$ with the induced action. An $E$-linear map from $V$ to $E(1)$ is equivariant exactly when it kills $\delta V$. Hence
$$
\dim_E\operatorname{Hom}_G(V,E(1))=\dim_E(V/\delta V)=\dim_E W.
$$
On $W$ the character is cyclotomic when $w=1$, and $W=0$ otherwise. So the analogous Hom dimension for $W$ also equals $\dim_E W$.

## Comparison of finite cocycles

Both $V,W$ are de Rham with filtrations by one-dimensional positive-weight characters, using the unramified twist of $\chi_\ell^w$ and the powers of the augmentation ideal. For such a representation $Q$, put $d=[K:\mathbb Q_\ell]$. Positive weights give $H^0=0$. Bloch–Kato gives $\dim_E H_f^1=d\dim_E Q$. Euler characteristic and local Tate duality give
$$
\dim_E H^1=d\dim_E Q+\dim_E\operatorname{Hom}_G(Q,E(1)).
$$
Therefore the dimension of $Z^1/Z_f^1$ equals that Hom dimension, since coboundaries belong to both spaces.

The positive-weight exactness theorem `posExact` states that for an equivariant injection between positive-weight representations, a cocycle in the smaller one is finite if and only if its image is finite. It also gives surjectivity of finite cocycles for a quotient of a positive-weight representation. Its period-ring argument uses vanishing of vectors invariant both under Galois and crystalline Frobenius in the positive-weight quotient, lifting splitting vectors and adjusting by base-field coboundaries. By the injection assertion, the $Z^1/Z_f^1$ map from $W$ to $V$ is injective, and the two just-computed dimensions are the same. Thus the class of $c$ comes from a cocycle in $W$. Subtract that cocycle to get $c_f$. For $w\ge2$ there is no exceptional part.

## The rank-three consequence

For an upper triangular representation with diagonal inertia weights $(e,0,-e)$, the diagonal characters are crystalline unramified twists of cyclotomic powers. Put $u=M_{01}/\kappa_1$, $v=M_{12}/\kappa_2$, $z=M_{02}/\kappa_2$. Multiplication makes $u,v$ cocycles in the adjacent character ratios. The pair $(z,v)$ is a cocycle in $V_{01}\otimes\kappa_2^{-1}$, where $V_{01}$ is the first two coordinates. If $u,v$ are finite, $V_{01}$ is crystalline since its extension splits over the period ring. The twist is positive weight with subrepresentation of weight $2e$ and quotient of weight $e$. Lift $v$ to a finite cocycle there by `posExact`. The difference from $(z,v)$ is a cocycle in the $2e$ line and is automatically finite by what was proved. Thus the full extension is crystalline.
