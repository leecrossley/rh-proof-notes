# The extra factor of two in the graded length bound

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E5%88%86%E6%AC%A1%E6%88%AA%E9%9D%A2%E7%9A%84%E9%95%BF%E5%BA%A6%E7%95%8C%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E6%81%B0%E5%A5%BD%E5%A4%9A%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E4%BA%8C.md)

Use the assumptions and [cotangent reduction](the-graded-length-lemma-reduces-to-valuations-on-finite-orbits.md) of the graded length lemma, with $F_i-\pi^{b_i}y_i\in S_x$ for $1\le i\le r$, $F_*\in S_x$, and the cut graded chart $\phi:A\to F[[V]]$ eliminating all zero-weight variables, killing $Z_i$, and with $\phi(a)\ne0$. Its nonzero-weight linear axes and variable counts are unchanged. For every finite two-sided orbit cover $T$ we show
$$
2^{r+r'}\sum_i b_i\le2^{r+1+r'}\sum_{\xi\in T}v(\xi(a)).
$$
Use the [graded Euler characteristic rules](koszul-euler-characteristics-compare-graded-lengths.md), always at sufficiently large coordinatewise degrees.

**Three grading directions.** Put $B=A_0$ and $N_-=A_{-2}$, $N_+=A_2$, $N_*=BF_*^2+A_{-2}^2A_2$ (products mean the spans of products). Let $\mathcal R\subset A[T_-,T_+,T_*]$ be generated by $B,N_\nu T_\nu$ in the three directions. Thus a multidegree $\mathbf d=(d_-,d_+,d_*)$ has coefficients $N_-^{d_-}N_+^{d_+}N_*^{d_*}$. Use the section list
$$
\mathscr S=( (F_i^2T_-)_{i=1}^r,(G_iT_-)_{i=1}^s,
           (E_i^2T_+)_{i=1}^{r'},(E'_iT_+)_{i=1}^{s'},F_*^2T_*).
$$
Let $J$ consist of the elements whose coefficients are killed by $\phi$; $M_{\rm gen}=\mathcal R/J$ has $a$ regular since $\phi(a)\ne0$ in a domain, and $Z_i\in J$. Let $\mathfrak I$ be the product of the three unit-degree ideals, $D=(A_{-2}A_2)\subset B$. By the [boundary argument](large-trigraded-pieces-only-see-two-sided-orbits.md), a prime $P$ containing $(\mathscr S,Z_i)$ and avoiding $\mathfrak I$ contains $I\cap B$, and its contraction is maximal if it contains $D$. If it contains $a$, its contraction must also be maximal: otherwise $B/(P\cap B)$ is a finite $O$-domain of characteristic zero, with fraction field admitting an embedding into $K$. The corresponding degree-zero point would extend to a two-sided point killing $I$ by $D\not\subset P$ and would have $a=0$, impossible. Intersect all primes containing $J+(\mathscr S,a)$, then use finite generation to get a power of $\mathfrak m_B\mathcal R\,\mathfrak I$ in this ideal. This admits $(\mathscr S,a)$ on $M_{\rm gen}$ and $\mathscr S$ on $M_{\rm gen}/aM_{\rm gen}$. **Lowest weight layer.** In a chart monomial let $n_-,n_+$ be its total negative magnitude and total positive weight, so its weight is $n_+-n_-$. The coefficients in degree $\mathbf d$ have $n_-\ge 2d_-+4d_*$: this is immediate in the first two directions; the third has either two weight $-2$ factors or $F_*^2$, with $F_*$ weight $-1$ and no linear term, forcing $n_-\ge2$ on its terms. At equality the weight fixes $n_+=2d_++2d_*$. Let $\mathfrak q$ be the ideal of elements vanishing on this lowest layer. It is an ideal by additivity of the lower bound. It contains $J$ and $\ker(x|_B)$, since a weight-zero series without constant term has $n_-\ge1$. The ideal is $O$-saturated by its coefficient definition. Hence $L=\mathcal R/\mathfrak q$ is $\pi$-torsion-free and a quotient of $M_{\rm gen}/aM_{\rm gen}$. In $L$, $F_i^2T_-=\pi^{2b_i}y_i^2T_-$: the difference factors, and the factor $F_i-\pi^{b_i}y_i$ has no linear term, giving total $n_-\ge3$. [Critical ideal](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/CommutativeAlgebra/GradedModulesLength/LkHolds.lean#L8)

Let $H(\mathbf d)=\operatorname{length}_B(L/\pi L)_{\mathbf d}$. It counts monomials with the two prescribed totals. Indeed the lattice of surviving coefficients injects into the corresponding finite monomial coordinate space. For each weight of magnitude one the chart provides an element with only that axis at minimal weight on its side. For magnitude two, the minimal layer may additionally contain quadratic terms in weight-one variables on the same side. Subtract suitable products of their axis elements, clearing denominators by some nonzero $O$-scalar, to isolate each weight-two axis. A monomial of even weight on each side can be made of blocks of weight magnitude two: group the weight-one variables in pairs. There are $d_-+2d_*$ negative blocks and $d_++d_*$ positive blocks; assign two negative blocks and one positive block to each third-direction factor. Every coordinate axis of the critical space is thus attained rationally. The finite $O$-lattice is torsion-free and $B$ acts through $x$, so its rank is $H(\mathbf d)$. [Critical coefficient lattice](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/CommutativeAlgebra/GradedModulesLength/ReesGradedPieceSurjection.lean#L199)

Extract $\pi^{2b_i}$ from each of the first $r$ sections on $L$. The resulting lower bound on $\chi(\mathscr S;L)$ is $\sum_i2b_i$ times the difference of $H$ in directions $\mathbf e_-$ repeated $r-1+s$ times, $\mathbf e_+$ repeated $r'+s'$ times, and $\mathbf e_*$ once. For variables $X,Y$ tracking $n_-,n_+$, the count has generating function $(1-X)^{-r}(1-X^2)^{-s}(1-Y)^{-r'-1}(1-Y^2)^{-s'-1}$. The difference directions multiply it by $(1-X^2)^{r-1+s}(1-Y^2)^{r'+s'}(1-X^4Y^2)$, leaving
$$
(1+X)^{r-1}(1+Y)^{r'}\frac{1-X^4Y^2}{(1-X)(1-Y)(1-Y^2)} .
$$
Write $1-X^4Y^2=(1-Y^2)+Y^2(1-X^4)$. The second term is polynomial in $X$ after cancellation, so disappears at large negative-side totals. The first term leaves the denominator $(1-X)(1-Y)$; at large totals on both sides the coefficient is $2^{r-1+r'}$. The quotient comparison now gives $2^{r+r'}\sum_i b_i\le\chi(\mathscr S;M_{\rm gen}/aM_{\rm gen})_{\mathbf d}$. **Upper bound.** Regularity of $a$, permutation and removal of kernel terms give
$$
\chi(\mathscr S;M_{\rm gen}/aM_{\rm gen})_{\mathbf d}
=\chi(a,\mathscr S;M_{\rm gen})_{\mathbf d}
\le\operatorname{length}_B(M_{\mathbf d}/aM_{\mathbf d})-\operatorname{length}_B\ker(a|_{M_{\mathbf d}})
$$
where $M_{\mathbf d}=(\mathcal R/(J+(\mathscr S)))_{\mathbf d}$. The [orbit upper bound](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E6%AF%8F%E5%B9%B3%E6%96%B9%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E6%96%B9%E7%A8%8B%E8%87%B3%E5%A4%9A%E8%AE%A9%E5%B1%80%E9%83%A8%E9%95%BF%E5%BA%A6%E7%BF%BB%E5%80%8D.md) applies to any homogeneous ideal containing $\mathscr S,Z_i$, under assumptions 2–5 and rational spanning in weights $-1,-2$; for large $\mathbf d$ it bounds this length difference by $2^{r+1+r'}\sum_{\xi\in T}v(\xi(a))$. It uses that each localized graded piece with unsquared equations has length at most one over its two-sided specialization, each squaring at most doubles that length, and a valuation determinant accounts for multiplication by $a$. The ideal here contains $Z_i$ and all $\mathscr S$. Combining the two inequalities proves the result with the indicated powers of two.
