# Repeated blocks leave one direction per complex place

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E9%87%8D%E5%A4%8D%E7%9F%A9%E9%98%B5%E5%9D%97%E4%BB%A5%E5%90%8E%E5%8F%AA%E5%89%A9%E6%AF%8F%E4%B8%AA%E5%A4%8D%E5%A4%84%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E6%96%B9%E5%90%91.md)

## Statement

Let $L$ be totally imaginary, $G_N=\mathrm{GL}_N(O_L)$, and $k\ge0$ even with $k+1<r_2(L)$. For an integer $\ell>0$, $n\ge2(k+1)+4$ and $x\in H_{k+1}(G_n,\mathbb Z)$, define $z_0(x)=x$ and
$$
z_{i+1}(x)=\psi_\ell z_i(x)-\ell^{i+2}\iota z_i(x),\qquad 0\le i<k .
$$
The maps go from rank $\ell^i n$ to $\ell^{i+1}n$ by block repetition and identity stabilization. The subgroup generated by all $z_k(x)$ has rational rank at most $r_2(L)$. This is `BStarOdd`, which yields `BOdd` in [cycle value counting](bockstein-and-stable-rank-count-cycle-values.md).

## Two differential-form inputs

Let $X_N$ be the product of the positive definite Hermitian cones at the complex places, with the congruence action of $G_N$ and identity base point in each factor. Integrate an invariant $(k+1)$-form $\omega$ over the affine simplex whose vertices are the base point moved by $g_0,\ldots,g_{k+1}$. Extend this value $I_{\omega,N}$ linearly on real homogeneous bar chains.

- [`SSurj`](hecke-iteration-represents-cycle-integrals-by-invariant-forms.md) supplies, above any prescribed rank, an $N$ where every real homogeneous invariant $(k+1)$-cocycle on $G_N$ with trivial coefficients has the same cycle values as an $\omega$ invariant under $\prod_{w\mid\infty}\mathrm{GL}_N(\mathbb C)$.
- [`SWtOdd`](matrix-traces-give-weights-to-cycle-values.md) supplies functionals $\tau_{w,N}$ indexed by infinite places and defined in all ranks. They vanish on boundaries, preserve values under stabilization, and scale by $d$ under $d$ repeated blocks. For each $N,\omega$ there are constants $c_w$ and families $a_{j,N'}$ of weights $2\le j\le k+1$ with
$$
I_{\omega,N}(y)=\sum_w c_w\tau_{w,N}(y)+\sum_{j=2}^{k+1} a_{j,N}(y)
$$
on all cycles at rank $N$. Each $a_j$ also kills boundaries and preserves stabilization, with block repetition scaling by $d^j$.

## Proof

Real bar homology is obtained by tensoring integral homology with $\mathbb R$, by flatness, compatibly with group maps. Write $M=\ell^kn$ and let $C$ be the span of the real cycles $z_k(y)$ obtained by the same recurrence on real cycles $y$ of $G_n$. Their homology classes contain all the real images of the $z_k(x)$ in the statement.

For a weight-$j$ family the $i$th step multiplies its value by $\ell^j-\ell^{i+2}$. The product over the $k$ steps contains zero for $2\le j\le k+1$. Hence every higher-weight family vanishes on $C$. Consider $T:C\to\mathbb R^{r_2(L)}$, the vector of $\tau_{w,M}$ values. Suppose $T(y)=0$ but the homology class of $y$ is nonzero. Stabilize to the rank $N\ge M$ supplied by `SSurj`. Homological stability above $2(k+1)+4$ preserves the nonzero class. Choose a real functional nonzero on that class and extend it from cycles to chains. It is a cocycle because it kills boundaries. By `SSurj` it agrees on cycles with an invariant-form integral, nonzero at the stabilized $y$.

On the other hand `SWtOdd` expresses that integral as weight-one terms, zero by $T(y)=0$, and higher-weight terms, zero on $C$. Stabilization preserves these values. This contradiction gives $\ker T$ contained in the kernel of the homology map. Thus the image in real homology has dimension at most $r_2(L)$, bounding the integral rank.
