# Floor coefficients recover a generalized Bernoulli valuation

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%8B%E5%8F%96%E6%95%B4%E7%B3%BB%E6%95%B0%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E7%BB%99%E5%87%BA%E5%B9%BF%E4%B9%89Bernoulli%E6%95%B0%E7%9A%84%E8%B5%8B%E5%80%BC.md)

Let \(\ell\) be odd, \(O\subset\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\) a DVR finite over \(\mathbb Z_\ell\), \(f\ge1\), \(e\ge1\), \(k=e+1\), and \(\psi\) a primitive Dirichlet character of conductor \(f\), valued in \(O\), with \(\psi(-1)=(-1)^k\). Let \(\psi_G\) be its character on \(G_{\mathbb Q}\) via \(\mu_f\), and \(\alpha=\varepsilon_\ell^{-e}\psi_G^{-1}\). Assume \(\alpha(g)-\varepsilon_\ell(g)\) is a unit for some \(g\). Define \(P_k=\prod_{p\le k+1}p\), \(B_{k,\psi}=f^{k-1}\sum_{u=1}^f\psi(u)B_k(u/f)\), and \(a=fP_k B_{k,\psi}\in O\), with \(B_1(X)=X-1/2\). For each \(a_0\in O\setminus\{0\}\) there exist \(m\ge1\), \(F=(f/\ell^{v_\ell(f)})\ell^m\), \(0\le b<F\), and lifts \(\sigma_t\) acting by \(t\) on \(\mu_F\) for each unit \(t\bmod F\), such that with standard residues \([t]\),
$$
\theta_b(t)=\left\lfloor\frac{b[t]}F\right\rfloor-
             \left\lfloor\frac{b[-t]}F\right\rfloor,\qquad
z=\sum_t\theta_b(t)\alpha(\sigma_t)^{-1},
$$
we have \(a_0z\mid\ell^m\), \(z\mid a\). Also \(\alpha\equiv1\bmod\ell^mO\) on the fixing subgroup of \(\mu_F\) and \(\alpha\equiv-1\bmod\ell^mO\) on inversions of \(\mu_F\). **Coefficient congruence.** Put \(D_k=2kP_k\), \(H(x)=F^{k-1}B_k(x/F)/k\). By von Staudt–Clausen the denominators of each \(B_j(0)\), \(j\le k\), divide \(P_k\): for positive even \(j\), the possible primes have \(p-1\mid j\); degrees \(0,1\) and odd \(j>1\) use \(1,-1/2,0\). Expanding the polynomial gives
$$
H(x)=x^k/(kF)-x^{k-1}/2+(2F/D_k)z_x,\qquad z_x\in\mathbb Z.
$$
It also proves \(a\in O\), since multiplying the original Bernoulli sum by \(fP_k\) clears the coefficients. For \(b\) prime to \(F>1\), let \(u=[t]\), \(y=[bt]\), \(j=\lfloor bu/F\rfloor\). Since \(bu=y+Fj\), the quotient for \(b[-t]\) is \(b-j-1\), so \(\theta_b(t)=2j-(b-1)\). Insert \(y=bu-Fj\) in the expansion, using \(y^k=(bu)^k-kFj(bu)^{k-1}\bmod F^2\) and \(y^{k-1}=(bu)^{k-1}\bmod F\). The leading term of \(2(H(y)-b^kH(u))\) is \(-2j(bu)^{k-1}\) up to terms killed modulo \(F\) after multiplication by \(D_k\). The next term contributes \((b-1)(bu)^{k-1}\). Together they cancel \(b^{k-1}\theta_b(t)u^{k-1}\). Thus \(D_k[b^{k-1}\theta_b(t)u^{k-1}+2(H(y)-b^kH(u))]\in F\mathbb Z\).

For a character \(\chi\) modulo \(F\), put \(S_b=\sum_t\theta_b(t)[t]^{k-1}\chi(t)\). Multiply the congruence by \(\chi(bt)\) and sum. Since \(t\mapsto bt\) permutes units, the two \(H\)-sums are \(B_{k,\chi}/k\) and \(\chi(b)B_{k,\chi}/k\). Consequently for \(\ell^m\mid F\),
$$
\left|D_k\bigl(b^{k-1}\chi(b)S_b-2(\chi(b)b^k-1) B_{k,\chi}/k\bigr)\right|_\ell\le|\ell^m|_\ell .
$$
The ultrametric inequality preserves the individual error because character values on units have norm one. **Nonvanishing and induction of the character.** Embed the finite root-of-unity field of coefficients in \(\mathbb C\). The special value formula \(L(1-k,\psi)=-B_{k,\psi}/k\) and primitive functional equation give
$$
|L(1-k,\psi)|=\frac{2(k-1)! f^{k-1/2}}{(2\pi)^k}\,|L(k,\psi^{-1})|.
$$
The parity assumption is the one in this formula. The Euler product at \(k\ge2\) is absolutely convergent and nonzero, hence \(B_{k,\psi}\ne0\). For a multiple \(M\) of \(f\), the induced character \(\psi^{(M)}\) has \(B_{k,\psi^{(M)}}=B_{k,\psi}\prod_{p\mid M}(1-\psi(p)p^{k-1})\). Indeed on raising the modulus to \(fp\), summation with the original periodic coefficients is unchanged by \(\sum_{j=0}^{p-1} B_k((x+j)/p)=p^{1-k}B_k(x)\), from the generating function. Removing multiples \(u=pv\) subtracts \(\psi(p)p^{k-1}B_{k,\psi}\); a prime already dividing the current modulus contributes no further factor. Thus for \(m\ge v_\ell(f)\) and \(\chi=\psi^{(F)}\), \(|B_{k,\chi}/k|_\ell=|B_{k,\psi}/k|_\ell>0\), since only \(\ell\) can be new and \(k-1\ge1\). **Exact valuation.** Choose \(m\ge\max(1,v_\ell(f))\) with \(|\ell^m|_\ell<|a_0|_\ell |D_k|_\ell |B_{k,\psi}/k|_\ell\). For the element \(g\) with unit difference choose \(b\) its exponent on \(\mu_F\). Then \(\varepsilon_\ell(g)\alpha(g)^{-1}-1\) is a unit, congruent to \(\chi(b)b^k-1\bmod\ell^mO\), which is therefore a unit as well. With \(2\) a unit, the weighted congruence now has strictly smaller error than the Bernoulli term after division by \(D_k\). Therefore \(|S_b|_\ell=|B_{k,\psi}/k|_\ell\). Since \(\alpha(\sigma_t)^{-1}\equiv[t]^{k-1}\chi(t)\bmod\ell^mO\), the difference \(z-S_b\) is again strictly smaller in norm. Hence \(|z|_\ell=|B_{k,\psi}/k|_\ell\). By choice of \(m\), \(|\ell^m|_\ell<|a_0z|_\ell\), and \(|a|_\ell\le|B_{k,\psi}|_\ell\le|z|_\ell\); both give the required divisibilities in the DVR. On a fixing element, the cyclotomic congruence is \(1\) and the character is \(1\). On an inversion the corresponding signs yield \((-1)^{e+k}=-1\). This gives the two congruences for [auxiliary-prime induction](auxiliary-primes-bound-the-cohomology-length-by-one-valuation.md).
