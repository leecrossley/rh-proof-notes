# Parallel Hasse directions can hit only one column per minor

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $K$ be a field and $P(Z)$ a product of determinant minors, with variables $Z_{s,a,b}$ grouped by slot $s$. An individual factor at slot $s$ has the form
$$
\det\biggl(\sum_a T_{ia}Z_{s,a,I(j)}\biggr)_{1\le i,j\le h},
$$
where $T$ is a fixed coefficient matrix and $I$ is a list of chosen column indices. For directions $B_k$ and multiplicities $\beta_k\ge0$ of finite support, the mixed Hasse derivative means $[u^\beta]P(Z+\sum_k u_k B_k)$. Let $\mathcal H$ be a subset of those directions, all in one slot $s_0$. Suppose each direction $k\in\mathcal H$ affects a single column $b_k$, with the same vector $c=(c_a)_a$ in that column. Then:

- If the derivative is nonzero, $\sum_{k\in\mathcal H}\beta_k$ is at most the number of determinant factors at $s_0$.
- If every $b_k$ for $k\in\mathcal H$ belongs to a prescribed set $J$ of columns, the same sum is at most $\sum_{C\text{ at }s_0}|\operatorname{im}I_C\cap J|$.

These statements hold over every characteristic. In the served jet they are `HitsCapPos` and `HitsColumnsPos`. Treat the parameters of directions outside $\mathcal H$ as part of the base matrix, over the polynomial ring they generate. A factor away from $s_0$ is independent of the parameters in $\mathcal H$. In a factor at $s_0$, the extra column $j$ arising from $\mathcal H$ equals the fixed vector $Tc$ times $\sum_{k\in\mathcal H:b_k=I(j)}u_k$. Expand by multilinearity in the columns. Any term with two extra columns vanishes because they are multiples of the same vector. Hence the factor has degree at most one in the parameters from $\mathcal H$, and degree zero if its chosen columns do not meet $J$. A factor with any repeated chosen column is identically zero under all perturbations. The degree bound for the product now follows by summing the degree bounds for its factors. It is at most the number of factors with a chosen column in $J$, which is at most the stated sum of cardinalities. A nonzero coefficient in that polynomial must obey both bounds. [Hasse expansion of the product](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Formalism/ThetaLogCoordinates/UniformThetaSubstitution/FrobeniusLiftNorms/FinsuppStepInduction.lean#L227)

In the application a direction indexed by source slot $s$, level $e$, and column $b$ goes to slot $s+2e+1$, with vector $(\xi_{s,a}^{p^{2e+1}})_a$. For fixed $s,e$ the vector is the same for every $b$, so the first bound gives the target capacity. The column bound is also used with multiple sources reaching one target. In this use the directions need only be supported in the specified target columns. Multilinearity limits the degree by the total number of eligible columns across the factors even when their vectors differ.
