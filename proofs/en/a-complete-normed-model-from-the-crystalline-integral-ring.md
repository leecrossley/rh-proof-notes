# A complete normed model from the crystalline integral ring

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E7%94%A8%E6%99%B6%E4%BD%93%E6%95%B4%E7%8E%AF%E6%9E%84%E9%80%A0%E5%8F%AF%E6%B1%82%E5%92%8C%E7%9A%84%E8%B5%8B%E8%8C%83%E5%91%A8%E6%9C%9F%E7%8E%AF.md)

Let \(L/\mathbb Q_\ell\) be finite with integer ring \(O\). Use the project's `LocTied` period model: \(A_{\inf}=W(\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_\ell^\flat})\), \(\theta\) the Fontaine map, and \(A_{\rm cris}\) the \(\ell\)-adic completion of the divided-power envelope along \(\ker\theta\), with its maps, actions and crystalline period \(\mathbf t\). Put \(A=O\otimes_{\mathbb Z_\ell}A_{\rm cris}\), \(M=A[1/\ell]\). Then \(M\) has a complete submultiplicative norm with \(\|x\|\le\ell^{-j}\) equivalent to \(x\in\ell^j A\) and \(\|\ell x\|=\ell^{-1}\|x\|\). Its comparison \(\kappa:M\to L\otimes B_{\rm cris}\) extends the integral one, the coefficient embedding is bounded and Frobenius and Galois are continuous. For \(\mu_s=[\varepsilon^{1/\ell^s}]-1\), \(s\ge0\), there are \(C_s>0\), \(0<r_s<1\) with \(\|\mu_s^j\|\le C_s r_s^j\) for all \(j\ge0\). For each coefficient embedding \(\iota:L\to B_{\rm dR}\) and \(m\ge0\), the inverse image of \({\rm Fil}^m B_{\rm dR}\) under \(F=(\iota\otimes1)\kappa\) is closed.

**Norm.** A finite \(\mathbb Z_\ell\)-basis of \(O\) identifies \(A\) additively with finitely many copies of the \(\ell\)-torsion-free, complete, separated \(A_{\rm cris}\). For \(x\ne0\), the integer \(v(x)=\max\{j\in\mathbb Z:x\in\ell^j A\}\) exists by localization and separatedness. Set \(\|x\|=\ell^{-v(x)}\) and \(\|0\|=0\). Addition and multiplication of the powers of \(A\) give the ultrametric and submultiplicative inequalities. Multiplication by \(\ell\) shifts the exponent by one. Subtract a fixed late term of a Cauchy sequence to put its tail in \(A\), where it converges, then add the term back. The integral actions extend to \(M\) and preserve the powers of \(A\), hence are continuous. If \(0\ne x\in L\), choose \(m\in\mathbb Z\) with \(\ell^{-1}<|\ell^m x|\le1\). Integrality of the coefficient embedding at \(\ell^m x\) gives \(\|c(x)\|\le\ell^m<\ell|x|\). **Cyclotomic powers.** Put \(q=[\varepsilon^{1/\ell}]\), \(\eta=\sum_{j=0}^{\ell-1}q^j\). Since \(\theta(q)\) is primitive of order \(\ell\), \(\eta\in\ker\theta\). Divided powers give \(\eta^\ell\in\ell A_{\rm cris}\). Modulo \(\ell\), \(\eta=(q-1)^{\ell-1}\) and \(q^\ell-1=(q-1)^\ell\), hence \(\mu_0^{\ell-1}=0\). Also \(\mu_s^{\ell^s}=\mu_0\bmod\ell\). Thus \(d=\ell^s(\ell-1)>0\) satisfies \(\mu_s^d\in\ell A\). For \(j=dq_j+r_j\), \(0\le r_j<d\), \(\mu_s^j\in\ell^{q_j}A\). This gives \(r_s=\ell^{-1/d}\), \(C_s=\ell\). The partial sums of \(\log(1+\mu_0)\) give the prescribed \(\mathbf t\): the formal result expresses the convergence by clearing the factorial denominators in \(A\) and dividing back in \(M\). **Closed filtration.** For fixed \(\iota,m\) put \(\Lambda_{m,n}={\rm Fil}^m B_{\rm dR}+\ell^n W_{\inf}\), with \(W_{\inf}\) the image of integral Witt vectors. The intersection over \(n\ge0\) is \({\rm Fil}^m\): choose a Witt representative modulo \({\rm Fil}^m\) at \(n=0\), then use \(\ell\)-adic separatedness of \(A_{\inf}/(\xi^m)\), \(\xi\) a generator of \(\ker\theta\). The bounded integral comparison at level \(m\) gives a common \(C\) with \(\ell^C F(A)\subset\Lambda_{m,0}\). It uses a finite basis of \(O\) and the finitely many divided-power degrees surviving in the truncated de Rham ring. For a norm limit \(x\) of elements \(x'\) in \(F^{-1}({\rm Fil}^m)\), approximate to \(x-x'\in\ell^{n+C}A\) for each \(n\). Then \(F(x-x')\in\ell^n\Lambda_{m,0}\subset\Lambda_{m,n}\) and \(F(x')\in{\rm Fil}^m\), so intersection proves the claim.
