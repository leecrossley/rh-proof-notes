# One character coordinate per new ramification orbit

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%AF%8F%E4%B8%AA%E6%96%B0%E5%88%86%E6%AD%A7%E5%A4%84%E4%B8%BA%E4%BD%95%E5%8F%AA%E5%A2%9E%E5%8A%A0%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E7%89%B9%E5%BE%81%E5%9D%90%E6%A0%87.md)

Let $F$ be a number field, $\Omega$ an algebraic closure and $G=\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/F)$ with the Krull topology. For a valuation ring $B$ of $\Omega$ write $v_B$ for the multiplicative valuation and $I_B$ for the subgroup preserving $B$ and acting trivially on its residue field. For $V\le G$, a prime $\ell$ and $N\in\mathbb N$, let $C_\ell(V;N)$ consist of locally constant homomorphisms $V\to(\mathbb F_\ell,+)$ vanishing on $I_B\cap V$ whenever $v_B(N)=1$. For $N>0$, these may ramify only above prime divisors of $N$; $N=0$ imposes no vanishing. This is `CharOff` for the indicated set of inertia subgroups.

For $N,p,n,k\in\mathbb N$, assume $(p,\ell)=1$, the $V$-action on $\{B:v_B(p)<1\}$ has at most $k$ orbits, and every such $I_B$ lies in $V$. If $C_\ell(V;N)$ is finite of size at most $n$, then $C_\ell(V;Np)$ is finite of size at most $n\ell^k$. **A single inertia coordinate.** If $v_B(\ell)=1$, there is a $\gamma_B\in I_B$ such that every locally constant additive $\mathbb F_\ell$-valued character of $I_B$ vanishing at $\gamma_B$ is zero. To construct it, set $P_B=\{\sigma\in I_B:v_B(\sigma y/y-1)<1\text{ for all }y\in\Omega^\times\}$. Valuations of nonzero rational integers are nonnegative powers of $v_B(r)$ for a single prime $r$ or $r=1$, since Bézout prevents two distinct primes having valuation below one. Choose $r_j^{j!}=r$. The ratios $\sigma(r_j)/r_j$ modulo principal units define homomorphisms $\theta_j$ on $I_B$ with finite cyclic image, since inertia acts trivially on unit residues and the ratios are $j!$-th roots of unity. The kernels are closed by local constancy. They decrease: $r_j/r_{j+1}^{j+1}$ is a unit and its ratio under inertia is a principal unit. Their intersection is $P_B$. Indeed any $y\ne0$ satisfies $v_B(y)^m=v_B(r)^b$ for $m>0,b\in\mathbb Z$ by comparing two terms of maximal valuation in an integral polynomial vanishing at $y$. For $m\mid j!$, $y/r_j^{bj!/m}$ is a unit, so every $\sigma\in\ker\theta_j$ acts trivially on its unit residue and has $\sigma(y)/y$ principal.

By compactness of $I_B$, the decreasing closed sets of generators in these cyclic quotients have a common element $\gamma_B$. In the closure of its powers, matching the $\theta_j$ of any given $\sigma\in I_B$ defines another decreasing family of nonempty closed sets. Thus some power in the closure agrees with $\sigma$ modulo $P_B$. Each locally constant character $t$ of $I_B$ kills $P_B$. To see this choose a finite layer $L/F$ whose fixing subgroup in $I_B$ is in $\ker t$. For $\sigma\in P_B$, some $\sigma^{m\ell^a}$ fixes $L$ with $m$ prime to $\ell$, by considering a finite normal closure. Put $\tau=\sigma^m$, still in $P_B$. If $x\in L$ and $d=\tau x-x\ne0$, then $\sum_{j<\ell^a}\tau^j d=0$. Divide by $d$ and subtract $1$ in each summand to express $-\ell^a$ as a sum of terms of valuation $<1$, impossible since $v_B(\ell)=1$. Thus $\tau$ fixes $L$, and $mt(\sigma)=0$ implies $t(\sigma)=0$. Killing $\gamma_B$ as well makes the closed kernel all of $I_B$. **Counting.** Choose one representative $B$ from each of the at most $k$ orbits, in a set $T$. Bézout gives $v_B(\ell)=1$ if $v_B(p)<1$. Evaluate $t\in C_\ell(V;Np)$ at $\gamma_B\in V$ for $B\in T$, giving a vector in $\mathbb F_\ell^T$. For a character in the kernel, check inertia at each $A$ with $v_A(N)=1$. If $v_A(p)=1$, its vanishing follows from the condition at $Np$. Otherwise $A=vB$ for $v\in V$, and $I_A=vI_Bv^{-1}$; the single inertia coordinate makes $t$ vanish on $I_B$, hence on $I_A$ by conjugacy. So the kernel is contained in $C_\ell(V;N)$ and each nonempty fiber has at most $n$ elements. There are at most $\ell^k$ fibers. This bound is used twice in the [quadratic tower](uniform-generator-count-for-the-varying-two-adic-tower.md).
