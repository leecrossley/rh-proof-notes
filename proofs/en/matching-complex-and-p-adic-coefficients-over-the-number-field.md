# Matching complex and p-adic coefficients over the number field

[Proof catalogue](../../PROOFS.en.md) · [Reading and review status](../../STATUS.md)

Let $L$ be a number field with embeddings $e:L\to\mathbb C$ and $i:L\to\mathbb C_p$, where $\mathbb C_p$ is the completed algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q_p$. There is an embedding $\lambda:\overline{\mathbb Q}\to\mathbb C_p$ on the algebraic numbers in $\mathbb C$ with $\lambda\circ e=i$, and any such $\lambda$ extends to a field isomorphism $\iota:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C_p$. The Lean source uses algebraic closedness of both fields. The purpose of the field isomorphism is to compare values of rational kernel coefficients at the chosen place. [Extending an embedding of the number field](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/SatakeParameters/NumberFieldEmbMemAlgebraicClosure.lean#L13) · [Extending to a ring equivalence](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/JetHarmonics/MkLeContinuumOfDenseRange.lean#L65)

Since $L/\mathbb Q$ is algebraic, $e(L)\subseteq\overline{\mathbb Q}$. Regard $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ as an algebraic extension of $L$ via $e$. The embedding $i$ into an algebraically closed field extends to that algebraic extension: a partial extension can always be extended across one algebraic element by taking a root of its transported minimal polynomial, and a maximal partial embedding given by Zorn's lemma is therefore defined on the full extension. This gives $\lambda$.

The cardinalities of both $\mathbb C$ and $\mathbb C_p$ are the continuum. For the upper bound on $\mathbb C_p$, $\mathbb Q$ is countable and dense in $\mathbb Q_p$, so each element is the unique limit of a chosen rational sequence. Passing to its algebraic closure does not increase an infinite cardinality because each polynomial has only finitely many roots. Passing to the metric completion gives at most the continuum again, since the set of countable sequences from a set of continuum cardinality still has continuum cardinality. For the lower bound one can inject binary sequences into $\mathbb Z_p$ by their base-$p$ expansions. The formal source gets this lower bound by the existence of binary branching in a nonempty perfect complete metric space. Now choose transcendence bases over the countable algebraic base $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ in both fields, using $\lambda$ on the $p$-adic side. Each basis has continuum cardinality. Indeed the field is algebraic over the rational function field on its basis, whose cardinality is at most the larger of the basis cardinality and $\aleph_0$. The reverse inequality follows because the basis lies in the field. A bijection between the bases extends $\lambda$ to an isomorphism of the rational function fields. Each ambient field is an algebraic closure of its rational function field. Extend the isomorphism to those closures by the same root argument. The image is algebraically closed and the target is algebraic over the rational function field it contains, so the extension is surjective. [Matching the transcendence bases](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-lean/blob/9110120cdc195ee8d2fb08d2adac8b1747912d8f/lean/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/JetHarmonics/MkLeContinuumOfDenseRange.lean#L9)

At a served site choose a local coefficient embedding into $\mathbb C_p$, integral on the local integer ring and compatible with the local unramified embeddings. Composing with the local comparison of $L$ gives the map $i$ above. The resulting equality $\iota(e(z))=i(z)$ for $z\in L$ is the equality used to read the series entries in the site construction.
