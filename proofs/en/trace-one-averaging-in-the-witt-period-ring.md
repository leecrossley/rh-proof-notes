# Trace-one averaging in the Witt period ring

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E5%9C%A8Witt%E5%91%A8%E6%9C%9F%E7%8E%AF%E9%87%8C%E7%94%A8%E8%BF%B9%E4%B8%80%E5%85%83%E6%B6%88%E5%8E%BB%E5%B1%80%E9%83%A8%E4%BD%99%E9%97%AD%E9%93%BE.md)

Let \(K_0/\mathbb Q_\ell\) be finite unramified, \(K_\infty=K_0(\mu_{\ell^\infty})\), and \(H=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell/K_\infty)\). Let \(O\) be the integers in a finite extension of \(\mathbb Q_\ell\) and \(T\) a finite free \(O\)-module with continuous \(H\)-action. Set \(W=W(\mathbb C_\ell^\flat)\) and \(P=(O\otimes_{\mathbb Z_\ell}W)\otimes_O T\) with diagonal action. Every continuous cocycle \(c:H\to T\) becomes a coboundary in \(P\): \(1\otimes c(h)=hb-b\) for some \(b\in P\) and all \(h\). Also \(\varphi-1\) on \(P\), with Frobenius on the Witt factor, is surjective with kernel \(1\otimes T\). These three facts are the inputs called `FNatW`.

**A trace-one element.** For an open normal \(H_1\subset H\) let \(F/K_\infty\) be its finite Galois fixed extension, and take representatives \(S\) for \(H/H_1\). Put \(\rho_\ell=\ell^{-1/(\ell-1)}\) and choose \(a\in K_\infty\) with \(\rho_\ell<|a|<1\). For any \(b_0\in\mathcal O_F\), take \(\beta^\ell=b_0\) in a finite Galois extension \(F'/K_\infty\) containing \(F\). The [almost-surjectivity of integral trace](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E5%88%86%E5%9C%86%E5%A1%94%E9%A1%B6%E4%B8%8A%E7%9A%84%E6%95%B4%E6%95%B0%E8%BF%B9%E5%87%A0%E4%B9%8E%E6%BB%A1%E5%B0%84.md) gives \(e\in\mathcal O_{F'}\) of trace \(a\) to \(K_\infty\). Thus \(d=\operatorname{Tr}_{F'/F}e\) has \(|d|\ge|a|\). Each conjugate of \(\beta\) over \(F\) differs from it by a quantity of norm at most \(\rho_\ell\), by the \(\ell\)-th roots of unity and integrality of \(\beta\). Hence
$$
b_1=\frac{\operatorname{Tr}_{F'/F}(e\beta)}{d}
\quad\text{has}\quad |b_1-\beta|\le r=\rho_\ell/|a|<1 .
$$
In particular \(b_1\in\mathcal O_F\) and \(|b_1^\ell-b_0|\le r\) by the difference-of-powers factorization.

First choose \(b_0\) so that its trace to \(K_\infty\) has norm \(>\max(r,|\ell|)\), using integral trace and a sufficiently high cyclotomic root minus one. Repeat the preceding step to obtain \(b_j\) with \(|b_{j+1}^\ell-b_j|\le r\), and choose \(k\) with \(r^{\ell^k}\le|\ell|\). Then \(x_i=b_{i+k}^{\ell^k}\bmod\ell\) form a compatible sequence under \(\ell\)-th powers: the difference modulo \(\ell\) is the \(\ell^k\)-th power of the corresponding error. Thus \(x\) is an \(H_1\)-fixed tilt integer. Raising integral elements to powers \(\ell\) does not increase distances, so \(|b_k^{\ell^k}-b_0|\le r\). The trace also does not increase errors; its value on \(b_k^{\ell^k}\) has the same norm as that on \(b_0\), hence exceeds \(|\ell|\). The trace of \(x\) has nonzero zeroth coordinate and is \(H\)-fixed. Dividing by it in the tilt field gives an \(H_1\)-fixed \(\beta_0\) of trace one. The Witt trace \(u=\sum_{s\in S}s([\beta_0])\) is \(1\bmod\ell\), so is \(H\)-fixed and a unit by completeness. The element \(\beta=[\beta_0]/u\) has Witt trace one.

**Cocycle averaging.** To precision \(\ell^{r_0}\), choose an open normal \(H_1\) with \(c(H_1)\subset\ell^{r_0}T\) by continuity at the identity. Set \(b_{r_0}=-\sum_{s\in S}s(\beta)(1\otimes c(s))\). Writing \(hs=s' u\), \(u\in H_1\), the cocycle identity gives
$$
h b_{r_0}\equiv -\sum_{s'\in S}s'(\beta)\bigl(c(s')-c(h)\bigr)\pmod{\ell^{r_0}P}.
$$
Summing the trace-one coefficients gives \(hb_{r_0}-b_{r_0}\equiv c(h)\). The same formula works for a \(P\)-valued cocycle tending to zero to arbitrary \(\ell\)-adic precision near the identity. Solve first modulo \(\ell\), divide the residual cocycle by \(\ell\), and repeat. After each correction this continuity at the identity persists: the correction is a finite sum of coefficients fixed by open subgroups times lattice vectors, and the original action on lattice vectors is continuous. Dividing by \(\ell\) just shifts the required precision. The corrections \(\ell^j d_j\) sum in the finite free \(W\)-module \(P\) by completeness. For each \(h\) its action preserves the filtration, so the resulting sum solves the equality.

**Frobenius.** In \(W/\ell W=\mathbb C_\ell^\flat\), algebraic closedness solves \(x^\ell-x=y\). Lift a solution and repeat on the error divided by \(\ell\) to solve \((\varphi-1)w=y\) in \(W\). The fixed Witt coordinates lie in \(\mathbb F_\ell\), so the kernel is \(\mathbb Z_\ell\). A \(\mathbb Z_\ell\)-basis of \(T\) transports both statements to \(P\).
