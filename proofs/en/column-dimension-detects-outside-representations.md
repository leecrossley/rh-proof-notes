# Column dimensions detect representations outside the good locus

[Chinese proof](../zh/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E5%88%97%E4%B8%8A%E7%9A%84%E7%BB%B4%E6%95%B0%E7%95%8C%E8%83%BD%E6%A3%80%E6%B5%8B%E5%A5%BD%E7%82%B9%E9%9B%86%E5%90%88%E4%B9%8B%E5%A4%96%E7%9A%84%E8%A1%A8%E7%A4%BA.md)

Let $A$ be a ring with a central map $T'\to A$ from a commutative ring. Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over a field $K$, with $A$ acting linearly on the left. For homomorphisms $r:G\to A^\times$, $\chi:G\to(T')^\times$, write $u(g)=\chi(g)^{-1}r(g)$. Suppose $E,e_1\in A$ and $\delta\in T'$ satisfy $E^2=\delta E$, $e_1^2=e_1$. On $V$, assume that $\delta$ acts by a scalar $\delta_x\in K^\times$, the images $EV,e_1V$ are one-dimensional and no nonzero vector of $EV$ is fixed by all $u(g)$.

For each column $l$ of a finite index set, let $D_l\subseteq G$ be a subgroup, $j_l\ge0$ an integer, and $K_{la},K^*_{l0}:G\to A$ arbitrary functions for $1\le a\le j_l$. Put $d_l(v)(g)=(u(g)-1)v$ on $D_l$. Let $L_l\subseteq(D_l\to V)$ be a $K$-subspace containing $\operatorname{im}d_l$ and all functions $g\mapsto K_{la}(g)s$ and $g\mapsto K^*_{l0}(g)\eta$ for $s\in e_1V$, $\eta\in EV$. Assume
$$
\dim_K(L_l/\operatorname{im}d_l)\le j_l+\dim_K\ker d_l.
\tag{1}
$$

**Proposition.** The column relation module $N$ defined below admits a nonzero $A$-linear map to $V$. This is `columnJ`.

## The relation module

Let $Y\subseteq A$ be the left ideal generated by $y(g)=(u(g)-1)E$. Start from the direct sum of $Y$ and the free module on $n_l,s_{la},z_l$ for each column. The quotient $N$ (`ColNNJ`) has relations $En_l=0$, $(1-e_1)s_{la}=0$, $(\delta-E)z_l=0$ and, for each $g\in D_l$,
$$
y(g)+(u(g)-1)n_l=\delta\left(\sum_{a=1}^{j_l}K_{la}(g)s_{la}+K^*_{l0}(g)z_l\right).
\tag{2}
$$

## A nonzero map satisfying the relations

Take $0\ne s\in e_1V$ and $\eta=Ew\ne0$, so $e_1s=s$, $E\eta=\delta_x\eta$. In each column we find a nonzero tuple $(v_l,(\alpha_{la})_a,\beta_l)$ with
$$
d_l(v_l)=\sum_a\alpha_{la}K_{la}s+\beta_l K^*_{l0}\eta
\tag{3}
$$
on $D_l$. If $\ker d_l\ne0$, choose a nonzero $v_l$ in it with all coefficients zero. Otherwise the coefficients on the right map from $K^{j_l+1}$ into a quotient of dimension at most $j_l$ by (1). Choose a nonzero tuple of coefficients in the kernel and take $v_l$ realizing the right side as a coboundary.

If $Ev_{l_0}=0$ in some column $l_0$, send $Y$ and the other columns to zero. In column $l_0$ take $n_{l_0}\mapsto\delta_xv_{l_0}$, $s_{l_0a}\mapsto\alpha_{l_0a}s$, $z_{l_0}\mapsto\beta_{l_0}\eta$. The first three relations are immediate from the action of $E,e_1,\delta$. Equation (2) is (3) multiplied by $\delta_x$. The map is nonzero because the tuple is nonzero and $\delta_x,s,\eta$ are nonzero.

In the other case each $Ev_l\ne0$. Rescale each tuple so that $Ev_l=\eta$, possible since $EV$ is a line. On $Y$ use $y\mapsto yw$ and on the generators take $n_l\mapsto\delta_xv_l-\eta$, $s_{la}\mapsto\alpha_{la}s$, $z_l\mapsto\beta_l\eta$. Then $E(\delta_xv_l-\eta)=0$. In (2) the term $(u(g)-1)\eta$ from $y(g)$ cancels the negative copy from $n_l$, leaving (3) multiplied by $\delta_x$. The map is nonzero because some $u(g)$ moves $\eta$. If there are no columns the same map on $Y$ already works.

At each point outside the good locus, the project gives the above hypotheses for the evaluation representation $V$. The resulting nonzero map forces vanishing of the determinant used in the [congruence ideal comparison](one-determinant-for-the-congruence-ideal.md).
